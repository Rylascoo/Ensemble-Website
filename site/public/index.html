A living Production

Build the conditions.
Let the people change them.

Establish people, circumstances, knowledge, relationships, possibilities, and pressures. AI performers portray persistent characters inside the world you create.

Persistent characters

People,
not prompts.

A Character is more than the model performing them. Identity, history, relationships, and what they have lived through can persist beyond any single performance.

The result is creative continuity: later scenes can belong to historically specific people rather than starting over from a blank conversation.

History with consequence

What happens
changes what comes next.

Accepted performances become causal history. Consequences can alter the conditions that future scenes inherit, so continuity is more than a transcript of what was said.

Creator authority

The Production
remains yours.

Kymaean can assist, perform, interpret, and propose. It does not silently replace creator authority. The creative world remains under your control.

Local-first by design

Your world should belong to you.

Local-first means local sovereignty: the complete Production remains under your control. When external models are used, Kymaean shares only the bounded context needed for a performance.
