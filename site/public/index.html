KYMÆAN / IN DEVELOPMENT

A generative theater for persistent characters.

Kymaean is a local-first creative simulation for people, circumstances, relationships, possibilities, and pressures. AI performers portray the cast; accepted performances become part of the world’s continuing history.

A native Windows experience is currently in development.

01 Characters persist.

Identity continues through changing scenes, context, and performance.

02 Actions leave consequence.

What is accepted into history changes what can happen next.

03 The creator remains author.

AI performs inside the world you establish; it does not replace your authority.
