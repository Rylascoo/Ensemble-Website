Wordmark Visual Reset 07

Only the singular K is treated as a finished identity object. The rest of the name is deliberately allowed to behave like typography again. These are personality studies, not final font selections: if one feels right, its proportions and rhythm can be redrawn as deterministic bespoke vectors.

Restrained, narrow, spacious and almost anonymous. The K carries nearly all of the symbolic density.
