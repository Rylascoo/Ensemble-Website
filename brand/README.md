# Brand masters

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `threshold-k-ivory.svg`, `threshold-k-black.svg` | The Threshold K symbol |
| `kymaean-wordmark-ivory.svg`, `kymaean-wordmark-black.svg` | The KYMÆAN wordmark, with the K built in |

Colours: ivory `#ddd5c7` on black; black `#111111` on light grounds.

The wordmark is custom monoline lettering; it is not set from a font. Do not retype it, redraw it
or substitute letters. Keep clear space around it of at least the height of the K.

Raster and image masters (the stage image and any future artwork) live in Google Drive,
`Ensemble Project`. The deployable stage derivative is `site/public/assets/img/stage.webp`;
its provenance is recorded in `docs/DESIGN.md`.
