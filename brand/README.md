# Brand masters

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `threshold-k-ivory.svg`, `threshold-k-black.svg` | The Threshold K: app icon, favicon, and the mark on its own |
| `kymaean-wordmark-ivory.svg`, `kymaean-wordmark-black.svg` | The name, "Kymaean hand" cut lettering |
| `kymaean-lockup-stacked-cut-*.svg` | K above the name, the K cut as an outline (preferred beside the name) |
| `kymaean-lockup-stacked-*.svg` | K above the name, solid K |
| `wordmark/` | The generator and its geometry. Every lettering file here and on the site comes from it. |

Colours: ivory `#ddd5c7` on dark; black `#111111` on light grounds.

The lettering is not a font: never retype the name. Keep clear space around the name of at least half
its cap height, and around the K of at least a quarter of its height.

Raster and image masters (the stage image and any future artwork) live in Google Drive,
`Ensemble Project`; the deployable stage derivative's provenance is in `docs/DESIGN.md`.
