## Working website / brand exploration thesis

The active principle-level thesis remains:

> **Human Causality, Living Consequence.**
>
> Kymaean should feel like a world that notices people, remembers what they do, and is changed by what follows.

This is an internal website/brand exploration thesis, not a public tagline, not an application-product constitution, and not a selected visual identity.

Durable visual-exploration laws:

- person before effect;
- cause before decoration;
- evidence before generic symbolism;
- consequence may change structure;
- history accumulates rather than resets;
- distinct identities influence one another without merging;
- mystery comes from bounded perspective;
- quiet normally precedes spectacle;
- humanity, agency, imperfection, relationship, and lived evidence outrank machinery;
- exploration remains broad until the user and assistant explicitly agree to converge.

A radically different visual world can still be valid Kymaean if it embodies these laws.

## Durable abstract discoveries

Carry discoveries forward only at the level of visual physics and experience, not surface styling.

Established durable discoveries include:

- **Presence has weight.** Human presence can matter to the state or meaning of a place.
- **Actions leave evidence.** Consequential events can leave traces.
- **Influence persists.** Effects can remain after the originating action or actor has passed.
- **History changes possibility.** Prior states can alter what becomes possible next.
- **Consequence can alter structure.** Meaningful change may affect composition, material, hierarchy, state, affordance, or constraint rather than merely color.
- **Distinct identities remain distinct while affecting one another.** Relationship is not merger.
- **Human habitation matters.** Lived-in specificity, imperfection, use, and relational evidence can carry more meaning than sterile spectacle.
- **Ensemble activity matters.** Multiple people acting, responding, disagreeing, observing, withholding, helping, resisting, or influencing one another can be more truthful than repeated solitary observation.
- **Consequence can be additive.** History need not look like damage; later states may incorporate earlier states and create new affordances.
- **What one person changes can alter another person's circumstances.**
- **History can become habitat.** Present conditions can embody prior human action without explanatory timelines or flashbacks.
- **Consequence can become affordance or constraint.** A past action can materially alter what another person can do next.
- **Bounded perspective can be experiential.** Different people can inhabit one reality without seeing, knowing, or interpreting the same portions of it.
- **Shared reality does not imply shared understanding.** Ensemble activity is not synchronized collective authorship.
- **Provenance can live in the world.** A present state can imply distinct origins, ages, intentions, and prior interventions without diagramming them.
- **People should live inside consequences.** They should inherit, use, misunderstand, resist, repair, exploit, adapt to, or further change conditions created earlier.
- **Human causality needs friction as well as care.** Misunderstanding, disagreement, withholding, accidental side effects, asymmetric benefit, interruption, affection, refusal, repair, compromise, and surprise remain valid causal material.
- **Artistic integrity precedes explanation.** Exploratory art should make the viewer feel the idea before copy explains it.

None of these discoveries selects a palette, medium, architecture, environment type, protagonist, composition, genre, or visual motif.

- praise or enthusiasm is not permission to refine a branch;

## V0.5B surface-blind isolation protocol

The active protocol is defined by:

`docs/HANDOFF_V0_5B_SURFACE_BLIND_DIVERGENCE.md`

Operational summary:

1. Begin V0.5B in a fresh project chat.
2. Carry product truth, creative laws, collaboration discipline, and abstract discoveries only.
3. Do **not** inspect or reconstruct previous generated imagery, screenshots, old image prompts, visual critiques, palettes, motifs, compositions, media, or archived visual handoffs before the first artwork unless explicitly requested.
4. Treat every surface choice as unselected at the start of the new chat.
5. Generate from a positive human causal premise rather than a blacklist of old motifs.
6. Avoid product, brand, website, marketing, design-system, moodboard, and presentation-board language in the image-generation instruction when the experiment is visual-world art.
7. Generate artwork as artwork: one uninterrupted full canvas with no labels, logos, wordmarks, UI, swatches, comparison framing, or explanatory copy.
8. If generation inserts brand collateral anyway, treat it as an execution failure rather than a candidate and do not refine the contaminated board.
9. Evaluate against stable experience dimensions after generation, not resemblance to prior work.
10. Continue broad exploration until explicit mutual convergence.
