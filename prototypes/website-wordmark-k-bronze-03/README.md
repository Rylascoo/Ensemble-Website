# KYMÆAN K-Derived Bronze Study 03

Status: PREVIEW-ONLY / NON-PRODUCTION.

## Recovered source evidence

Historical Threshold K Drive studies use:
- primary bronze/accent: `#c8a56a`;
- soft warm companion: `#d8c9ad` / `#d9cbb3`.

Historical design law:
**The K should be discovered in the void, not drawn by the pieces.**

Historical wordmark consequence:
Threshold K carried the compressed symbolic event while KYMÆAN remained readable, and the Æ was the primary place where shared authorship could quietly appear.

The Director's current exploration intentionally goes further than that old scaffold: Y M Æ A N may now be constructed from K-derived formal logic, while the exact K geometry remains fixed.

## A — Threshold Bronze Structural

- entire wordmark uses recovered Threshold bronze;
- supporting letters use K-derived facets/planes;
- strongest one-material unity;
- lowest risk of the bronze becoming decorative.

Pressure:
may be too uniformly bronze and flatten the distinction between discovered K and readable name.

## B — Stone + Bronze Core

- K remains bronze;
- major supporting-letter planes use warm stone;
- internal structural hinges/diagonals use bronze;
- bronze becomes a discovered inner construction rather than a color wash.

Pressure:
at smaller sizes, selective bronze details must not disappear or look accidental.

## C — Chamber Bronze Hybrid

- K remains bronze;
- supporting letters are denser K-derived chamber glyphs;
- bronze defines the outer/surrounding forms;
- warm stone appears inside selected chambers/inlays.

Pressure:
strongest symbolic identity, but highest risk of excessive emblematic density.

## Fixed constraints

- exact singular K geometry unchanged;
- Stage image unchanged;
- one-screen placeholder unchanged;
- Coming Soon unchanged;
- no mascot integration;
- no product copy;
- no new navigation/UI.

## Review target

The best option should feel materially connected to the historical Threshold K **and** structurally native to the new Stage.

The bronze should read as:
- architectural;
- mineral;
- quiet;
- discovered in darkness;

not:
- gold;
- glossy;
- luxury;
- metallic effect.
