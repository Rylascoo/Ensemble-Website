MINERAL HUMANIST R2
The first bespoke pass exposed an unintended dark diamond at the M valley. R2 removes that hinge and lets white space resolve the center.
The direction uses:
- tapered/soft mass;
- broad M;
- asymmetrical A pressure;
- a bowed E middle arm;
- a strong but non-mechanical N diagonal;
- quiet Y transition.

INCISED ARCHITECTURAL R2
The first bespoke pass overused wedge extensions and approached Roman/serif territory. R2 removes those extensions.
The direction now uses:
- taper;
- clipped pressure;
- close spacing;
- restrained apex treatment;
- bar hierarchy rather than classical serif language.
