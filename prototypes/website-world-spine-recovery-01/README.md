## Study thesis

The broader website should be explored as a **continuous world spine**, not a sequence of interchangeable marketing components.

Current five-part visual grammar:

1. **PLACE / CONTINUITY**  
   Establish a world before explanation.

2. **PERSPECTIVE / ONE WORLD**  
   Show that multiple views belong to one shared environment without importing app semantics.

3. **CONSEQUENCE / MATERIAL SHIFT**  
   Let later composition visibly inherit earlier change.

4. **PARTICIPATION / SPATIAL RELATION**  
   Express degrees of relation spatially rather than as a feature-card row. Product-mode names are intentionally withheld.

5. **RETURN / IDENTITY**  
   End with restrained identity; do not escalate into a generic conversion dashboard.

## Main critique of the old Site Spine

The **order and ideas were stronger than the execution**.

Old execution pressures:
- many rows/cells read like documentation;
- relationship could resemble comparison tables;
- product vocabulary arrived too early;
- large quantities of fine text weakened theatrical scale;
- sections felt assembled rather than spatially continuous.

Recovery direction:
- fewer words;
- bigger spatial transitions;
- visual causality rather than explanatory annotation;
- material shifts with narrative purpose;
- one continuous environment rather than repeated card scaffolds.
