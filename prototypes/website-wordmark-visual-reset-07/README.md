# KYMÆAN Wordmark Visual Reset 07

Status: PREVIEW-ONLY / NON-PRODUCTION / TYPOGRAPHIC PERSONALITY STUDY.

## Why W06 was not continued

W06 made the inherited mechanism too visible. Counterweights, missing strokes, relational bridges, and resolution changes became the subject before the name.

This reset deliberately reverses the priority:

1. read KYMÆAN;
2. feel the word's atmosphere and rhythm;
3. only later discover its relationship to the K.

## Fixed controls

- exact singular K geometry unchanged;
- A and E remain separate;
- Reference-17/Crop-04 Stage unchanged;
- one-screen website composition unchanged;
- Coming Soon unchanged;
- K uses recovered Threshold bronze #c8a56a;
- supporting name uses warm stone #d9cbb3;
- no mascot integration;
- no explanatory product copy.

## Important prototype rule

The supporting names in W07 use common browser type skeletons intentionally.

They are NOT font selections and NOT production artwork.

The purpose is to determine which broad typographic personality and proportion should be redrawn as bespoke deterministic vector geometry afterward.

## W07-A — Quiet Monumental

The supporting name becomes restrained, narrow and spacious.

Hypothesis:
the K should carry almost all symbolic density, while the name behaves like calm architectural labeling.

Watch for:
- generic luxury drift;
- excessive distance between K and name;
- whether the quietness actually strengthens the K.

## W07-B — Spatial Inscription

A more material/historical skeleton, but without a fused Æ.

A and E are separately positioned and subtly differentiated to test whether the center can feel authored without a ligature.

Watch for:
- heritage/classical drift;
- whether serif rhythm belongs to the Stage;
- whether separate A/E finally feels intentional.

## W07-C — Human Architectural

A warmer humanist skeleton with less geometric severity.

Hypothesis:
the supporting name can feel designed by a person rather than generated by a system while still belonging beside the architectural K.

Watch for:
- excessive friendliness;
- ordinary corporate-humanist appearance;
- whether this creates the strongest tension between symbolic K and readable name.

## Next step

Do not refine all three.

Choose the personality or combination of qualities that feels most native beside the K. Then redraw that direction as bespoke vectors, introducing at most one or two subtle K-derived spatial behaviors.
