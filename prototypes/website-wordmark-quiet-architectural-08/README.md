# KYMÆAN W08 — Quiet Architectural Synthesis

Status: PREVIEW-ONLY / NON-PRODUCTION / SINGLE LEAD SYNTHESIS.

## Purpose

W07 showed that the project benefits from restoring typographic calm after W05/W06 over-exposed the construction system.

W08 therefore combines:
- W07-A's restraint and narrow architectural proportion;
- W07-C's less mechanical rhythm;
- only two subtle authored behaviors.

The supporting name is now bespoke deterministic SVG vector geometry rather than browser text.

## Fixed controls

- exact singular K geometry unchanged;
- A and E remain separate;
- Reference-17/Crop-04 Stage unchanged;
- one-screen website composition unchanged;
- Coming Soon unchanged;
- K uses recovered Threshold bronze #c8a56a;
- Y M A E A N use warm stone #d9cbb3;
- no mascot integration;
- no explanatory product copy.

## Letter character

### Y
Quiet, narrow, slightly asymmetric fork. No explicit K-like segmentation.

### M
Architectural but readable. The internal diagonals are subordinate to the outer supports.

### First A
Slightly asymmetric construction with a broad readable aperture.

### E
Simple structural spine and three restrained horizontal statements.

### A / E relationship
A and E remain fully separate.

Their only authored relationship is a shared horizontal datum:
- A crossbar;
- E middle arm.

The intent is for the relationship to register subconsciously rather than behave like a puzzle or ligature.

### Second A
A calmer echo of the first A.

### N
The final diagonal carries slightly greater force to close the word without becoming a separate emblem.

## Review criterion

The first read should be:
KYMÆAN.

The second read may reveal:
- unusual calm beside the K;
- slight asymmetry;
- a quiet A/E relationship;
- stronger closure at N.

If the custom construction itself becomes the first thing noticed, W08 has failed.
