STUDY 02 R2 CRITIQUE

MINERAL HUMANIST
Survives for:
- broad, breathable word rhythm;
- optical asymmetry and worked/human pressure;
- strongest non-mechanical M/A/N foundation.

Does not survive unchanged because:
- K-to-Y remains too detached;
- the Y foot is a fragile, unnecessary micro-feature at phone scale;
- M center still makes the valley a conspicuous event even after the R1 hinge was removed;
- E middle-bar bow becomes mannered at display size and unstable at small raster scale;
- N diagonal is slightly too dark against the quieter E/A sequence.

INCISED ARCHITECTURAL
Survives for:
- compact discipline;
- clear E bar hierarchy;
- controlled spacing;
- strong reverse and phone survival.

Does not survive unchanged because:
- Y and M junction/notch behavior reads as constructed mechanism before lettering;
- the broad clipped A apex becomes a fragile micro-feature at phone scale;
- support word is too geometrically regular beside the sculptural fixed K;
- N closure is stable but too stock if carried forward literally.

STUDY 03 CONSTRUCTION
This is not a 50/50 average.

Incised Architectural supplies:
- proportion discipline;
- compact spacing;
- E bar hierarchy;
- small-scale restraint.

Mineral Humanist supplies:
- taper;
- optical asymmetry;
- broader M authority;
- living white-space tension.

Bespoke corrections in R3:
- Y: lower/quieter junction, no terminal foot;
- M: broad control letter with center resolved as continuous white space, no detached notch/cut motif;
- A: one genetic glyph reused twice; contextual distinction is spacing only; crossbar raised and softened;
- E: unequal bar hierarchy retained with only a slight human middle-arm tension;
- N: diagonal optically lightened and used for firm closure;
- K-Y / Y-M / M-A / A-E / E-A / A-N approximate drawing-unit gaps: 29 / 31 / 39 / 25 / 26 / 31.

REPEATED-A LAW
Both A instances use the same exact glyph definition.
No alternate-A logo has been introduced.
Contextual coherence is created only by adjacent white shapes at AE and AN.

RECURSIVE VISUAL AUDIT
Pass 1 found:
- A right diagonal too dark;
- E middle-arm bow too visible;
- N diagonal too heavy.
All three were corrected.

Pass 2 found:
- Y-M white shape too open relative to K-Y and downstream rhythm.
M and all later letters were shifted left 7 drawing units; glyph geometry was not changed.

Pass 3 found:
- proof-sheet annotation collision and an overlong explanatory line.
Only proof-sheet typography was corrected; wordmark geometry was unchanged.

Small-scale raster stress:
- the supporting word remains legible at approximately 180 px word width;
- removed Y foot, A cap and M notch mechanisms no longer depend on subpixel survival;
- reverse monochrome remains coherent.
