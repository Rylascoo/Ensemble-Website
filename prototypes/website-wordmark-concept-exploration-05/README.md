# KYMÆAN Wordmark Concept Exploration 05

Status: PREVIEW-ONLY / NON-PRODUCTION / CONCEPT EXPLORATION.

## Reopened assumptions

The prior fused `Æ` is explicitly treated as a placeholder, not identity law.

Only the singular K geometry is fixed.

The visible word is explored as:

`K + Y M A E A N`

## Shared constants

- exact K geometry unchanged;
- new Reference-17/Crop-04 Stage unchanged;
- one-screen placeholder composition unchanged;
- Coming Soon unchanged;
- historical Threshold material family reused:
  - bronze `#c8a56a`;
  - warm stone `#d9cbb3`;
- no mascots;
- no app UI;
- no explanatory copy.

## W05-A — Assembled Planes

Each letter is constructed from discrete pieces rather than conventional strokes.

Purpose:
test the most direct extension of the K as an object-construction language.

## W05-B — Void Alphabet

Letters are partly perceived through material absence, interruptions and cutouts.

Purpose:
test the deepest conceptual inheritance from the Threshold K law:
**the identity may be discovered in the void rather than fully drawn.**

This is intentionally the least conventional family.

## W05-C — Interrupted Inscription

A quieter incised/inscription system with missing joins and selective bronze cuts.

Purpose:
test whether the K principle can survive in a refined, highly readable website wordmark without every glyph becoming an emblem.

## W05-D — Threshold Sequence

Y/M/A/E/A/N are treated as different architectural opening/support conditions.

Purpose:
test a wordmark that belongs directly to the Stage/threshold world rather than to typography alone.

## Review law

Do not judge by polish or novelty.

The useful question is:
**Which family reveals an identity principle worth refining?**

After review, the next round should synthesize/refine only surviving mechanisms rather than repeat four broad families.
