## Design question

The prior study treated the supporting wordmark as typography.

This study instead treats the fixed K as the **formal generator** for Y M Æ A N.

Extracted K DNA:
- assembled facets rather than normal font strokes;
- meaningful internal segmentation;
- directional diagonals;
- asymmetrical but balanced construction;
- dense emblematic form;
- controlled negative space.

## 01 — Structural Lineage

Supporting letters are constructed from filled planes and supports.

Intent:
- highest readability;
- immediately related to the K;
- avoids feeling like a font sitting beside a logo.

Likely pressure:
- can still remain too conservative if the Æ does not become distinctive enough.

## 02 — Chamber Glyph

The K’s density and internal voids are pushed further into the supporting alphabet.

Intent:
- more symbolic;
- internal chambers/apertures become a recurring language;
- Æ and A become secondary identity anchors.

Likely pressure:
- can become too emblematic or game/fantasy-adjacent if made heavier.

## 03 — Faceted Relay

The K’s separate pieces become the defining behavior.

Intent:
- strongest transformation;
- controlled gaps and varying facet opacity imply an identity assembled from related pieces;
- maximum distance from conventional typography.

Likely pressure:
- readability and small-scale stability.

## Review target

Do not choose based on novelty.

The successful direction should make the reaction:
"Of course the rest of KYMÆAN is built this way once you see the K."

not:
"They designed a strange custom font around the logo."

A hybrid after this comparison is expected and allowed.
