# KYMÆAN W09 — Sculptural Typographic Synthesis

Status: PREVIEW-ONLY / NON-PRODUCTION / SINGLE LEAD SYNTHESIS.

## Why W09 exists

W08 successfully restored restraint and readability after the over-systematized W05/W06 rounds, but its supporting Y M A E A N remained too rational and architectural-sans-like beside the fixed K.

W09 keeps W08's hierarchy while introducing character through proportion and tension rather than construction tricks.

- A and E remain separate;

## W09 principles

### Typography first

The name should read immediately.

No supporting letter is designed as a miniature emblem.

### Sculptural rather than modular

Curved and bowed transitions are permitted where they improve tension and humanity.

Repeated facets, cuts, chambers, and modular component counts are deliberately absent.

### Varied rhythm

The supporting letters do not share one mathematical width:
- Y is moderate and open;
- M is broad;
- first A is narrower and asymmetric;
- E is compact but not rigid;
- second A echoes without being mechanically identical in feel;
- N closes with greater diagonal force.

### Material relationship

K retains historical Threshold bronze #c8a56a.

Supporting letters use #d3c2a5: still mineral/warm-stone in character, but slightly closer to the bronze than the previous #d9cbb3. This reduces the visual sensation of "bronze symbol + separate white word."

Color remains secondary to geometry.

## Letter-specific intent

### Y
A softer fork and calm stem answer the K's curved tension without copying its silhouette.

### M
Broad outer supports and curved inward transitions create weight without a modular construction-set effect.

### First A
The left structure is gently bowed and the crossbar subtly changes thickness/trajectory. The aperture stays immediately readable.

### E
The spine is not perfectly mechanical and the middle arm bows gently. This is intended to remove the generic engineered-E character.

### Second A
A quieter echo of the first A rather than a new identity event.

### N
A stronger terminal diagonal closes the word, but its diagonal is described with a gentle curve rather than an aggressive straight slash.

## Review criterion

The desired experience is:

1. KYMÆAN reads immediately.
2. The K still feels like the identity anchor.
3. The supporting name feels authored and unusual without displaying a visible system.
4. Only closer inspection reveals asymmetry, curvature and varied rhythm.
