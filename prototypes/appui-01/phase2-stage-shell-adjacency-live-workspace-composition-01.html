B

Authority partition — what belongs to Stage, frame, inspector, or deeper surface

The old raster is not blanket authority for every historical shell element it depicts.

Stage field

Character presence + identity, live action/dialogue/silence/refusal, truthful state, listening presence, and materially relevant opportunity/relationship salience when supported.

Live-workspace frame

Production/Scene identity, current-vs-history status, capability truth, selected-entity context, creator commands and Perspective status when Product authority supports it.

Contextual inspector

What matters about the selected entity here. Adjacent when invoked on wide layouts; overlay/substitute/sequential on smaller layouts.

Deep/transient surface

History/cause, Character-bounded disclosure, creator intervention and later Product-authorized consequence/Take flows. Never permanent merely because important.

G

Phase-2 composition laws and explicit non-authority

This study resolves adjacency and posture only; it is not a Stage visual successor yet.

Stage primacy

The dark ensemble field is the largest contiguous live-work surface and remains visually dominant over shell, inspector and support material.

Phase-1 shell stays frozen

No Studio/Stage/Archive mode topology returns. Durable scope recedes before the current task and history remains contextual/deep.

Frozen carrier semantics survive

Cone-of-light presence, floor/name hinge, explicit identity, listening presence and state separation remain binding Stage meanings.

Five Characters are a witness, not a limit

The current five-cone baseline is preserved for this composition study but does not establish maximum or required active Scene cardinality.

Static only

No transcript-dependent motion, turn-taking animation, cadence token or behavioral integration claim is made while Q-DESIGN-02 remains blocked.

Final visual language remains later

Final palette, typography, materials, iconography, motion tokens, native WinUI implementation and Phase-3 system integration remain open.
