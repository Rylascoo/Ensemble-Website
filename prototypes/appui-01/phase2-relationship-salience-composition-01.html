C

Interpretation guards - relational visibility must not manufacture psychology

Only materially supported relationship salience may appear; the treatment cannot invent its meaning.

Salience != relationship type

The witness does not label friendship, conflict, affinity, distrust, intimacy, hierarchy or another relationship category.

Salience != strength or score

No meter, rank, percentage or stronger/weaker claim is inferred from proximity or shared floor treatment.

Salience != obligation

Neither member is required to speak, act, respond or receive the next opportunity because the relationship is salient.

Salience != permanent graph

The Stage does not become a node-link map. Relationship visibility may recede when it is no longer materially relevant.

D

No relationship ontology

No graph or arc standard

No selection/opportunity coupling

No motion/runtime rule

No animation, transition, automatic grouping, salience algorithm, transcript trigger or provider behavior is established.
