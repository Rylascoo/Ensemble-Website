G

Section laws and explicit non-authority

Character reach is resolved; cast mechanics are not invented.

No permanent People silo

Whole-Production Character access is a deliberate Production-scope capacity. Everyday shaping still begins from the current situation and follows meaning.

No implicit Scene membership

Opening, selecting, editing, or creating a persistent Character at Production scope does not add/remove them from the current Scene. Exact participation controls remain Product-defined.

Character identity is durable

Name/text and semantic continuity are sufficient for recognition. Portraits, scenic imagery, color, or Performer/provider metadata are never required to establish identity.

Performer remains secondary machinery

Provider/model/Performer assignment may affect capability later, but it cannot replace, rename, erase, or fictionalize a persistent Character.

Creation capacity ≠ cast control

The architecture can host persistent Character creation without freezing add/remove-to-Scene, casting, participation, lifecycle, duplicate/merge, deletion, or archival semantics.

Final visual language remains later

Exact labels, controls, typography, palette, materials, icons, motion, WinUI behavior, runtime/package behavior, and Phase-2 Stage visuals remain open.
