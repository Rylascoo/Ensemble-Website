B

Inherited survivor mechanisms — composed without promotion beyond evidence

Each mechanism stays subordinate to content and Product meaning.

MAT-01 F1 · Subtractive Channel

The Home content sits on one continuous field with a restrained edge-open top channel and right channel. It replaces generic nested-card emphasis without becoming an icon, direction cue or product state.

TYP-02 F1 · Ratio Ladder

Hierarchy relies primarily on the accepted bounded size ladder: 0.75rem eyebrow, 1.5rem primary heading, 1rem body, 0.875rem support and 0.85rem state/control labels. System UI remains the carrier.

STA-01 F2 · Ring + Edge Witness

The selected Home destination uses a persistent edge witness; keyboard focus remains a separate outer ring on Resume. Selection and focus remain independently legible and do not encode fictional meaning.

C

Integration guards — visual language may clarify, never rewrite the product

The first Phase-3 pass is deliberately color-independent and motionless.
One field, not a card stackSubtractive channels may author the surface, but Current Scene / What matters / Recent movement remain the same functional groups and reading order.
Typography remains subordinateThe Ratio Ladder strengthens hierarchy without bespoke type, wordmark dependence or a theatrical editorial treatment that competes with content.
Selection ≠ focusThe edge witness marks durable selection while the focus ring marks keyboard focus. Neither implies Character attention, opportunity or agency.
Light/Dark parityThe two themes preserve hierarchy, commands, state distinctions and material topology. The Stage/Scene preview remains dark in both.
CLR-01 remains deferredNo F2/F1 color preference is encoded here. Neutral grayscale assembly exists specifically to give the later CLR reentry a representative app context.
No motion or runtime semanticsNo transition, cadence, micro-interaction, loading behavior, intervention effect, Take state, timer or provider/Performer meaning is introduced.
D

Explicit non-authority

This is an integration witness, not a final visual-system declaration.
No final material systemMAT F1 is being tested in representative app composition; this does not retire Mineral Theater or create production material tokens.
No final typographyTYP F1 remains role-grammar evidence only; no final typeface, font token or wordmark change is selected.
No final component skinSTA F2 is a bounded selection/focus mechanism; exact native control geometry and implementation remain open.
No final paletteNeutral Light/Dark values are study fixtures. CLR-01 F2/F1 adjudication remains a later explicit convergence decision.
