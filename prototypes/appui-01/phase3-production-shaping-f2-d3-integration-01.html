Interaction priority — what Shaping is allowed to optimize

Meaning before metadata; context before catalog.
01

ORIENT

Read the current Scene and situation as a coherent creative whole: what is true now, who is here, what matters, and what remains unresolved.

02

FOLLOW MEANING

Choose a person, relationship, pressure, question, or world condition because it matters in this situation—not because its data type owns a navigation tab.

03

INSPECT / SHAPE

Open nearby detail first. Deep object-oriented inspection or editing appears only when the creator deliberately follows the selected meaning further.

04

RETURN

Close or Back returns to the same current situation and semantic origin. Navigation does not undo an already-effective creator change.

F

Section laws and explicit non-authority

Detailed enough to implement hierarchy later; not a final visual system or Product-control contract.

Situation before ontology

People Here, Relationships in Motion, Pressures Now, Open Questions, and World Context summarize what matters in the current situation. They are not frozen top-level navigation or database silos. “Current possibility” names the creator’s working frame; it does not imply predictive or generated next-action authority.

Selection is not fictional attention

Selecting a Character, relationship, pressure, question, or condition means “inspect this.” It does not assert Director opportunity, relationship salience, or Character agency.

Editing stays creator-authored

Creator shaping/editing belongs outside fictional Character carriers. Provisional work remains distinguishable from effective Production truth.

History stays causal, not a global mode

History is reached from meaningful current material and may expand when needed. It does not become a permanent top-level Archive destination.

Still explicitly open

Final labels, icons, typography, palette, materials, motion, exact Scene lifecycle, consequence review, Perspective switching, Take/Rehearsal/branching, transcript/timer/Pause, provider/model/cost, Performer assignment, portable Production, export/manuscript roles, specialized maps, and native implementation.

No Stage redesign here

This study resolves the shaping workspace only. It does not alter the frozen dark Stage baseline or begin Phase-2 Stage/live-production visual work.
