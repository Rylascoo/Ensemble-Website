No permanent Archive/History mode

History is reached from meaningful current material. It may temporarily become the primary task but is not promoted to a permanent top-level application destination.

Historical never masquerades as current

Selection, expansion, theme, layout or focus cannot remove historical status or silently replace the current Scene.

Causal relevance ≠ exhaustive causality

The interface may explain why a prior witness is relevant without freezing an exhaustive causal graph, inference model, scoring system, or generated explanation authority.

Disclosure remains bounded

Private/Character-bounded knowledge, claims, beliefs, memories and provenance remain distinct where supported; history inspection cannot leak creator-global truth into bounded views.

Back/Close ≠ Undo/replay

Navigation restores the current semantic origin. Exact consequence review, rollback, replay, branching and mutation semantics remain Product-defined.

Final visual language remains later

Exact labels, chronology graphics, icons, typography, palette, material, motion, specialized maps, WinUI/runtime behavior and Phase-2 Stage visuals remain open.
