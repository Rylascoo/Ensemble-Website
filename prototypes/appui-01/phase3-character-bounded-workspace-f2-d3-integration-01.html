E

Boundary rules — a bounded workspace cannot advertise what it withholds

Disclosure is enforced in representation, not simulated with decorative concealment.

No hidden-item counters or placeholders

Out-of-bound material is not represented through counts, blank rows, redactions, summaries or “something hidden” hints unless Product semantics authorize that meta-knowledge.

No creator-only causal leakage

Historical cause, consequence or relationship context visible globally does not automatically become Character knowledge merely because the bounded workspace is open.

No infrastructure leakage

Provider/model/cost, capability failures, diagnostics and recovery state remain creator/application information and cannot become Character perception or fictional explanation.

No cosmetic Perspective substitution

A theme, tint, portrait treatment or label alone cannot stand in for materially different disclosure. The represented information itself must obey the active boundary.

G

Section laws and explicit non-authority

Workspace behavior is resolved; final Perspective/product controls are not.

Same Production, same Character

Character-bounded work never forks identity or creates a second Production merely to change disclosure.

Disclosure must be material

Bounded work changes represented information and access, not merely color, theme, wording or decorative treatment.

Agency remains Character-owned

Action, speech or silence may remain available forms of agency, but creator navigation/selection does not itself become Character action.

Perspective controls remain Product-defined

This study does not freeze a “Take a Seat” command, Perspective selector, entry/exit labels, timing, confirmation or performance input grammar.

Bounded knowledge schema remains open

Exact fact/claim/belief/memory/observation rules, provenance, uncertainty and knowledge-transfer semantics remain Product-defined.

Final visual language remains later

Exact typography, palette, materials, icons, motion, Stage treatment and native WinUI behavior remain open.
