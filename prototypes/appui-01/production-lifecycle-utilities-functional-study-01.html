G

Section laws and explicit non-authority

Utility capacity is resolved; product mechanics are not.

Secondary route, not creative home

Utilities are deliberately reachable from application/Production scope without displacing current-situation-first creative work.

No literal portable format authority

This study does not define files, bundles, manifests, merge/overwrite behavior, compatibility rules, destinations or import/export commands.

Provider machinery remains subordinate

Exact provider/model/cost policy, authentication, assignment and availability behavior remain Product-defined and outside ordinary Character identity.

No hidden configuration mutation

Opening, selecting, closing or navigating a utility surface cannot silently apply settings, reconnect providers, repair data, import/export, or mutate Production truth.

Diagnostics stay non-fictional

Technical state remains infrastructure explanation and never becomes Character action, fictional failure, causal history or Stage performance.

Final visual language remains later

Exact labels, icons, hierarchy styling, dialogs, notifications, typography, palette, motion, native WinUI behavior and Phase-2 Stage visuals remain open.
