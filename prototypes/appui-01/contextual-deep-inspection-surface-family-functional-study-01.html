G

Surface-family laws and explicit non-authority

One grammar, multiple entity meanings; no final component styling.

Contextual means relevant-here

The nearby inspector summarizes what matters about the selected entity in the current task/context. It should not attempt to expose every persistent field simply because room exists.

Deep means enough room to understand or shape

Deep inspection may become the primary task temporarily, but it remains a continuation of the selected entity and retains the route back to its semantic origin.

Entity meaning outranks shared chrome

Character, relationship, pressure/question, and world-context witnesses can share surface grammar without pretending they are the same kind of thing or flattening their semantics into one form.

No permanent right-edge law

The adjacent right-side witness demonstrates capacity only. Medium/narrow layouts may overlay, substitute, or sequence the same inspector while preserving meaning and focus restoration.

Exact edits remain Product-bound

This study reserves inspect/edit capacity but does not freeze field schemas, commit/save semantics, validation, undo, Scene lifecycle, consequence review, Perspective controls, or other unresolved workflows.

Final visual language remains later

Typography, palette, materials, icons, motion, component styling, native WinUI behavior, package/runtime behavior, and Phase-2 Stage visuals are not selected here.

Section conclusion: contextual + deep inspection now has one reusable functional grammar across the frozen application architecture.NO NEW APP MODE · NO STAGE CHANGE
