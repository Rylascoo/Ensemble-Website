Cross-cutting invariants under stress

Focus ≠ SelectionKeyboard traversal may move focus without changing the selected Character/entity.
Selection ≠ OpportunityUI selection cannot masquerade as fictional Director attention or obligation to act.
Back ≠ UndoNavigation closes surfaces; it does not reverse effective Production changes.
Historical ≠ CurrentCausal inspection is always marked as history while the current Scene remains recoverable.
Provisional ≠ EffectiveNon-effective work must not look committed; exact final terminology remains Product-open.
Creator action ≠ Character agencyEdits, recovery and intervention live outside Character fictional carriers.
Character ≠ PerformerRecasting or provider availability cannot erase persistent Character identity.
Infrastructure ≠ FictionProvider/persistence failures never become fictional silence, refusal, action or causal history.
