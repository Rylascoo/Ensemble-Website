B

Performance grammar — action/refusal do not require dialogue bubbles

The same Stage carrier can hold different modalities without changing Character importance or inventing motive.

Listening

Character is present and not currently speaking. Listening does not itself assert silence-as-performance, refusal, emotion, agreement, passivity or incapacity.

Nonverbal action

A Character-legible action may be current performance without becoming quoted dialogue or a message bubble. This study does not freeze an action taxonomy or infer motive or consequence.

Refusal

Refusal may be Character-legible fictional performance. It is not missing output or infrastructure failure and does not require a motive, emotional diagnosis or explanatory speech.

C

Interpretation guards — performance modality must not manufacture meaning

The distinction must survive narrow layout and high-contrast presentation without motive, emotion or system-state inference.

Nonverbal action ≠ dialogue

An action can be current Character performance without quoted language, a speech bubble or transcript-sized visual weight.

Refusal ≠ missing output

A valid refusal is explicit fictional agency. Blank, failed or unavailable application output must not be silently reclassified as refusal.

Modality ≠ motive or emotion

Action or refusal does not establish anger, fear, resistance, preference, incapacity, intention or another psychological interpretation.

Performance ≠ selection or opportunity

Creator selection and Director opportunity remain independent meanings. Performing does not imply selection, and opportunity does not require response.

D

Explicit non-authority — this is a performance-presentation study only

Transcript-dependent Stage motion remains blocked by Q-DESIGN-02.

No action ontology

No gesture vocabulary, action categories, refusal taxonomy, intent model or multimodal representation contract is frozen.

No timing or cadence semantics

No duration, turn length, pause interval, beat, transcript cadence, animation timing or response threshold is established.

No consequence or acceptance semantics

Showing an action or refusal does not establish causal effect, accepted Take, history mutation, Scene progression or changed opportunity.

No Stage successor authority

PKT-STAGE-CORE-02 remains the Stage anchor. This study does not redesign the cone/name/floor carrier, motion, transcript, palette or native controls.
