B

Orthogonal state grammar — absence of speech is not one meaning

Visual treatment must communicate the state without inventing motive or backend behavior.

Listening

Character is present and not currently speaking. Listening does not itself assert silence-as-performance, refusal, emotion, agreement, passivity or incapacity.

Performed silence

Silence is Character-legible fictional performance/agency. It must be explicit enough not to read as missing output, but this study does not infer motive, duration or refusal.

Technical non-response

Application/infrastructure state remains outside fiction. It must not create or overwrite Character silence, refusal, indecision, dramatic tension or causal history.

C

Interpretation guards — keep presentation from manufacturing meaning

The same distinction must survive narrow layout and high-contrast presentation.

Silence ≠ missing output

A valid performed silence has an explicit Stage witness. A blank, failed or absent technical result is not silently reclassified as Character behavior.

Silence ≠ refusal

Both may be representable agency, but this composition does not equate them or infer refusal from the absence of speech.

Listening ≠ silence

Listening remains a truthful present-state witness. It is not a weaker form of performed silence and does not establish a dramatic choice.

System status ≠ fictional state

Technical non-response belongs to application status outside the Character carrier. Stage truth is retained rather than rewritten to explain infrastructure.
