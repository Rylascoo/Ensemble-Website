G

Section laws and explicit non-authority

Disclosure grammar is resolved; the epistemic data model is not.

No ontology dashboard requirement

Truth/knowledge/disclosure is a deep-inspection capacity reached from meaningful current material; it does not require a permanent Knowledge top-level mode.

Disclosure ≠ identity

Character-bounded presentation changes visibility, not the persistent Character or underlying Product-supported Production state.

No metadata leakage by default

Material outside a bounded view is not enumerated, counted, hinted, summarized or exposed through placeholders unless Product semantics explicitly authorize that meta-knowledge.

Witness types are provisional

Fact, claim, belief, suspicion, memory, private/withheld information, provenance and uncertainty prove capacity only; they do not freeze exhaustive entity types or field schemas.

Temporal truth remains separate

Memory, belief and claim do not silently become objective history; historical status and truth status remain independently represented.

Final visual language remains later

Exact labels, icons, diagrams, typography, palette, materials, motion, specialized maps, WinUI/runtime behavior and Phase-2 Stage visuals remain open.
