Context result

The original Threshold K does not need geometric modification to function across these four static roles. Its strongest deployments preserve breathing room around the mark and keep it visually independent from the name. It performs less well when tightly fused to the text or scaled until the surrounding frame begins participating in the symbol.

Wordmark implication

The future KYMÆAN wordmark should not compete with Threshold K or absorb it. It needs exceptional spacing, a deliberate Æ, and enough authorship to stand alone—but lower internal ornament and lower emblematic density than the mark. The strongest next wordmark question is therefore not “how do we repeat the K?” but “how does the name carry equal ownership with quieter means?”
