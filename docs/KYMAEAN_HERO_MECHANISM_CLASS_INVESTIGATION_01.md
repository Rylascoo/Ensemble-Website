## Mechanism classes considered

### M1 — Gravity-dependent support-state deformation

**Core physics:** a broad shared material field has a visibly altered load-bearing deformation. At least one person's depicted body position depends on that deformation resisting gravity. If the material is mentally restored to its baseline state, the depicted body position materially collapses.

**Why it attacks the repeated failure:** the counterfactual is carried by gravity and load path rather than by a viewer imagining whether someone could walk around, reach over, or choose another route.

**Potential human relation:** two distinct adults occupy different, compositionally interdependent parts of the same changed field. Neither is shown causing or maintaining the deformation. The people remain the semantic subjects; the load-bearing change explains what one or both can presently do.

**Strengths:**

- high static causal observability;
- high Removal-Test potential;
- no need for private knowledge, intention or prior-author proof;
- materially different from Round 01 occlusion and Round 02 separator/contact;
- compatible with sparse human-first composition;
- can use one continuous field rather than a clue prop.

**Risks:**

- familiar seat/hammock/ledge/furniture reading;
- sculpture/installation or architecture becoming protagonist;
- spectacular suspension becoming the whole image;
- support object rather than changed state becoming the explanation.

**Required safeguards if selected:** baseline portions of the same material remain visible; deformation is visibly continuous with them; support is broad/distributed rather than one device; no familiar furniture form; no gap/bridge; body posture is calm rather than stunt-like; people must carry more optical weight than the support geometry.

### M2 — Counter-tension / equilibrium-state field

**Core physics:** a changed tensile field holds a settled asymmetric equilibrium that makes a body position or use possible only while that altered tension geometry exists.

**Strengths:**

- physical dependency can be strong;
- relationship can be distributed across one field;
- visually distinct from a divider.

**Risks:**

- causal explanation may depend on invisible tension vectors;
- renderer may invent ropes, cables, rigging or engineered contraptions;
- if one person supplies the counterweight, the image drifts into helper/controller semantics;
- if anchors dominate, people cease to be primary.

**Disposition:** retain as secondary research class, not preferred.

### M3 — Retained plastic deformation / body-imprint affordance

**Core physics:** one broad material field visibly retains a large-scale deformation that is no longer being made. A current body position/use depends on the retained depression, ridge or brace.

**Strengths:**

- `changed` can be legible through residual deformation;
- prior action need not be shown or attributed;
- persistence is naturally visible;
- can remain quiet and materially restrained.

**Risks:**

- quickly becomes chair/bed/nest/furniture shorthand;
- a depression can read as sculptural form rather than changed state;
- ordinary resting behavior may weaken Surface Escape/category distinctiveness;
- could merely support a comfortable pose rather than materially alter possibility.

**Disposition:** useful subfamily of M1, but weaker as an independent direction unless the body/load counterfactual is unmistakable.

### M4 — Interlocking brace / compression-state geometry

**Core physics:** a continuous field has been folded/compressed into a self-bracing form whose changed geometry physically carries one or more bodies.

**Strengths:**

- strong causal necessity;
- high changed-state legibility if baseline portions remain visible;
- supports non-symbolic physical evidence.

**Risks:**

- mechanism can become monumental architecture, bridge, sculptural installation or contraption;
- compressed form can dominate S1/S4;
- renderer may over-explain the engineering.

**Disposition:** promising only if subordinated to M1's human-first load-state logic; not preferred as an autonomous mechanism class.

### M5 — Distributed multi-contact support network

**Core physics:** several portions of one changed material configuration jointly support distinct body positions such that no single local contact is sufficient.

**Strengths:**

- can make two people structurally necessary;
- could express shared condition without one hub.

**Risks:**

- PAC-01E already demonstrated the central trap: visually distributed topology does not prove distributed dependence;
- additional constraints are likely to produce contraption/installation behavior;
- difficult to read in a mobile crop.

**Disposition:** reject for the next direction. It repeats a known empirical boundary rather than reducing uncertainty.

### M6 — Capture / retention of a secondary object or substance

**Core physics:** a changed condition traps, holds or exposes something that a person can now inspect/use/reach.

**Strengths:**

- counterfactual can be physically clear;
- changed state can be legible.

**Risks:**

- one clue prop or retained object can carry nearly all meaning, directly threatening Brief 02;
- can become ordinary work/retrieval/repair;
- people risk becoming operators of a mechanism.

**Disposition:** reject.

### M7 — Visibility / reflection / aperture state

**Core physics:** a changed condition alters what one person can see or inspect.

**Strengths:** static visibility can be externally observable.

**Risks:** this remains access mediation and is too close to Round 01's visibility/occlusion family; it also invites portal/window/threshold shorthand and viewpoint reconstruction.

**Disposition:** reject for Round 03 search.

### M8 — Temporal-trace inference in one still

**Core physics:** residue, correspondence or pattern exception implies a prior change and a present possibility.

**Strengths:** historically strong for changed-state/history recovery.

**Risks:** R0 evidence already shows that temporal comparison materially improves enabled-possibility observability; compressing that burden back into one static trace risks repeating the exact causal ambiguity now under reconciliation.

**Disposition:** retain as supporting evidence language only, not the primary next mechanism.

## Synthesis

The investigation eliminates a tempting but low-value move: simply make Round 02's barrier taller, opening smaller, or contact more impossible. That would remain separator/access geometry and risk becoming a disguised correction reroll.

It also eliminates another attractive dead end: distributed multi-object support. Historical PAC work already established that visual distribution can look useful without making any one configuration causally indispensable.

The highest-value mechanism changes the **kind of counterfactual**.

Rounds 01 and 02 asked:

`if the condition were restored, could the person still access/reach the other side/person?`

The preferred class asks:

`if the condition were restored, could this body physically remain where it is against gravity?`

That is a stronger one-frame test because gravity supplies the missing reference law. The viewer need not reconstruct intention, route choice, private state or a hidden prior frame.

## Recommended specific visual direction for Director decision

### GRAVITY-DEPENDENT SUPPORT-STATE CAUSALITY

Proposed commitment, if approved:

Develop Candidate 03 around a **broad continuous material field whose visibly changed deformation physically bears human body weight in a way its recoverable baseline state could not**.

Key direction laws for later specification development:

1. The altered state and altered possibility are the same visible physical fact: changed material geometry is visibly carrying a gravity-dependent body position.
2. Restoring the material to its recoverable baseline must make the depicted body position physically collapse, not merely become less convenient.
3. Two specific adults remain compositionally necessary; neither causes or maintains the changed state in the depicted moment.
4. Do not use a familiar chair, bed, hammock, ledge, bridge, platform, staircase, portal, hole or opening as the explanatory carrier.
5. Do not turn bodily support into a stunt, rescue, injury, accident, ritual or work task.
6. One broad shared field remains subordinate to the people; no engineered rig, cables, machinery or sculptural monument.
7. Calm left identity/copy region must be prospectively tonal-compatible with the frozen light identity system; no later scrim rescue.
8. Center-right semantic cluster must be capable of preserving both people + load-bearing deformation + gravity-dependent relation in the fixed 72% mobile crop.

This direction is intentionally narrower than M1 but remains pre-specification. Exact people, pose, material, deformation geometry, setting, palette, style and renderer text remain unselected.

## Why this is the recommended direction

- It attacks Altered Possibility and Removal Test at their physical root rather than altering scoring language.
- It uses a visible carrier as RST/PAC evidence recommends while avoiding PAC's `useful but not necessary` failure by tying possibility to body weight.
- It preserves independent agency because no person needs to control/help another to make the causal relation visible.
- It is materially different in mechanism from both prior qualifying rounds.
- It can preserve the human-first, quiet, restrained, non-ordinary gains of Round 02.
- Its main risks are known and falsifiable before rendering.
