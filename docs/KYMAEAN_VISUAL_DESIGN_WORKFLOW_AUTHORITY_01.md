# Kymaean Visual Design Workflow Authority 01

Updated: 2026-09-06

## Director approval

The Director has approved an optimized visual-design workflow for all future Kymaean visual asset development.

This approval modifies process efficiency, not design authority.

## Working principle

Use ChatGPT 5.6 Sol High Reasoning visual-design sessions efficiently by maximizing deep analysis, synthesis, audits, and construction work before consuming image generations.

Image generations remain a constrained experimental resource.

Do not generate images merely to explore concepts that can first be resolved through:

- repository analysis;
- visual mechanism investigation;
- semantic construction;
- geometry scaffolding;
- recursive audits;
- engineering-informed product understanding.

## Approval discipline

Routine approval requests are reduced.

Within an already-authorized design lane, proceed through:

1. analysis;
2. synthesis;
3. recursive audit;
4. construction;
5. documentation;
6. presentation of consequential decisions only.

Director approval remains required for:

- committing to a specific visual direction;
- changing frozen measurement contracts;
- authorizing image generation;
- transferring references/scaffolds into renderer workflows;
- changing product/design authority boundaries.

## Image generation discipline

Before any image generation:

- maximize non-render reasoning value;
- verify the candidate mechanism is sufficiently constructed;
- preserve experimental accounting;
- avoid spending generations on unresolved semantic problems.

After failed image experiments:

- analyze failure mechanisms;
- improve construction methods before additional generations;
- do not simply increase prompt complexity.

## Cross-repository continuity

Fresh visual-design chats should read:

1. `CURRENT_STATE.md`;
2. this workflow authority;
3. relevant handoff/evidence documents;
4. engineering truth from `Rylascoo/Ensemble-Project` when visual identity benefits from product semantics.

Engineering remains a coherence input, not aesthetic authority.

## Active trust model

The Director has delegated normal workflow execution judgment inside approved visual-design lanes while retaining gates for consequential decisions.

The goal is disciplined progress: fewer administrative interruptions, deeper reasoning, stronger audits, and deliberate image-generation usage.
