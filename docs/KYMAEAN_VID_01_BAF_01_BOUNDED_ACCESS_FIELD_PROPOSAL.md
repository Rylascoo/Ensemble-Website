# KYMAEAN VID-01 — BAF-01 BOUNDED ACCESS FIELD PROPOSAL

Primary workstream: `SHARED BRAND`

Preceding evidence:

- Director carrier map H4 `Bounded Field`

## 1. Purpose

V0R2 demonstrated that one shared condition can organize visibly unequal burden, readiness, dependence and access without requiring private-state claims. It did not establish a stronger H4 property:

> **Can one shared present reality make different evidence or access physically available to different people, while the artwork remains unified and never claims what anyone privately knows?**

BAF-01 distinguishes **bounded availability** from **bounded knowledge**.

The target is not mind-reading. The target is a visible present in which people occupy materially different relations to the same evidence, route, opening, trace, event or consequence.

## 2. Governing distinction

```text
different physically available evidence/access
!=
different Knowledge/Belief/Memory
```

A static artwork may establish that:

- one person can directly see, reach or inspect something another cannot;
- one person is positioned after an obstruction/opening another is before;
- one person has access to a material trace or consequence that is occluded or unavailable to another;
- one person acts on a part of the shared situation that another person cannot currently access.

It may not establish merely from this that someone:

- noticed the evidence;
- understood it;
- remembers it;
- believes it;
- knows its cause;
- interprets it correctly.

## 3. Unified-reality requirement

The image must remain one shared present reality.

Do not solve boundedness by:

- split panels;
- separate portraits;
- duplicated alternate realities;
- thought bubbles;
- tinted `perspective modes`;
- diagrammatic visibility cones;
- labels or legends.

The viewer should be able to understand that all people inhabit the same world while different parts of that world are presently available to them.

## 4. Human consequence requirement

Bounded access must matter to human possibility rather than existing as a clever visibility puzzle.

At least two people should have materially different present possibilities because their access differs.

Examples of admissible consequence classes, not required stories:

- one can continue through an opening another cannot use;
- one can reach or inspect evidence another cannot reach;
- one can act on a consequence that another can only encounter indirectly;
- one person's route or choice exposes a new region to another while leaving a third person without that access;
- one person's current position gives them material access to a trace that another person's position withholds.

No private motive or awareness need be asserted.

## 5. Asymmetry without hierarchy

The field should not imply that more access makes one person more important, more intelligent, more correct or more privileged by the artwork.

Boundedness is relational and situational.

A strong result should allow different people to have different kinds of access rather than reducing the image to `one informed person vs everyone else`.

## 6. Carrier threshold

H4 reaches provisional carrier viability only if the artwork demonstrates all of the following:

1. one unified present reality;
2. at least three distinct people;
3. materially different physically available evidence/access across at least two of them;
4. the asymmetry is recoverable from the scene without explanatory text;
5. the difference changes current human possibility or action;
6. no private Knowledge/Belief/Memory claim is required;
7. no split-panel or diagrammatic explanation carries the distinction;
8. human specificity remains primary;
9. the carrier survives removal of decorative atmosphere;
10. exact surface remains quarantined.

## 7. Falsification conditions

BAF fails if:

1. everyone effectively has the same access and only their reactions differ;
2. boundedness is inferred only from facial expression;
3. one person is visually coded as `the one who knows` without visible access evidence;
4. the image requires a thought bubble, label, diagram, view cone, map or separate panel;
5. separate scenes replace one shared reality;
6. access difference does not alter any present possibility;
7. the image becomes primarily an architecture puzzle with people as scale figures;
8. one giant barrier/metaphor overwhelms the human field;
9. a successful bounded-access mechanism is mistaken for a selected Kymaean visual family.

## 8. Relationship to current product truth

BAF-01 is visual carrier research only. It is informed by, but does not implement, the accepted product law that Presentation Perspective bounds disclosure and that social/co-present visibility does not itself establish Observation, Knowledge, Belief or Memory.

It must therefore preserve:

> **Representation can depict; authority must establish.**

and:

> **Social/relational visibility != epistemic authority.**

The artwork is allowed to depict visibly unequal access. It is not allowed to promote that access into fictional epistemic truth.

## 9. Formal-control strategy

The next probe isolates the semantic carrier and leaves medium, palette, setting, era, scale and degree of abstraction open.

Reason:

- V0R7A already established that positive depiction-level formal control can generate whole-surface orthogonality at probe level;
- BAF-01 needs first to determine whether bounded availability itself can organize a compelling human artwork;
- adding a strong formal grammar now would confound carrier success with surface success.

Any recurring painterly prior remains quarantined and receives no incumbency.

It does not select a visual family, authorize convergence, change app/website architecture, authorize implementation or make runtime/Store claims.

Preparing and executing one sterile V0R9 renderer packet therefore requires no additional intermediate Director approval.
