## Overall result

> **STRONG TEMPORAL-OBSERVABILITY PASS / ACCESS-SHORTCUT LIMITATION**

The pair provides materially more causal information than either moment alone. Specific authorship, persistence and later use are all directly recoverable through comparison.

## Limitation

The result achieves that clarity through an extremely literal physical-access route:

> stool repositioned -> later person can reach higher shelf

This is semantically adjacent to the earlier access/accommodation/crossing attractor family even though it is ordinary and non-monumental.

The two-panel split also introduces explicit storyboard/sequential grammar.

Therefore TEO-01 establishes **temporal information gain**, but does not yet establish **semantic-route robustness**.
