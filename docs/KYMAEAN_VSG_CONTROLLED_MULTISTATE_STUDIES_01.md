# 1. Controlled semantic packet

The study uses deliberately neutral semantic facts so the visual grammar, not narrative scenery, carries the experiment.

No study may alter these facts to make its design easier.

## 1.1 Persistent scope

```text
PRODUCTION: P0
SCENE: S0
PARTICIPANTS: Character A, Character B, Character C
CURRENT DISCLOSURE PERSPECTIVE: Creator / Production unless otherwise stated
```

`Character A/B/C` are controlled placeholders only. They are not proposed creator-facing names or a future portrait system.

## 1.2 Present / Watch facts

```text
A, B and C are present.
A and B currently carry the strongest interaction focus.
C is present and relevant but is not the current attention center.
Relationship condition R1 is salient now.
Pressure P1 is active now.
A compact transcript/supporting language excerpt exists but is subordinate to the people and relationship.
Creator commands are available but not primary content.
```

No private mental state is asserted by this packet.

## 1.3 Causal Trace / Historical Focus facts

```text
CURRENT CONDITION: R1
RELEVANT EFFECTIVE PRIOR EVENT: E1
PRIOR CONDITION: R0
CAUSAL RELATION: E1 contributed to R0 -> R1
CURRENT AUTHORITATIVE PRODUCTION remains current while E1/R0 are inspected.
BACK means prior focus, not undo.
```

The study does not freeze a one-parent causal model. E1 is the minimum controlled cause needed for comparison.

## 1.4 Character-Bounded Perspective facts

```text
PERSPECTIVE: Character B
SAME PRODUCTION: P0
SAME SCENE: S0
SAME PARTICIPANTS: A, B, C
CREATOR-ONLY relationship/pressure detail is not disclosed.
Only information available to Character B under the controlled disclosure packet remains present.
Privileged creator commands are absent.
```

The visual study must remove/omit privileged information rather than merely dim it.

## 1.5 Causal-effect sequence facts

Moment 1 — non-effective material:

```text
Performance material M1 exists for review.
M1 is not yet effective Production history.
```

Moment 2 — effective causal change:

```text
An authoritative causal-effect boundary succeeds.
The controlled effective event becomes E2.
Current relationship condition changes from R1 to R2.
Pressure P2 remains unresolved / still active.
```

Moment 3 — changed present:

```text
PRODUCTION: still P0
SCENE: still S0
PARTICIPANTS: still A, B, C
CURRENT CONDITION: R2
UNRESOLVED / STILL ACTIVE: P2
NEXT POSSIBILITY differs because R2/P2 are now current.
```

This packet intentionally avoids final creator-facing Take/Accept/Commit terminology. Exact Take acceptance and causal effectiveness remain distinct authority dimensions.

# 3. VSG-A — Human Editorial / Typographic

## 3.1 Hypothesis

> **Kymaean can feel human, authored and theatrical when people, names, language scale, whitespace and editorial hierarchy carry most of the identity — with very little ornamental UI geometry.**

This study tests typography as an experiential material without restoring O0's exact dark/gold/serif surface.

## 3.2 Deliberate surface posture

Study-only posture:

- predominantly high-key / light-neutral field;
- mostly flat composition with negligible simulated material depth;
- one restrained non-semantic accent family for emphasis, with all meaning surviving without it;
- operational text quiet and systematic;
- expressive narrative type large, spatial and human-centered;
- almost no universal cards;
- hard alignment, whitespace and typographic scale do most of the grouping;
- human representation is figurative enough to read as people but simplified enough that names/posture/placement remain important identity invariants.

No exact font, hue, portrait medium, radius or spacing token is selected by this study.

## 3.3 Control 1 — Present / Watch

```text
P0 / S0                                      creator perspective
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

            CHARACTER A                 CHARACTER B
            large identity              large identity
                 [human]       ↔            [human]

                        CHARACTER C
                           [human]

              R1 — current relational condition
                     P1 — active pressure

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
small supporting language / transcript excerpt

                         Direct     Perspective     More
```

Editorial intention:

- the eye lands on specific people before controls;
- names and human presence work together;
- relationship is communicated through whitespace, orientation and alignment rather than a graph;
- transcript reads as supporting editorial copy, not a chat column;
- commands sit on a lower visual register.

## 3.4 Control 2 — Causal Trace / Historical Focus

```text
P0 / S0                     HISTORICAL FOCUS             Current →
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHY R1 IS TRUE NOW

R1   current condition
│
│   E1   relevant effective event
│
R0   prior condition

short causal explanation / affected relationship

Back                                                   Return current
```

Editorial intention:

- history feels like an explanatory reading state, not a timeline destination;
- current condition is typographically dominant even while historical evidence is inspected;
- E1 and R0 use a clearly different temporal register without relying on fading alone;
- no permanent chronology rail appears.

## 3.5 Control 3 — Character-Bounded Perspective

```text
P0 / S0                         PERSPECTIVE: CHARACTER B
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

            CHARACTER A                 CHARACTER B
                 [human]       ↔            [human]

                        CHARACTER C
                           [human]

          information available to this perspective only

       [creator-only relationship/pressure material absent]

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
available action if permitted                     Exit perspective
```

Editorial intention:

- the composition remains recognizably the same Production;
- bounded perspective is experienced as subtraction and re-prioritization;
- the visual identity of B becomes more prominent without recoloring the entire application around B;
- the absence of privileged information creates the experiential difference.

## 3.6 Control 4 — Causal-effect sequence

```text
MOMENT A                         MOMENT B                       MOMENT C
non-effective                   effective change               changed present

M1                              E2                             P0 / S0
PERFORMANCE MATERIAL            R1 → R2                        A  B  C
not effective yet               P2 remains active              CURRENT: R2
                                                               ACTIVE: P2

review language                 what changed                   Continue
```

Editorial intention:

- non-effective/effective meaning is carried by wording, hierarchy and structural placement before color;
- the changed-present frame visually resembles Control 1 but its relational text/hierarchy is changed;
- continuity is demonstrated by recognizable people/Production returning after the explanatory moment.

## 3.7 Strengths

- strongest direct inheritance of O0's `the people are the scene` experiential intelligence without copying its surface;
- strong narrative/theatrical identity potential;
- excellent transcript restraint;
- naturally supports a quiet shell around people;
- typography can carry Character/Scene/history identity at several scales.

## 3.8 Failure risks

- can drift into magazine/editorial-portfolio aesthetics rather than a living interactive Production;
- expressive typography can become the spectacle and displace people;
- large type can become difficult under localization/text scaling if treated as rigid composition;
- human representation may remain under-specified if typography does too much identity work.

# 4. VSG-B — Relational Field / Abstract Human

## 4.1 Hypothesis

> **Kymaean can become visually distinctive when persistent people are represented through specific invariant human forms and relationship itself organizes the field — without becoming a social graph, causal diagram or anonymous token system.**

## 4.2 Deliberate surface posture

Study-only posture:

- mid-value continuous field rather than high-key editorial page;
- human representations are reduced/abstracted but retain unmistakable human-form cues and individually persistent structural signatures;
- typography is restrained and mostly operational;
- spatial relation carries more weight than typographic scale;
- each Character may have a restrained accessible identity accent, but identity survives monochrome;
- line/shape language is open, thin and connective rather than boxed/contained;
- material depth is minimal; composition is primarily planar and relational.

No force-directed physics, animated graph solving or generic circular-avatar nodes are permitted.

## 4.3 Control 1 — Present / Watch

```text
P0 / S0                                              Creator


        [ A human-form ]  ───── current attention ─────  [ B human-form ]
                 \                                      /
                  \                                    /
                   \                                  /
                          [ C human-form ]

                       R1        P1


supporting language appears locally near the human action, not as a transcript wall

                                                     Direct   More
```

Relational intention:

- the primary composition is a social field, not panels;
- A/B relation becomes immediately legible through position/orientation;
- C remains a specific present person rather than disappearing because C is not current focus;
- relationship lines/spacing are descriptive aids, never the only semantic carrier.

## 4.4 Control 2 — Causal Trace / Historical Focus

```text
P0 / S0                     HISTORICAL FOCUS                  Current →

CURRENT
     [ A ]  ───────────────  [ B ]
                     R1

                     ↑ E1

PRIOR
     [ A ]  ───────  [ B ]
             R0

     [ C ] remains represented where relevant to the shared state

Back                                                       Return current
```

Relational intention:

- causal explanation appears as a comparison of relational organization rather than a timeline;
- earlier/later geometry provides observability while labels preserve accessibility;
- the interface avoids implying that distance itself is a quantified relationship score.

## 4.5 Control 3 — Character-Bounded Perspective

```text
P0 / S0                               PERSPECTIVE: CHARACTER B

        [ A ]  ───────────────  [ B ]
                                 /
                                /
                           [ C ]

        only disclosure-available relation/context markers remain

        creator-only R1/P1 annotation layer is absent

                                                Exit perspective
```

Relational intention:

- B's bounded experience changes which relations/annotations are available, not the underlying people;
- no privileged line remains visible as a dim ghost;
- identity invariants keep A/B/C recognizable despite reduced information.

## 4.6 Control 4 — Causal-effect sequence

```text
MOMENT A                    MOMENT B                    MOMENT C

[ A ]   [ B ]              [ A ]────[ B ]             [ A ]──────[ B ]
   M1 non-effective          E2: R1 → R2                  current R2
      [ C ]                     [ C ]                     [ C ]
                              P2 active                    P2 active
```

Relational intention:

- the changed present is legible as changed organization, not celebration animation;
- E2 is an explanatory transition, not a permanent connector overlay;
- the composition can settle completely still after the state change.

## 4.7 Strengths

- strongest embodiment of R0's relationship-as-visual-structure law;
- potentially the most category-distinctive app language;
- naturally keeps people rather than data objects at the center;
- strong opportunity for persistent identity through invariant forms;
- makes changed-present continuity visible without requiring a separate Archive destination.

## 4.8 Failure risks

- highest risk of collapsing into diagram/social-graph language;
- abstract people can become anonymous tokens if human specificity is too reduced;
- spatial semantics can become inaccessible or fragile under narrow layouts if equivalents are weak;
- visual relationship may be overread as quantified psychological truth.

# 5. VSG-C — Material / Living Record

## 5.1 Hypothesis

> **Kymaean can make continuity and consequence feel physically persistent through quiet digital material depth and changed registration/layering — without resorting to parchment, collage, antiquarian archives, magical traces or glassmorphism as identity.**

## 5.2 Deliberate surface posture

Study-only posture:

- predominantly matte digital surfaces with shallow, restrained depth;
- current state occupies the clearest/frontmost readable layer;
- contextual/historical material can occupy subordinate registered layers without becoming a permanent stack of cards;
- human representations use concise figurative contour/relief cues with persistent identity features;
- typography is compact and precise rather than editorially oversized;
- color posture is low-chroma with structural value/contrast doing most semantic work;
- shape language uses registration, edge, inset and shallow layer relationships rather than universal rounded containers.

No paper fibers, torn collage edges, sepia/antique coding, magical glow or simulated physical damage is part of the hypothesis.

## 5.3 Control 1 — Present / Watch

```text
P0 / S0                                           Creator
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
│                                                            │
│        A relief-form       B relief-form                   │
│              \               /                             │
│               \    R1       /                              │
│                 C relief-form                              │
│                                                            │
│                 P1 — active                                │
│                                                            │
╰────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯
      supporting language appears on the same current layer

                                               Direct   More
```

Material intention:

- one readable current surface dominates;
- people appear embedded in the living state rather than floating as cards;
- shallow material difference supports hierarchy but is not needed for comprehension.

## 5.4 Control 2 — Causal Trace / Historical Focus

```text
CURRENT LAYER
╭─────────────────────────────────────────────╮
│ R1 — current condition                     │
╰─────────────────────────────────────────────╯
             ↑ consequence / E1

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ R0 — prior condition                       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

HISTORICAL FOCUS                 Current →
Back                             Return current
```

Material intention:

- history is a subordinate explanatory layer attached to a present question;
- layer ordering reinforces current authority without implying the past is physically buried or damaged;
- solid/high-contrast fallback can express the same relation through borders/headings/order alone.

## 5.5 Control 3 — Character-Bounded Perspective

```text
P0 / S0                        PERSPECTIVE: CHARACTER B
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
│                                                            │
│        A relief-form       B relief-form                   │
│                              \                             │

│                                                            │
╰────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯

[creator-only registered layers are absent, not translucent]

                                               Exit perspective
```

Material intention:

- disclosure difference comes from which layers exist, not an atmospheric filter;
- same base Production surface remains recognizable;
- material metaphor never implies that hidden information is physically present underneath and merely obscured.

## 5.6 Control 4 — Causal-effect sequence

```text
MOMENT A                     MOMENT B                    MOMENT C

M1                            E2                           CURRENT P0 / S0
non-effective                causal-effect               current layer
review layer                 boundary                    registered as R2
                                                         P2 remains active

[separate from current]      R1 → R2                    same A / B / C return
```

Material intention:

- non-effective M1 occupies a clearly separate review layer;
- effective E2 changes the authoritative current registration rather than simply stamping an `accepted` badge;
- the final surface is stable and still, with R2/P2 now part of current organization.

## 5.7 Strengths

- strongest native language for persistence, authority and state transition;
- potentially strong bridge between current Production and causal history without a permanent timeline;
- can make consequence feel durable while remaining quiet;
- naturally compatible with quiescent rendering if depth is mostly static/system-managed.

## 5.8 Failure risks

- material metaphor can become more important than people;
- shallow layers can regress into card stacks or glassmorphism;
- `living record` can drift toward archive/paper/antiquarian shorthand;
- historical layers can imply that hidden/old information is literally underneath current state rather than conceptually related.

# 7. Grammar comparison

## 7.1 Human primacy

- **A:** strongest immediate human/name prominence.
- **B:** strong when human forms remain specific; weakest if abstraction slips into nodes.
- **C:** adequate but most vulnerable to material/state layers outranking people.

## 7.2 Relationship as visual structure

- **A:** relationship primarily through whitespace/orientation and typographic grouping.
- **B:** strongest direct expression of relational structure.
- **C:** relationship readable but secondary to persistence/layer logic.

## 7.3 Identity continuity

- **A:** promising through name + figure + typographic signature.
- **B:** strongest test of invariant identity under abstraction.
- **C:** promising through recurring contour/relief identity marks.

## 7.4 Bounded perspective

- **A:** strong through editorial subtraction/reflow.
- **B:** strong through removal of unavailable relational/context annotations.
- **C:** strong only if absent information is truly absent rather than implied underneath.

## 7.5 Causal history

- **A:** clearest verbal/explanatory reading.
- **B:** clearest relational before/after organization.
- **C:** strongest persistence/effect distinction, but highest metaphor-overreach risk.

## 7.6 Changed-present return

- **A:** recognizable through repeated editorial human composition with changed R2/P2 hierarchy.
- **B:** potentially strongest visual `same people, changed relationship` return.
- **C:** potentially strongest `same Production, newly registered current state` return.

## 7.7 Accessibility

- **A:** strongest natural reading-order potential; large expressive composition must reflow responsibly.
- **B:** highest burden to provide non-spatial equivalents and deterministic narrow ordering.
- **C:** high-contrast/solid fallback must preserve every layer distinction without material depth.

## 7.8 ARM64 / battery

All three can be quiescent.

- **A:** lowest baseline rendering complexity.
- **B:** low if positions are deterministic and no physics/continuous animation exists.
- **C:** low to moderate depending on eventual material implementation; must not depend on continuous custom effects.

# 8. Design-team interpretation — no convergence decision

The studies reveal three genuinely different strengths rather than one obvious winner.

### VSG-A currently carries the strongest O0 experiential continuity

It most naturally preserves:

> **The people are the scene.**

It also gives Kymaean literary/theatrical character without requiring decorative AI spectacle.

Its risk is becoming an editorial product *about* the Production rather than the Production itself.

### VSG-B currently carries the strongest uniquely Kymaean structural possibility

It most directly expresses:

> **people + relationship as the interface**

and offers the clearest path to making the changed present visually undeniable.

Its risk is severe: one wrong reduction turns human beings into graph nodes.

### VSG-C currently carries the strongest authority/history material logic

It gives causal persistence and current/history distinction a quiet physical intuition.

Its risk is allowing `record/history/material` metaphor to dominate human presence or slide toward historical-surface attractors.

Therefore:

> **No branch should be selected or synthesized yet.**

The correct next evidence is rendered/visualized comparison, not more abstract argument.
