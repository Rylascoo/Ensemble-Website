# KYMAEAN LANE B — WORDMARK DIVERGENCE 01

Status: FIXED THREE-DIRECTION ROUND COMPLETE / DIRECTOR SELECTION GATE  
Date: 2026-09-05  
Workstream: SHARED BRAND / W0 WORDMARK

## Scope

A bounded wordmark-only divergence. No primary symbol, app icon, palette, Stage carrier, website composition, or typography system is selected here.

Historical evidence establishes that a deliberate KYMAEAN word treatment is valuable, but historical strength is not selection. This round therefore starts from the name itself rather than embedding Threshold K, Consequence Aperture, or another prior symbol.

## Frozen round criteria

A viable direction must:
1. read clearly as `KYMÆAN`;
2. make the `Æ` deliberately authored without turning it into a pasted icon;
3. survive monochrome and small-scale use;
4. work in both app-titlebar and website-hero roles;
5. remain understandable without colour;
6. avoid dependence on AI/model, fantasy, Greek/classical, portal/threshold, or luxury shorthand;
7. remain buildable as deterministic vector geometry;
8. preserve accessible text identity independently of vector decoration.

Exactly three directions were built. No fourth direction is introduced after seeing the results.

## A — Tensional Æ

**Result: RANK 1 / RECOMMENDED SURVIVOR**

An open, lightly inscriptional construction with one deliberate moment concentrated in the `Æ`: its A/E structure shares a spine while a restrained horizontal extension creates tension at the ligature.

Strengths:
- strongest sense that the word itself has been designed;
- `Æ` remains legible at small scale;
- works as hero and quiet titlebar mark;
- monochrome integrity is strong;
- does not need a separate symbol to feel owned.

Risk:
- serif terminals + all-capital construction can drift toward classical/heritage shorthand. Refinement must move away from monumental inscription rather than toward it.

## B — Causal Cut

**Result: RANK 2 / VIABLE BUT NOT RECOMMENDED**

A heavier constructed system using small discontinuities at selected joints.

Strengths:
- robust at small scale;
- clearest engineered rhythm;
- strong monochrome behavior.

Failure pressure:
- the cuts read increasingly like a tech/identity system rather than literary/theatrical authorship;
- the special treatment is less naturally concentrated in the name's unique `Æ`.

## C — Open Counter

**Result: RANK 3 / DO NOT ADVANCE**

A monoline, spatially generous system with softened joins.

Strengths:
- calm, humane, highly legible;
- least likely to feel ornamental;
- technically flexible.

Failure:
- insufficient ownership. Removing the study label leaves a competent generic modernist construction rather than a specifically Kymaean word.

## Recommendation

Advance **A — Tensional Æ** as the W0 refinement incumbent.

The recommendation is for its **structural idea**, not its exact stroke weights, serif shape, spacing, or final drawing. The next refinement should preserve:
- word-first identity;
- the authored `Æ`;
- small-scale clarity;
- monochrome independence;

while explicitly reducing:
- classical/inscriptional associations;
- decorative serif excess;
- any tendency for the `Æ` to become a standalone emblem inside the word.

Director selection is still required before A becomes shared-brand authority.
