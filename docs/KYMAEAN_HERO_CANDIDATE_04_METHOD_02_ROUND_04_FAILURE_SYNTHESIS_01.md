The Stage 01–06 construction was internally valid before rendering. The approved scaffold explicitly contained:

## 3. Primary failure mechanism — causal-geometry naturalization

The renderer preserved the scaffold's **gross composition** but not its **causal topology**.

Preserved:

- two people center/right;
- A lower, B higher;
- direct arm contact;
- one broad continuous ground-like field;
- large calm-left region;
- approximate mobile-safe clustering.

Lost:

- finite-width baseline correspondence as a readable changed-state witness;
- A load as visible source of the local deformation;
- B support as a consequence of that same deformation;
- physically necessary baseline-restoration failure;
- the distinction between a changed field and terrain that simply has that shape.

The line-based structural reference functioned as a **composition sketch** to the renderer. Its three field contours were reinterpreted as ordinary grass/earth relief rather than as redundant correspondence constraints on one deforming finite-width field.

This is the central finding.

## 4. Secondary failure mechanisms

### 4.1 Neutral relation -> pastoral intimacy drift

Once the causal field became ordinary terrain, the remaining visible explanation for the arm contact became interpersonal staging. Gaze, proximity, age contrast, soft natural light, and pastoral setting then pulled the image toward a quiet intimate/editorial reading.

This does not violate Epistemic Restraint because no private state is asserted as required truth. It does show that **neutral contact cannot carry the intended relation by itself after causal geometry disappears.**

### 4.2 Calm-left geometry -> integration-luminance drift

Stage 05/06 successfully preserved a low-information left region, but the renderer realized it as high-key pale sky/water. Harness 01 fixes light Threshold K/O3/copy over that region with no scrim.

Thus `calm` was preserved while `identity-compatible` was not.

This is not a reason to alter Harness 01 after the fact. It exposes an under-controlled prospective translation variable: the future sterile translation must keep the calm-left region optically compatible with the fixed identity scaffold without selecting a final brand palette.
