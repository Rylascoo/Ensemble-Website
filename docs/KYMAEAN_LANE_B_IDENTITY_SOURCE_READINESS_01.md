## 01 — Threshold K deterministic trace

The only current geometric authority for Threshold K is the isolated historical raster control.

A deterministic local SVG trace was generated from that control solely to support stable design testing.

Static equivalence evidence at the 170 × 233 source scale:
- connected fields: `3`;
- silhouette IoU: `0.9616` / `96.16%`;
- mean symmetric boundary delta: `0.475 px`;
- 95th-percentile boundary delta: `0.955 px`.

Interpretation:

**PASS for deterministic testing adapter.**

This is strong evidence that the historical candidate can be represented deterministically without materially redesigning its silhouette.

It is **not** sufficient to call the trace a final production logo master because:
- Threshold K itself is not finally selected;
- the source authority is raster, not an original authoring vector;
- the trace intentionally discards the source board's subtle surface texture in favor of `currentColor`;
- final optical cleanup would require a later explicit production-master decision.

Therefore the SVG is named and governed as a **working candidate trace**, not a final logo asset.
