# KYMAEAN ENTRY EXPERIENCE DECISION PROPOSAL 01

Status: DIRECTOR PROPOSAL FOR REVIEW

Phase: Blueprint 01 experience-model refinement

Authority:

- This document is a design proposal.
- It does not freeze final onboarding, UX, terminology, visual identity, or implementation.
- Production code is not authorized from this document.

## 1. Purpose

Resolve the smallest high-leverage question after the Product Experience Model Proposal:

> When a new person encounters Kymaean, what should they immediately understand they are doing?

This decision must precede visual-system selection because identity, website narrative, app structure, and artwork strategy depend on the user's mental model.

## 2. Established foundation

Validated:

- Creator authority is primary.
- Characters are persistent creative entities, not models.
- Performance, consequence, and continuity are meaningful product concepts.
- Causal integrity matters.
- Privacy and bounded disclosure are foundational principles.

Open:

- Final creator terminology.
- Final Studio/Production language.
- Final Take/branch/rehearsal experience.
- Final Character Context disclosure.

## 3. Entry perspective candidates

### Creator-first

Initial understanding:

> I create a living world.

Strengths:

- immediately establishes agency;
- aligns with creator authority;
- supports authoring workflows.

Risks:

- consequence may appear secondary;
- can resemble ordinary creation software if not differentiated.

### Witness-first

Initial understanding:

> I experience a world that continues.

Strengths:

- communicates living consequence;
- emphasizes characters as persistent entities.

Risks:

- may obscure creator responsibility;
- may imply passive consumption.

### Understanding-first

Initial understanding:

> I discover why this world became what it is.

Strengths:

- emphasizes continuity and causal reasoning;
- aligns with history and provenance concepts.

Risks:

- may feel analytical before emotional connection.

### Cycle-first

Initial understanding:

> I participate in an ongoing creative process.

Strengths:

- represents the complete lifecycle;
- avoids reducing Kymaean to one surface.

Risks:

- requires careful communication.

## 4. Proposed direction for review

Do not select one perspective as the whole identity.

Proposal:

Kymaean should be understood as a continuous creative lifecycle, while the first entry experience should prioritize creator agency.

Conceptual model:

Create
  ↓
Experience
  ↓
Understand
  ↓
Change
  ↓
Continue

This preserves:

- creator authority;
- character continuity;
- consequence visibility;
- future participant/observer possibilities.

## 5. Design implications

If approved as direction:

App:

- should not open as a passive viewer;
- should not expose engineering authority concepts by default;
- should allow progressive movement from creation into consequence.

Website:

- should introduce Kymaean through agency and continuity rather than generic AI capability claims;
- should explain difference from chat/content generation through persistence and consequence.
