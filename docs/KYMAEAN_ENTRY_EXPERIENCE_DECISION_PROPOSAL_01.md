## 1. Purpose

Resolve the smallest high-leverage question after the Product Experience Model Proposal:

> When a new person encounters Kymaean, what should they immediately understand they are doing?

This decision was originally assumed to precede visual-system selection because identity, website narrative, app structure, and artwork strategy depend on the user's mental model.

That sequencing assumption is now superseded by the recovered ontology synthesis.

## 3. Historical entry perspective candidates

### Creator-first

Initial understanding:

> I create a living world.

Strengths:

- immediately establishes agency;
- aligns with creator authority;
- supports authoring workflows.

Risks:

- consequence may appear secondary;
- can resemble ordinary creation software if not differentiated.

### Witness-first

Initial understanding:

> I experience a world that continues.

Strengths:

- communicates living consequence;
- emphasizes characters as persistent entities.

Risks:

- may obscure creator responsibility;
- may imply passive consumption.

### Understanding-first

Initial understanding:

> I discover why this world became what it is.

Strengths:

- emphasizes continuity and causal reasoning;
- aligns with history and provenance concepts.

Risks:

- may feel analytical before emotional connection.

### Cycle-first

Initial understanding:

> I participate in an ongoing creative process.

Strengths:

- represents the complete lifecycle;
- avoids reducing Kymaean to one surface.

Risks:

- requires careful communication.

## 4. Historical proposed direction

The original proposal suggested understanding Kymaean as a continuous creative lifecycle while prioritizing creator agency in the first entry experience:

Create
  ↓
Experience
  ↓
Understand
  ↓
Change
  ↓
Continue

This remains potentially useful exploratory framing, but it is not current authority and does not replace the richer recovered causal/product-space/posture model.

## 5. Historical design implications

The original proposal suggested that the app should not open as a passive viewer, should avoid exposing engineering authority concepts by default, and should allow progressive movement from creation into consequence. It also suggested introducing Kymaean on the website through agency and continuity rather than generic AI capability claims.

Those observations may be reconsidered later, but they are not current approved design decisions.
