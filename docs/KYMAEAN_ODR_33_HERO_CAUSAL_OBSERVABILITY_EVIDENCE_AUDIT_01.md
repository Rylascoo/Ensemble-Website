## Question

**ODR-33 — Website hero causal-observability burden**

Should the hero itself make one specific earlier human author directly recoverable, or should later deterministic site sections carry that burden while the hero carries a lower causal burden?

## Evidence

### Site Spine 01
Approved hero job: identity, `local-first generative theater`, Stage/product-truth evidence, invitation into the explanatory sequence.

Exact causal truth is allocated later to the causal-cycle interlude and Causal Scar / History.

Narrative order:

`FEEL THE WORLD -> UNDERSTAND THE PRODUCTION -> EXPERIENCE UNEQUAL PERSPECTIVE -> UNDERSTAND CAUSAL COMMIT -> SEE HISTORY -> ...`

So the approved page architecture already distributes semantic burden.

### Hero Brief 01
This later Lane B brief increased the local burden by requiring present evidence of prior human action and `prior-cause recoverability >= 4`.

Attributable Aftermath later strengthened that again into recovery of one specific earlier human source.

Neither escalation came from frozen Blueprint/ODR authority.

### R0 cross-medium audit
Static evidence supports people/relationships and conditional present-history recovery, but classifies specific prior authorship and the full prior-choice -> persistence -> later-enabled-action chain as **PARTIAL / STATIC BOUNDARY**.

Its consolidated contract explicitly says the evidence does **not** justify requiring one static artwork to prove specific prior authorship, the full enabled-possibility chain and the entire internal Kymaean thesis simultaneously.

### TEO-01 / TEO-02
Both scored **Visible authorship PASS** using two-moment comparison.

TEO-02 law:

**When specific human authorship, persistence and changed possibility must be directly recoverable, before/after state information can carry semantic burden that one static surface cannot reliably carry alone.**

This selects no hero treatment, animation, split panel, or interaction pattern.

### R1 / A1
The reconciled record makes these single-still transfer tests, not tests of whether visual authorship is possible.

A1's independent attribution failure was A5; the other attribution failures were downstream. It therefore shows fragility in compressing TEO-level authorship into one aftermath-only generated still.

## Options

### A — hero must prove one specific earlier author
Benefit: immediate causal thesis.

Costs: exceeds R0 static evidence, duplicates later causal sections, increases explanatory/renderer pressure, and constrains the site's primary world-setting artwork.

### B — hero carries living consequence; later sections carry exact authorship
Benefits: matches Site Spine and R0 evidence, preserves artistic headroom, keeps the hero human/relational/consequential, and leaves proposal/Take/commit/history precision to deterministic sections.

Cost: hero alone does not prove the whole causal chain.

## Recommendation and decision

**Option B approved.**

**The website hero is not required to make one specific earlier human author directly recoverable. It should carry human presence, a changed shared condition, living consequence and altered present possibility. Exact authorship / commit causality belongs to the later deterministic causal-cycle and history sections.**

Incidental authorship clarity remains welcome; it ceases to be a mandatory hero advancement gate.
