## 1. TCV-01 research question

Primary question:

> **How can Kymaean show that circumstances, relationships, opportunities, and available actions changed over time while the Character remains the same person?**

The experiment must distinguish:

```text
stable Character identity
from
changed Production circumstance
```

and avoid requiring:

- Character morphing;
- emotional-state visualization;
- personality-evolution meters;
- motion-only temporal meaning;
- permanent before/after storyboard structure;
- private-mind inference.

## 4. What the single-surface result establishes

The experiment supports a more precise changed-present law:

```text
stable Character identity
+
changed relational organization / opportunity
+
trustworthy current authoritative evidence
=
legible causal continuity
```

The audit rejects an artificial requirement that body position alone must encode the complete causal history.

The stronger product principle is:

> **Relationship and current evidence share the burden of making the changed present understandable.**

This is consistent with the accepted A/B convergence thesis:

> **Relationship organizes the living Production; editorial hierarchy explains only what the relationship cannot responsibly carry alone.**

Temporal continuity therefore does not require a new temporal visualization layer to exist permanently on the Stage.

---

## 5. Current evidence vs historical explanation

TCV-01 distinguishes two forms of information.

### Legitimate current evidence

A current Character-bounded surface may present facts that are true and available now, such as:

- the revised agreement is present;
- updated terms are operative;
- a current constraint is absent/present;
- an available action/opportunity changed;
- current authoritative state differs from an earlier state even when that earlier state is not displayed.

### Historical explanation

The Stage should not automatically narrate:

- what changed;
- when it changed;
- why it changed;
- a before/after causal chain;
- a thematic interpretation of the change.

Those jobs may later belong to causal trace/history when the creator asks `why is this true now?`.

Required distinction:

> **Present evidence may carry provenance without becoming a permanent history summary.**

## 6. What remains partial

TCV-01 is not a full Stage-surface pass because:

- the human relational arrangement still carries only part of the temporal signal;
- current textual/object evidence carries significant explanatory weight;
- editorial narration such as `New possibilities build from what's real.` and `The work moves forward.` remains renderer contamination;
- a persistent Recent Performance strip remains an unproven design hypothesis;
- Patch 0014 currently keeps canonical `recentPerformances` empty in Context v2;
- generic `Unknown` wording such as future/additional opportunities can reveal categories not legitimately known to exist;
- photoreal meeting-room staging remains test scaffolding rather than selected Character/Stage medium.

These are known boundary issues. Another TCV render is not justified merely to force all causal meaning into human pose/geometry.

## 8. Identity / temporal continuity law

CRV + TCV jointly support:

```text
Character identity
    remains stable

Production circumstance
    may change

relationship / evidence / opportunity
    may reorganize

current Stage
    reflects the changed reality
```

A Character depiction changes only when fictional appearance legitimately changes, not merely because Production state changed.

Required law:

> **The world around the Character may change without requiring the Character's identity representation to change.**

## 9. Accessibility + ARM64 implications

Temporal meaning must survive:

- still-frame presentation;
- reduced motion;
- monochrome/high contrast;
- reduced-detail Character representation;
- alternate textual/programmatic equivalents;
- image-unavailable states where appropriate.

Do not require:

- continuous animation;
- animated morphing;
- graph physics;
- persistent GPU/NPU rendering;
- decorative inference of emotional state;
- background AI to keep the Stage visually alive.

The product remains computationally quiescent when Production state is unchanged.

## 11. Next unresolved design boundary

- Character-bounded evidence;

`SIPD-01 — Stage Information Presence & Progressive Disclosure`
