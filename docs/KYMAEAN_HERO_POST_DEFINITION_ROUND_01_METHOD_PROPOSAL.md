# KYMAEAN — HERO POST-DEFINITION ROUND 01 METHOD PROPOSAL

Status: DIRECTOR METHOD PROPOSAL / NO RENDERER PACKET OR RENDER AUTHORIZED
Date: 2026-09-06

## Purpose

Define the smallest defensible first qualifying experiment under the approved Hero Surface Ownership Operational Definition and frozen Falsification Criteria without turning the renderer prompt into a checklist, brand imitation exercise, or post-hoc optimization loop.

This is method design only. It contains no renderer-facing prompt.

## Authority entering

- Hero Artwork Brief 02 remains the hero-burden authority.
- ODR-33 remains resolved: the hero carries human presence, changed shared condition, living consequence and altered present possibility; exact authorship/commit causality remains later deterministic-site work. Specific prior-author recovery is advisory only.
- Hero Surface Ownership Operational Definition 01 is approved.
- Hero Surface Ownership Falsification Criteria 01 is Director approved and frozen.
- Surface Escape remains mandatory.
- Quiet Stage, Mineral Theater, Threshold K and O3 retain their current temporary/non-final authority boundaries.
- No prior hero image is promoted.

## Experimental question

Can one newly generated, surface-independent hero artwork satisfy the full Brief 02 + S1-S6 + Surface Escape set when the renderer is given only the minimum picture-making conditions needed to make the experiment possible, while brand coexistence and crop behavior are tested deterministically after generation?

## Method architecture

### 1. One isolated candidate, one untouched first output

Use one fresh regular non-project Renderer Context. It receives no prior hero image, no historical Kymaean artwork, no G1/R1/A1 critique, no Stage/Threshold K/O3 image, and no cross-candidate comparison.

One first image only. No correction chain, retry, in-chat refinement, alternate version, or second interpretation inside this qualifying round.

### 2. Keep Director criteria out of the renderer packet

The renderer-facing packet must not reproduce the scoring rubric or name the S-gates. Measurement language is Director-side authority, not image-generation vocabulary.

The future packet should encode only four positive picture-making necessities:

1. **Necessary specific people** — at least two distinct people whose relationship is structurally necessary to the artwork.
2. **Changed shared condition** — the present situation visibly contains a material/spatial condition produced by prior human involvement; exact prior author need not be recoverable.
3. **Altered present possibility** — that changed condition materially changes what another depicted person can presently do, occupy, reach, use, avoid, approach or otherwise physically negotiate.
4. **Asymmetric compositional hierarchy** — substantial quiet/low-information space coexists with a denser consequential human region so desktop identity/copy and tall/mobile crop can be tested later.

All other requirements remain evaluation constraints unless a later audit proves that one must be explicitly exposed to the renderer.

### 3. Do not seed brand geometry or historical failure vocabulary

The future packet must not name Kymaean, Quiet Stage, Mineral Theater, Threshold K, O3, cones, logos, negative-space K geometry, prior failed genres, prior candidate labels, or historical attractor inventories.

This prevents the measurement contract from becoming the visual seed.

### 4. Surface role guidance must remain pictorial, not stylistic doctrine

The packet may require restraint in density, shallow-to-moderate visual hierarchy, meaningful quiet, and a materially coherent field, but it must not prescribe a named art movement, production medium, historical period, culture, architecture, cinematic genre, fantasy vocabulary, luxury treatment, or fixed palette.

Mineral Theater conformance is tested after generation through the approved role-remap criterion; it is not converted into literal mineral subject matter or forced color values in the renderer packet.

### 5. Brand coexistence is a deterministic post-render test

Threshold K and O3 remain separate identity assets. They are not embedded in the artwork prompt.

After the raw image is archived and independently scored, one deterministic hero-integration check may place unchanged temporary identity assets and normal website copy geometry beside/over the candidate solely to evaluate S6, Brand Integration and responsive coexistence. This integration is not a second artwork and does not modify the raw image.

## Qualifying-round validity

A round counts against the three-round stop budget if the returned image is a single evaluable artwork produced from the approved frozen packet and can be scored against the hard gates.

A render is method-invalid rather than a scored failure only when the output is not meaningfully judgeable because of execution failure such as a branded presentation board instead of artwork, a multi-panel comparison when one artwork was required, corrupt/incomplete output, or another direct format-level failure that prevents the defined tests from being applied.

Do not reclassify a weak image as method-invalid merely because it fails semantic, surface, crop, identity-coexistence or removal gates.

## Evaluation order

Archive the untouched raw output before critique. Then evaluate in this order:

1. format/method validity;
2. Hero Brief 02 hard gates;
3. S1-S6 independently, with no averaging;
4. frozen Surface Escape;
5. deterministic desktop identity/copy coexistence;
6. deterministic meaningful tall/mobile crop;
7. only after independent scoring, compare against prior evidence for diagnostic lineage.

No aesthetic preference may rescue a failed hard gate.

## Round-01 accounting

Method approval does not consume a qualifying round. The counter changes from 0/3 to 1/3 only after an actual method-valid first output is archived and scored.

If Round 01 fails, record the smallest failed requirement set and whether any failure appears to be a requirement collision. Do not automatically revise the prompt or run a correction. A second round requires a new bounded method decision informed by the frozen evidence.

If Round 01 passes every Brief 02 hard gate, S1-S6 and Surface Escape, it becomes eligible for Director hero-candidate review only. It does not become a final hero or final visual-world authority automatically.

## Recursive audit findings

The method deliberately separates **what must be true of the result** from **what must be said to the renderer**. This avoids repeating the earlier pattern in which accumulating causal, material and anti-attractor language itself became a visual seed.

The four renderer necessities are the minimum currently justified by the hero job. S3 Mineral Theater conformance, S5 non-imitation, and most of S6 brand coexistence are intentionally post-render tests because exposing them directly would increase style seeding without being necessary to generate the semantic event.

The round-validity rule prevents both budget gaming and unfair budget consumption from a non-artwork execution failure.

No additional positive style doctrine is introduced.

## Boundary / next gate

Director approval of this method is required before a sterile renderer packet is written.

This proposal authorizes no renderer-facing prompt, render, Candidate 03, final hero selection, final style system, implementation, or Lane A change.
