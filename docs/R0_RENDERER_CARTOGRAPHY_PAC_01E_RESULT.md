## Interpretation

PAC-01E successfully reduced the dominant-hub shortcut seen in PAC-01D. The carrier is distributed across a dense compact field of ordinary chairs, crates and small surfaces rather than centered on one tabletop.

However, the critical no-single-element condition is not robustly observable.

The left writing action remains largely explainable through a familiar seat + work-surface relationship. The standing folding/organizing action is still largely explained by horizontal support surfaces. The foreground shoe-tying posture is mostly explainable through a chair. The right drawing/sketching action is mostly explainable through a crate/seat and lap.

The arrangement contributes to the scene, but most uses remain interpretable as conventional local affordances.

## Principal finding

> **Distributed carrier topology is not distributed configuration dependence.**

Eliminating one dominant hub can produce a dense constellation of ordinary objects without proving that multi-element relationships are causally necessary for the human actions.

## PAC empirical ladder

- PAC-01A: visible built carrier -> plural supported use;
- PAC-01B: repositionable ordinary objects -> plural object-level affordance;
- PAC-01C: relationships among ordinary objects -> local emergent affordance;
- PAC-01D: one shared arrangement -> overlapping use with dominant-hub collapse;
- PAC-01E: distributed object cluster -> reduced hub dominance, but no robust no-single-element sufficiency.

## Methodological consequence

The PAC branch has reached diminishing returns under the current carrier topology.

Increasingly forceful prompt language about distributed necessity is now more likely to produce:

- contraptions;
- modular furniture systems;
- installations;
- engineered products;
- semantically dominant objects;

than to yield cleaner evidence of human-created persistent capability.

Do not automatically proceed to PAC-01F.

The next checkpoint should synthesize PAC-01A through PAC-01E and decide whether a different observability mechanism is warranted before any new render.
