## 4. VID-01 / V0 sterile controlled render packet 05

Use verbatim:

```text
Create one complete, uninterrupted artwork centered on several distinct people sharing an active present situation.

Give each person a different action, timing, orientation, and degree of participation. Let one person's action visibly redirect, interrupt, open space for, constrain, or alter the compositional situation another person is acting within. Keep the people independently specific rather than synchronized into one collective gesture.

Make the expressive structure of the picture respond to those human actions. Use repeated shapes, interruptions, overlaps, gaps, directional marks, changes of density, and shifts of visual weight so the field itself carries timing and mutual effect. Treat those changes as part of depiction and composition rather than as literal magical emissions, diagrams, arrows, or explanatory symbols in the fictional world.

Keep individual identity legible through several coherent human cues such as body proportion, posture, hair shape, garment construction, characteristic gesture, and local detail. No person should collapse into a generic icon or anonymous silhouette.

Use a shallow, visibly constructed picture plane. Organize the artwork with broad shape masses, sparse contour, deliberate negative space, restrained texture, and a small number of clear tonal levels. Let the surrounding world appear through selective planes and spatial cues rather than deep modeled scenery. Preserve enough human detail for each person to remain specific while allowing the image to operate as an expressive field rather than a naturalistic scene.

Invent the exact people, circumstance, palette, shapes, marks, scale, rhythm, local objects, and degree of abstraction yourself within that formal grammar. The result should stand first as a compelling artwork, not as a demonstration.

Return only the uninterrupted artwork. Add no readable text, labels, interface, logo, promotional framing, presentation board, explanatory border, split panel, portrait lineup, arrows, graph, timeline, or legend.
```

## 6. What would count as H5 evidence

Strong H5 evidence requires more than a graphic-looking image.

The artwork should allow a viewer to recover at least some of this structure without explanatory text:

```text
person acts
-> shared field changes
-> another person's available/action space or response is visibly affected
-> people remain independently themselves
```

The field may be expressive, but it must remain human-dependent.

Removal test:

> Removing or neutralizing one person's action should materially weaken the field's organization or the visible relation among people.

## 7. Falsification conditions

V0R5 fails the central H5 test if:

- the people become generic decorative figures;
- action is merely posed rather than structurally affecting the shared field;
- repeated marks/shapes exist independently of what people are doing;
- all people synchronize into one harmonious collective pattern with weak independent agency;
- the image becomes an infographic, graph, diagram or symbolic map;
- the formal grammar overwhelms human specificity;
- a literal stage/show/performance venue becomes the primary explanation;
- readable text becomes necessary to understand the image;
- the renderer ignores the shallow controlled grammar and returns another deep painterly naturalistic interior;
- the result is visually different only by palette while preserving the same underlying pictorial logic;
- private emotion/intent is required to explain the visible relationship.
