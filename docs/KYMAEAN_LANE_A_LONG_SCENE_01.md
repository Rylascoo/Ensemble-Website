## Purpose

Stress the revived O0 Stage at an illustrative 40 accepted Performances without letting chronology overtake social presence:

```text
live Stage
-> recent accepted Performance + current opportunity
-> open Scene thread on demand
-> recent chronological slice
-> earlier accepted Performances on demand
-> jump/return to current Stage
```

`40` is a design stress state, not a Scene limit or frozen Turn ontology.

## Authority basis

SIPD establishes recent Performance/transcript as progressive/on-demand, not permanent Stage furniture. The live Stage keeps Production/Scene/Perspective/Posture continuity, people/current action, and materially active opportunity ahead of full chronology.

The Scene thread is deliberately distinct from causal Archive history:

- **Scene thread** answers what happened in accepted-Performance sequence;
- **Archive causality** answers what became effective, what changed, what remains unresolved, and why.

Opening either must not mutate or rewind current Production state.

## Surface result

Default Stage around Performance 40 shows:

- three present Characters;
- explicit current opportunity + non-obligation language;
- most recent accepted Performance;
- one `Open Scene thread` action;
- no permanent transcript rail.

When opened wide, one chronological inspector sits adjacent to the still-visible Stage. The prototype initially exposes Performances 33–40 and makes 1–32 explicitly expandable. The 8/32 split is testing instrumentation, not a selected retention or virtualization threshold.

On a narrow layout, opening the thread becomes sequential focus: Stage -> Scene thread -> Return to Stage. No essential meaning depends on simultaneous columns.

## Long-scene laws supported

- Importance does not imply permanence.
- People/current action remain the Stage's first responsibility even after substantial history exists.
- Full transcript/history remains discoverable without becoming default chrome.
- Current accepted Performance and Current Opportunity stay distinct.
- Accepted chronology does not become causal-state authority merely because it is inspectable.
- Rejected/cancelled/alternate material is not silently inserted into the accepted Scene thread.
- Exact motion/pacing timings remain open until Read-Through evidence exists.

## Interaction/accessibility requirements

`Open Scene thread` exposes `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`, transfers focus to the visible return control, and closing returns focus to the invoking control. Earlier history uses a reversible `aria-expanded` disclosure instead of removing its focused trigger. `Jump to current` restores the latest accepted Performance locus.

This is DOM/static interaction evidence only; no WinUI or assistive-technology runtime validation is claimed.
