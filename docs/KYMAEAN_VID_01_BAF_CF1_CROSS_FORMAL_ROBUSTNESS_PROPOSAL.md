## 1. Why this test exists

V0R9 gives H4 `Bounded Field` provisional carrier-level viability:

> one continuous shared present can contain materially different physical access across several specific people without claiming what anyone privately knows.

But V0R9's exact surface falls back into the repeated painterly / worn / lived-in human-world renderer prior.

V0R7A separately demonstrated that a positive depiction-level formal grammar can break that prior at probe level, but its H5 action-interdependence carrier did not pass.

The next unresolved question is therefore not another H4 semantic variant and not another arbitrary style search.

It is:

> **Can the passing H4 bounded-access carrier survive deliberate replacement of the painterly/naturalistic surface grammar?**

## 2. Experimental separation

BAF-CF1 keeps two axes distinct:

```text
semantic carrier viability
!=
formal-family viability
```

The test deliberately combines:

- one already-supported semantic carrier: H4 bounded physical availability;
- one controlled positive depiction grammar: shallow constructed plane / figure-ground organization.

This does **not** make the controlled grammar an incumbent or candidate identity by default.

The grammar is an experimental instrument.

## 3. Semantic kernel held constant

The image must preserve:

1. one unified present reality;
2. at least three specific, non-interchangeable people;
3. materially unequal physical access across those people;
4. at least two materially different present action possibilities because access differs;
5. no requirement to infer Knowledge, Belief, Memory, understanding, motive or emotional state;
6. no all-knowing focal figure;
7. human primacy;
8. no split-panel, diagram or explanatory text solution.

The target distinction remains:

> **physically available evidence/access != epistemic authority.**

## 4. Positive formal grammar under test

The depiction should be organized as a shallow constructed field rather than a conventionally modeled deep room or cinematic scene.

Required formal properties:

- broad interlocking planes and shape masses;
- strong figure-ground separation;
- substantial negative space;
- restrained depth cues;
- access relationships expressed through adjacency, overlap, cut edges, occlusion, openings and reachable/unreachable regions in the picture plane;
- environment reduced to only what is necessary to make the shared reality and differing access legible;
- people remain humanly specific rather than becoming anonymous icons, symbols or game pieces;
- no one large physical prop may substitute for the formal field.

No exact V0R7A palette, composition, cast, setting, clothing, geometry or object vocabulary is inherited.

## 5. Why this is not convergence

A successful result would show that H4 survives at least one materially different formal grammar from V0R9.

That would be evidence for:

```text
carrier robustness across surface replacement
```

It would **not** prove:

- that the controlled grammar is Kymaean's identity;
- that V0R9's painterly family is a candidate;
- that H4 is the governing shared-brand carrier;
- that H2 should be rejected;
- that a final symbol, wordmark, palette, typography, app surface or website composition is selected.

If V0R10 succeeds strongly enough to create a second genuinely distinct surviving visual family, that only satisfies a prerequisite for a **Director convergence decision**. The project must stop before automatically entering convergence.

## 6. Falsification

BAF-CF1 fails semantically if:

1. different access collapses into different unrelated tasks with no coherent shared present;
2. everyone effectively has the same access;
3. facial reaction rather than visible physical availability carries the distinction;
4. a private-state claim is needed to make the image meaningful;
5. one giant barrier/object becomes the idea;
6. people become scale figures around architecture.

BAF-CF1 fails formally if:

1. conventional volumetric/deep naturalistic environment again dominates;
2. broad-plane language is literalized as one giant scene object;
3. the image becomes a poster/diagram/infographic;
4. human specificity collapses into generic symbols;
5. the exact V0R7A surface is merely copied or recolored.

## 7. Success threshold

A strong pass requires all of:

- H4 bounded-access semantics remain legible;
- epistemic restraint remains intact;
- people remain specific and primary;
- the controlled formal grammar is materially different from the recurring painterly/naturalistic family;
- the formal grammar appears at depiction level rather than as one literal prop;
- the result stands as one coherent artwork;
- exact surface remains quarantined pending Director comparison.
