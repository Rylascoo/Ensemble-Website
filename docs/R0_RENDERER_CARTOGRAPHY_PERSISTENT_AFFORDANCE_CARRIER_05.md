## PAC-01E

Name:

**Persistent Affordance Carrier 01E — Distributed Dependency / No-Single-Element Sufficiency**

Research question:

> Can one modest arrangement of ordinary movable elements visibly enable several different independent actions through distributed relationships among its parts rather than through one dominant hub object?

## Exact sterile packet used

> Create one uninterrupted artwork centered on several distinct people whose presence and relationships are necessary to the image.
>
> A few ordinary movable human-made elements were arranged before this moment into one compact shared configuration. Several people are independently using that same configuration at the same time for different actions. At least two different actions should each depend on the relationship among several elements, and those actions should rely on overlapping parts of the same arrangement. No single element should be sufficient by itself for those uses; the arrangement should work through relationships among its parts rather than through one dominant object.
>
> Keep the elements recognizable as ordinary everyday things. They should not appear specially built or modified for this purpose. The original act of arranging them is not shown.
>
> Keep every person specific and self-directed. No one should guide, help, direct, rescue, or control anyone else. Keep the shared configuration visually subordinate to the people.
>
> Keep the surrounding world restrained. Let posture, orientation, spacing, and independent use reveal why several elements working in relation to one another matter.
>
> Return only the uninterrupted artwork, with no text or surrounding presentation layout.
