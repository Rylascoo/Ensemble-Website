# Kymaean Renderer Cartography & Prompt Intelligence Protocol

Status: **ACTIVE R0 PROJECT LAW FOR VISUAL-GENERATION RESEARCH**

Updated: 2026-09-03

Purpose: convert Kymaean image-generation research from repeated open-ended prompting into a durable, evidence-based model of how the current renderer responds to semantic, compositional, lighting, medium, material, and constraint language.

## 1. Why this protocol exists

Renderer Isolation remains necessary, but the first four Cross-Surface Robustness experiments established that isolation alone does not produce genuinely unrelated visual grammars.

The renderer repeatedly translated abstract causal language into familiar high-probability visual shortcuts even when those surfaces were not named:

- Experiment 01: changed condition / prior cause -> monumental inherited architecture and opening/threshold logic;
- Experiment 02: unseen earlier choice / relational consequence -> period-coded painterly interpersonal drama;
- Experiment 03: immediate changed possibility -> literal accessibility/crossing intervention in ruined architecture;
- Experiment 04: visible relational change -> monumental antiquarian interior with a literal suspended bridge.

Therefore the project must stop treating the renderer's spontaneously chosen surface as a useful independent variable.

> **Renderer Isolation controls project-context leakage. Renderer Cartography controls experimental variables.**

## 2. Governing correction

For cross-surface research, the Director controls both:

1. the **semantic kernel** that must survive; and
2. the **formal grammar** being tested.

The Renderer invents only the unconstrained local manifestation.

Do not ask the renderer to discover an orthogonal artistic language while simultaneously proving the semantic law. That leaves the variable under test uncontrolled and mainly measures the model's default semantic-to-image shortcuts.

## 3. Evidence classes

Every prompt/generation claim must be labeled by source class.

### A. PROJECT EMPIRICAL LAW

Observed repeatedly in Kymaean experiments and supported by archived prompt/output evidence.

Examples:

- examples are visual seeds;
- broad invented-world language can activate cinematic concept-art priors;
- history/consequence language can collapse into age, ruin, craft, repair, or monumentality;
- changed-possibility language can collapse into access, traversal, accommodation, route, bridge, or repair;
- open-ended relational drama can collapse into period-coded figurative melodrama;
- one-image disposable renderer chats materially reduce project-context inheritance but do not remove base-model priors;
- changing many prompt variables at once destroys attribution.

### B. OFFICIAL OPENAI GUIDANCE

Use only where current OpenAI documentation supports the claim.

As of 2026-09-03, official OpenAI image-prompt guidance supports:

- prompts can be short; 1–3 clear sentences are often enough;
- clarity and specificity are more useful than ornamental wording;
- specify main subject/action, setting when needed, desired visual style, and important framing/lighting/constraints;
- direct constraints are useful when something must stay fixed or absent;
- small targeted revisions are generally preferable to broad uncontrolled changes;
- multiple reference images can be used deliberately, with clear roles for each.

Do not elevate undocumented implementation claims into project law.

### C. EXTERNAL HYPOTHESIS — TEST BEFORE ADOPTION

External prompting guides may propose useful control vocabulary, but claims remain hypotheses until either official documentation or Kymaean experiments support them.

Current external hypotheses worth testing include:

- camera/lens vocabulary may influence apparent field of view, depth of field, perspective, and framing;
- lighting vocabulary may influence direction, hardness, contrast, color temperature, and atmospheric behavior;
- explicit material language may influence surface roughness, reflectivity, translucency, and tactile character;
- composition vocabulary may provide stronger control than vague aesthetic adjectives;
- deliberate de-biasing descriptions may suppress some default polished/cinematic rendering tendencies.

### D. GENERATOR-SPECIFIC / NOT TRANSFERABLE BY DEFAULT

Do not apply syntax from another generator to ChatGPT Images unless OpenAI explicitly supports it.

Examples:

- Midjourney `::` weighting;
- Stable Diffusion `(term:1.3)` weighting;
- assumptions about sampler, CFG, seed, denoising, LoRA, or checkpoint controls unavailable in the active ChatGPT image surface.

## 4. Claims explicitly NOT project law

The project currently has no verified basis for these strong claims:

- that the first 3–5 words always receive the highest mathematical weight;
- that every additional word mathematically dilutes previous words in a predictable way;
- that technical jargon targets specific hidden "neural pathways";
- that naming a real camera/lens forces physically accurate optical simulation;
- that a particular adjective or technical term has a stable numerical weight;
- that the Director can inspect or directly manipulate the renderer's latent/vector space.

These may inspire experiments, but fresh chats must not repeat them as facts.

- image model/product surface when known;
- source asset location and exact asset state;
- semantic kernel;
- subject/person requirements;
- temporal/causal requirements;
- relational requirements;
- setting language;
- composition/framing language;
- camera/lens language;
- lighting language;
- medium/formal-language instructions;
- material/surface language;
- technical/rendering language;
- positive constraints;
- negative constraints;
- reference-image use;
- what the renderer invented without being asked;
- dominant surface family;
- recurring attractors;
- causal-gate result;
- artistic-language-gate result;
- user signal;
- Director judgment;
- transferable discovery;
- surface quarantine;
- confidence / missing evidence.

Unknown fields remain UNKNOWN or PENDING RECOVERY. Do not reconstruct false precision.

## 6. Retrospective-first rule

Before spending additional image generations, audit all recoverable evidence already created:

- RI-001A/001B through RI-011;
- AL-A01 through AL-H01;
- Round 1 studies and synthesis images;
- Cross-Surface Robustness Experiments 01–04;
- prior website/brand visual branches still available through Drive, File Library, repository evidence, or current runtime;
- prompt/evaluation records and explicit user reactions.

The goal is to extract a prompt -> renderer-response map before designing the next generation round.

## 7. Controlled Surface Matrix

After the retrospective dataset is sufficiently populated, test cross-surface robustness with controlled formal grammars.

Keep the semantic kernel substantially constant. Change one meaningful formal variable or one tightly coupled grammar family at a time.

Possible formal-control dimensions include:

- picture-plane depth: shallow / volumetric;
- contour behavior: continuous / broken / absent;
- massing: large contiguous fields / fragmented elements;
- negative-space proportion;
- figure completeness: full / cropped / partial;
- overlap / occlusion behavior;
- edge quality: hard / soft / mixed;
- tonal range: compressed / expanded;
- color organization: restrained / distributed / local-accent;
- texture density: smooth / sparse / tactile;
- perspective: flat / deep / axonometric-like / close observational;
- lighting structure: diffuse / directional / high-key / low-key;
- representational distance: literal / simplified / abstracted while retaining person-specific identity.

These dimensions are controls, not selected Kymaean aesthetics.

Avoid named artists, cultures, movements, or historical eras unless the experiment intentionally tests those seeds.

## 8. Contrastive experiment law

When diagnosing prompt behavior:

> **Change one meaningful variable; preserve the rest.**

Do not rewrite the entire prompt after each failure.

For every contrastive pair, record:

- variable changed;
- variable held constant;
- predicted effect;
- observed effect;
- unintended effects;
- confidence that the variable caused the change.

If attribution is weak, do not promote the result to project law.

## 9. Render-budget law

Every generation round must define a maximum render budget and a stop condition before execution.

Default R0 rule:

- design a small orthogonal matrix;
- generate one image per renderer chat;
- stop early if two or more probes collapse into the same attractor family;
- diagnose and update the dataset before generating again.

No endless corrective prompt chains.

## 10. Reference-image portability tests

Once an independently interesting formal grammar exists, text-only recreation is not the only test.

A later controlled experiment may use the artwork itself as a visual reference while explicitly changing people, relationships, or local circumstances.

The question becomes:

> **Can the formal grammar transfer without copying the literal scene?**

Reference-image use must be logged as a separate experimental variable. A successful reference transfer is evidence of system portability, not automatic brand selection.

## 11. Renderer Isolation remains active, but with a narrower claim

Continue using:

- persistent Ensemble project = Director Context;
- fresh regular non-project chat = short-lived Renderer Context when image generation is required;
- normally one image per renderer chat.

However, do not claim this creates a mathematically memory-free renderer. It is an operational isolation method that reduces known project-context contamination.

Do not spend additional render budget on memory-negation preambles such as "ignore all memories" or "start with no image memory." They do not provide a verifiable isolation control and may themselves add irrelevant prompt content.

Temporary Chat is not the project default because image generation has been observed unavailable there in the user's current product configuration.

## 12. Fresh-chat project intelligence

Fresh Director chats must read this protocol before designing new renderer experiments.

They must distinguish:

- durable Kymaean semantic law;
- empirical renderer behavior;
- current formal-control hypothesis;
- user preference;
- Director judgment;
- external prompt-engineering hypothesis;
- generator-specific syntax;
- selected identity, which remains NONE during R0.

A fresh chat must not restart prompt-engineering research from generic internet advice if the Kymaean dataset already contains relevant evidence.
