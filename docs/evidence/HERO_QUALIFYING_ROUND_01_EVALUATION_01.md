### Human necessity — **3 / 5 — FAIL**

The three adults are visibly distinct through silhouette, age, garment structure and posture, and they create a human triangle. However the broad planar environment remains at least as strong as the people as the image's primary event. Removing or genericizing the figures would leave a coherent abstract architectural/material composition with much of its visual identity intact. Human presence contributes meaning but does not yet dominate the proposition strongly enough for the >=4 floor.

### Changed-condition legibility — **2 / 5 — FAIL**

The image clearly contains angular misaligned planes, but it does not make a prior coherent alignment or a visibly *changed* band recoverable from the still. The geometry reads primarily as deliberately designed abstract architecture/material organization rather than as a shared condition that has materially changed. Packet knowledge is required to identify which planes are the displaced condition.

### Altered possibility — **2 / 5 — FAIL**

The image does not independently establish the intended coupling `changed field -> materially different present physical availability for another person`. The center figure is visible to the external viewer, while the claimed difference between Person A's and Person C's positional availability is not recoverable from this third-person still without the renderer packet explanation. Separation/occlusion is present, but changed possibility is not legible enough for the >=4 floor.

### Epistemic restraint — **5 / 5 — PASS**

No Knowledge, Belief, Memory or intent is asserted as fact. Facing direction and posture remain observable behavior rather than proof of private state.

### Removal test — **2 / 5 — FAIL**

Because the consequential change is not independently legible as a changed condition, there is no clearly recoverable causal feature whose removal would collapse the image's primary proposition. Re-aligning or simplifying the planes would still leave a composed three-person encounter in an abstract material setting. The consequential change is therefore not sufficiently load-bearing for the >=4 floor.
