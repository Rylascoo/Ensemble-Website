This is now the strongest orthogonal lane. Color is explicitly named by the roadmap as a first-class SHARED BRAND divergence dimension. That makes the primary variable the system of color roles and contrasts, not inherited Mineral Theater hues or generic brand polish.

SELECTED QUESTION

CLR-01 — CROSS-SURFACE COLOR-LOGIC INDEPENDENCE

Question:
Can Kymaean exhibit a distinctive, accessible and product-compatible shared-brand color logic across both a restrained app-like utility context and an expressive public-web context when symbol/wordmark, bespoke typography, material/texture, imagery, Quiet-Stage composition, motion signature, product-state red/green semantics and historical palette incumbents are removed as identity carriers?

The experiment tests whether color relationships can contribute an independently recognizable identity mechanism. It does not select a final production palette, semantic status colors, Windows theme implementation, website theme, material system or final identity.

WHY CLR-01 IS THE BEST NEXT LANE

- It is first-class Phase-B SHARED BRAND scope in the canonical roadmap.

- It avoids reopening Stage motion, Character representation, hero composition, iconography or app implementation.

MANDATORY ISOLATION LAW FOR THE LATER METHOD

The CLR-01 method must freeze before specimen execution:

- neutral geometry and system typeface carrier;
- no symbol, wordmark, icon family, imagery, material texture, gradient spectacle, blur/glow effects or bespoke type as identity carriers;
- motion disabled or held identical and non-scored;
- no success/error/warning/loading semantics encoded by candidate hues;
- identical content/state across families;
- color-role definitions expressed structurally (field, foreground, secondary, accent, boundary, de-emphasis), not as named brand colors;
- APP/WEB adaptation rules that preserve family logic while allowing bounded intensity/value adaptation;
- WCAG/accessibility floors and non-color meaning equivalence before subjective review;
- historical Mineral Theater/current palette quarantine;
- at least three materially different color-logic families plus generic control(s);
- deterministic 320px and wide contexts where text remains readable and no meaning depends on color alone;
- no post-result reroll of failed families.

INITIAL FALSIFICATION TARGETS

A CLR-01 family should fail if:

- its distinctiveness collapses to generic dark-tech neon, generic SaaS blue, generic luxury black/gold, generic pastel wellness, rainbow gradient, or another obvious category convention;
- identity depends on material/texture/glow/gradient spectacle instead of color relationships;
- APP and WEB require unrelated palettes rather than one coherent logic with bounded adaptation;
- required text/controls fail contrast or color-only meaning rules;
- the family relies on red/green or other status semantics not present in the neutral fixture;
- neutral/high-contrast/reduced-color fallback destroys content/state comprehension;
- the family silently inherits Mineral Theater or another historical incumbent;
- reviewer preference is used to promote a final palette rather than evaluate a bounded independent mechanism.
