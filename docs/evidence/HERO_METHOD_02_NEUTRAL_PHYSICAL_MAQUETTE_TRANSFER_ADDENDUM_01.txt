Status: METHOD ADDENDUM CONSTRUCTED / RECURSIVE AUDIT REQUIRED / NO CANDIDATE MAQUETTE OR RENDER AUTHORIZED

Parent method: HERO SUCCESSOR DESIGN-CONSTRUCTION METHOD 02
Trigger evidence: HERO METHOD 02 TRANSFER-SURFACE SUFFICIENCY INVESTIGATION 01

PURPOSE

Define a mechanism-neutral deterministic physical-maquette transfer representation that can carry causally necessary support/contact/depth geometry more directly than the current line/silhouette scaffold, without selecting final aesthetic variables or authorizing any renderer execution.

This addendum does not replace Method 02 Stages 1–7. It adds one optional transfer representation between an approved Stage 6 construction and any later Stage 7 renderer package.

CORE PRINCIPLE

When a candidate's causal proposition depends on physical support, body stability or surface depth that prose cannot reliably preserve, the transfer surface may embody those relationships as deterministic neutral physical geometry.

The maquette carries only physical relational geometry.
It carries no final visual authorship.

Optional inserted representation layer after Stage 6 approval:

Stage 6M — Neutral Physical Maquette Transfer Representation.

Stage 6M may exist only when:
- Stage 6 is already approved;
- a clean method/candidate audit establishes that critical physical geometry is not sufficiently transfer-visible in the ordinary sanitized Stage 6 reference;
- the maquette can be generated deterministically from the approved Stage 6 construction rather than artistically improvised.

Stage 6M does not alter frozen post-render criteria.

COORDINATE / PROJECTION LAW

The maquette uses a normalized 2.5D coordinate system derived directly from the approved Stage 6 source artboard:
- x = normalized source-artboard horizontal coordinate;
- y = normalized source-artboard vertical coordinate;
- z = bounded depth/volume coordinate used only to make physical thickness/contact/support visible;
- Stage 6 x/y relationships must remain invariant unless a separately audited candidate correction explicitly changes them;
- all maquette geometry must be reproducible from a machine-readable manifest.

Transfer projection:
- orthographic;
- aligned to the Stage 6 source plane;
- no perspective lens effects;
- no candidate-specific camera rotation for beauty/drama;
- one fixed projection definition per approved maquette format;
- responsive crop windows remain Director-side audit facts and are not drawn into the transferable output.

ALLOWED STRUCTURAL VARIABLE CLASSES

1. shared_surface_volume
- one finite-thickness surface generated from approved Stage 6 condition geometry;
- local changed geometry and baseline witness geometry may be volumetric;
- no final material identity.

2. human_proxy_volume
- anonymous volumetric proxy for Person A or B;
- may encode approved body-region proportion, body axis, torso mass, lower-body geometry and support footprint;
- may encode distinct anonymous proportions only where required by approved construction;
- no facial identity or demographic coding.

3. foot_support_geometry
- explicit lower contact geometry generated from the approved support polygon/footprint;
- must be part of the human proxy, not a labeled technical rectangle in the transferable output;
- intended to make support extent physically visible without arrows/measurements.

4. body_contact_geometry
- actual proxy adjacency/contact patch at an approved causally necessary contact locus;
- may encode contact area/orientation;
- no force arrows, labels or emotional/narrative semantics.

5. required_negative_space_volume
- empty volume that must remain free of alternate supports;
- Director-side audit object only unless its absence is naturally visible in the maquette;
- no wireframe box may transfer.

6. baseline_witness_geometry
- portions of the same shared surface that preserve approved simpler organization on both sides of the changed zone;
- no labels or registration arrows.

7. causally_required_occlusion
- only when approved Stage 6 construction requires a front/behind relation for physical legibility.

EXCLUDED VARIABLES

Stage 6M must not encode:
- final palette or brand color;
- final material identity;
- surface texture/patina;
- final lighting design;
- camera drama, depth-of-field or lens effects;
- clothing style;
- facial features;
- hair style as identity;
- age, ethnicity, race, culture or historical period;
- expression, gaze meaning, emotion, motive, knowledge, belief or memory;
- props or symbolic objects;
- setting/architecture beyond the approved shared-condition geometry;
- Threshold K/O3 geometry;
- brand marks;
- text, labels, arrows, dimensions, grids or explanatory annotation;
- final visual style or named artist/style reference.

INSTRUMENTATION SHADING LAW

A volumetric transfer view needs minimal depth legibility. Therefore Stage 6M may use only fixed instrumentation shading:
- one uniform neutral base value for all physical geometry;
- no hue coding;
- no material differentiation by color;
- no specular highlight;
- no texture;
- no atmospheric effects;
- no cinematic shadow design;
- fixed low-amplitude diffuse/ambient depth cue sufficient only to reveal volume/contact;
- no candidate-specific light placement or tuning;
- transparent background by default unless the renderer interface requires otherwise;
- if a non-transparent background is technically required, use one fixed neutral value defined by the method implementation and treat it as non-aesthetic instrumentation.

The exact implementation must be frozen before candidate-specific use and may not be tuned after viewing a candidate maquette for beauty.

HUMAN PROXY LAW

The transferable human proxy must:
- remain recognizably human enough for body/support geometry to transfer;
- remain anonymous enough not to seed a character identity or statue/sculpture aesthetic;
- encode actual support footprint visibly;
- encode body-axis/torso relation visibly;
- encode the approved physical contact as actual adjacency;
- avoid facial/head detail beyond a generic head volume;
- avoid hand/arm detail unless hands/arms are themselves causally required by an approved candidate; for D9-like passive-brace work, hands/arms remain non-supporting and should not become focal transfer geometry.

SUPPORT-VISIBLE PREFLIGHT

Before a candidate-specific Stage 6M can advance, the transferable view must pass without Director-only guides:

1. identify A's support footprint from proxy geometry;
2. identify B's support footprint from proxy geometry;
3. visually recover B's changed-state body-axis/support relation;
4. visually infer that the depicted B proxy would not remain statically stable without the approved A contact under the candidate's already-frozen conservative model;
5. preserve A as independently stable;
6. show no alternate support contact.

This is a prospective transfer sufficiency test, not a new post-render score.

BASELINE / CHANGED-STATE PREFLIGHT

The transferable maquette must pass:

1. surface-only: a neutral reviewer can identify one larger simpler surface organization and one local departure;
2. surface-only: the local departure does not read primarily as a complete purpose-built ramp/platform/chair/device/installation;
3. B removed: A + surface does not read as a purpose-built human support;
4. all people removed: the surface does not require damage/rubble/accident spectacle to explain the change;
5. baseline witness evidence exists on both sides where required by the candidate construction;
6. the changed-state inference does not depend on a technical grid, seam legend, arrow or label.

MAQUETTE / MANNEQUIN LEAKAGE PREFLIGHT

Reject before renderer transfer if the sanitized Stage 6M view reads primarily as:
- sculpture installation;
- gallery maquette;
- mannequin tableau;
- gray-clay concept art;
- CAD/engineering diagram;
- architectural model;
- videogame blockout;
- wireframe/reference sheet.

A neutral physical reference can be visibly non-final, but its primary read must remain physical relational geometry rather than a named/recognizable production medium.

SANITIZED TRANSFER LAW

A future Stage 7 package may transfer only one byte-pinned sanitized Stage 6M view that contains:
- physical maquette pixels/geometry needed to carry relational structure;
- no project/candidate filename or metadata exposed to renderer;
- no Director-only guides;
- no manifest;
- no labels;
- no score/evaluation information;
- no brand/project vocabulary.

If the renderer interface supports a direct 3D asset instead of a raster/vector view, that is NOT automatically authorized. Transfer mechanism must be separately specified and audited because additional model/material/camera metadata may leak.

Default permitted transfer form under this addendum:
ONE SANITIZED 2D RASTER OR VECTOR VIEW OF THE DETERMINISTIC MAQUETTE.

REFERENCE-STRENGTH / EXECUTION LAW

Existing Stage 7 reference-strength discipline remains unchanged:
- no control assumed;
- if a user-set reference-strength control is exposed, stop before generation until one exact value is fixed/audited;
- one candidate attempt remains one request/one image unless separately authorized;
- visible maquette/CAD/mannequin leakage in final pixels consumes the attempt and is not a free reroll.

RESPONSIVE LAW

Stage 6M may not move the fixed Harness windows.

Before transfer, Director-side crop analysis must confirm:
- both necessary human proxies survive the fixed mobile source window;
- required contact survives;
- critical support geometry survives;
- required changed-state/baseline witnesses survive;
- no essential evidence is moved into the far-left desktop identity/copy region.

METHOD-DIFFERENCE DECLARATION REQUIRED

Any future candidate using Stage 6M must explicitly state why the maquette is a materially different transfer representation rather than a disguised rerender of an exhausted line/fill package.

For a D9-derived candidate, that declaration must at minimum identify:
- transfer-visible foot/support footprint;
- transfer-visible body-axis/support instability;
- actual volumetric contact adjacency;
- actual surface depth/change rather than annotation;
- how the representation differs from C04 line transfer, C04 filled mass and C06 abstract silhouette/path transfer.

FAIL-CLOSED RULES

Any one of the following blocks renderer work:
- causally necessary support geometry is still audit-only;
- B appears independently stable in sanitized maquette despite construction claiming otherwise;
- A appears to carry B vertically when not authorized;
- extra support appears;
- surface becomes a self-sufficient sculptural/architectural object;
- maquette reads primarily as mannequin/sculpture/CAD/blockout;
- final aesthetic variables are encoded;
- fixed responsive semantic evidence does not survive;
- sanitized transfer bytes are not exact/pinned;
- transfer mechanism or reference-strength setting is ambiguous.
