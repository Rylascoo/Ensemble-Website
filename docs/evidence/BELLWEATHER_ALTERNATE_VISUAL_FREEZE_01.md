## Director decision

During the 2026-09-18 website Stage exploration, a compact contemporary Bellweather interpretation emerged: brunette, youthful-adult in presentation, short black dress, dark over-knee/leg styling, compact silhouette and direct small-scale readability. The Director explicitly asked to **freeze this version as ?Bellweather Alternate.?**

This decision preserves the interpretation. It does not replace canonical Bellweather, change the frozen Bellweather master, create universal mascot anatomy, or authorize app/website production use.

## Relationship to canonical Bellweather

Canonical Bellweather remains the primary persistent mascot identity under:

- the frozen Bellweather master;
- the foundational front-facing brand references;

Bellweather Alternate must remain separately named in continuity so its styling cannot silently contaminate or supersede the canonical identity.

## Why preserve the Alternate

The Alternate revealed useful contrast properties:

- immediate legibility at environmental/small scale;
- compact silhouette that can be recognized quickly against darkness;
- contemporary directness and visual separation from the Stage;
- strong ability to expose whether a composition has become Character-first.

Those same properties create known risks:

- anime/game/avatar read;
- character-introduction / hero-shot framing;
- mascot-poster hierarchy;
- a younger-feeling presentation than canonical Bellweather;
- the Stage becoming merely a dark backdrop for the figure.

The risks are part of why the Alternate is useful as a research asset.

## Stage-refinement use

Bellweather Alternate may appear in future **non-production Stage studies** when her visual pull helps test environmental authority, small-scale recognition, occlusion, negative space, lighting hierarchy, or discovered-not-presented behavior.

Do not place canonical Bellweather and Bellweather Alternate in every exploration merely to create variants. Use either one when it makes a specific visual question more diagnostic. Use both only when the comparison itself is the experiment.

## No semantic meaning

The Alternate's short black dress, legwear, hair styling or contemporary appearance do not encode product state, timeline, Character class, world type, consequence, morality, relationship state or any other KYM?AN semantic.

It is a visual alternate only.
