## Findings
The motion family demonstrates that Bellweather identity survives walking, stairs, sitting, turning, backward glance, dance and changing scale. The gown gains credible drape, train and swing behavior, but exact bead paths, slit, jewelry, hair ornament and garment construction remain generated variation.

The mascot-vision family clarifies three useful presentation roles: **Human Bellweather** (close realism and ordinary private behavior), **Persistent Bellweather** (recurring spaces/people and history changing later meaning), and **Iconic Bellweather** (selective full formal ambassador presentation).

Deep identity continues to precede costume: facial construction, eye/brow relationship, brunette hair mass and loose framing strands, posture, and restrained observational presence. Hair ornament, pearls, gloves, clutch, exact bead geometry, slit, lighting, props and companions remain variable.
