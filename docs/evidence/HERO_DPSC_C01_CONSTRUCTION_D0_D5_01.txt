D1 — DISTRIBUTED CORRESPONDENCE MAP

DPSC-C01 uses four independent correspondence families. They are properties of the depicted field, not overlay graphics.

F1 — PRIMARY FLOW CONTINUATION

Outside the changed zone, the broad field has one shallow longitudinal flow/curvature. The portions immediately before and after the changed zone independently extrapolate one continuous simpler trajectory through it. The changed zone is locally offset from that trajectory while remaining continuous with the whole.

Why independent:
F1 is a large-scale shape/continuation relation. It does not depend on texture, repeated marks or thickness cues.

F2 — SECTION / THICKNESS RELATION

Where the field's physical section is naturally visible in separated locations, local thickness and surface-normal relationship remain coherent. Around the changed zone, those separated section relationships predict the same simpler orientation that F1 predicts, while the changed zone carries the same local thickness through a different orientation.

Why independent:
F2 uses physical section/normal relationship rather than the field's longitudinal silhouette or internal directional structure.

F3 — LOW-AMPLITUDE DIRECTIONAL STRUCTURE

A sparse, non-semantic internal directional structure belongs to the field. Outside the changed zone it follows one quiet orientation family. Inside the changed zone it reorients with the same physical displacement. It is not a grid, symbol, stripe code, measurement mark or high-contrast pattern.

Why independent:
F3 can remain visible even where the outer contour is absent from the crop, and its orientation can predict the simpler relation without relying on section thickness.

F4 — IRREGULAR RELIEF-INTERVAL RELATION

A small number of broad, low-relief features occur at non-periodic intervals. Their local spacing and approach/departure relationship on both sides of the changed zone imply how the interval would continue through the simpler state. The features are deliberately irregular enough to avoid a repeated-pattern puzzle.

Why independent:
F4 depends on relative interval/placement among sparse physical features, not on F1 curvature, F2 thickness or F3 directional microstructure.

CONVERGENCE TEST

All four families predict the same simpler baseline transformation: reverse the one local oblique displacement/reorientation while preserving continuity. No family predicts a different prior state.

NO-SINGLE-CLUE TEST
