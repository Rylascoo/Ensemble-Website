## Findings

### A1 — distant standing presence

A valid restrained control. Bellweather is discovered after the identity and the Stage remains primary. On wide the placement is slightly too peripheral/small to become the preferred static composition.

### A2 — distant departing/crossing presence

The behavior remains conceptually strong, but the current source figure is front-facing. Translating that frontal carrier toward the edge does not honestly prove departing/crossing behavior. A2 therefore remains an unresolved behavior study pending an appropriate pose rather than an accepted static result.

### A3 — stronger threshold presence

Strongest static placement in this pass. Bellweather occupies the Stage's blue/right field at sufficient human scale to matter while remaining outside the wordmark axis and subordinate to the centered identity. This placement preserves the architectural threshold and adds human consequence without turning the hero into a mascot poster.

### A4 — delayed reveal

During recursive audit A4 was refined to use the A3 placement with delayed-reveal behavior rather than duplicating A1 placement. This is the preferred opening-state concept from the study: the visitor first receives essentially the current empty Stage, then Bellweather becomes discoverable in the stronger A3 location.

## Responsive interpretation

Wide supports Bellweather as environmental presence. Mini-wide retains the same hierarchy cleanly. Tall naturally increases human readability without requiring the wordmark to move or compress around her. The study supports responsive repositioning as a storytelling tool rather than one universal fixed character coordinate.

## Current synthesis

The study strengthens the existing sequence:

`PLACE -> PRESENCE -> PERSON -> WORLD`

A4 is the preferred opening-state direction; A3 is the preferred static control. A2 should not be advanced until an actual motion-compatible pose exists.

The Director-favorite closer near-front middle-image treatment remains reserved for the next **PERSON-state encounter** study. That study should ask how the site transitions from distant Bellweather recognition into a closer human encounter while preserving the same Stage and system-level identity.
