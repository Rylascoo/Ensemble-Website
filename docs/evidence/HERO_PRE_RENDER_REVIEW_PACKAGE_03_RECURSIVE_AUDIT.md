### A3 — CORRECTION APPLIED: alternate body support remained a loophole

The first packet draft prohibited hidden floor/furniture/rigging beneath Adult A but still allowed the renderer to make hands, arms or another body contact independently support the same pose. That could leave the same depicted position physically plausible after mental baseline restoration and recreate the Round 02 failure under a different picture.

Correction applied before final audit:

> `do not let the hands, arms or another body contact independently support the same position once the deformed support is mentally removed.`

This does not add a new criterion. It closes an implementation loophole in the already-approved no-alternate-support requirement.

### A4 — CORRECTION APPLIED: Adult B relation was too weak

The first packet draft allowed Adult B to be merely `positioned by` a shallow continuation. That wording could produce touch/proximity without materially consequential body support, leaving Adult B removable or witness-like.

Correction applied:

> Adult B is `visibly and materially borne by a shallower continuation of the deformation rather than merely touching it`.

This preserves the specification's asymmetric jobs: Adult A remains the clearest causal-necessity anchor, while Adult B has a genuine physical stake in the shared condition without becoming helper/rescuer/controller or creating a separate mechanism.

### A5 — CORRECTION APPLIED: renderer-facing anti-attractor language was too list-like

The initial exact packet contained a longer object/effect blacklist. Although those risks are legitimate Director-side diagnostics, Renderer Method 01 warns that long negative menus become visual seeds.

The renderer-facing wording was simplified to the smaller construction rule:

> keep the changed portion `broad, continuous, settled and integrated with the larger field rather than turning it into a familiar support object, architectural element, engineered apparatus or spectacular centerpiece`.

Action/drama wording was similarly reduced to `settled, physically credible and non-action-driven` and the hidden-support instruction was generalized.

The full risk list remains Director-side in the packet/package, where it belongs.

## Recursive Pass 02 — reverse audit

Question: could the exact packet still satisfy the prose while producing an image whose support causality is ambiguous?

**Yes.** The renderer may still create hidden-looking support, unclear baseline geometry, a pose that seems self-supported, or a familiar support object. Therefore returned pixels remain binding and those outcomes must fail rather than be rescued by packet intent.

Question: could Adult B still be removable?

**Yes.** Physical bearing increases structural relevance but does not guarantee Human Necessity. If removal leaves essentially the same complete human/causal proposition, Human Necessity fails. No extra third mechanism should be added merely to force B importance.

Question: could the broad material field become sculpture/architecture protagonist?

**Yes.** Human-primary massing and low-detail formal controls reduce this risk without eliminating it. A returned environment-first/monument-first result fails S1; no prompt rewrite is available within the consumed round.

Question: could dark-left integration control create generic dark luxury imagery?

**Yes, empirically.** The packet confines darkness to the left functional region and keeps material/chroma restrained, but pixels must still pass S2-S4 independently. This risk is not a reason to remove the contrast precommitment because Round 02 produced a demonstrated independent desktop integration failure.

Question: does the packet violate the one compact Surface Escape rule by adding construction negatives?

**No after correction.** The ordinary-task/accident sentence is the sole renderer-facing Surface Escape rule. The shorter support-object/apparatus wording protects the approved gravity mechanism from literalizing into a different causal object class and is part of picture construction, not a second hard-gate definition.

Question: does the package accidentally authorize rendering because reconciliation is already complete?

**No.** The document repeatedly states that Round 03 remains unauthorized until the Director explicitly approves this complete pre-render package and thereby authorizes one reconciled Option A execution.
