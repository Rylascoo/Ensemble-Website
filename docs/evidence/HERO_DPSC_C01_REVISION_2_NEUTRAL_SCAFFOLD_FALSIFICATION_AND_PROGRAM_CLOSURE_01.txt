REVISION-2 COLLISION

Across the three private realizations, the same protected trade-off recurred at body height:

1. Give the changed condition a distinct visible boundary strong enough to contact B and carry F1/F2/F3 -> it becomes an independent wall/ribbon/panel/overhang/installation/environmental form, so P7 and often P3 fail.

2. Absorb the condition into the whole surrounding field to resist the independent-object reading -> the distributed correspondence must become a stratum/formal field system to make the simpler baseline recoverable, causing P7/P8 pressure; if subdued, P3 fails.

3. Localize the body contact strongly enough to make B's physical dependency unmistakable -> the contact becomes a dominant explanatory prop/point and increases P6/P7 pressure.

The collision is representation-level, not a palette, texture, material, setting, camera or rendering-quality defect. No renderer is justified to discover a failure already exposed by the frozen pre-render rules.
