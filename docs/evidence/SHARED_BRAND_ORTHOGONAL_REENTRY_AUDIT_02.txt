INITIAL FALSIFICATION TARGETS FOR THE LATER METHOD

A MOT-01 family should fail if any of the following occurs:

- distinctiveness collapses to generic fade, slide, scale, spring, carousel, reveal or presentation-template motion;
- WEB and APP require unrelated temporal grammars rather than one mechanism with bounded amplitude adaptation;
- recognizable identity depends on Threshold K, O3, brand palette/material, imagery, Quiet Stage, hero geometry, bespoke type or product labels rather than the motion mechanism itself;
- the mechanism implies product authority, causality, state change or user action that the neutral fixture does not actually possess;
- timing is tuned to mimic assumed Stage/Character behavior or future runtime architecture;
- motion creates vestibular, flashing, focus, readability or interaction risk that cannot be removed under a reduced-motion equivalent;
- disabling/reducing motion causes loss of content, function or state comprehension;
- the experiment promotes fixture duration/easing values into production design tokens;
- reviewer preference or polish is used to select a final motion system rather than falsify a bounded mechanism.
