SELECTED QUESTION

TYP-01 — TYPOGRAPHIC / EDITORIAL IDENTITY INDEPENDENCE

Question:
Can Kymaean carry a distinctive, product-true shared-brand identity through typographic and editorial behavior across both an expressive public-web context and a restrained app-like utility context when existing symbol/wordmark lockup, palette/material cues, bespoke imagery, Quiet-Stage composition, relational traces, hero mechanism geometry and motion are removed as identity carriers?

This is an identity-independence test, not a typeface-selection exercise.

WHY TYP-01 IS THE BEST CURRENT RE-ENTRY

- It is explicitly authorized as an independent Phase-B SHARED BRAND lane.
- O3 approval expressly leaves the broader typography system unresolved.
- It is materially orthogonal to the static causal-carrier collision that closed current hero research.
- It can be investigated non-render and deterministically before any image-generation dependency.
- It has direct cross-surface value: the website is expressive/explanatory while the app is immersive/working, yet both need one identity.
- It can expose whether Kymaean's identity is overdependent on mark, material, atmosphere or spatial metaphor.
- O0 preserves a durable conceptual precedent that typography may participate in theatrical/literary identity without granting any historical type surface inheritance rights.

INITIAL FALSIFICATION CONTRACT FOR THE LATER METHOD

A TYP-01 direction should fail if any of the following is true:

- recognizable Kymaean character disappears when Threshold K/O3 lockup or Mineral-Theater/Quiet-Stage cues are removed;
- the result collapses into generic luxury editorial, generic AI branding, generic SaaS/product typography or generic Windows-system styling;
- web expressiveness and app utility require unrelated typographic grammars rather than one adaptable identity logic;
- distinctiveness depends on a decorative display treatment that breaks ordinary reading, hierarchy, large-text/reflow, contrast or reduced-information use;
- typography implies false product semantics, theatrical spectacle without human consequence, or branded ornament unrelated to task/state;
- the experiment selects a final typeface/system through polish or preference rather than evidence.

A pass would establish only that a typographic/editorial identity mechanism is viable enough for further Phase-B comparison. It would not satisfy the roadmap's two-family convergence prerequisite by itself and would not select a final identity.
