Parent hypothesis: DPSC-01 — DISTRIBUTED PRESENT-STATE CORRESPONDENCE

PURPOSE

Test whether DPSC-01 can survive final artwork translation as a human-first Kymaean hero while making the simpler prior condition intrinsically recoverable and making B's present possibility physically depend on the changed condition.

This is a new program. It is not D9 continuation, not Round 09, not Candidate 09, and not a C08 repair. Candidate identities in this program use `DPSC-C01` and, only if lawfully reached, `DPSC-C02`. Judgeable renderer expenditures use `DPSC-E1` and `DPSC-E2`.

PARENT CONTRACT — FROZEN / UNCHANGED

Every rendered output remains governed without amendment by:
- Hero Artwork Brief 02;
- Hero Surface Ownership Falsification Criteria 01, including its whole-approach stop law;
- Hero Surface Escape Gate 01;
- corrected/re-frozen Harness 01.

No score, threshold, S1-S6 property, Surface Escape rule, Harness geometry/crop/identity treatment, or qualifying-round definition changes here. No compensating average is permitted.

DPSC-01 SOURCE LAW

The successful HTCR-01 result is carried forward as construction law, not as a pixel-level pass:

1. at least three independent present-state correspondence families must predict the same simpler baseline;
2. those families must be coherently displaced by one changed zone, not several unrelated anomalies;
3. B's support/contact/posture must depend on that same displacement;
4. mentally restoring the simpler correspondence must materially change B and the necessary A↔B passive relation;
5. preserve the validated principle of separated-head, passive posterior/lateral human support without copying C08's closed geometry;
6. no single clue, edge, object, mark, color or prop may carry the full proposition;
7. correspondence must be carried by depicted relationships rather than arrows, labels, grids, measurement marks, before/after panels or emblem imitation;
8. calm identity/copy space must arise through hierarchy and lower information density inside the same world, not a fixed pale illumination patch;
9. the frozen meaningful mobile crop must retain both necessary people, the changed zone and enough independent correspondence to recover the causal read.

These are pre-render construction controls. They do not replace or relax the frozen post-render contract.

PROGRAM BUDGET — FROZEN BEFORE CANDIDATE CONSTRUCTION

Maximum judgeable renderer expenditures: **2 total**.

Possible expenditure labels:
- `DPSC-E1` — only for an independently approved `DPSC-C01`;
- `DPSC-E2` — only for a materially different, independently approved `DPSC-C02` after a judgeable DPSC-E1 failure and only if the program has not otherwise stopped.

There is no DPSC-E3.
There is no automatic second expenditure.
There is no free reroll.
Each candidate may produce at most one judgeable returned image.

A judgeable returned image consumes its expenditure regardless of pass/fail, attractiveness, off-target rendering, prompt adherence, crop quality, or whether the Director wishes the renderer had interpreted the input differently.

A genuine no-image technical failure does not consume an expenditure. It creates no automatic retry. The exact same candidate inputs may be re-presented only after a recorded technical-failure audit establishes that no judgeable image was returned and that no design/reference/prompt mutation is being smuggled into the retry.

WHY TWO IS THE TOTAL BUDGET

DPSC-01 is one new translation mechanism, not an open-ended family search. One render tests a fully audited construction. A second render is useful only if a genuinely different construction can test whether a failure belonged to the first carrier/composition rather than to the DPSC mechanism itself. More than two would reopen iterative prompt/geometry tuning without a new decision. The predeclared two-image ceiling therefore provides falsification value while preventing unbounded succession.

CANDIDATE-CONSTRUCTION METHOD

A candidate earns identity only after Stages D0-D5 all pass recursive audit. No stage implies a later stage automatically.

D0 — CAUSAL PROPOSITION / RESTORATION STATEMENT

State in ordinary language, without material or setting nouns:
- the simpler baseline relation;
- the one present changed relation;
- B's present altered possibility/support consequence;
- A's necessary passive role;
- the exact mental restoration that would materially change B and the A↔B relation.

Reject if the proposition needs private intention, hidden history, one named damage event, or explanatory text to be intelligible.

D1 — DISTRIBUTED CORRESPONDENCE MAP

Define at least three independent correspondence families. Independence means the families are not three restatements of one contour or one repeated decorative pattern. Eligible formal families may include continuity, interval/spacing, orientation, local thickness relationship, occlusion rhythm, directional rhythm, optical-weight correspondence or another ordinary depicted relation.

All families must independently predict the same simpler baseline through the changed zone. The current configuration must read as the local exception to a wider coherent system rather than as an equally simple purpose-designed whole.

D2 — HUMAN DEPENDENCY GEOMETRY

Construct two specific, non-interchangeable people. B's current support/contact/posture must depend on the changed DPSC zone. A must provide a passive posterior/lateral stabilizing relation rather than active lifting, gripping or hand/arm support. Heads remain visibly separated. The human relation must be physically plausible at depicted-pose level and must not reduce to ordinary assistance, intimacy, choreography or posing.

The candidate may use neutral geometric/statics checks internally where helpful, but no prior C08 scaffold, dimensions, reference image or closed candidate geometry is inherited by default.

D3 — COUNTERFACTUAL / REMOVAL FALSIFICATION

Test the construction against all of these before aesthetics:
- restore the simpler correspondence: B's present support/posture must materially change;
- remove A: B's depicted supported relation must materially fail/change;
- remove B: the image must lose the altered-human-possibility proposition rather than remain an environmental spectacle;
- remove the changed condition: the core A↔B dependency must not survive unchanged;
- simplify one correspondence family away: at least two independent families must still support baseline recovery, so no one clue owns the proposition.

Internal counterfactual drawings may exist for design reasoning but may never become renderer-visible before/after or diagram evidence.

D4 — SURFACE ESCAPE / IDENTITY / RESPONSIVE COMPOSITION

The candidate must preflight:
- no architecture/terrain/purpose-built installation reading is materially simpler than the intended changed shared condition;
- no damage/accident/device/platform/portal/bridge/ruin shorthand carries the proposition;
- no ordinary-task tableau survives removal of the transformed condition;
- calm desktop identity/copy space is achieved through hierarchy/sparsity within one world, not a fixed pale light patch or hard artwork partition;
- the frozen mobile crop contains both necessary people, the changed zone and at least two independent baseline-correspondence families sufficient for the semantic core;
- Threshold K and O3 remain separate identity assets.

D5 — TRANSFER / RENDERER-ISOLATION PREFLIGHT

Choose the smallest neutral transfer representation capable of preserving the approved relations without becoming aesthetic authority. The renderer-visible package must be sanitized of project history, criteria, scores, coordinates, internal counterfactual diagrams, Director-side analysis and previous candidate imagery.

Renderer-facing prose must state observable positive visual relationships in ordinary language. It may not seed a solution by naming an architecture type, terrain feature, damage trope, device/platform, explanatory diagram, prior candidate or brand-symbol geometry.

A candidate that needs a before/after reference, labeled diagram, multiple explanatory images, hidden project context, or prose that tells the renderer what causal conclusion to infer fails D5.

PRE-RENDER REJECTION RULES

Reject the candidate before renderer expenditure if any one applies:

P1. fewer than three independent correspondence families survive D1;
P2. the families do not converge on one simpler baseline;
P3. the present configuration is equally or more plausibly read as an always-purpose-designed whole, so the simpler baseline is not intrinsically recoverable;
P4. restoring the simpler relation leaves B's support/posture materially unchanged;
P5. A's passive role is optional, decorative, active-hand/arm support, or reducible to an ordinary interpersonal pose;
P6. one clue/edge/object/mark/color/prop is sufficient by itself to carry the complete proposition;
P7. architecture, terrain, damage/accident, device/platform, ordinary task, or another forbidden shorthand is materially simpler than the intended relation;
P8. the correspondence system becomes a diagram, grid, repeated-symbol puzzle, emblem imitation or decorative pattern that outranks the people;
P9. desktop identity coexistence requires a fixed pale illumination zone or independent artwork partition;
P10. the frozen meaningful mobile crop cannot retain both people, the changed zone and enough independent correspondence for the causal read;
P11. renderer transfer requires project history, a before/after pair, labeled explanatory media, previous candidate imagery or Director-only causal explanation;
P12. the candidate is a disguised C08 retry or copies closed C06/C07/C08 geometry rather than using only their lawful positive evidence;
P13. any frozen Hero Brief, S1-S6, Surface Escape or Harness requirement is already foreseeably violated by the construction.

A pre-render rejection consumes **0** renderer expenditures. Rejection is evidence, not permission to patch indefinitely. Material correction may restart the relevant construction stage only while the candidate has not earned renderer authorization and only if the correction stays within this frozen method.

MATERIAL-DIFFERENCE RULE FOR DPSC-C02

DPSC-C02 may exist only after a judgeable DPSC-E1 failure and only if a clean post-result diagnosis can propose a construction that is materially different from DPSC-C01 without changing the frozen program or post-render contract.

To qualify as materially different, C02 must differ substantially from C01 in at least two of the existing Falsification Criteria dimensions:
- composition;
- human spatial arrangement;
- changed-condition mechanism;
- dominant material/surface organization;

and must also differ in at least two DPSC construction dimensions:
- which correspondence families carry baseline recovery;
- spatial organization/extent of the changed zone;
- B support/contact geometry and A passive-brace geometry;
- distribution of causal evidence across desktop/mobile composition.

Palette, texture, camera, setting name, material name, atmosphere, stronger prose, minor crop, or local geometry tuning alone never establishes material difference.

If no materially different C02 survives pre-render construction, the program closes after 1 of 2 possible expenditures. The second expenditure is not owed merely because budget remains.

This program contract authorizes **no renderer call by itself**.

A specific candidate may earn one expenditure only after:
- D0-D5 are complete;
- every P1-P13 rejection rule is cleared;
- the exact renderer-visible reference/input bytes are frozen;
- a candidate-specific recursive pre-render audit is clean;

Execution remains isolated under the established project renderer discipline: one fresh regular chat outside the Ensemble project, one request, one image, only the frozen sanitized inputs, no project-history preamble, no execution-time input mutation and no free reroll. If the renderer UI exposes a material user-set reference/image/structure-strength control not already frozen, stop before generation for an exact audited amendment.

POST-RENDER EVALUATION

A returned judgeable image is evaluated only against the unchanged frozen post-render law. Intent, construction success or prompt fidelity cannot rescue pixels.

Full program success requires one rendered output to pass every mandatory Hero Brief gate, Surface Escape and S1-S6. No threshold may be lowered and no failure may be averaged away.
