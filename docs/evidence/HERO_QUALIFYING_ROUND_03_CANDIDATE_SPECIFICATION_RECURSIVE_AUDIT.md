## Pass 2 — causal necessity / repeated-failure attack

**PASS prospectively.**

- Altered Possibility;
- Removal Test.

The specification correctly rejects weaker substitutes:

- comfort;

No packet knowledge or private state is needed by definition.

## Pass 3 — Human Necessity / Adult B risk

**PASS with risk explicitly contained.**

The major structural risk is Adult B becoming a witness, scale figure or decorative second person because Adult A carries the strongest gravity proof.

The specification addresses this without inventing a second unrelated mechanism:

- Adult B is comparably substantial and specific;
- Adult B has a different materially consequential relation to the same continuous changed field;
- the two bodies and field must form one compositionally interdependent cluster;
- Adult B removal is pre-registered as Human Necessity failure if the complete human/causal meaning survives essentially unchanged.

This is preferable to forcing Adult B into a helper, rescuer or controller role, which would conflict with the approved independent-agency direction and increase ordinary-scene risk.

No further semantic job should be added before rendering because doing so would risk overconstraint and mechanism multiplication.

## Pass 4 — changed condition / baseline recoverability

**PASS prospectively.**

The specification requires one broad continuous field with visible near-planar/low-relief baseline portions on more than one side and one large continuous deformation.

It prohibits a detached prop or arbitrary object explanation. Baseline recovery must come from contour, continuity, edge/structural alignment and geometry rather than color.

This addresses the Round 01 failure where the changed condition required packet knowledge and preserves the Round 02 gain where the fold itself became visibly recoverable.
