One broad continuous finite-thickness field has a simpler low-relief baseline organization recoverable outside a modest local oblique residual flexure/shear band.

Unlike C06, there is no closed or self-contained support patch under B.

B's two visible foot/support roots occupy the same continuous field on opposite sides of the oblique changed band. The changed field therefore alters B through split support topology:

recoverable residual cross-band shear
-> B's two field-borne support roots become relatively converged/skewed
-> B's depicted torso/root relation is oblique and its COM projection lies outside the changed support polygon toward A
-> one broad passive posterior-lateral upper-back/shoulder contact with independently stable A supplies the required lateral/rotational reaction

A occupies an unaffected baseline-adjacent support region.

The A/B body orientations are deliberately oblique rather than substantially parallel or face-to-face. The contact is B posterior-lateral upper back/shoulder to A lateral/upper torso or shoulder region. Arms and hands remain non-supporting.

Baseline restoration:
- removes the local cross-band shear/roll;
- restores the relative separation/alignment of B's two material support roots;
- places B's projected COM inside the restored support polygon by a positive margin;
- re-centers/rotates the B torso away from A;
