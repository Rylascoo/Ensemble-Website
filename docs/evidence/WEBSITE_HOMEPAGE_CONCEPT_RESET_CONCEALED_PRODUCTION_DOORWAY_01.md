## Authority and trigger

L-204 restored the exact pre-Bellweather V2.2 public tree and opened one isolated non-production homepage concept proposal. The Director then developed and approved continued exploration of a Late Arrival / fixed-Stage / single-passage homepage concept and explicitly proposed a new center-doorway mechanism grounded in real stage masking rather than portal-like transformation.

This record preserves the current concept checkpoint; it does not authorize another production candidate.

## Homepage concept currently surviving

- Fixed Stage / fixed camera.
- Visitor arrives mid-moment rather than summoning Bellweather.
- Bellweather follows one continuous physical trajectory; recognition occurs during motion rather than as a hero pose.
- The Stage remains primary; Bellweather is an encountered inhabitant, not homepage furniture.
- Scroll/touch may later be studied as time-scrubbing rather than discrete slide/state navigation.
- The center doorway must remain materially believable and should not abruptly transform into overt magical space.

## Doorway family — Concealed Production
The center arch remains structurally stable. Behind it, layered black stage masking / curtains create a withheld rear-stage zone. Multiple near-black values, folds and material response provide depth without exposing literal backstage mechanics.

Two soft, differently colored cones of light sit deeper to the left/right of the concealed zone. They function as latent cast/presence cues: quiet evidence that additional characters or roles may be waiting within the broader production. They are not literal people, UI elements, active spotlights, or explanatory graphics.

The intended reading is:

> something larger is being prepared behind what is currently shown.

The intended reading is NOT:

> a magical portal has activated.

## Preserved predecessor / upper bound

The earlier “Impossible Depth” doorway family remains preserved visual evidence. Its recursive corridor established a useful upper bound for spatial impossibility but became too explicit, portal-like and fantasy/sci-fi coded when repeated arches, bright paths or sudden depth escalation dominated the frame.

It must not be deleted or rewritten as though the current direction was inevitable.

## Current Director + Design Sol convergence
The Director’s “gently corporeal” criterion is adopted for the successor study:
- physical architecture, fabric, air, reflection and light should carry the mystery;
- no vortex, particles, magical energy, sudden activation or fantasy gateway language;
- curtain/masking logic should feel stage-authentic without exposing rigging, ropes, sandbags or literal backstage explanation;
- waiting cones should stay peripheral and restrained;
- cone color may imply plurality without turning into a palette demo or competing hero event;
- the outer arch remains stable.

Design Sol interpretation: Concealed Production currently appears more brand-intelligent than the explicit recursive corridor because it connects the center threshold to the production/cast metaphor while reducing supernatural affect.
