## Design interpretation

The empty intervals remain explicit states rather than timed slideshow gaps: each requires a visitor progression action and supports reverse navigation. They restore the unchanged Stage before the next evidence carrier and prevent object-to-object crossfades. Bellweather remains absent after PERSON; TRACE recedes before OTHER LIFE.

The interaction model remains KYMÆAN-first because PLACE is always the initial state and no automatic progression occurs. The prototype discloses no product mechanism or explanatory copy.
