## Pass 3 — frozen-contract preservation

**PASS after correction.**

Initial risk: the construction-stage rejection rules could be misread as new post-render hard gates.

Correction applied:

- returned artwork remains judged only under the existing frozen criteria;

## Pass 4 — two-person Human Necessity

**PASS.**

The Semantic Dependency Record + four-removal dependency matrix directly address the Round 03 failure where two independently supported people did not make one another necessary.

The method does not preselect helper/recipient, rescuer/endangered, author/recipient, or another narrative role. It requires only that each person carry a different externally observable semantic job in the same proposition and that removal of either materially collapse that proposition.

This is a construction safeguard against redundancy, not a new story doctrine.

## Pass 5 — Changed-condition legibility

**PASS after simplification.**

Initial proposal required at least three geometric correspondences. That was stronger than necessary and risked forcing visual complexity merely to satisfy a method count.

Correction applied:

- require at least **two independent correspondence cues**;
- explicitly permit more only when they arise naturally;
- retain the essential no-single-clue rule.

This is sufficient to create redundant evidence while remaining compatible with meaningful quiet and S4 restraint.

## Pass 6 — Altered Possibility / Removal necessity

**PASS.**

The Counterfactual Ledger preserves the exact lesson of Round 03 without selecting gravity or another future mechanism.

It requires one externally visible changed-condition -> present-possibility dependency and records plausible alternate support/path/contact explanations before rendering.

The construction fails if baseline restoration leaves the same possibility plausible or merely makes it less convenient.

No private mental state or prior authorship is introduced.

## Pass 7 — S1 remains distinct

**PASS.**

The method does not collapse Human Necessity into S1.

Human Necessity asks whether both depicted people are structurally necessary to the shared situation.

S1 still asks whether people/specificity remain the primary proposition rather than environment/mechanism/spectacle.

The pre-render construction tests both:

- individual person-removal dependency;
- genericization/interchangeability;
- whether environment/mechanism still carries the proposition after people are mentally removed.

These are related but separately preserved.

## Pass 8 — responsive/S6 construction

**PASS.**

This addresses S6 without blaming or moving the harness.

## Pass 10 — neutral scaffold / aesthetic contamination

**PASS prospectively.**

The scaffold is restricted to relational geometry:

- figure regions / axes;
- critical support/contact points;
- field continuity;
- baseline witness regions;
- negative space;
- responsive boundaries;
- causally necessary occlusion order.

It explicitly excludes palette, material style, clothing, faces, ethnicity, historical period, named style, texture, lighting mood, brand geometry and symbolism.

The proposed deterministic-coordinate/vector construction makes the transferred variable auditable and avoids generative aesthetic inheritance.

No scaffold has been authorized or created by this proposal.

## Pass 11 — renderer isolation / reference transfer
