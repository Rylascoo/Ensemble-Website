## Recovered visual thesis

Across the strongest current and historical branches, five ideas recur:

1. **Stage as place.** The homepage Stage should feel spatial, architectural and materially consequential, not like a UI card, fantasy portal or decorative backdrop.
2. **Negative space as narrative capacity.** Monumental emptiness, threshold depth and partial concealment communicate more possibility than filling the frame with spectacle.
3. **Restrained illumination.** Darkness is strongest when practical warm structure and subordinate cool/plum evidence reveal depth indirectly rather than behaving like an effects system.
4. **Character discovered inside a prior world.** Human presence works when the viewer encounters someone already moving through a world that existed before arrival. The Character should not automatically own the composition.
5. **Occlusion and absence are active states.** Partial view, architectural masking, departure and the empty Stage afterward can carry history and possibility without explanatory copy.

These findings are compatible with A-prime and broader than the current exact-Stage rendering.
