FAMILY T1 — SUPPORT-EXPLICIT 2D HUMAN GEOMETRY

Description:
Keep the current mechanism-neutral vector surface, but make causally necessary human support geometry visible as part of the anonymous figure construction rather than hidden audit guides:
- explicit foot/support extents integrated into A/B figure shapes;
- B's narrow changed support footprint visibly bounded by actual foot geometry;
- B body mass/body axis visibly displaced outside that footprint toward A;
- A's broader independently stable footprint visible in its body geometry;
- no support rectangles, COM plumb lines, arrows, labels or numerical annotations transfer.

Strengths:
- directly addresses the Round 06 stability-necessity attenuation;
- keeps geometry, rather than prose, responsible for support extent;
- remains deterministic and candidate-auditable;
- avoids transferring private-state or narrative meaning.

Weaknesses:
- does not materially strengthen changed-from-baseline surface evidence by itself;
- same surface paths could still naturalize into a pre-existing depression;
- exaggerated lean/foot asymmetry can drift toward dance/fall/performance shorthand.

Disposition: RETAIN AS A NECESSARY COMPONENT, NOT SUFFICIENT AS A COMPLETE NEXT TRANSFER FAMILY.

FAMILY T2 — CORRESPONDENCE-AMPLIFIED 2D SURFACE

Description:
Attempt to make changed-from-baseline state harder to naturalize by strengthening visible registration in a 2D transfer surface through one or more of:
- stronger local boundary offset;
- repeated spacing/rhythm cues;
- additional seam/registration contours;
- visible local shear markers or paired correspondence accents.

Strengths:
- can make baseline/change reconstruction explicit before rendering;
- remains deterministic and easy to audit.

Weaknesses:
- stronger offset changes candidate geometry rather than merely transfer representation;
- repeated seams/rhythm can become architecture, designed surface or clue mechanism;
- added correspondence marks increase diagram/reference-sheet leakage risk;
- stronger fold/offset approaches C05's platform/device failure family;
- more lines can cause the renderer either to literalize technical marks or to naturalize them into engineered joints rather than changed-state evidence.

Disposition: REJECT AS PRIMARY NEXT FAMILY. It increases the exact diagram/device/pre-shaped-form risks already evidenced.

FAMILY T3 — DETERMINISTIC NEUTRAL PHYSICAL MAQUETTE

Description:
Represent the already-approved semantic/stability geometry as a deliberately non-aesthetic 3D/2.5D physical maquette rather than as a line diagram or filled cross-section.

Required mechanism-neutral characteristics:
- one fixed declared source coordinate system;
- deterministic volumetric human proxies with explicit feet/support footprint and body-axis relation;
- one continuous finite-thickness surface whose local residual displacement is actual geometry, not annotation;
- A/B physical contact represented by actual proxy adjacency rather than a technical connector;
- no faces, clothing, hair, race/ethnicity, age, expression, props or identity styling;
- no final material identity, texture, color palette or brand asset;
- fixed neutral orthographic or tightly bounded projection chosen as instrumentation rather than composition styling;
- fixed uniform neutral shading sufficient only to reveal depth/contact, with no cinematic lighting;
- no arrows, labels, measurements, grids or scoring guides in transferable output;
- all audit guides remain Director-only.

Why materially different from C04 R05 filled mass:
C04 R05 strengthened only the surface as a filled structural mass while human/support mechanics remained abstract. T3 would encode the actual human support footprint, lean, contact and surface depth as one physical geometry model. The transferred variable is full relational physical arrangement, not merely a heavier rendering of the field shape.

Potential advantages:
- foot/support necessity can be embodied rather than described;
- body/surface contact can be physically coherent before renderer interpretation;
- depth and local displacement can be read from actual volume instead of technical registration lines;
- the renderer receives fewer opportunities to invent conventional stance geometry;
- correspondence can be carried by surface continuity and depth rather than extra diagram marks.

Risks:
- neutral clay/maquette appearance could leak into final artwork;
- renderer may still naturalize the surface into sculpture/terrain;
- fixed camera/projection becomes an additional controlled transfer variable;
- any lighting/shading variation could become accidental aesthetic authorship;
- volumetric human proxies could seed mannequin/statue imagery;
- if the maquette itself reads as a designed installation, it fails before renderer.

Prospective pre-render falsifiers:
1. people removed: surface must not read primarily as a complete sculptural/architectural support object;
2. B removed: A + surface must not read as a purpose-built human support installation;
3. support read: B proxy must be visibly unstable without A by geometry alone, with no audit overlay;
4. baseline read: neutral reviewer must recover a simpler larger surface organization and local displacement without labels;
5. leakage read: transferable view must not require grids, outlines, arrows, measurement marks or technical legend;
6. mannequin read: proxy simplification must not make the transferable image's primary visual identity a figurative sculpture scene;
7. responsive read: both people, contact, support geometry and baseline witnesses must remain inside fixed mobile semantic coverage;
8. instrumentation read: camera/projection/shading must be fixed by method law rather than tuned candidate-by-candidate for beauty.

Disposition: SELECT AS THE ONLY MATERIALLY DIFFERENT NEXT METHOD-LEVEL HYPOTHESIS WORTH FORMALIZING.
