EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS

1. METHOD 01 — COMPONENT ACHIEVABILITY + METHOD EXHAUSTION

Three materially different qualifying rounds exhausted the one-shot method.

Across those rounds:
- Epistemic Restraint repeatedly passed;
- Surface Escape repeatedly passed;
- S2-S5 repeatedly passed;
- numeric Mobile Survival repeatedly passed;
- Round 02 simultaneously demonstrated Human Necessity + Changed-condition legibility + S1;

Therefore the frozen hero requirements are not shown to be individually impossible.

But no Method-01 round achieved the necessary conjunction. The method-level diagnosis was that prompt-only rendering did not reliably control relational geometry tightly enough to combine:
`two-person necessity + changed-state legibility + altered possibility/removal + human-first salience`.

Method 01 remains closed. No fourth Method-01 round is justified.

3. HTCR-01 — DPSC MECHANISM-LEVEL SUCCESS, OTHER CARRIER FAMILIES REJECTED

HTCR-01 preserved the frozen contract and used zero renderer expenditures.

It rejected:
- negative-space / affordance compression because ordinary passage/opening/path/channel readings remained simpler and Surface Escape was unstable;
- human-condition counterform because baseline evidence became circular and body-conforming support/furniture became an attractor;
- relational occlusion topology because it did not force a unique simpler baseline and strengthened forms became a dominant boundary/formal puzzle.

It promoted DPSC-01 — Distributed Present-State Correspondence — as the one surviving mechanism-level hypothesis:
several independent present-state correspondences predict the same simpler baseline; one changed zone displaces them coherently; B's support/posture depends on that same displacement; restoration changes B and the necessary A↔B relation.

This was a mechanism-level success, not a candidate or rendered success.
