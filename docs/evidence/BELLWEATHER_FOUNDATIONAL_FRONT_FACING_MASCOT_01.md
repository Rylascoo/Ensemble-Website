## Foundational identity
Bellweather is one clearly adult persistent person. Recognition should survive before costume through facial construction, eye/brow relationship, freckles/natural skin texture, brunette hair mass and loose framing strands, posture, and restrained direct presence.

The target expression language is natural and legible: closed-mouth, softly asymmetrical, or subtly parted-lip expressions. Broad visible-teeth smiles are not the current Bellweather target because they reduce naturalism. Emotion should be carried primarily by gaze, brows, posture, and fine mouth tension.

## Presentation boundary
The black sheer / warm-metal formal language remains iconic presentation. Exact bead paths, slit placement, hair ornament, pearls, gloves, clutch, jewelry, props, generated typography, and hotel/environment details are not deterministic mascot anatomy unless separately frozen.

This foundational mascot authority does not require every Kymaean Character or Production to use Bellweather's face, does not turn Bellweather styling into app semantics, and does not by itself authorize app UI layout/behavior or website-production changes.

## Successor rule
Future frontal studies may tighten face geometry or vary restrained emotion one variable at a time, but they are successors to this frozen set. Replacing the foundational set requires an explicit later design decision.
