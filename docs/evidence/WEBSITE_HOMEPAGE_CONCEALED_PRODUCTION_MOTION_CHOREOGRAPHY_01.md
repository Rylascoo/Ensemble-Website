## Historical visual synthesis applied

Website Sol reviewed the relevant Drive visual lineage before motion convergence, including original concept/V0 world studies, V2.2 Stage doctrine, Bellweather motion/position studies, mascot vision/natural-expression studies, website mascot integration, prior temporal choreography, Late Arrival reset and current Concealed Production refinement.

The recurring constraints were consistent: the Stage establishes place; Bellweather should feel discovered rather than presented; crossing/departing behavior is stronger than posing; recognition should be subtle and human; absence restores Stage authority; and latent environmental evidence must not behave like a magical activation.

## Motion exploration

The first bounded prototype established a single 6.8 second passage with Bellweather entering from the right/blue territory, crossing inward at environmental scale, briefly recognizing the visitor, then receding toward the concealed threshold where working-black geometry physically occludes her. A soft contact shadow and faint threshold reflection are the only environmental consequences. Plum-violet and mineral-teal latent presences remain static throughout.

The Director then reviewed an A/B/C comparison:

- **A - Current balance:** brief recognition while continuing passage.
- **B - Pure transit:** no recognition; strongest mystery but emotionally cooler.
- **C - Held recognition:** clearer human acknowledgment but too close to a staged character presentation.

The Director agreed with Design Sol's recommendation to refine A toward B rather than select either extreme.

## A-prime - Quiet Recognition

The accepted motion architecture is **A-prime - Quiet Recognition**.

The refinement removed the local body slowdown around recognition. Bellweather now maintains uniform visible forward travel, with easing only at hidden endpoints. The recognition Gaussian width is reduced from 0.115 to 0.10, a 13.04% reduction, while maximum head turn is reduced from 3.2 degrees to 2.55 degrees and recognition-specific body lean from 0.9 degrees to 0.35 degrees.

The resulting narrative is:

`empty Stage -> Bellweather already moving -> brief quiet recognition while continuing -> threshold approach -> physical masking takes her from view -> faint consequence settles -> empty Stage regains authority`

The Director explicitly agreed that A-prime is the correct motion direction and then agreed that the motion architecture is converged.

## Recursive audit

The final audit confirms:

- visible body travel is monotonic with no recognition plateau;
- the Stage and camera do not move;
- latent plum/teal evidence remains constant and non-reactive;
- disappearance is physical occlusion, not opacity fade or portal effect;
- no particles, activation beams, slideshow states, second figure, props, furniture or marketing copy are present;
- wide, tall and mobile diagnostics report no horizontal overflow;
- `site/public/**` remains unchanged.

The motion study is therefore sufficient to freeze behavior but not final Bellweather appearance.
