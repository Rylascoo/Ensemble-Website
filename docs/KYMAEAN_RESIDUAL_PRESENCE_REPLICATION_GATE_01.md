# Kymaean — Residual Presence Replication Gate 01

Status: FROZEN BEFORE RENDER / MANDATORY PASS CONDITIONS
Date frozen: 2026-09-05
Applies to: exactly one Residual Presence replication render

## Purpose

Test whether G1's success belongs to Residual Presence rather than to the specific Batch-02 blocking or to milk/spill legibility.

This gate is written before the replication render exists.

## Fixed composition-divergence requirements

The replication must use exactly 2 adults; no child; no elder-in-doorway composition; no doorway/threshold as causal geometry; no liquid/milk/water/ice/spill/cleaning scene; no trench/bridge/wall-crossing/archive mechanism/giant prop; no active execution of the causal act; and a blocking that is not central woman + entering elder + child at right.

Failure of any item invalidates the replication.

## Mandatory binary gates

Every gate must pass. There is no compensating average.

1. **R1 Completed act** — the causal act is visibly over. No one is actively performing the act that created the condition.
2. **R2 Persistent residue** — a changed material/spatial condition remains independently of current action; no aura/beam/force effect.
3. **R3 Prior human cause** — a specific earlier human action is reasonably inferable; natural accident/weather/unexplained anomaly is not equally strong.
4. **R4 Altered present possibility** — residue materially changes what the second adult can occupy, place, use, reach, avoid, perceive or do now.
5. **R5 Human necessity** — both adults are necessary to authorship/consequence.
6. **R6 Distributed cause** — no portable prop carries most causal meaning; the changed field is distributed through shared space/material.
7. **R7 Composition independence** — 2-person cast; neither staged as doorway entrant; no child; different dominant axis/spacing.
8. **R8 G4 / epistemic restraint** — one authoritative shared world; physical position/availability only; no private knowledge/belief/memory depiction.
9. **R9 Desktop copy zone** — left ~40% is genuinely quiet/expendable and contains no indispensable person/evidence.
10. **R10 4:5 semantic cluster** — one plausible portrait crop retains both adults, residue and altered present possibility.
11. **R11 Removal test** — removing the residual condition destroys the causal/time read.
12. **R12 Temporal residue** — the still clearly reads as aftermath rather than present-tense action.

## Promotion rule

If R1–R12 all pass:

`RESIDUAL PRESENCE — REPLICATED ACROSS TWO DISTINCT COMPOSITIONS / TEMPORARY VISUAL-PHYSICS LAW`

`RESIDUAL PRESENCE — PROVISIONAL / G1-SPECIFIC SUCCESS NOT GENERALIZED`
