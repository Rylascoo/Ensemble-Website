## Probe

`CFM-01C — Shallow Overlap / Occlusion`

Fixed semantic kernel:

> Three distinct people share one present moment. One person takes a visible action that changes the relationship among the three. The other two respond differently in ways that make unequal understanding legible. The people must carry the structure and meaning.

Formal package:

- compact human forms in shallow depth;
- controlled overlap and occlusion;
- relationships alter edges, visibility and shared regions;
- each person remains separately traceable;
- environmental depth/background description remains minimal;
- individual recognizability through silhouette, bodily proportion, posture and characteristic gesture;
- even tonal field with moderate contrast.

## Result

The returned image contains exactly three distinct people in a tight, shallow, nearly backgroundless field. The central figure overlaps both neighboring figures and visually mediates the shared region between them. The central raised palm addresses the right-hand figure while the left-hand figure responds differently. The renderer independently selected painterly intimate figurative realism.
