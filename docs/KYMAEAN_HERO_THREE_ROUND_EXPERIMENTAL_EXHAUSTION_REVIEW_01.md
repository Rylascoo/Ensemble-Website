## What is now strongly supported

### 1. Epistemic restraint is not the blocker

All three rounds passed Epistemic Restraint at 5/5. The project can depict consequential human situations without relying on knowledge, belief, memory, motive or intent.

### 2. Surface Escape is not the blocker

All three rounds passed the frozen Surface Escape hard gate. The experiment successfully escaped the historical/domestic/ordinary-task attractor without needing to weaken the gate.

### 3. Material restraint / authored quiet are not the blocker

S2-S5 passed in all three rounds. The project repeatedly produced meaningful quiet, disciplined material behavior, restrained hierarchy and no emblem imitation.

### 4. Basic mobile cropping is not the main blocker

Numeric Mobile Survival passed all three rounds. The fixed crop repeatedly retained at least two people plus changed-condition evidence at the minimum threshold.

The persistent S6 failures are therefore not simply "mobile cannot show two people." They concern the **complete semantic core** and identity coexistence across surfaces.

### 5. Gravity-dependent support solved the repeated causal-necessity root

Round 03 is the first qualifying candidate to pass both:

- Altered Possibility >=4;
- Removal Test >=4.

This means the previous Rounds 01–02 causal ambiguity was contingent on those mechanisms, not intrinsic proof that a static still cannot depict altered present possibility.

## What remains unresolved

The three rounds expose a recurring conjunction problem rather than one permanent single-gate failure.

### Round 01

Environmental architecture dominated. Human Necessity, Changed-condition legibility, Altered Possibility, Removal Test and S1 all failed.

### Round 02

Human salience and changed-state legibility improved enough to pass Human Necessity, Changed-condition legibility and S1. But the fold did not physically cause the depicted contact; Altered Possibility and Removal Test still failed.

### Round 03

Gravity-dependent support finally made causal necessity and Removal Test pass. However the support field now read too readily as designed sculptural/topographic seating rather than a visibly changed state; the second person became causally redundant; and the material mechanism regained too much surface ownership. Human Necessity, Changed-condition legibility and S1 failed again.

## Smallest unresolved conjunction

The evidence now identifies the smallest high-value conjunction as:

1. **two-person Human Necessity**;
2. **Changed-condition legibility**;
3. **Altered Possibility / Removal necessity**;
4. **S1 Human-first salience**.

These are not proven mutually inconsistent.

However no qualifying candidate has yet demonstrated all four simultaneously:

- Round 02 showed 1 + 2 + 4 without 3;
- Round 03 showed 3 without 1 + 2 + 4;
- Round 01 showed none robustly.

That is stronger evidence than the earlier two-round root set and should control the next methodological decision.

## S6 interpretation

S6 failed in all three rounds, but the failure mechanism changed:

- Round 01: the raw semantic core was too weak to survive responsively;
- Round 02: causal core remained weak and desktop identity contrast independently failed;
- Round 03: desktop identity coexistence became strong, but raw changed-state evidence remained incomplete and the fixed mobile crop removed much of the clearest baseline evidence.

Therefore S6 is a persistent **conjunction-surface symptom**, not yet evidence that Harness 01 itself is the root blocker.

No Harness change is justified by the current evidence.

## Method-level diagnosis

The current one-shot sterile-render sequence has served its purpose as a falsification instrument.

Its weakness is now clear: a single long renderer packet must simultaneously instantiate:

- two-person necessity;
- visible changed-state evidence;
- externally necessary causal physics;
- human-first surface ownership;
- responsive placement;
- restrained material behavior;
- no ordinary-task shorthand;
- no explanatory graphics.

The renderer can satisfy different subsets, but the three qualifying outputs show that a one-shot natural-language prompt is not reliably controlling the **relational geometry** tightly enough to realize the complete conjunction.

Further prompt-only rerolling would therefore be low-information and contrary to the frozen exhaustion law.
