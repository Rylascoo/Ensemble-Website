## Hero job

The hero must create a felt understanding of Kymaean before product vocabulary:

**Distinct people inhabit one changing situation. What one person has done materially alters another person's present possibilities, while people may have unequal physical access to the cause or consequence.**

Artwork-level thesis:

**HUMAN CAUSALITY, LIVING CONSEQUENCE.**

It must stand first as artwork, not UI, diagram, brand poster, AI metaphor, or explanatory board.

## Required truths

A candidate must visibly establish:
1. at least two specific, non-interchangeable people;
2. present evidence of prior human action;
3. consequence that changes what another person can presently reach/use/enter/avoid/inspect/resist/repair/inherit/further alter;
4. one shared reality with unequal physical availability, without inventing Knowledge/Belief/Memory;
5. relationship carried by composition rather than connector diagrams;
6. quiet before spectacle.

Identity may not depend only on color, portrait likeness, labels, or exact position.

## Quiet Stage + Mineral Theater

Quiet Stage does not require literal Stage cones in the hero. Preserve theatrical absence, human priority, restraint, one shared situation, and living consequence.

Mineral Theater is a material-role lens, not literal mineral subject matter:

- limestone / material neutrality;
- oxidized-copper causal/human emphasis;
- blue-green presence/counter-signal.

Warm/cool may not carry identity or epistemic state alone. Candidate must survive desaturated review.

## Immediate failure

Reject if:
- people are decorative/interchangeable;
- prior cause cannot be inferred;
- consequence changes appearance but not possibility;
- internal epistemic state is invented;
- one dominant prop carries almost all causal meaning;
- portal/bridge/wall/ruin/fantasy/AI shorthand dominates;
- it reads like app UI or requires Stage cones;
- color alone carries meaning;
- mobile causal read collapses;
- removing the causal change leaves the composition substantially intact.
