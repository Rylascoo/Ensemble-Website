## Core job

The hero artwork must make Kymaean feel like a world in which:

**distinct people share one present, an earlier human choice has changed that present, the consequence changes another person’s possibilities, and the people do not all understand the cause in the same way.**

The image must communicate that through one uninterrupted artwork rather than through captions, interface, diagrams or explanatory panels.

The artwork should stand first as a compelling image. Product meaning should be discoverable inside it rather than diagrammed on top of it.

## Required semantic content

### 1. Distinct people are necessary

Use a small ensemble of **2–4 specific people**.

Each person must be visually non-interchangeable through some combination of age/build/posture/gesture, clothing silhouette/material, relationship to surrounding objects, distinct attention/reaction, and spatial role in the shared circumstance.

Do not rely on color alone to distinguish them.

They are **reference people for the website artwork**, not canonical product Characters and not user-uploaded identities.

### 2. One earlier human choice is causally present

Something one person chose or did earlier must have materially changed the shared situation.

The earlier act does not need to be shown as a flashback. Instead, the present image must contain **visible evidence of the earlier cause**: a changed object, missing object, altered path, broken arrangement, displaced material, opened/closed access, residue, damage, repair, trace or other consequence-bearing evidence.

The image must give the viewer enough evidence to infer that the present did not arise randomly.

### 3. Consequence changes another person’s present possibilities

The earlier act must not be merely emotional backstory. Its consequence must materially alter what at least one other person can do, reach, know, choose, enter, recover, avoid or become responsible for **now**.

The consequence should therefore influence body position, attention, path, distance, gesture or available space.

### 4. Awareness is asymmetric

The people must not all understand the cause or consequence equally. Their reactions should make that asymmetry legible without text: one may recognize the trace immediately; one may react only to the changed circumstance; one may misunderstand the cause; one may know more but reveal less.

This is the artwork analogue of bounded perspective.

### 5. Relationship is composition, not connectors

Do not use lines connecting people, floating nodes, diagram arrows, split-screen explanation, labels or UI overlays.

Relationship should emerge from gaze, obstruction, distance, negative space, shared material, changed access and the consequences of prior action.

## Visual-world requirements

### Quiet Stage inheritance

The artwork may inherit theatrical absence, directional or volumetric light, charged negative space, strong spatial staging, human presence as the primary signal, and a sense that the surrounding world continues beyond the frame.

It must **not** simply reproduce the app’s cone-on-dark-floor carrier.

No Stage name pools, selection rings, audience chrome, transcript, controls or app interface may appear.

### Mineral Theater inheritance

Use Mineral Theater as atmosphere, not color-coding:
- deep slate / near-black spatial field;
- limestone / mineral / weathered material where light surfaces are useful;
- oxidized-copper warmth for causal/human emphasis;
- blue-green mineral coolness for counter-presence.

These are temporary material roles, not literal paint-by-number requirements.

Warm/cool may reinforce composition but must not tell the viewer who someone is or what state they are in.

### Broader Kymaean creative range

The artwork should be free to draw from otherworldly, symbolic, organic, architectural, historical, relational, theatrical and unexpected qualities, but avoid collapsing those into fantasy illustration, sci-fi interface, luxury branding or literal portal imagery.

## Composition contract

### Desktop master

Primary target:
- landscape master approximately **3:2 to 16:10**;
- intended for a wide acquisition hero.

Protect the **left 34–40%** as relatively quiet identity/copy territory.

The semantic center of the artwork should occupy the **right 55–65%**, but the artwork must remain complete if viewed without overlaid website copy.

Do not place indispensable causal evidence under the reserved copy zone.

### Mobile crop

The composition must survive a portrait crop approximately **4:5**.

The mobile crop should preserve at least two people, the key consequence-bearing evidence and the asymmetric reaction.

Do not make the entire meaning depend on an extreme-left or extreme-right figure.

### Negative space

Use negative space to create tension and accommodate identity, but do **not** force Threshold K, a letterform or logo geometry into the artwork.

## Style / rendering guidance

Preferred:
- fine-art cinematic realism, painterly realism, or materially rich illustrative realism;
- believable human gesture;
- restrained surrealism or impossible spatial/material behavior only when it deepens causality;
- tactile surfaces and atmospheric depth;
- compositions that reward a second look.

Avoid:
- generic photorealistic stock scene;
- fashion editorial posing;
- characters staring theatrically at camera;
- videogame key art;
- fantasy party lineups;
- cyberpunk neon;
- holograms / HUDs;
- magic portals;
- floating AI symbols;
- abstract network graphics;
- obvious “AI” imagery;
- giant logos or text inside the art.

## Human specificity law

**The artwork may be strange. The people may not be generic.**

Every rendered person should feel like someone with a different relationship to the same event, not one repeated model in different clothing.

If a person appears in reflection, shadow, memory-like residue or another representation, they must remain recognizably the same person without requiring an identical portrait.

## Product-truth boundaries

The artwork may imply persistent identity, shared world, bounded access, pressure, consequence and causal history.

The artwork must not imply multiple authoritative realities, provider/model identity as Character identity, technical failure becoming fiction, acceptance alone establishing history, or the creator losing ownership of the Production.

No one image needs to explain the architecture. The deterministic Site Spine handles explicit explanation.

## Brand-overlay boundary

Threshold K and O3 belong to the website composition layer.

**Do not render either mark inside the artwork.**

The artwork must remain usable with Threshold K + O3 overlaid, with O3 alone, or with no brand overlay in editorial contexts.

## Candidate-round discipline

The next renderer round should produce **three genuinely different artworks**, not three style variants of one composition.

All three must satisfy the same semantic contract above.

They should diverge primarily through the kind of consequence-bearing evidence, spatial relationship among people, material/architectural circumstance, and how awareness asymmetry is expressed.

Do not vary by merely changing palette, camera angle or rendering style.

## Candidate evaluation criteria

Score each candidate against these ten questions:

1. **Artwork first** — would it stand as compelling artwork without website context?
2. **Human specificity** — are the people distinct and non-interchangeable?
3. **Causal inference** — can an earlier choice be inferred from visible present evidence?
4. **Material consequence** — has that choice changed another person’s current possibilities?
5. **Asymmetric awareness** — do reactions imply unequal understanding?
6. **Relationship by composition** — is the relationship legible without connectors/explanation?
7. **Kymaean belonging** — does it feel specifically compatible with the current visual world?
8. **Anti-cliché survival** — does it avoid AI/sci-fi/fantasy/luxury/portal shorthand?
9. **Responsive survival** — does the meaning survive the portrait crop?
10. **Identity coexistence** — can Threshold K/O3 overlay without fighting the artwork?

A candidate cannot advance if it fails items 2, 3, 4 or 5 even if it is visually spectacular.

## Renderer handoff prompt

Create one uninterrupted artwork centered on distinct people whose presence and relationships are necessary to the image.

A choice made earlier by one person has materially changed the situation they now share. The present must contain enough visible evidence for that earlier cause to be inferred, and its consequence must change another person’s current possibilities. The people should not have equal awareness of the cause or consequence; their different responses should make that asymmetry legible without explanatory text.

Keep each person specific and non-interchangeable. Their interaction may alter the composition as a whole, but it must not erase individual identity. Relationship should emerge through gesture, distance, obstruction, changed access, material evidence and shared circumstance—not connector lines or interface.

Let the image inherit theatrical absence, charged negative space and materially rich spatial staging without reproducing the Kymaean app interface or its cone carrier. Mineral Theater may influence atmosphere through deep slate space, limestone-like material, restrained oxidized-copper warmth and blue-green mineral coolness, but color must not encode identity or state.

Choose all unstated visual circumstances yourself. Favor fine-art cinematic or painterly realism, believable human gesture, tactile surfaces and restrained otherworldliness. Avoid generic stock photography, videogame key art, fantasy-party staging, cyberpunk neon, holograms, magic portals, AI symbols, floating UI, logos or text.

Compose for a wide website hero with relatively quiet space on the left while keeping the artwork complete on its own. The semantic center should remain recoverable in a 4:5 portrait crop, preserving at least two people, the key consequence-bearing evidence and their asymmetric reactions.

Return only the uninterrupted artwork. Do not add text, branding, interface, promotional layout, explanatory panels or presentation framing.
