# KYMAEAN LANE B — BESPOKE WORDMARK CONSTRUCTION 02

Status: INTERNAL STATIC SURVIVOR IDENTIFIED / O3 RECOMMENDED FOR DIRECTOR REVIEW / NOT SELECTED  
Date: 2026-09-05  
Workstream: SHARED BRAND / W0 WORDMARK

## Question

How can `KYMÆAN` carry equal ownership with quieter means while historical Threshold K remains the unchanged working symbol control?

## Frozen constraints

The wordmark must:
- read as `KYMÆAN`;
- stand alone without Threshold K;
- keep a deliberately resolved `Æ`;
- use lower emblematic density than Threshold K;
- avoid copying, containing or completing the logo;
- avoid K-shaped cuts, classical/heritage shorthand and decorative complexity;
- remain deterministic local vector geometry;
- preserve accessible text identity separately from visible vector construction.

## Construction 02

Three custom vector systems were built beside the unchanged Threshold K:

- **Q — Quiet Structure:** ownership through proportion, spacing and Æ construction.
- **H — Human Modulation:** softer curves and joins.
- **R — Rhythmic Width:** ownership through unequal letter widths and pacing.

Static comparison across standalone, titlebar, website-header, hero and monochrome conditions found Q the strongest structural foundation. H softened toward generic neutrality; R became too diagrammatic beside Threshold K.

No direction was selected at this stage.

## Q controlled refinement

Q was then changed one variable at a time:

- narrower Æ reduced ligature dominance;
- shallower M materially reduced constructed/diagrammatic character;
- softer tracking made the six glyphs read more continuously as one name.

Those successful corrections were combined as **Q4**.

Q4 still had one weakness: equal stroke weight made every line demand equal attention.

## Optical hierarchy

Q4 geometry and spacing were fixed while stroke hierarchy alone changed:

- O0 monoline control;
- O1 lighter horizontals;
- O2 slightly heavier vertical anchors;
- **O3 balanced hierarchy:** modest vertical anchoring + lighter horizontals.

O3 produced the strongest internal hierarchy without adding ornament, calligraphic contrast or logo echo.

## Legibility / identity gate

O3 was browser-rastered at 14 / 16 / 18 / 22 / 28 px CSS heights.

Static result:
- 14–16 px: the `Æ` middle extension is the first pressure point, but the ligature structure remains recoverable;
- 18 px and above: `Æ` is clear in this browser raster;
- the full word remains readable and intentionally drawn without Threshold K;
- no extra logo echo is justified.

A layout bug that stretched Threshold K in the name-first study was caught and corrected before evidence closure.

These are browser/static probes, not device or Windows typography validation.

## Current judgment

**O3 is the first bespoke wordmark candidate in this branch that survives the internal static design gates.**

Recommendation: advance O3 to **Director review as a temporary wordmark incumbent candidate**, paired contextually with the unchanged historical Threshold K.

This does **not** select:
- final logo or wordmark;
- final production vector master;
- typeface or broader typography system;
- palette;
- app-icon treatment;
- trademark clearance;
- Windows/runtime/accessibility behavior.
