## Mandatory reintegration laws

Every Lane A refinement must preserve:

- **stable Production, fluid focus** — durable scope persists while attention follows meaning;
- Watch / Direct / Perform / Write are postures, not product silos;
- Creator/Production, Audience and Character-bounded perspectives change disclosure, not truth;
- Stage is human-first and quiet by default; transcript, evidence, diagnostics and generic AI composer remain progressive;
- Current Opportunity is salience, not obligation, intent or turn order;
- Character identity is independent of Performer, portrait, color and invented psychology;
- portrayal may depict but may not establish Observation, Knowledge, Belief, Memory or private intent;
- generated/proposed material must remain distinguishable from accepted history;
- Archive/history is causal first: what happened → what changed → why it matters now → what remains unresolved;
- contextual answers emerge from the thing/question that raised them, not a generic Context silo;
- provider/capability failure may reduce generation but cannot corrupt, fictionalize or hide locally sovereign Production state.

## Accessibility boundary

O0 color cannot be a sole identity channel. The ancestor amber/green pair is approximately 1.03:1 in relative luminance; other cast pairs are also low-separation. This is arithmetic from declared tokens, not device/AT validation.

Lane A requires:

- stable programmatic Character identity/name;
- non-color visual redundancy;
- identity recoverable without portrait recognition;
- state meaning recoverable without color, motion, exact position or hover;
- normal text evaluated against applicable text-contrast requirements.

Shape is a viable channel to test, not a frozen final medium.
