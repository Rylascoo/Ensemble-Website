## 4. Formal O0 revival

### 4.1 O0-B — primary experiential/compositional ancestor

The O0-B Studio, Stage Audience View, Character View and Archive screens are revived as the **Lane A incumbent interface family**.

They are not frozen pixel specifications.

They earn inheritance because they already express core product relationships that later architecture independently strengthened:

- creative setup as people / relationships / conditions rather than agent configuration;
- live performance as human/social presence rather than AI machinery;
- Character-bounded perspective as a materially different disclosure experience over the same Production;
- history as consequence and unresolved continuity rather than transcript alone.

O0-B3 remains especially important because it demonstrates the experiential value of bounded Character perspective without requiring a second reality or omniscient creator disclosure.

Replacing O0-B as the Lane A baseline requires an explicit Director decision. Refining it does not.

### 4.2 O0-C1 — executable token/state/motion ancestor

O0-C1 is revived as the Lane A **executable ancestor**, not as product authority.

It may contribute:

- token relationships;
- CSS-state structure;
- state-class concepts;
- transition primitives;
- executable evidence of how the original concept expressed speaking/focus/handoff.

It does not automatically freeze its exact palette, typography, spacing, geometry or motion values.

### 4.3 Three O0-C1 hazards are explicitly non-inheritable

1. `a stage for language models` framing is obsolete. Kymaean is a persistent creative simulation for Characters, performance, consequence and continuity.
2. Provider/model identity must not become persistent Character identity. `Character != Performer` remains binding.
3. Runtime remote font loading is not an acceptable shipping assumption for the native local-first application. Any eventual font selection must record license and packaged/embedded delivery.

## 5. Mandatory post-O0 reintegration layer

O0 revival does **not** restore the original interface unchanged.

Every Lane A refinement must preserve the later architecture gains below.

### 5.1 Stable Production, fluid focus

The Production remains the persistent creative scope.

Possibility, present performance and causal history are connected information orientations, not three independent applications.

Navigate between durable scopes; transition within one Production by following meaning.

### 5.2 Creator posture remains orthogonal

Watch / Direct / Perform / Write are relationships to one engine, not four mandatory navigation silos.

### 5.3 Presentation Perspective remains orthogonal

Creator/Production, Audience and Character-bounded views are disclosure projections over one authoritative Production.

Changing perspective changes permitted disclosure, not truth.

### 5.4 Stage is human-first and quiet by default

The later Stage research established that the live experience can remain semantically coherent without permanently displaying:

- transcript/history;
- evidence taxonomies;
- Character dossiers;
- generic AI composer;
- provider/model diagnostics;
- causal graph;
- duplicate roster;
- global `People / Context / Progress` silos;
- product-thesis/marketing narration.

Trust machinery should govern behavior more strongly than it occupies the interface.

### 5.5 Current Opportunity is not turn order

Current Opportunity communicates salience/opportunity, not obligation, intent, readiness, speaking certainty or gameplay turn ownership.

It must resolve to the human-readable Character identity without requiring opaque A/B/C token translation.

### 5.6 Character identity is not portrait, color or psychology

Character representation must preserve identity across recasting and transformation.

Direct identity association may replace duplicate roster identity.

Identity recovery should reveal **who the Character is**, not summarize invented personality/professional archetypes.

Visual portrayal may depict presence, gesture, expression or orientation; it must not silently establish Observation, Knowledge, Belief, Memory, intent or private psychology.

### 5.7 Authority status must remain legible

The interface must not collapse:

- current authoritative state;
- proposed/draft/non-effective material;
- accepted historical performance;
- rejected/cancelled/alternate material;
- unresolved information.

A generated Performance is not history merely because it appeared on screen.

### 5.8 History is causal first, chronological second

Archive/history should answer:

```text
what happened
    ↓
what changed
    ↓
why it matters now
    ↓
what remains possible / constrained / unresolved
```

Accepted historical texture may remain true without becoming a permanent state field.

### 5.9 Context is invoked from meaning, not a generic silo

Context should emerge from the Character/event/condition/question that raised it.

Bounded answers should present supported current truth first, expose exact authority limits only when necessary, and return the creator to the same live Production without causal mutation.

### 5.10 Graceful degradation preserves creative truth

Provider/local capability failure may reduce automation. It must not become fictional Character behavior, fabricate consequence or make existing Production state unusable for safe inspection.

## 6. Accessibility correction carried into Lane A

The O0 character-color system is insufficient as a sole identity channel.

Measured ancestor-token contrast includes an amber/green pair near 1:1 luminance contrast. The correct conclusion is not that every accessibility environment literally becomes a luminance-only renderer; it is that **color-only identity is unreliable under grayscale, color-vision deficiency, forced-color/high-contrast remapping and other color-reduction/remapping conditions**.

Lane A therefore requires redundant identity that survives without color.

At minimum:

- stable programmatic Character name/identity for assistive technology;
- a non-color visual invariant when multiple Characters must remain distinguishable without labels;
- identity recoverable without portrait recognition;
- state meaning recoverable without motion, exact spatial position or hover;
- accepted/proposed/history distinctions preserved in reduced-motion/high-contrast modes.

Shape is a viable redundant visual channel to test. It is not automatically the only final representation system.

Normal text contrast must be evaluated under the applicable text contrast requirements; non-text thresholds must not be substituted for text.

No claim of real WinUI screen-reader, forced-color or device accessibility validation is made here.

## 8. App IA and website IA remain distinct responsibilities

The app preserves one Production and the connected functions of possibility, performance and causal history.

The website is an explanatory/acquisition surface. It must not copy the native app shell literally.

Website sections such as Characters, World, Director or Continuity are allowed when they explain the product clearly.

The prohibition is narrower:

> **The website must not imply that explanatory topics are peer native application workspaces if the app architecture does not work that way.**

Therefore a website navigation count does not need to equal the number of conceptual app spaces.

Choose the highest-leverage state that can be designed without unresolved performance-rhythm evidence and render it inside the revived O0 visual/interaction language while applying the post-O0 reintegration laws.

Preferred first candidate:

> **Production entry / empty-production / open-existing flow leading into the persistent Production shell.**

- it is currently absent from O0;
- it tests Production lifecycle and `stable Production, fluid focus`;

- it gives the native app a realistic front door without choosing Watch/Direct/Perform/Write as the product identity.

This first refinement should be evaluated against the O0 family for continuity, not against a clean-slate renderer.
