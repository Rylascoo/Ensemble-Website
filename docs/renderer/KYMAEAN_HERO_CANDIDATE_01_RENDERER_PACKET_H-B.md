# KYMAEAN HERO CANDIDATE 01 — RENDERER PACKET H-B

Use this packet only in a fresh regular non-project renderer chat.

Create one uninterrupted artwork centered on distinct people whose presence and relationships are necessary to the image.

A choice made earlier by one person has materially changed a situation they now share. The present condition must contain enough visible evidence for that earlier human cause to be inferred. Its consequence must materially change another person's present possibilities: what they can reach, use, enter, avoid, inspect, resist, repair, inherit, or further alter.

The people must not have equal physical availability to the cause or consequence. Do not explain or assert what anyone knows, believes, remembers, or understands. Let their different present relationships to the shared situation make the asymmetry visible.

Keep each person specific and non-interchangeable. Their interaction may alter the image as a whole, but it must not erase individual identity.

Let the artwork be quiet before spectacular. Mystery should come from relationship, evidence, and changed possibility rather than generic fantasy effects.

The image must stand first as a complete artwork, not as an explanation, product diagram, interface, promotional layout, or presentation board.

Do not include text, logos, UI, captions, product screens, or surrounding presentation layout.

Choose the unstated setting, culture, time, medium, objects, clothing, and exact causal situation yourself.

## Formal variable — constructed relational field

Organize the depiction through shallow interlocking planes, strong figure-ground separation, substantial negative space, selective occlusion, and distributed human presence.

The formal organization must appear across the depiction itself; do not let one giant physical prop carry most of the composition.

The specific earlier action and its downstream changed possibility must remain causally legible even though the surface is constructed rather than conventionally naturalistic.

Return only the uninterrupted artwork.
