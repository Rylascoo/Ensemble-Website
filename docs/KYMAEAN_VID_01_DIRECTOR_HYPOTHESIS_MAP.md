# KYMAEAN VID-01 — DIRECTOR HYPOTHESIS MAP

Status: DIRECTOR-CONTEXT DIVERGENCE MAP / NOT RENDERER INSTRUCTIONS / NO FAMILY SELECTED

Date: 2026-09-04

Primary workstream: `SHARED BRAND`

## Purpose

Provide a small set of genuinely orthogonal **carrier hypotheses** for true Kymaean visual-identity divergence without converting those hypotheses into a style menu or seeding the first isolated renderer.

This map remains inside Director Context. It is not appended to the sterile V0 packet and must not be shown to the first Renderer Context before its independent artwork exists.

These are not candidate aesthetics. They are different answers to the question:

> **Where could Kymaean's identity live visually if no historical surface is allowed to win by default?**

## H1 — LIVING RELATIONAL FIELD

Hypothesis:

> Kymaean's strongest visual identity may live primarily in the specific people and the way their presence, attention, distance, interruption, alignment, refusal, and mutual effect organize the image.

What it tests:

- human primacy;
- relationship as composition;
- Character specificity without roster/token dependence;
- consequence expressed through changed social organization.

Failure mode:

The work becomes conventional portraiture or ensemble illustration with no causal structure beyond expressive posing.

## H2 — INHERITED CONDITION

Hypothesis:

> Kymaean's identity may live in a present world whose affordances, constraints, access, and organization visibly bear the effects of earlier human choices, with people now inhabiting those changed conditions.

What it tests:

- history carried by present organization;
- consequence changing possibility;
- people living inside consequences;
- causality without flashback or timeline dependence.

Failure mode:

The environment/object becomes the protagonist and people collapse into decorative witnesses or scale figures.

## H3 — TRANSFORMATIONAL CONTINUITY

Hypothesis:

> Kymaean's identity may live in the tension between visual transformation and persistent individual identity: representation can change materially while coherent perceptual invariants keep a person recognizably themselves.

What it tests:

- Character != portrait;
- identity continuity across representation depth;
- potential translation into temporal/motion language;
- change without identity collapse.

Failure mode:

Transformation becomes spectacle, morphing, or generic surrealism with weak human specificity or no causal reason for change.

## H4 — BOUNDED FIELD

Hypothesis:

> Kymaean's identity may live in how one authoritative situation is composed through unequal access: different people occupy the same reality without equal visibility, interpretation, opportunity, or awareness.

What it tests:

- bounded perspective;
- asymmetric evidence;
- mystery from incomplete access rather than arbitrary obscurity;
- shared reality without shared understanding.

Failure mode:

Partial access becomes decorative hiding, visual confusion, voyeurism, or an unsupported claim about private knowledge/intent.

## H5 — EXPRESSIVE PERFORMANCE FIELD

Hypothesis:

> Kymaean's identity may live in an expressive field where human performance, spatial rhythm, language, timing, and authored presentation become part of the same visual organism rather than being separated into artwork plus software chrome.

What it tests:

- generative-theater ancestry without literal stage cliché;
- the relationship between expressive narrative and operational information;
- potential shared-brand translation into typography, web editorial rhythm, motion, and app presentation;
- human action as authored yet independently performed.

Failure mode:

The work collapses into literal theater imagery, poster design, branded editorial layout, or typography that explains instead of embodying the experience.

## Orthogonality rule

A later experiment does not count as a new family merely because it changes palette, camera, costume, setting, rendering style, or composition.

To count as lateral visual-universe evidence, the new branch should materially change at least three of the following while preserving the relevant durable laws:

- where visual identity primarily resides;
- degree and role of human depiction;
- relationship between people and surrounding world;
- abstraction depth;
- spatial logic;
- temporal/consequence carrier;
- material/shape grammar;
- information/language role;
- atmospheric register;
- relationship between representation and identity continuity.

## Use order

1. Execute the completely surface-blind VID-01 V0 packet first.
2. Critique that artwork before opening historical image binaries or using this map to steer a renderer.
3. Determine which carrier hypothesis, if any, the independent render accidentally explored.
4. For later lateral experiments, choose a carrier hypothesis that is genuinely underrepresented by surviving evidence.
5. Build a fresh sterile renderer packet around the smallest law necessary to test that hypothesis; do not transfer this entire map to the renderer.
6. Continue until at least two genuinely distinct families survive evidence review.
7. Only then consider a Director convergence gate.

## Anti-incumbency law

The first successful independent artwork does not become H1, H2, H3, H4, or H5's canonical manifestation. These labels organize questions, not styles.

A historical branch may later resemble one of these hypotheses. Similarity is not failure, but any renewed direction must earn itself again through current evidence rather than lineage inertia.
