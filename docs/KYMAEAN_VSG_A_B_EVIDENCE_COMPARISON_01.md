## 1. Methodological correction — evidence is asymmetric

The original isolated-render protocol intended four controlled states for every branch before comparison:

1. Present / Watch;
2. Causal Trace / Historical Focus;
3. Character-B Bounded Perspective;
4. Causal-Effect / Changed Present.

Actual exploration did not remain symmetric.

VSG-A received useful Present/Watch exploration and early comparison evidence, but did not receive architecture-corrected parity testing across the later bounded-perspective, posture/perspective, Patch-0013 opportunity, and changed-present questions.

VSG-B received substantially deeper falsification work, including:

- Present/Watch relational composition;
- removal of graph connectors and instrumentation;
- Character-bounded perspective;
- Character-bounded Watch vs Perform separation;
- evidence-class cleanup;
- causal change research;
- single-surface changed-present attempts;
- post-consequence Current Opportunity / salience testing against Patch 0013;
- repeated removal of omniscient and psychological summaries.

Therefore:

> **Current B strength cannot be interpreted as proof that B beats A. B has been tested more deeply.**

The fair current question is:

> **Which laws has each branch already demonstrated, and what minimum A parity test is still required before convergence can be considered?**

## 2. Shared laws already supported by both A and B

Both branches provide positive evidence for several accepted visual-system laws:

### 2.1 People before chrome

Both can make specific people the first visual read rather than transcript/provider/system state.

### 2.2 Transcript as support

Both can keep language available without requiring the live Production to become a chat thread.

### 2.3 Continuous Production

Both can plausibly preserve one Production/Scene rather than turning semantic state changes into separate products or page silos.

### 2.4 Relationship can organize composition

A does so primarily through editorial spacing, alignment, name/human hierarchy and whitespace.

B does so primarily through human orientation, scale, proximity, negative space, attention and relational placement.

### 2.5 Surface remains open

Neither branch earns its rendered palette, photographic/illustrative treatment, exact panel geometry, typography, environment, or Character imagery as product inheritance.

## 3. VSG-A — evidence actually earned

Original hypothesis:

> **Human presence, names, language scale, whitespace and editorial hierarchy can make Kymaean feel authored and theatrical while conventional software chrome remains quiet.**

### 3.1 Strong positive evidence

A has demonstrated:

- immediate human specificity;
- strong people-before-chrome hierarchy;
- strongest continuity with O0's experiential intelligence without requiring O0's exact surface;
- strong potential for expressive Character identity through a combination of people, names and typography;
- strong transcript restraint;
- strong whitespace and compositional breathing room;
- a plausible path to a quiet Windows shell around a theatrical human center.

### 3.2 Particularly valuable A law

A demonstrates that Kymaean does not need dense bespoke geometry to feel authored.

The branch's strongest transferable idea is:

> **Human presence can be amplified by editorial hierarchy without turning the humans into data objects.**

### 3.3 Exposed failure modes

A render evidence also exposed:

- cast-dossier / profile-product drift;
- magazine/editorial-portfolio drift;
- conventional productivity UI accumulating beneath an attractive human header;
- expressive typography potentially becoming the spectacle;
- Character identity becoming too dependent on name/type treatment rather than persistent human invariants;
- insufficient evidence that the branch can carry changed relationship and causal consequence without explanatory copy.

### 3.4 What A has NOT yet proven

A has not yet been adequately falsified against the architecture-corrected requirements for:

- Character-bounded Watch under strict subtractive disclosure;
- Character-bounded Perform while preserving identical disclosure authority;
- independent visual channels for perspective vs current opportunity;
- opportunity as salience rather than obligation;
- changed-present legibility after an effective causal change;
- current opportunity changing after consequence without becoming a turn indicator;
- reduced explanatory software around the human field;
- architecture-corrected removal of generic psychology/tension/progress/risk summaries.

These are unknowns, not failures.

## 4. VSG-B — evidence actually earned

Original hypothesis:

> **Persistent specific people and relationship itself can organize Kymaean's visual field, making social structure and changed possibility directly perceivable without reducing people to data nodes.**

### 4.1 Strong positive evidence

B has now demonstrated:

- specific people can remain primary while relationship organizes the field;
- A/B/C relation can be legible without graph connectors;
- relationship can be conveyed through orientation, proximity, scale, attention and negative space;
- Character-bounded perspective can preserve the same Production while disclosure changes;
- Character-bounded Watch and Perform can share disclosure authority while creator agency changes;
- perspective identity and Current Opportunity can be represented independently;
- Current Opportunity can read as salience rather than forced turn-taking;
- causal consequence can reorganize the human field;
- the same people/Scene can return as a materially changed present;
- post-consequence salience can shift to another Character while Production continuity remains intact;
- the final current field can be completely still.

### 4.2 Strongest B laws

B's most defensible transferable laws are now:

> **Relationship as composition, not relationship as visualization.**

and:

> **The changed present can be carried by changed human organization.**

A third strengthening law is:

> **Perspective, creator agency, and current salience are independent dimensions of one living relational workspace.**

### 4.3 Exposed failure modes

Repeated B exploration exposed and rejected:

- people reduced to nodes;
- halos, rings, particles, connectors and graph-like instrumentation;
- analytical AI surfaces that appear to score people;
- global Tension/Progress/Risk/Relationship summaries;
- private-mind inference from posture/dialogue;
- dark cinematic photography becoming mistaken for the branch identity;
- over-the-shoulder / first-person cinematography becoming mistaken for Character perspective;
- global recoloring becoming mistaken for Watch/Perform semantics;
- prescriptive action menus becoming mistaken for Character agency ontology;
- dense dashboard chrome surrounding a strong human field;
- Character personality dossiers becoming static identity authority;
- explanatory causal summaries doing work the changed relational field should perform.

### 4.4 Current near-pass boundary

The latest changed-present B experiment substantially supports:

```text
same Production
+ same Scene
+ same persistent people
+ Character-B bounded Watch
+ already-effective changed current reality
+ Current Opportunity = C
+ response not required
```

while allowing C's new salience to emerge through the human field.

Remaining cleanup items are known rather than foundational:

- remove inferred tone/atmosphere;
- verify or remove synthesized consequence statements not explicitly available to B;
- remove predictive Scene-note language;
- simplify Character dossier copy;
- progressively disclose transcript/notes where possible.

This is sufficient evidence to stop polishing B merely for aesthetic improvement.

## 5. Direct A vs B comparison by question

### Human specificity

**A:** strong.

**B:** strong once reduced/abstract-node interpretation was abandoned and specific humans were restored.

Current conclusion: **no meaningful winner.**

### Human primacy

**A:** naturally strong through whitespace/editorial hierarchy.

**B:** strong when software panels are aggressively reduced; weaker when renderer adds dashboards.

Current conclusion: **A has the cleaner default tendency.**

### Relationship as primary visual structure

**A:** relationship is legible through editorial placement and alignment.

**B:** relationship can become the organizing geometry of the primary field itself.

Current conclusion: **B has substantially stronger evidence.**

### Character-bounded disclosure

**A:** conceptually plausible, not adequately tested at current architecture precision.

**B:** repeatedly tested and substantially passed.

Current conclusion: **B leads on evidence, not yet on fair branch preference.**

### Watch vs Perform orthogonality

**A:** untested at parity.

**B:** passed dedicated visual falsification after correcting initial conflation.

Current conclusion: **B has evidence; A remains unknown.**

### Current Opportunity / salience

**A:** not tested against Patch 0013 visual requirements.

**B:** substantially passed independent perspective + opportunity representation and `opportunity != obligation`.

Current conclusion: **B has evidence; A remains unknown.**

### Changed-present / living consequence

**A:** original controlled hypothesis supports it in theory, but no architecture-corrected render has demonstrated it.

**B:** substantially passed after repeated removal of timelines, summaries and generic meters.

Current conclusion: **B currently owns the strongest evidence for Kymaean's central causal-loop experience.**

### Transcript restraint

**A:** naturally strong.

**B:** achievable but renderer repeatedly tries to reintroduce a transcript/support dashboard.

Current conclusion: **A has the cleaner default tendency.**

### Risk of conventional software

**A:** magazine/editorial/cast-dossier/productivity risk.

**B:** AI-dashboard/social-analytics/game-camera risk.

Current conclusion: **different failure modes; neither is safe by default.**

### Cross-product distinctiveness potential

**A:** strong authored/theatrical brand potential, but risks looking like premium editorial software.

**B:** stronger potential for a uniquely Kymaean relational grammar if it remains human rather than analytical.

Current conclusion: **B has greater category-distinctive potential; A may carry stronger human/editorial refinement value.**

## 7. Minimum fair A parity test

A full repetition of every historical B render is unnecessary.

The smallest unresolved fairness question is whether A's editorial/human grammar can carry the same architecture-critical states that made B strong **without becoming explanatory software**.

Therefore the minimum A parity packet should contain two isolated renders from the same corrected semantic packet:

### A-P1 — Character-Bounded Watch + independent Current Opportunity

Required:

- same P0/S0/A/B/C;
- Character-B bounded disclosure;
- posture = Watch;
- Current Opportunity belongs to a Character other than B;
- opportunity is explicitly non-obligatory;
- no Perform controls;
- no creator-only data;
- no personality dossiers or generic psychology metrics;
- people/names/editorial hierarchy remain primary;
- transcript remains subordinate.

Question:

> **Can A preserve bounded disclosure and independent salience without turning the screen into a dossier or editorial report?**

### A-P2 — Single-surface Changed Present

Required:

- same P0/S0/A/B/C;
- one prior causal change is already effective;
- no before/event panel;
- no timeline;
- no `what changed` summary;
- no generic Tension/Progress/Risk/Relationship meter;
- changed current human/typographic organization reflects the new reality;
- next Current Opportunity is legible as salience, not obligation;
- no private-mind inference;
- current state remains understandable in stillness and monochrome.

Question:

> **Can A make `same world, different now` perceptible through people, hierarchy and editorial organization without relying on explanatory copy?**

If A fails either test materially after one clean correction pass, its role should likely narrow from full visual-system candidate to transferable supporting grammar.

If A passes both, a genuine A/B convergence comparison becomes justified.
