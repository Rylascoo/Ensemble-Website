# 2. Shared render protocol

## 2.1 Four independent state renders per branch

Do not ask one image to contain the entire experiment.

For each branch, produce four isolated renders in this order:

1. `PRESENT / WATCH`
2. `CAUSAL TRACE / HISTORICAL FOCUS`
3. `CHARACTER-B BOUNDED PERSPECTIVE`
4. `CAUSAL-EFFECT SEQUENCE / CHANGED PRESENT`

State 4 may use a restrained three-moment progression within one composition because temporal comparison is the variable being tested there.

This yields twelve controlled renders total across the three branches.

## 2.2 Common output character

Every render should read as:

- a serious Windows desktop application visual-system study;
- one persistent Production rather than unrelated pages;
- low-to-mid fidelity enough that design grammar is visible without pretending final product polish;
- human-centered rather than dashboard-centered;
- computationally still at rest;
- semantically understandable without ornamental animation.

Use only a small amount of short interface text. The experiment is primarily compositional, relational, typographic, and material rather than a test of long-form generated UI copy.

## 2.3 Comparison discipline

The Director evaluates each branch only after all four states for that branch exist.

No branch earns preference from its Present/Watch frame alone.

No branch is refined until all three branches have completed their four-state packet and the cross-product transfer check is performed.

# 3. VSG-A — isolated positive packet

## 3.1 Director-side hypothesis

> **Human presence, names, language scale, whitespace, and editorial hierarchy can make Kymaean feel authored and theatrical while conventional software chrome remains quiet.**

## 3.2 Renderer-visible formal packet

Use a predominantly light-neutral, high-key Windows application field with a mostly flat visual hierarchy. Let whitespace, alignment, typographic scale, and the placement of three specific people organize the experience. Human representation should be simplified but unmistakably human and individually recognizable through recurring form, posture, placement, and name treatment. Operational UI text is quiet and systematic. Expressive typography is larger and more spatial around Character identity and Scene meaning. Containers are sparse; grouping is carried mainly by composition. Use one restrained accent family as secondary emphasis while preserving the hierarchy through value, scale, and structure.

Do not name the branch inside the rendered UI.

## 3.3 A1 — Present / Watch

### Renderer packet

Create a Windows desktop application visual study of one live creative Production with three distinct people, A, B, and C. A and B are the current interaction focus; C remains visibly present and relevant. The composition is spacious and editorial: the people and their names occupy the strongest visual register, their relationship is legible through alignment, orientation, and whitespace, and a compact current-condition / active-pressure signal sits near the human field without becoming a dashboard. A small supporting transcript excerpt and a few creator actions sit on a clearly quieter lower register. Preserve one continuous workspace and a restrained native application frame.

### Director checks

- eye lands on people before transcript or controls;
- Character identity is more than typography alone;
- interaction reads as current and live;
- composition does not become a magazine spread detached from interactive software.

## 3.4 A2 — Causal Trace / Historical Focus

### Renderer packet

Create the same Production and the same three people in a focused historical-inspection state. Keep the current condition R1 visually primary while revealing a concise causal reading from prior condition R0 through effective event E1 to R1. Use typographic hierarchy, indentation, alignment, and restrained rule structure rather than a permanent timeline. Make historical focus explicit while retaining a clear route back to the current Production. The people remain part of the living context rather than disappearing behind a report.

### Director checks

- current condition remains the reason for inspection;
- history explains rather than replaces the present;
- chronology does not become the dominant metaphor;
- return to current is visually predictable.

## 3.5 A3 — Character-B Bounded Perspective

### Renderer packet

Create the same Production, Scene, and three people from Character B's bounded disclosure perspective. Preserve recognizable continuity with the prior application state, but recompose the editorial hierarchy around what is available to B. Creator-only relationship and pressure detail is absent, not ghosted or dimmed, and privileged creator actions are absent. B gains stronger identity prominence without recoloring or retheming the entire application. The experiential change comes from subtraction, reflow, and re-prioritization of available information.

### Director checks

- same reality remains recognizable;
- missing privileged information feels intentionally unavailable rather than broken;
- B is more experientially central without becoming a cosmetic theme;
- no unsupported private mind is visualized.

## 3.6 A4 — Causal-Effect Sequence / Changed Present

### Renderer packet

Create a restrained three-moment visual progression within the same editorial application grammar. First show performance material M1 as clearly non-effective review material. Then show a concise causal-effect transition E2 changing relationship condition R1 to R2 while pressure P2 remains active. End by returning to the same Production, Scene, and three people with R2 and P2 now part of the current present. Make the final moment compositionally echo the Present/Watch state so continuity is unmistakable, while changed hierarchy and relational emphasis make the altered future possibility visible.

### Director checks

- non-effective and effective states are distinct without depending only on color;
- E2 does not become spectacle;
- final frame feels like the same living world, changed;
- people remain primary throughout the transition.

# 4. VSG-B — isolated positive packet

> **Persistent specific people and relationship itself can organize Kymaean's visual field, making social structure and changed possibility directly perceivable without reducing people to data nodes.**

## 4.2 Renderer-visible formal packet

Represent three specific people through reduced but unmistakably human forms with individually persistent structural signatures: distinct proportions, silhouettes, orientations, or recurring internal form characteristics. Typography is restrained and secondary. Spatial relation, orientation, negative space, and a small number of open connective forms carry more composition than boxes. Material depth is minimal and planar. Identity may receive subtle accessible accent differentiation, but each person must remain distinguishable in monochrome. The overall experience should feel human and relational rather than diagrammatic.

# 5. VSG-C — isolated positive packet

## 5.1 Director-side hypothesis

> **Quiet digital material depth and state registration can make persistence, current authority, and consequence feel durable while human presence remains primary.**

## 5.2 Renderer-visible formal packet

Use low-chroma matte digital surfaces with shallow, restrained depth. One clear current layer should dominate. Contextual and historical material may appear through subordinate registration, edge, inset, or shallow layering rather than a wall of cards. Represent the three people through concise figurative contour or relief-like forms with individually recurring identity characteristics. Typography is compact and precise. Value contrast and registration carry more meaning than vivid color. The visual system should feel digitally material and persistent, with depth clarifying current versus contextual versus historical information while remaining completely understandable as a flat, high-contrast fallback.

Do not name the branch inside the rendered UI.

## 5.3 C1 — Present / Watch

### Renderer packet

Create a Windows desktop application visual study of one current Production surface containing three distinct figurative contour or shallow relief-like human forms A, B, and C. A and B carry the strongest interaction focus; C remains visibly present. R1 and P1 register on the same authoritative current surface near the relevant human relationship. Supporting language shares the current layer at a quieter hierarchy. Use shallow digital registration and restrained edge relationships to establish persistence and focus without breaking the primary human field into cards.

### Director checks

- people outrank the material metaphor;
- current layer reads as authoritative without requiring translucency;
- surface feels digital and living rather than archival;
- human identities remain specific.

## 5.4 C2 — Causal Trace / Historical Focus

### Director checks
