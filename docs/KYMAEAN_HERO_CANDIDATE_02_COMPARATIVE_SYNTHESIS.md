## Comparative read

### G1 — first real semantic survivor

G1 is the first hero experiment in this branch to satisfy the complete mandatory semantic + responsive envelope.

The important improvement is not the rustic room. It is the visual physics:
- an earlier human action is recoverable;
- the shared ground has failed to return to neutral;
- that non-neutral field changes another person's usable space;
- the causal cluster stays compact enough for mobile;
- the left desktop region can genuinely disappear under identity/copy.

This is the strongest evidence yet for **Residual Presence** as a viable Kymaean website-art law.

Its weakness is significant: the renderer still retreats toward historical domestic realism. Therefore the image itself should not be promoted as final hero art.

### G2 — strongest shared-field study

G2 most clearly proves that a shared substrate can carry relation and altered possibility without a visible "power" system. It also has the best natural desktop breathing room.

But human prior cause is not recoverable. The field could have changed naturally or contemporaneously. It therefore fails the core causal-history requirement.

Preserve the field behavior, not the picture.

### G3 — strongest translation study

G3 proves that a recognizably specific person can remain continuous while their depiction materially translates into the surrounding world. This is valuable.

But the action reads as present cleaning rather than a recoverable prior cause whose consequence persists. It therefore fails the same causal gate as G2.

Preserve the identity-continuity finding, not the picture.

## G4 pressure

All three keep one authoritative shared world and do not require private-state depiction. G1 is strongest because the same residue has visibly different physical implications depending on where each person stands.
