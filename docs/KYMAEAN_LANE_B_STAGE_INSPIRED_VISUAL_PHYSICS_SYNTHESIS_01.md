## Proposed physics laws

1. **Presence is a local law of shared space, not an object placed inside it.**
2. **Visual identity continuity may be supported by repeatable matter/space behavior without replacing the person.**
3. **Consequence is the shared field failing to return to neutral after human action.**
4. **Relationship is how one person's presence changes another person's available space, not a connector.**
5. **Grounding matters:** impossible presence still has contact, weight, occlusion, residue or displacement.
6. **The Stage is meaningful emptiness around specific people, not literal stage scenery.**
7. **Transformation may reveal identity, but humanity stays primary.**
8. **Color reinforces; it never carries identity, knowledge, authority or state alone.**

## Fresh grammar hypotheses

### G1 — Residual Presence
A person's local environmental response remains after action. Another person must occupy a scene that has not returned to neutral.

### G2 — Shared Pressure Field
Distinct local spatial/material pressures share one substrate and bend, compress, occlude or redirect one another without beams/connectors.

### G3 — Translated Identity
A recognizably specific person continues across materially different states while transformation serves relationship rather than spectacle.

### G4 — Occluded Chorus
One shared world contains layered evidence. Physical position/presence reveals, blocks or distorts different parts without depicting private mental state.
