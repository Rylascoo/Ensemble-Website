## PAC-01C

Name:

**Persistent Affordance Carrier 01C — Relational Configuration / Emergent Affordance**

Research question:

> Can ordinary movable elements become a capability-bearing configuration when multiple independent human actions visibly depend on their relationship to one another?

## Exact sterile packet used

> Create one uninterrupted artwork centered on several distinct people whose presence and relationships are necessary to the image.
>
> A few ordinary movable human-made elements were arranged before this moment into one modest shared configuration. Several people are independently using that same configuration at the same time for different actions. At least two of those actions should visibly depend on the relationship among multiple elements, rather than on any single element by itself, so the arrangement itself is what makes those uses workable.
>
> Keep the elements recognizable as ordinary everyday things. They should not appear specially built or modified for this purpose. The original act of arranging them is not shown.
>
> Keep every person specific and self-directed. No one should guide, help, direct, rescue, or control anyone else. Keep the configuration visually subordinate to the people.
>
> Keep the surrounding world restrained. Let posture, orientation, spacing, and independent use reveal why the relationship among the elements matters.
>
> Return only the uninterrupted artwork, with no text or surrounding presentation layout.
