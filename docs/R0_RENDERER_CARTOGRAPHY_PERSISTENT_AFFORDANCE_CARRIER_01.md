# R0 Renderer Cartography — Persistent Affordance Carrier Design 01

Status: **DIRECTOR DESIGN CHECKPOINT — NO RENDER AUTHORIZED**

Updated: 2026-09-03

## Purpose

The corrected SOV/RST sequence established a persistent limitation:

- people can remain primary;
- people can remain individually agentic;
- relational history and consequence can be suggested;
- asymmetric relational/evidentiary position can be shown;
- but `enabled possibility` is not reliably recoverable when every persistent carrier is prohibited.

Canonical evidence synthesis:

`docs/R0_RENDERER_CARTOGRAPHY_SEMANTIC_OBSERVABILITY_SYNTHESIS_02.md`

The next test deliberately permits a visible carrier of `history + persistence + changed capability`, while controlling that carrier so it cannot become the identity, protagonist or metaphor.

## Core hypothesis

> **A persistent enabling condition may become visually recoverable when an ordinary human-made carrier remains present and supports several independent human actions.**

The carrier is an experimental measurement device. It has no aesthetic or metaphorical inheritance rights.

## What the carrier must prove

A successful carrier should allow the image itself to support four claims without explanatory text:

1. **Human-shaped persistence** — the condition is visibly made, arranged or configured by people rather than a natural accident.
2. **Present persistence** — it remains available after the originating action is over.
3. **Plural affordance** — more than one person can use the condition in meaningfully different ways.
4. **Independent agency** — each person's present action remains their own rather than assistance, instruction or control by another person.

## Carrier constraints

The carrier must be:

- modest in scale;
- ordinary rather than monumental;
- visibly subordinate to the people;
- human-made or visibly human-shaped;
- persistent rather than a momentary gesture;
- non-symbolic;
- usable in multiple ways;
- compatible with several people acting independently;
- visually integrated rather than displayed as a special object;
- free of bridge, door, path, portal, threshold, gateway, staircase, crossing or route logic;
- free of rescue, repair, accommodation, triumph or achievement framing;
- free of branded technology or product-demo staging.

## Candidate carrier classes

### A. One modest shared arrangement with several usable regions

Definition:

A small human-made arrangement remains present while several people use different parts of it for different independent actions.

Strengths:

- clearest test of persistence + plural affordance;
- supports distributed use without one helper/recipient pair;
- can remain visually subordinate.

Risks:

- may collapse into generic workplace/table/furniture imagery;
- may become a product demonstration if too object-centered.

Status: **PRIMARY CANDIDATE**

### B. Several coordinated human-made elements forming one shared condition

Definition:

Multiple simple elements together create a persistent arrangement that people use differently.

Strengths:

- reduces single-object symbolism;
- can distribute agency across the composition.

Risks:

- renderer may invent a workshop, equipment system or installation;
- complexity makes causal history harder to read.

Status: **SECONDARY / DEFER**

### C. Human-adjusted spatial organization without a discrete object

Definition:

The arrangement of ordinary built elements itself carries the affordance.

Strengths:

- potentially closest to system-level condition rather than object fetish.

Risks:

- high probability of architecture, access, threshold, room-design or environmental-protagonist regression.

Status: **REJECT FOR FIRST TEST**

## Rejected carrier classes

### Literal route/access carrier

Reject bridges, passages, doors, paths, ramps, openings, stairs, thresholds and similar traversal devices.

Reason: these collapse changed capability into physical access and revive quarantined Crossing/Boundary imagery.

### Repair or damage carrier

Reject broken/repaired structures, patched objects, restoration or visible damage history.

Reason: prior research maps history/consequence language strongly toward ruin, repair and archaeology.

### Tool handed from one person to another

Reject.

Reason: creates giver/recipient hierarchy and makes enabled agency look like assistance.

### Branded or advanced technology

Reject.

Reason: invites product-demo language and can falsely imply application functionality or selected technological identity.

### Monumental shared structure

Reject.

Reason: environment becomes protagonist and people become scale figures.

## First experiment — PAC-01A

Name:

**Persistent Affordance Carrier 01A — Ordinary Shared Arrangement / Distributed Use**

Question:

> Can one modest persistent human-made arrangement make prior human agency and expanded present capability visually observable while remaining subordinate to distinct people?

### Controlled semantic requirements

- several distinct people must remain the primary visual structure;
- the originating act that created/configured the condition is not shown;
- one modest human-made arrangement remains present;
- several people use different parts/aspects of it simultaneously for different actions;
- no person's use should depend on another person guiding, helping, directing or controlling them;
- the arrangement should appear intentionally shaped/configured for shared use, but not like a symbolic object or exhibit;
- the scene should make sense as ordinary lived reality rather than an explanation of a concept.

### Formal control

Use the validated human-dominant Cartography discipline:

- people larger/more visually important than the carrier;
- shallow-to-moderate spatial depth only as needed to make plural use legible;
- clear silhouettes and body orientation;
- restrained descriptive environment;
- carrier occupies a minority of visual attention;
- moderate contrast;
- no presentation-board framing;
- no text.

This is an experimental control and has no identity inheritance rights.

## Draft sterile packet — NO RENDER AUTHORIZED

> Create one uninterrupted artwork centered on several distinct people whose presence and relationships are necessary to the image.
>
> A modest human-made arrangement remains from earlier human activity and is now part of ordinary shared life. Several people are using different parts or aspects of it at the same time for different independent actions. The arrangement should make those actions possible without anyone guiding, helping, directing, or controlling anyone else.
>
> Keep the human-made arrangement visually subordinate to the people. It should feel ordinary, integrated, and intentionally shaped for shared use rather than displayed as a special object or symbol. The original act of making or configuring it is not shown.
>
> Keep every person specific and individually active. Let posture, orientation, spacing, and use reveal how the persistent condition supports different actions.
>
> Keep the surrounding world restrained. The people should carry most of the composition and visual attention.
>
> Return only the uninterrupted artwork, with no text or surrounding presentation layout.

## Evaluation gates

### PAC1 — Carrier visibility

Can the viewer identify a persistent human-made condition without it becoming the protagonist?

### PAC2 — Human-shaped history

Does the carrier plausibly look intentionally configured/shaped by human activity rather than accidental or purely natural?

Do not require exact reconstruction of the originating event.

### PAC3 — Plural affordance

Are at least two different independent present actions visibly supported by the carrier?

### PAC4 — Independent agency

Do people act without helper/recipient, leader/follower or controller/controlled hierarchy?

### PAC5 — Human primacy

Would the image lose most of its meaning if the people were removed?

### PAC6 — Metaphor suppression

Does the result avoid route/access/threshold/repair/progress symbolism and environment-first spectacle?
