## 0. Purpose

Run one genuinely lateral visual-world experiment after V0R1.

V0R1 strongly demonstrated a living relational field but under-carried recoverable cause and materially changed present possibility. V0R2 therefore tests a different carrier:

> **Can a prior human decision become recoverable because the present environment itself has a changed usable structure that affects different people's possibilities?**

This is a lateral evidence test, not refinement of V0R1 and not a request for a more explicit version of the same domestic scene.

## 1. Director-only rationale

V0R1 showed:

```text
human specificity + relational tension + historical atmosphere
!= sufficient causal recoverability
```

The next experiment shifts the burden of evidence from emotional/conversational staging toward present affordance.

The environment should function as causal evidence because of what it now permits, prevents, redirects, divides, or enables for the people who inhabit it.

This does not authorize environmental spectacle to outrank people. Human consequences remain primary.

## 3. VID-01 / V0 sterile render packet 02

Use verbatim:

```text
Create one complete, uninterrupted artwork centered on distinct people inhabiting a shared present condition whose usable structure has been materially altered by an earlier human decision.

Make the earlier choice recoverable primarily through what the present now permits, prevents, redirects, divides, or makes possible for different people. Show the people actively living with, using, confronting, adapting to, or being constrained by those changed possibilities rather than relying mainly on facial expression or a conversation to explain that something happened.

Do not make everyone equally affected or equally positioned in relation to the changed condition. Let that asymmetry be visible in the present organization without explanatory text and without claiming private thoughts the image cannot establish.

Keep each person specific and non-interchangeable. The altered condition should matter because of how it changes human possibility and relationship, not because it is visually spectacular on its own.

Invent every unstated visual circumstance yourself, including medium, palette, setting, era, scale, material language, atmosphere, composition, and degree of abstraction. Do not optimize for software, branding, explanation, or category familiarity. The result should stand first as a compelling artwork.

Return only the uninterrupted artwork. Add no text, labels, interface, logo, promotional framing, presentation board, or explanatory border.
```

## 4. What changed from packet 01

The renderer is not told what V0R1 looked like.

The semantic change is narrow:

- packet 01 asked for a present condition changed by an earlier choice;
- packet 02 places the burden of causal evidence more strongly on changed present affordance and active human use/constraint;
- packet 02 discourages relying mainly on facial expression or conversation as the carrier of causality;
- packet 02 preserves open medium, setting, palette, era, material, composition, and abstraction.

This is enough to create a lateral causal-carrier test without seeding a new surface grammar.

## 6. Falsification conditions

V0R2 fails the central test if:

- the environment is merely decorative;
- the work again depends on viewers inventing an unspecified backstory;
- consequence is conveyed only through emotional poses;
- an impressive environment becomes the protagonist while people become scale figures;
- the altered condition is legible but no human possibility is actually different;
- asymmetry is asserted through inferred psychology rather than visible access/use/constraint;
- the render becomes a causal diagram, storyboard, split panel, or explanatory concept board;
- the output merely restyles V0R1.
