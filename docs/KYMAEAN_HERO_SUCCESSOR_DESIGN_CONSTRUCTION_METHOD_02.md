## 0. Purpose

Define a successor method after the completed three-round one-shot qualifying sequence exhausted at 3 / 3.

The successor method preserves the frozen hero contract and changes only **how a future candidate is constructed before rendering**.

Its core proposition is:

> **Do not ask a renderer to invent semantic causality, two-person necessity, changed-state evidence, responsive geometry and surface hierarchy simultaneously. Construct and falsify those relationships first in a non-aesthetic geometry model; only then translate one Director-approved construction into artwork.**

This document is a method proposal. It does not authorize Candidate 04, a visual direction, a geometry scaffold, a renderer packet, image generation, a fourth qualifying round, final hero selection, implementation, motion, Lane A change, or any frozen-criterion modification.

## 2. Problem statement

The unresolved problem is not simply “write a better prompt.”

The smallest high-value unresolved conjunction is:

1. two-person Human Necessity;
2. Changed-condition legibility;
3. Altered Possibility / Removal necessity;
4. S1 Human-first salience.

The prior one-shot method delegated too many coupled geometric decisions to a clean-slate renderer at once. The renderer could satisfy subsets, but not reliably the full conjunction.

Method 02 therefore separates **semantic/relational construction** from **aesthetic rendering**.

## 3. Method architecture

Method 02 has seven ordered stages:

1. **Semantic Dependency Construction**
2. **Baseline / Change Reconstruction Proof**
3. **Counterfactual Possibility Proof**
4. **Two-Person Necessity Proof**
5. **Responsive Semantic Envelope**
6. **Neutral Geometry Scaffold**
7. **Sterile Artwork Translation**

A future candidate may advance only if each earlier stage passes before the next begins.

No compensating average exists between construction stages.

These stages are **conservative pre-render admission controls**, not new post-render measurement criteria. Final artwork remains scored only by the already-frozen Hero Brief 02 / Falsification Criteria 01 / Surface Escape / Harness authority. Method 02 may reject a construction that might hypothetically have passed, but it may not manufacture a pass or add a new hard gate after pixels exist.

## 4. Stage 1 — Semantic Dependency Construction

Before choosing style, material identity, detailed setting, clothing, camera language or renderer wording, define one **Semantic Dependency Record**.

Required fields:

- Person A observable role;
- Person B observable role;
- one shared baseline condition;
- one visibly changed condition;
- one altered present possibility;
- the directed dependency between changed condition and present possibility;
- why Person A is necessary;
- why Person B is necessary;
- what proposition collapses if either person is removed;
- what proposition collapses if the changed condition is restored to baseline.

### 4.1 No private-state semantics

All roles must be externally visible. Do not use knowledge, belief, memory, intention, emotion, consent, motive or hidden authorship as required evidence.

### 4.2 Relational necessity requirement

The candidate may not consist of two independent demonstrations of the same mechanism.

PASS requires one shared proposition in which **both people are semantically load-bearing**.

Removing Person A must destroy one necessary part of the proposition.
Removing Person B must destroy a different necessary part of the proposition.

If either person can be removed while the other still communicates essentially the complete causal proposition, reject the construction before visual development.

This requirement does not preselect which person causes, receives, enables, blocks, inherits or alters anything. It only requires the final proposition to be irreducibly two-person.

## 5. Stage 2 — Baseline / Change Reconstruction Proof

The construction must prove that “changed” is visible from the still, not supplied by prompt history.

Create a **Baseline / Change Evidence Record** with at least **two independent geometric correspondence cues** between unchanged and changed portions of the same condition, chosen as appropriate from:

- continuous edge alignment;
- repeated spacing or interval;
- matching surface orientation before divergence;
- contour continuation;
- repeated thickness;
- repeated structural rhythm;
- continuous material boundary;
- clear local displacement from a recoverable larger organization.

The exact correspondence types are not a checklist and do not become style law. The requirement is redundancy: no single crease, color patch, hole, prop or spectacular effect may carry the entire changed-state claim. More than two cues may be used when they arise naturally, but the method should not force extra visual complexity merely to satisfy a count.

### 5.1 Baseline recovery test

A neutral reviewer should be able to answer:

> “What simpler organization is being treated as baseline, and which visible part departs from it?”

without being told the candidate history.

If the answer is “the unusual shape might simply have been designed that way,” the construction fails this stage.

## 6. Stage 3 — Counterfactual Possibility Proof

Define one observable relationship:

`visible changed condition -> present possibility for one depicted person`

Then define the frozen removal counterfactual:

`restore visible baseline -> required physical/spatial relation disappears -> same present possibility cannot remain`

The proof must be based on externally visible geometry or physics.

### 6.1 Counterfactual ledger

Record:

- exact changed geometry that creates the possibility;
- exact body/object/spatial relation that uses that geometry;
- every visible alternate path/support/contact that could preserve the same possibility;
- why each alternate is absent or insufficient;
- what physically changes under baseline restoration.

Any unresolved plausible alternate path/support/contact is a construction failure.

### 6.2 No convenience substitution

The changed condition must not merely make the situation easier, closer, more comfortable, more elegant, more visible, more emotionally meaningful or more convenient.

The required possibility must materially disappear or become physically unavailable under baseline restoration.

## 7. Stage 4 — Two-Person Necessity Proof

Run a **four-removal dependency matrix** before any artwork rendering:

| Removal | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| remove Person A | proposition materially collapses |
| remove Person B | proposition materially collapses |
| restore changed condition to baseline | altered possibility collapses |
| genericize/interchange both people | primary proposition materially weakens |

A candidate fails construction if any row does not hold.

### 7.1 Distinct semantic jobs

The two people must not be redundant examples, witness + protagonist, helper + recipient by default, or two generic occupants of a mechanism.

Each must carry a different observable semantic job in the same shared relation.

The method does not preselect what those jobs are. That remains future candidate-design scope.

## 8. Stage 5 — Responsive Semantic Envelope

Responsive survival must be designed before a candidate receives visual styling.

Use unchanged Harness 01 geometry:

- desktop 1280 x 720, artwork 50% 50%, fixed left identity/copy region;
- mobile 390 x 488 at 72% 50% with separate identity band.

Construct a **Semantic Envelope Record** dividing evidence into:

### A. Core evidence

Every fact necessary to pass:

- Human Necessity;
- Changed-condition legibility;
- Altered Possibility / Removal Test;
- S1;
- S6.

Core evidence must remain inside the region that survives the fixed mobile crop.

### B. Supplemental desktop evidence

Additional atmosphere, balance, baseline continuation or material context may occupy the wider desktop frame, including the calm identity-bearing left region, but the candidate may not depend on that supplemental evidence to establish the core proposition.

### 8.1 Redundant baseline witness

Because Round 03 lost the clearest baseline evidence on mobile, at least one strong baseline witness must exist inside the mobile semantic envelope while another may extend into the wider composition.

The left desktop identity region must remain calm and naturally compatible with the fixed identity/copy system. It cannot become the only location containing essential baseline evidence.

## 9. Stage 6 — Neutral Geometry Scaffold

After Stages 1-5 pass and **only after a later Director gate approves one specific candidate direction**, translate the approved construction into a deterministic **Neutral Geometry Scaffold**.

The scaffold is not artwork and carries no visual-brand authority.

Its job is to freeze the relational geometry that the one-shot method previously delegated to the renderer.

### 9.1 Allowed scaffold information

Only structural information may be encoded:

- approximate figure bounding regions / body-axis orientation;
- required critical body/contact/support points;
- changed-condition contour/continuity path;
- baseline witness regions;
- required negative-space regions;
- desktop calm-region boundary;
- mobile semantic-envelope boundary;
- occlusion order where causally necessary.

### 9.2 Excluded scaffold information

Do not encode:

- final palette;
- material style;
- clothing style;
- facial appearance;
- ethnicity;
- historical period;
- named artistic style;
- texture;
- lighting mood;
- Threshold K/O3 geometry;
- brand colors;
- scene symbolism;
- narrative labels/arrows intended to survive into artwork.

### 9.3 Deterministic construction

The scaffold should be generated from explicit geometry coordinates or vector primitives, not by generative image synthesis.

This makes the transferred variable auditable: **relational composition only**.

A later Director-approved execution may use the scaffold as a structural reference only if that reference-transfer method is explicitly approved. Method 02 proposal itself does not authorize creating or sending such a scaffold.

## 10. Stage 7 — Sterile Artwork Translation

Only after the Semantic Dependency Record, Baseline/Change Proof, Counterfactual Ledger, Two-Person Necessity Matrix, Responsive Semantic Envelope and Neutral Geometry Scaffold are approved may a future renderer packet be drafted.

The renderer-facing packet should contain:

- the minimum semantic relation necessary to interpret the scaffold;
- human distinctness requirements;
- changed-condition continuity requirements;
- bounded formal-grammar controls;
- restrained material behavior;
- compact Surface Escape constraint;
- artwork-only output contract.

The scaffold carries spatial relationships. The text carries only what geometry cannot unambiguously carry.

This prevents the packet from becoming another long all-purpose prose specification.

## 13. Construction falsification before rendering

A candidate construction is rejected before rendering if any of the following is true:

1. either person is semantically removable;
2. generic occupants preserve essentially the same proposition;
3. baseline state cannot be recovered from multiple visible correspondences;
4. changed state depends on prompt history rather than still-image evidence;
5. baseline restoration leaves the altered possibility plausibly intact;
6. alternate support/path/contact preserves the same possibility;
7. environment/mechanism remains the main proposition after people are mentally removed;
8. core evidence does not fit inside the fixed mobile semantic envelope;
9. left desktop region must carry essential semantic evidence that conflicts with identity/copy coexistence;
10. the concept primarily collapses into an ordinary task/ordinary accident tableau;
11. the planned geometry requires private-state interpretation;
12. the construction requires explanatory graphics to be understood.

Any one failure returns the design to construction, not rendering.

These rejection conditions are prospective method filters. They do not modify post-render scoring and cannot be invoked to retroactively invent a new failure category.

2. **two-person necessity is falsified before rendering through a removal matrix;**
3. **baseline/change evidence is redundantly preconstructed;**
4. **counterfactual causal necessity is explicitly audited for alternate physical explanations;**
5. **all semantic-core evidence is precommitted to the fixed responsive overlap rather than distributed opportunistically across the wide frame;**
6. **a deterministic neutral geometry scaffold may carry relational composition into the renderer instead of relying on prose alone;**
7. **renderer prose becomes shorter because geometry is no longer invented there.**

These are method controls, not visual-style choices.

## 18. Success criterion for the method itself

Method 02 is successful as a **construction method** if, before rendering, it can produce a candidate package where all of the following can be shown without aesthetic interpretation:

- both people are individually necessary to one proposition;
- changed state is recoverable from redundant visible geometry;
- altered possibility fails under baseline restoration;
- no alternate visible explanation preserves that possibility;
- people remain the proposition rather than mechanism spectators;
- the complete semantic core fits the fixed responsive envelope;
- no private-state or ordinary-task logic is required.

This still does not guarantee the renderer will instantiate the construction. It makes renderer deviation observable rather than conflating design failure with uncontrolled geometric invention.
