## Validated visual / semantic laws

### 1. Distinct people can remain structurally primary

Repeated evidence across AL, CFM, SOV/RST, PAC and TEO shows that people can carry the composition, action and meaning without becoming decorative scale figures.

Transferable law:

> **People should matter structurally, not merely populate the surface.**

This does not specify count, portrait style, realism, medium or composition.

### 2. Relationship can function as visual geometry

Distance, orientation, overlap, negative space, interruption, occlusion, recurrence, attention and material relation can carry social or causal structure.

Transferable law:

> **Relationship may be encoded in how the field is organized, not only through explicit interaction.**

### 3. Identity can persist through transformation

AL-H01 established that recurring people can remain recognizable across changes in scale, crop, orientation, pose, completeness and viewpoint through a family of perceptual invariants.

Transferable law:

> **Identity need not depend on a fixed portrait.**

This does not select a character system or require repeated figures in production.

### 4. Shared reality can remain asymmetrical

The strongest still-image proxy for unequal understanding is not facial expression or inferred private knowledge but asymmetric evidentiary position: different access, visibility, occlusion, orientation or relationship to the same condition.

Transferable law:

> **One reality can be differently available from different positions.**

Do not claim static artwork proves private belief, memory, intention or interpretation.

### 5. Present state can contain recoverable history

RI demonstrated that exception against repetition, interrupted correspondence and altered organization can make a prior state inferable from the present.

Transferable law:

> **History can exist as structure rather than age, ruin, patina or flashback.**

### 6. Visible authorship and independent agency are in tension

Direct physical causality improves actor attribution but repeatedly tends toward control, rescue, guidance or conflict semantics.

Removing control preserves agency but weakens visible causal linkage.

Transferable law:

> **Causal clarity must not be purchased by turning one person into another person's controller.**

### 7. Independent agency is not enabled agency

RST and PAC repeatedly separated self-directed activity from visibly expanded possibility.

Transferable law:

> **A person acting independently does not, by itself, prove that a prior condition enabled that action.**

### 8. Persistent carriers can make present affordance visible, but object presence is not systemic causality

PAC progressively established:

- supported action is not necessarily carrier-dependent action;
- object affordance is not configuration affordance;
- local relational affordance is not shared systemic affordance;
- shared-element overlap is not distributed configuration dependence;
- distributed carrier topology is not distributed configuration dependence.

Transferable law:

> **Do not mistake visible infrastructure, shared objects or dense organization for proof of a system-level enabling cause.**

### 9. A single static artwork has a semantic observability boundary

Static work robustly supports:

- human specificity and primacy;
- relational geometry;
- transformed-but-recognizable identity when deliberately tested;
- asymmetric evidentiary position;
- independent agency;
- present consequence;
- some recoverable prior state/history when internal evidence exists;
- ordinary persistent affordance.

Static work did not robustly prove, under the project's non-literal/non-hierarchical controls:

- specific prior authorship in every case;
- definite prior human creation/configuration of a persistent condition;
- the full chain `prior human choice -> persistent condition -> newly enabled independent action`;
- distributed no-single-element common-cause capability;
- private mental state.

Transferable law:

> **Not every system law must be directly observable in one static visual surface.**

### 10. Minimal temporal comparison materially increases causal observability

TEO-01 established strong temporal information gain for authorship, persistence and later enabled use, though through a physical-access shortcut.

TEO-02 then preserved that information gain while changing the semantic route to ordinary reorganization of a shared workspace rather than access.

Final temporal finding:

> **Earlier/later state comparison can carry authorship, persistence and changed possibility more reliably than one static moment alone.**

Companion safeguard:

> **Temporal observability is a transferable semantic resource, not a selected motion treatment or split-panel visual identity.**

## Quarantined surfaces — no inheritance rights

This includes, but is not limited to:

## Internal thesis status

The internal principle-level thesis remains useful:

> **Human Causality, Living Consequence.**
>
> Kymaean should feel like a world that notices people, remembers what they do, and is changed by what follows.

This remains an internal design principle, not a public tagline and not an aesthetic prescription.

The consolidated non-aesthetic system hypothesis remains:

> **Distinct people are structurally important; human choices can change shared conditions and future possibilities; identities can persist across transformed representations; relationships can structure the visual field; visibility/access can be asymmetric; and present arrangements can contain evidence of prior human causes.**

## What the design phase may inherit

The app and website design phase may inherit **constraints and questions**, not renderer surfaces.

### Human primacy

Interfaces and brand surfaces should avoid treating people as decoration or anonymous scale figures when people are present.

### Relational structure

Layout, hierarchy, grouping, spacing, state relationships and transitions may carry meaning rather than relying only on labels or metaphor.

### Persistent identity

Where identity continuity matters, recognition may be supported by multiple coherent invariants rather than one permanent portrait/icon treatment.

### Bounded perspective

Different views of the same underlying state may legitimately reveal different evidence without implying different realities.

### Causality and persistence

When the product or website needs to communicate `what changed because of what happened earlier`, state transition or before/after evidence is now empirically justified as a candidate semantic technique.

### Anti-literalization

Do not force bridges, portals, pathways, repair scenes, AI-glow, futuristic machinery or other familiar metaphors to carry internal product philosophy.

### Multi-surface burden sharing

Do not require a logo, hero image, screen, animation or artwork to explain the entire Kymaean thesis alone.

## Why transition is justified now

The research has answered the questions it was uniquely suited to answer:

1. Which semantic laws survive unrelated formal grammars?
2. Which renderer shortcuts recur and should be quarantined?
3. Which human/relational properties are visually robust?
4. Which causal claims exceed one still image?
5. Does minimal temporal information improve causal recoverability?
6. Does that temporal gain survive at least one non-access semantic route?

The remaining questions are now **design questions**, not renderer-calibration questions.

Examples:

- What should Kymaean's actual interface hierarchy feel like?
- How should the app expose context, memory, causality and bounded perspective?
- What visual system best serves ARM64-native Windows use while remaining distinctive and efficient?
- How should the website translate product truth into brand experience?
- What symbol, typography, palette, motion and composition earn their place through use rather than metaphor?

Renderer Cartography cannot answer those by itself. They require app/website design synthesis against actual product structure.
