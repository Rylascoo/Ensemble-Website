## Working website / brand exploration thesis

The website/brand stream has a principle-level thesis strong enough to guide exploration without freezing a surface aesthetic:

> **Human Causality, Living Consequence.**
>
> Kymaean should feel like a world that notices people, remembers what they do, and is changed by what follows.

This is **not** a public tagline, application-product constitution, selected visual identity, or permission to converge. It does not choose the mark, wordmark, palette, typography, materials, website layout, or animation stack.

Use it as a visual/experience test:

- person before effect;
- cause before decoration;
- evidence before generic symbolism;
- consequence may change structure;
- history accumulates rather than resets;
- distinct identities influence one another without merging;
- mystery comes from bounded perspective;
- quiet normally precedes spectacle.

A radically different visual style that obeys these laws remains valid Kymaean website/brand exploration.

2. Attention changes composition.
3. Actions leave traces.

## What recent experiments taught us

### V0.1 Integrated Visual World

The ingredients could coexist, but surface styling collapsed into dark-gold ancient/fantasy/luxury language. The grammar was stronger than the scenery.

### V0.2 Stage Without Scenery

The Stage survived removal of arches/ruins, proving it is a design principle. Generation substituted premium sci-fi/luxury-tech scenery: monolithic light, reflective grids, particles, translucent ribbons, cosmic motifs.

### V0.3 Three Expressions

- Abstract/spatial: scale and stillness worked; genre drift toward speculative tech.
- Tactile/human: extremely important proof that Kymaean history/persistence works without futurism; needs more anticipation.
- Strange/otherworldly: recovered strangeness but entered science-fiction concept-art territory.

Emerging synthesis: **human materiality inside impossible space**.

### V0.4 Presence / History / Encounter

The triptych reintroduced literal scenery and therefore failed as a clean test, though the concepts remained valid.

The latest valid pre-handoff single-image Presence experiment was closer. It used a quiet monumental room and one figure, but the key causal idea was still missing: it looked like a person inside an extraordinary place rather than the place becoming extraordinary **because that person was present**.

It also retained monumentality, polished stone, gold tracery, and prestige-tech/fantasy associations.

### V0.4.1 Generic-execution / genre-attractor failure

A later generic execution brief embedded alongside the intended website persona strongly biased image generation toward integrated marketing boards. Repeated attempts produced the same attractor even when asked for a single artwork:

- dark charcoal + antique gold;
- luxury serif typography;
- portal/aperture/threshold imagery;
- monumental architecture with a tiny human figure;
- orbital/celestial diagrams;
- UI panels, logos, navigation, and design-system collateral;
- premium fantasy / speculative-technology spectacle.

Those outputs are **invalid as V0.4 Presence results**. They should not be treated as candidates or evidence of convergence.

The useful discovery is methodological: preserve the full expert website persona, but isolate focused image-generation experiments from the brand/marketing/technology language that causes the generator to reconstruct collateral.

After generation, evaluate the artwork against the website/brand thesis and visual laws. Do not ask the generator to package the thesis into the picture.

### Creative problem

Create an almost ordinary, tactile, human-scale space whose physical composition changes subtly and impossibly **because one person is present**.

The causal dependence must be visually legible without explanatory text.

### Desired qualities

- human-scale rather than monumental;
- tactile, believable material;
- quiet and restrained;
- emotionally charged without spectacle;
- one impossible spatial/material response localized to the person's presence;
- the response should feel inherent to the world's physical behavior rather than an effect added on top;
- no requirement to use gold;
- no UI, logos, labels, or presentation framing.

### Avoid

- arches, portals, doorways as the central metaphor;
- ruins and archaeological props;
- ancient masonry as shorthand for history;
- giant monoliths or ceremonial chambers;
- science-fiction grids/holograms;
- cosmic scenery, stars, planets, occult diagrams;
- energy ribbons, particle fields, glowing network lines;
- ornamental gold tracery;
- generic cracks/kintsugi/plant-growth metaphors;
- obvious game/fantasy concept-art composition.

### Useful conceptual examples — do not copy literally

The impossible response might involve:
- shadows retaining a prior orientation near the person;
- a surface exposing earlier layers only within the person's sphere of presence;
- material seams shifting around the person's occupancy;
- ordinary geometry presenting two simultaneously plausible spatial readings around them;
- evidence of prior states becoming temporarily physically present because they are there.

These are examples of causal laws, not a mandatory motif list.
