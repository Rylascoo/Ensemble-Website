## Purpose

This audit answers one bounded question after PAC-01A through PAC-01E:

> Which Kymaean system laws are actually supported as static-artwork responsibilities, which are only partially supported or unresolved in a still image, and which have no empirical basis yet for reassignment to motion or interaction?

It explicitly corrects premature Director-chat speculation that treated a static hero, motion hierarchy, interactive revelation, or an M1/M2/M3 sequence as approved direction. Those ideas are not project authority.

## Classification law

Three evidence classes are used:

- **SUPPORTED FOR STATIC** — repeated still-image evidence supports treating the law as directly or defensibly observable in static artwork.
- **PARTIAL / STATIC BOUNDARY** — still images can carry part of the law, but the full claim was not robustly recoverable under current controls.
- **UNRESOLVED / OTHER-MEDIUM HYPOTHESIS ONLY** — current evidence does not justify assigning the law to static artwork, motion, interaction, or any other medium yet.

No category is an aesthetic selection or website-composition decision.

## Evidence allocation

### 1. Distinct people are structurally important

**Classification: SUPPORTED FOR STATIC**

Evidence:

- RI/AL established human structural importance as a transferable law.
- CFM-01A/B/C all kept people structurally primary across materially different formal grammars.
- SOV/RST and PAC repeatedly preserved human specificity and distributed importance.

Permissible claim:

> Static artwork can make distinct people structurally and semantically primary.

Not implied:

- a required crowd size;
- a selected portrait style;
- a selected hero composition.

### 2. Relationships can structure the visual field

**Classification: SUPPORTED FOR STATIC**

Evidence:

- AL demonstrated distance, orientation, overlap, negative space, interruption, occlusion and recurrence as relational geometry.
- CFM preserved relational action across flat mass, contour/void and shallow-overlap grammars.
- SOV/RST repeatedly made relational state, consequence and asymmetry visible.

Permissible claim:

> Static artwork can carry relationship through spatial and formal organization rather than explicit narrative explanation.

### 3. Identity can persist across transformed representations

**Classification: SUPPORTED FOR STATIC, WITH SCOPE LIMIT**

Evidence:

- AL-H01 demonstrated recurring individuals remaining recognizable across scale, crop, orientation, pose, completeness and viewpoint through perceptual invariants.

Scope limit:

This is evidence that one static composition can contain multiple transformed representations of recurring people. It does not establish a required Kymaean portrait system or prove cross-session identity continuity.

### 4. Visibility / evidentiary position can be asymmetric

**Classification: SUPPORTED FOR STATIC**

Evidence:

- RI-007 and later work used partial visibility and occlusion to create unequal access to one authoritative reality.
- Semantic Observability explicitly corrected `unequal understanding` into the more defensible `asymmetric evidentiary position`.

Permissible claim:

> Static artwork can show unequal visual access or relational position.

Not permissible:

> Static artwork proves private knowledge, belief, intention, memory or interpretation.

### 5. Independent agency can remain visible within a shared condition

**Classification: SUPPORTED FOR STATIC**

Evidence:

- RST-03A/B and later RST/PAC experiments repeatedly preserved self-directed people once direct physical guidance/control was removed.
- PAC-01A through PAC-01E consistently maintained independent agency while introducing persistent carriers.

Permissible claim:

> Static artwork can show several people acting independently within one shared relational or material condition.

### 6. Present arrangements can contain evidence of prior state or history

**Classification: SUPPORTED FOR STATIC, CONDITIONALLY**

Evidence:

- RI established exception-against-pattern and interrupted correspondence as present-state mechanisms for recovering prior organization or continuity.
- RST-04B/C showed persistent relational consequence and human history can be suggested without showing the original event.

Condition:

The image needs an internal comparison baseline, persistent trace, exception, correspondence or similarly observable evidence. Generic atmosphere or posture alone is insufficient.

Permissible claim:

> A static present condition can make some prior state recoverable when the image contains its own evidence.

### 7. Specific prior human authorship can be recovered

**Classification: PARTIAL / STATIC BOUNDARY**

Evidence:

- SOV-04 improved visible authorship when the acting person was physically continuous with the changed configuration.
- Direct physical causality repeatedly drifted toward control/help/conflict semantics.
- RST-04B/C could suggest human-authored conditions while specific authorship remained unresolved.
- PAC did not solve this through persistent ordinary carriers.

Current conclusion:

> Static artwork can sometimes make authorship stronger, but specific prior human authorship is not robustly recoverable under the current non-hierarchical/non-literal controls.

Do not infer that motion is therefore proven to solve authorship. That remains untested.

### 8. Human choices can create persistent changed conditions

**Classification: PARTIAL / STATIC BOUNDARY**

Evidence:

- RI and Round 1 showed present altered organization, affordance and environmental state can imply prior intervention.
- RST could imply human history and persistent consequence.
- PAC showed that concrete persistent carriers improve present-affordance readability but still do not robustly prove that people created/configured the condition rather than merely use it.

Current conclusion:

> Persistence and changed condition are observable; definite prior human creation/configuration remains fragile.

### 9. Human choices can expand later people's possibilities

**Classification: PARTIAL / STATIC BOUNDARY**

Evidence:

- Round 1 made changed possibility highly legible when literal affordance/access mechanisms were allowed.
- Those mechanisms repeatedly collapsed into bridge/threshold/access/repair shorthand.
- RST-05A/B failed to recover enabled possibility from human-only abstraction.
- PAC improved present affordance but did not robustly prove configuration-dependent enabled capability.

Current conclusion:

> Static artwork can show present affordance and sometimes changed possibility, but the full chain `prior human choice -> persistent condition -> newly enabled independent action` remains unproven without literalization or narrative explanation.

### 10. One shared multi-element condition can be the necessary common cause of several independent capabilities

**Classification: PARTIAL / STATIC BOUNDARY**

Evidence:

- PAC-01C showed local relational affordance.
- PAC-01D showed one shared arrangement but collapsed into a dominant hub object.
- PAC-01E reduced hub dominance but remained explainable through familiar local object affordances.

Current conclusion:

> The tested still-image regime did not robustly prove distributed configuration dependence or no-single-element sufficiency.

### 11. Private knowledge, belief, memory, intention or understanding

**Classification: UNRESOLVED / NOT A STATIC CLAIM**

Evidence:

- CFM and Semantic Observability repeatedly establish that posture/expression cannot independently prove private mental state.

Current conclusion:

The project may internally model these states, but static artwork should not be scored as though it can prove them directly.

This audit does not assign them to motion or interaction; that would require separate evidence.

### 12. Motion or interaction as the solution to authorship / enabled possibility

**Classification: UNRESOLVED / OTHER-MEDIUM HYPOTHESIS ONLY**

Evidence:

- PAC Synthesis explicitly says sequence, motion or interaction **may** carry before/after, authorship, propagation and enabled possibility.
- No current R0 experiment has empirically tested motion or interaction under Renderer Cartography controls.

Therefore:

> Motion and interaction are candidate research media, not validated semantic carriers.

Premature claims about subtle motion, interactive revelation, homepage hero behavior or website state transitions are withdrawn from authority.

## Consolidated static-artwork contract — evidence only

The evidence currently supports static artwork carrying:

- distinct human presence;
- structural human primacy;
- relational geometry;
- transformed-but-recognizable identity when deliberately tested;
- asymmetric evidentiary position;
- independent agency;
- present consequence;
- recoverable prior state/history when an internal visual baseline exists;
- ordinary persistent affordance.

The evidence does **not** justify requiring one static artwork to prove all of:

- specific prior authorship;
- private mental state;
- distributed common-cause capability;
- full enabled-possibility chain;
- the entire internal Kymaean thesis.

This is a measurement boundary, not a hero-image specification.

## Recursive contradiction audit

### Potential contradiction: RI says present state can prove prior action, while PAC says prior authorship is unproven

Resolved:

RI demonstrates recoverable prior state/action under explicit visible mechanisms such as pattern/exception, interrupted continuity or changed affordance. PAC demonstrates that when the project also requires ordinary, non-symbolic, non-hierarchical, people-primary distributed capability, specific human authorship becomes fragile. These are different evidentiary burdens, not contradictory findings.

### Potential contradiction: Round 1 says consequence changes possibility, while PAC says enabled possibility is unproven

Resolved:

Round 1 often achieved changed possibility through highly literal affordance/access mechanisms, which failed cross-surface robustness and surface-quarantine criteria. PAC tested whether the same semantic law could remain legible with ordinary subordinate carriers and preserved agency. It did not robustly do so. The project law remains conceptually important; static visual proof remains incomplete.

### Potential contradiction: PAC Synthesis suggests cross-medium allocation, while this audit refuses to assign laws to motion/interaction

Resolved:

PAC Synthesis correctly identifies cross-medium allocation as the next **research question**. It does not provide motion/interaction evidence. This audit preserves that distinction.

## Smallest unresolved question after this audit

The evidence does not justify another ordinary-object PAC render and does not justify designing website motion or interaction yet.

The smallest unresolved research question is:

> **Does adding minimal temporal evidence materially improve recoverability of prior human authorship and enabled possibility without forcing literal mechanism, hierarchy or narrative explanation?**

That is a research question only.

Before any generation or implementation, the Director should decide whether it is worth designing one tightly controlled temporal-observability experiment. No packet is authorized by this audit.
