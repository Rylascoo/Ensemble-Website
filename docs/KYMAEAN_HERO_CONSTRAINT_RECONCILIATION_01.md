## Root-cause interpretation

The two qualifying mechanisms differ materially, but both express altered possibility through a form of **separation / access mediation**:

- Round 01: rigid spatial planes and viewpoint-dependent availability;
- Round 02: flexible band and direct reach/contact.

In both, the image asks a viewer to infer a counterfactual: what would be impossible if the material condition were restored.

Round 02 makes the changed state visible, but the counterfactual remains too weak because the unchanged geometry would still permit the depicted contact.

The smallest evidence-supported design problem is therefore not `causality cannot be shown`. It is:

> **The tested mechanisms did not make the changed condition physically necessary to the depicted present possibility.**

This distinction prevents premature contract revision.

## Reconciliation options

### Option A — Preserve all frozen requirements; change the hero-art mechanism class

Treat the stop as a method/search-space finding rather than a contract defect.

A future separately authorized experiment would need a changed condition whose removal makes the depicted possibility visibly impossible by geometry or material fact, without relying on private state or explanatory graphics.

The next candidate mechanism must not be a correction variant of Round 02 and should avoid another simple divider/opening/reach construction unless its counterfactual necessity is visibly undeniable.

Any future candidate must also deliberately provide a left-region tonal condition compatible with the already-frozen Harness 01 identity/copy system; this is an integration construction constraint, not a reason to modify the harness after seeing the candidate.

**Advantages:**

- preserves ODR-33 and Hero Brief 02 intent;
- respects evidence that several formerly failing requirements were solved in Round 02;
- tests whether the blocker is mechanism selection rather than the contract;
- avoids changing thresholds after adverse evidence.

**Risks:**

- another static mechanism may still fail the same counterfactual burden;
- designing for visibly necessary causality can drift toward diagrammatic or overly literal illustration;
- a new experiment requires explicit Director authorization because the frozen stop has been entered.

### Option B — Clarify, but do not lower, the Altered Possibility / Removal Test interpretation

Prepare a Director-authored clarification specifying exactly what visual evidence qualifies as a materially altered present possibility and how counterfactual necessity is judged in a static still.

This option is valid only if the clarification preserves the existing >=4 burdens and does not convert `materially changes present possibility` into mere thematic association or proximity.

**Advantages:**

- can reduce evaluator/packet ambiguity;
- may help distinguish `visible consequence` from `proof of prior event` without lowering the standard.

**Risks:**

- any post-result clarification can accidentally become a disguised relaxation;
- Candidate Specification 02 already contained a precise failure condition, so the Round 02 failure itself does not appear to result from unclear scoring language.

### Option C — Revise one frozen requirement

The Director may explicitly revise or retire one specific implicated requirement, as the frozen stop law allows.

Potentially implicated requirements are Altered Possibility and Removal Test. No evidence supports revising Surface Escape, Human Necessity, S1-S5, Brand Integration or Mobile Survival merely because Round 02 failed.

**Advantages:**

- reduces the static image's causal proof burden if the Director concludes it exceeds the intended hero job.

**Risks:**

- changes a core part of Hero Brief 02 / ODR-33's `altered present possibility` thesis;
- would be a substantive product/design-law decision, not an experimental adjustment;
- evidence does not yet establish that the requirement itself, rather than the two tested mechanisms, is the blocker.

### Option D — Preserve all requirements and pause static hero rendering

Stop hero-image experimentation and continue other authorized website/brand work until a different medium, composition system or later product truth justifies reopening the hero-art problem.

**Advantages:** avoids spending another qualifying round without a stronger mechanism theory.

**Risks:** leaves the Website V0 hero unresolved.

## Recommendation

**Recommend Option A: preserve every frozen requirement and authorize design of one genuinely new mechanism class — design/review only, not rendering yet.**

Rationale:

1. Round 02 demonstrates substantial convergence: it solved Human Necessity, Changed-condition legibility and S1 while preserving Surface Escape, Epistemic restraint and S2-S5.
2. The repeated failures are narrow and concrete: causal necessity / counterfactual collapse.
3. The Round 02 failure follows the pre-registered test exactly; there is no evidence that scoring ambiguity manufactured the failure.
4. Two mechanisms are insufficient evidence to amend a core hero requirement when the experiment itself has already shown major progress under the unchanged contract.
5. The correct next intelligence task is to identify a mechanism where the changed condition and altered possibility are the same visible physical fact, rather than a change merely adjacent to a human relation.

This recommendation does **not** pre-authorize Candidate 03 or a renderer request. It authorizes nothing by itself.
