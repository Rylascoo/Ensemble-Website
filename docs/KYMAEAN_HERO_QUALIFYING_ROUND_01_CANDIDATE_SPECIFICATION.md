## Candidate question

Can one static hero candidate make necessary people, a visibly changed shared condition and an altered present possibility legible inside a restrained shallow material field—without ordinary-task staging, private-state claims, explanatory graphics or one spectacular effect—and survive the fixed Hero Integration Harness 01?

## Participant structure

Three adults.

All three are structurally necessary to the proposition:

- Person A establishes one side of the changed availability relation.
- Person B is the person whose physical availability changes because of the shared condition.
- Person C establishes the contrasting availability relation inside the same authoritative shared field.

The image must not rely on color, face likeness, labels or exact position alone for identity. Each person should remain distinguishable through a redundant combination of body proportion, silhouette, hair geometry, garment construction and posture/gesture.

No biography, profession, motive, belief, memory, knowledge or intent is specified.

## Changed shared condition

The shared reality is a shallow, continuous material field organized through broad interlocking planes.

Across the center-right, one broad layer is visibly displaced from an otherwise coherent alignment. The change is distributed across the field rather than carried by one object, prop, gadget or symbolic mechanism.

The displacement must read as a materially changed condition, not arbitrary decorative asymmetry.

Exact prior authorship is not required.

## Altered present possibility

The required observable coupling is:

`displaced shared layer -> different physical visual availability between depicted people`

From Person A's position, the displaced field leaves an unobstructed line to Person B's figure. From Person C's position, the same shared layer still materially occludes Person B.

This establishes different present physical availability inside one shared reality. It does not assert that A or C actually observes, understands, knows or interprets anything.

If the returned image does not make the changed field materially responsible for that difference, Altered Possibility has failed even if the composition is attractive.

## Relational composition

The people and the changed field must generate the composition together.

Required relationship logic:

- Person A and Person B are coupled across the displaced region;
- Person C is materially separated by the same field;
- the proposition collapses if the people become generic or are removed;
- no connector, arrow, caption, panel, diagram or single clue prop explains the relation.

The left side of the image remains low-information but must still participate in balance and tension; it is not decorative blank padding.
