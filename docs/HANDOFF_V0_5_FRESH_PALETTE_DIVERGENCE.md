## Why V0.5 exists

The previous visual-development chat produced useful discoveries, but the accumulated conversation developed strong **contextual visual inertia**. Even deliberate requests for divergence were repeatedly pulled back toward related palettes, compositions, material families, protagonists, spatial structures, and presentation habits.

The correction is methodological rather than aesthetic:

> **Continuity of meaning without continuity of appearance.**

V0.5 therefore begins with a clean visual palette. Prior surface vocabulary receives **no inheritance rights** merely because it was attractive, recent, repeated, expensive to discover, or heavily discussed.

## Fresh-Palette Isolation Protocol

For genuinely new visual-world exploration:

1. Begin the divergence round in a fresh project chat.
2. Carry forward product truth, creative laws, collaboration discipline, and abstract discoveries only.
3. Do **not** reconstruct previous imagery from chat history, screenshots, old prompts, generated boards, or verbal descriptions unless the user explicitly asks to revisit a prior branch.
4. Do not proactively inspect earlier generated visual assets before the first fresh-palette experiment. Their existence is preserved, but they are not automatic inspiration.
5. Do not transfer previous palette, materials, architecture, lighting, protagonist, composition, medium, cultural references, ornament, scenery, or typography unless one has explicitly been promoted into durable project law.
6. Prefer a **positive artistic premise** for the new world. Do not begin with a long blacklist of previous motifs, because exclusions can keep the old vocabulary semantically active.
7. Generate artwork as artwork. Do not make a presentation board, taxonomy, style guide, moodboard, website hero, logo sheet, comparison board, or explanatory diagram unless specifically requested.
8. Do not place explanatory design-thesis text inside exploratory artwork unless typography itself is the experiment.
9. Praise does not authorize refinement. Exploration remains broad until the user and assistant explicitly agree to converge.
10. Once a fresh chat begins repeating its own visual grammar, stop refining, extract transferable discoveries, create a new handoff, and start another fresh-palette chat rather than fighting the attractor indefinitely.

## Durable across fresh-palette handoffs

Carry these forward as abstract visual/experience laws rather than motifs:

- **Presence has weight.** Human presence can matter to the state or meaning of a place.
- **Actions leave evidence.** A consequential action should be capable of leaving a trace.
- **Influence can persist.** What happens can continue affecting later conditions after the originating moment has passed.
- **History changes possibility.** Prior states should be capable of altering what can happen next rather than existing only as nostalgia or playback.
- **Distinct identities can influence one another without merging.** Relationship is not sameness.
- **The world need not be neutral.** Human action, attention, relationship, and history may affect composition or state.
- **Human habitation matters.** Lived-in specificity, imperfection, use, and relational evidence can carry more meaning than sterile spectacle.
- **Ensemble activity matters.** Multiple people acting, responding, creating, disagreeing, observing, or affecting one another can be more truthful to the product than a repeated solitary observer.
- **Consequence can be additive.** Change need not look like damage; later states may incorporate earlier ones and create new affordances.
- **What one person changes can alter another person's circumstances.** This is a strong visual translation of Ensemble's causal premise.
- **Artistic integrity precedes explanation.** The image should create an experience before it explains a concept.

These laws may be expressed through entirely different aesthetics in future chats.

## Evaluation discipline

After a fresh-palette artwork is generated, evaluate it internally against the same stable dimensions:

- **Emotional magnetism:** do we actually want to enter or remain with this world?
- **Distinctiveness:** could the image easily belong to another game, film, AI product, luxury brand, or generic concept-art universe?
- **Human importance:** are people consequential, relational, or meaning-bearing rather than decorative scale markers?
- **Kymaean causality:** does something change because something happened?
- **Historical specificity:** does what came before matter to what exists now or what can happen next?
- **Mystery:** is something compellingly incomplete without being arbitrarily obscure?
- **Artistic integrity:** is it art first rather than an illustration of the design brief?
- **Expandability:** could its underlying language eventually support a website, motion, typography, and broader identity system without forcing all surfaces to look identical?
- **Attractor risk:** is the work repeating a familiar visual solution because of contextual inertia rather than because the concept earned it?
- **Surprise:** did the experiment reveal something we had not already articulated?

Do not collapse this into a numeric score. Preserve observations and artistic judgment.

## Round structure

A fresh-palette chat should not immediately attempt a giant cross-project synthesis.

Recommended rhythm:

1. Establish one positive artistic premise that is not derived from reconstructing prior imagery.
2. Generate one full-canvas artwork.
3. Evaluate what is genuinely new in its visual physics, emotional effect, social structure, or medium.
4. Continue within that chat only while the branch is still producing new discoveries.
5. When repetition begins, extract abstract discoveries and stop.
6. Start another fresh chat for a genuinely different palette/world.
7. Compare independent worlds only after several fresh-palette branches exist.
8. Converge only when a direction is emotionally exceptional, unusually specific to Kymaean, and generative enough to become a system rather than one attractive image.
