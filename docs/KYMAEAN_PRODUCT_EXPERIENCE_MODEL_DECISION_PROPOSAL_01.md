# 1. Purpose

Before selecting visual identity, interface language, or website storytelling structure, define the experience model Kymaean should communicate.

The question is not:

> What should Kymaean look like?

The question is:

> What fundamental relationship does a person have with a living creative system?

The answer must remain compatible with current product truth and open engineering ontology.

---

# 2. Authority boundaries

VALIDATED PRODUCT TRUTH:

- Ensemble/Kymaean is built around persistent creative entities, performance, consequence, and continuity.
- Creator authority is primary.
- Causal integrity matters.
- Production truth, audience disclosure, and character access are distinct concepts.

APPROVED BUT UNIMPLEMENTED:

- Atomic causal commit architecture.
- Future expanded creator workflows.
- Future Windows-native application realization.

OPEN:

- Studio terminology.
- Take/branch/rehearsal terminology.
- Character Context disclosure experience.
- Final creator-facing workflow model.

No proposal below converts open areas into frozen product decisions.

---

# 3. Core experience hypothesis

Working hypothesis:

Kymaean is not primarily a content generator.

It is a system where:

Creation establishes possibility.
Performance explores possibility.
Consequence preserves change.
Continuity allows identity and history to persist.

This is a structural model, not a screen hierarchy.

---

# 4. Candidate experience relationships

## Model A — Studio First

Primary relationship:

Creator shapes a world.

Strengths:

- aligns with creator authority;
- naturally supports setup, constraints, and intentional design;
- easier to communicate ownership.

Risks:

- may make living characters feel like tools;
- may overemphasize configuration.

---

## Model B — Stage First

Primary relationship:

Creator witnesses and guides unfolding performance.

Strengths:

- emphasizes emergence and consequence;
- aligns with human primacy and relationship structure.

Risks:

- may hide creator authority;
- may imply autonomous behavior beyond product truth.

---

## Model C — Archive First

Primary relationship:

Creator understands what happened and why it matters.

Strengths:

- strongly supports continuity;
- naturally communicates history and provenance.

Risks:

- may feel retrospective rather than creative;
- may over-index on inspection.

---

## Model D — Continuous Loop

Primary relationship:

Creation, performance, consequence, and continuity are recurring states.

Strengths:

- most closely reflects the product thesis;
- avoids reducing Kymaean to one metaphor.

Risks:

- requires careful information architecture;
- must avoid exposing internal mechanics.

---

# 5. Preliminary direction for evaluation

The strongest current candidate is not a single environment.

A continuous relationship model appears most compatible:

Creator
↓
Create possibility
↓
Experience performance
↓
Understand consequence
↓
Shape future possibility

However:

This remains a proposal for evaluation, not a frozen navigation model.

---

# 6. Design implications

If the continuous model survives review:

APP:

Should reveal changing states without forcing users into engineering concepts.

WEBSITE:

Should explain Kymaean through human experience rather than feature inventory.

VISUAL SYSTEM:

Should represent relationships, continuity, and transformation without requiring dashboards, fantasy tropes, or fixed metaphors.

---

# 7. Validation questions

Before visual commitment:

1. Can a new user understand what they do in Kymaean?
2. Can a creator understand where authority exists?
3. Can observers understand what is current versus historical?
4. Can character identity remain meaningful without claiming private mental access?
5. Can the model survive future ontology changes?
