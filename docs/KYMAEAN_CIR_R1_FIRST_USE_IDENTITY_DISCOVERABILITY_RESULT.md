## 1. Formal result

> **CIR-R1 — FIRST-USE IDENTITY DISCOVERABILITY PASS / IDENTITY-SEMANTIC CONTAMINATION FAIL**

The experiment proves the narrow first-use question positively: Riven, Wren and Vale can be associated directly with their living Stage representations without restoring a permanent duplicate portrait roster.

However, the renderer contaminated identity with invented personality/professional summaries and retained several surface/state failures. Therefore the rendered label system receives no inheritance rights.

## 2. Strong passes

### First-use identity discoverability

A first-time viewer can determine:

```text
left = Riven
center = Wren
right = Vale
```

without requiring:

- a permanent portrait roster;
- prior familiarity with the cast;
- A/B/C alias translation;
- color-only identity;
- face recognition as the sole channel.

Promoted finding:

> **Direct identity association can replace duplicate roster identity.**

### Perspective / Posture integrity

`Perspective — Wren` resolves to the same Wren identity presented in the living field while `Watch` remains separately expressed.

The experiment therefore preserves:

```text
Character = Wren
Presentation Perspective = Wren
Creator Posture = Watch
```

without `(You)`, creator embodiment, Perform implication or account/profile ownership.

### Human primacy

People and relational presence remain the first read. Identity labels do not require a separate participant rail.

## 3. Material failures

### Identity-semantic contamination — FAIL

The renderer added:

- Riven — `Strategic / Systems Thinker`;
- Wren — `Analytical / Bridges Ideas`;
- Vale — `People Focused / Turns Insight Into Action`.

These statements were neither required for identity nor supplied by authoritative Character state. They read as persistent personality/professional archetypes and therefore collapse identity into characterization/psychology.

Required correction:

> **Identity recovery should reveal who the person is, not summarize what kind of person the system thinks they are.**

Identity presentation must stop at authoritative creator-facing identity unless a separate future task explicitly authorizes additional Character material.

### Current Opportunity alias regression — FAIL

The rendered Opportunity treatment still foregrounds `C` while the same Stage establishes the human-readable identity `Vale`.

CIR already requires:

> **Opportunity may point to a Character; it must not reduce the Character to a turn token.**

Future Opportunity presentation must resolve directly or programmatically to the human-readable Character identity without requiring `C -> Vale` translation.

### Adaptive explicitness — PARTIAL / NOT PROVEN

The experiment proves the deliberately explicit first-use state, but not the later reduced state.

It does not establish that all three visible name treatments should persist forever.

Required distinction:

```text
valid first-use identity treatment
!=
validated permanent Stage treatment
```

### Connector dots / leader lines — QUARANTINE

The identity connectors may help first-use spatial association, but state-looking dots can plausibly imply selection, activity, observation, availability or Opportunity.

Any future association treatment must have one defined meaning and must not resemble an undocumented fictional/application state.

### Stage marketing narration — FAIL

`A shared perspective moves what's possible.` is product-thesis copy, not current Production information.

Promoted Stage rule:

> **Active Stage surfaces contain no product-thesis or marketing narration by default; such explanation belongs only to deliberately invoked onboarding/help or other justified application-level contexts.**

### Watch eye icon — CAUTION

The eye symbol is not selected. It risks semantic collision between Creator Posture `Watch` and fictional Character Observation authority. Patch 0014's `visible != observed` boundary remains binding.

### Expressive staging / Opportunity coupling — PARTIAL

Vale is simultaneously strongly lit, gesturing and Current Opportunity. This may imply speaking, leadership, intent or obligation. Current Opportunity must remain legible without requiring the Character portrayal itself to perform Opportunity.

## 6. Primary conclusion

CIR-R1 proves the narrow visual proposition:

> **A no-roster live Stage can make all current Characters directly identifiable in the living field on first use.**

But it also proves that identity UI has a dangerous attractor toward personality profiling and permanent label clutter.

The smallest unresolved question is therefore temporal rather than merely spatial:

> **How should identity explicitness increase when ambiguity is high and recede when identity is established, without ever reducing recoverability or confusing identity with state?**

Proceed to a non-render adaptive-explicitness contract rather than another general CIR Stage render.
