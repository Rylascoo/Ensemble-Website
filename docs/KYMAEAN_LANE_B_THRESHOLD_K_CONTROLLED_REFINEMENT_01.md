# KYMAEAN LANE B — THRESHOLD K CONTROLLED REFINEMENT 01

Status: CONTROLLED REFINEMENT COMPLETE / ORIGINAL CANDIDATE RETAINED AS WORKING CONTROL  
Date: 2026-09-05  
Workstream: SHARED BRAND / I0 PRIMARY SYMBOL

## Purpose

This round corrects the overreach in `KYMAEAN_LANE_B_THRESHOLD_K_WORDMARK_INTEGRATION_01.md`.

The previous study changed several properties of the historical Threshold K at once and then recommended M2 Open Threshold + W2 Threshold Echo too confidently. That recommendation is withdrawn.

This round isolates the mark before returning to wordmark work.

## Historical control

The recovered Threshold K remains the control:

- three surrounding fields;
- the `K` exists only in the negative space;
- the letter itself is not drawn;
- the right field carries the dominant curved mass;
- the two left fields provide quieter counterweight;
- historical project evidence recorded strong small-scale ownership and cautioned against forcing the K.

Working law:

**The K should be discovered in the void, not drawn by the pieces.**

The raster trace in the executable study is evidence derived from the historical candidate image. It is not proposed production vector geometry.

## Controlled variants

Each candidate changes exactly one property relative to the same recovered trace.

### S — Open the void

Change:
- spacing only;
- left fields translate left;
- right field translates right.

Observed design effect:
- the K remains recoverable;
- the mark loses some compact tension and begins to feel like three arranged pieces;
- no compensating improvement is evident.

Result: **do not advance**.

### C — Gentler waist

Change:
- only the inner waist of the right field becomes less concave.

Observed design effect:
- the right field becomes more bracket-like;
- the negative-space K feels more deliberately manufactured;
- some of the original strangeness is lost.

Result: **do not advance**.

### M — Reduce left mass

Change:
- left-field horizontal mass reduces about the existing inner K boundary.

Observed design effect:
- the void itself remains largely stable;
- the left/right counterweight weakens;
- the right field becomes too dominant.

Result: **do not advance by default**.

### μ — Simplify contour

Change:
- contour complexity only;
- placement, spacing and intended mass remain unchanged.

Observed design effect:
- display identity remains close to the control;
- this may be useful later as a production-size simplification strategy.

Result: **retain only as an implementation hypothesis**, not a new visual direction.

## Micro probes

The executable study includes 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 px browser-raster probes.

These are comparative static evidence only. They do not establish:
- production app-icon rendering;
- Windows shell/icon packaging behavior;
- device pixel behavior;
- accessibility runtime behavior.

No claim that any candidate “survives 12 px” is made by this study.

## Decision

**No modified mark earns promotion.**

The recovered historical Threshold K remains the temporary working symbol candidate/control.

This is a positive result: the original candidate's balance appears more specific than the earlier redesign assumed. Future mark work should begin from the recovered structure rather than regularizing it.

## Wordmark consequence

The previous M2 + W2 recommendation is withdrawn.

The broader role hypothesis remains worth testing:

`Threshold K = compact symbolic event`

`KYMÆAN = readable name`

But the wordmark must now be evaluated beside the **historical Threshold K control**, not beside M2.

Next valid step:
1. use the recovered Threshold K unchanged as a temporary scaffold;
2. test its proportion and spacing in real app-titlebar, app-icon, website-header and website-hero lockup contexts;
3. only then construct a bespoke KYMÆAN wordmark around the contextual evidence.
