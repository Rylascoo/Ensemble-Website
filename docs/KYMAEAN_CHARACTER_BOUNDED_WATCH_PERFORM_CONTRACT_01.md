# KYMAEAN CHARACTER-BOUNDED WATCH / PERFORM CONTRACT 01

Status: DIRECTOR PROPOSAL FOR REVIEW / SEMANTIC CORRECTION AFTER VSG-B RENDER EVIDENCE / NO PRODUCTION IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORIZED

Phase: Kymaean App + Website Design Synthesis — relational-field bounded-perspective correction

## 0. Why this contract exists

Recent VSG-B bounded-perspective rendering exposed a contradiction:

```text
POSTURE: WATCH
+
CREATOR ACTS THROUGH CHARACTER B
```

That combination collapses two orthogonal dimensions which the accepted experience architecture keeps separate:

- **Presentation Perspective** controls disclosure over one authoritative Production;
- **Creator Posture** controls how the creator relates to and may act within that Production.

The accepted ontology states:

- **Watch** — experience the cast as an ensemble with minimal visible machinery;
- **Perform** — temporarily occupy a Character's bounded perspective using the same information-authority boundary as an AI Performer.

Therefore:

> **Character-bounded Watch and Character-bounded Perform may share the same disclosure boundary and relational workspace, but they must not expose the same agency.**

This document defines that distinction for future visual testing. It does not freeze final labels, controls, Character Context ontology, Take UX, or implementation.

---

## 1. Orthogonal model

The minimal model is:

```text
AUTHORITATIVE PRODUCTION
        |
        +-- PRESENTATION PERSPECTIVE
        |      creator/production
        |      audience
        |      character-bounded (B)
        |
        +-- CREATOR POSTURE
               watch
               direct
               perform
               write
```

For this experiment only, compare:

```text
CHARACTER-B PERSPECTIVE + WATCH
versus
CHARACTER-B PERSPECTIVE + PERFORM
```

The Production, Scene, people, current authoritative state, and disclosure boundary remain the same unless the creator takes an authorized action that later changes them.

Changing posture does not reveal privileged information.
Changing perspective does not grant performance agency.

---

## 2. Shared invariant packet

Both states must preserve:

```text
Production P0
Scene S0
Characters A / B / C
same current authoritative Production state
same Character-B disclosure boundary
same people and relational situation
same current temporal locus
same technical capability state
```

Both must omit creator-only information unavailable to B.

Neither may infer private mental states from posture, gaze, spatial placement, or dialogue alone.

Both must remain recognizably the same living Production.

---

## 3. Character-Bounded Watch contract

### 3.1 Meaning

> **The creator witnesses the current Production through what B may perceive or legitimately access, without taking over B's performance agency.**

This is not first-person control.

The creator may understand the Scene from B's disclosure boundary while B remains portrayed by the currently assigned Performer or other approved performance mechanism.

### 3.2 Available interaction classes

Character-Bounded Watch may permit:

- inspect information legitimately available to B;
- follow currently observable people/actions;
- inspect permitted bounded history/context;
- navigate focus within the bounded projection;
- exit or change Presentation Perspective through an explicit creator-level control;
- use creator-level operational controls that do not masquerade as B's action, where the final product later permits them.

### 3.3 Must not expose as B agency

Do not show:

- `Speak as B`;
- `Act as B`;
- `Challenge the terms` as an action B will take;
- `Propose counterterms` as a B action;
- `I act through B`;
- any composer/input whose submission would portray B.

If the creator wants to portray B, the interface must cross an explicit posture boundary into Perform.

### 3.4 Visual implication

The relational workspace may become B-centered through disclosure and attention, but B remains **a Character being witnessed**, not the creator's current action channel.

A strong Watch rendering should feel:

```text
I am limited to B's side of the reality.
I can understand what B can legitimately encounter.
I am not yet speaking or acting for B.
```

---

## 4. Character-Bounded Perform contract

### 4.1 Meaning

> **The creator temporarily assumes the Performer role for B while remaining inside B's bounded disclosure authority.**

Perform changes creator agency, not Production truth or disclosure privilege.

### 4.2 Entry boundary

Entering Perform should be explicit enough that the creator understands:

- they are now portraying B;
- available information remains bounded to B;
- their actions are candidate Performance material, not automatic authoritative history;
- creator-only truth is not revealed merely because the human creator is acting.

Exact interaction wording remains open.

### 4.3 Available interaction classes

Perform may expose Character-appropriate performance affordances such as:

- speak;
- act;
- remain silent;
- defer/refuse;
- other future approved Character-performance expressions.

The exact action taxonomy is open. Renderer-invented actions such as `Challenge`, `Nudge`, `Test limits`, or `Propose counterterms` are examples only and have no inheritance rights.

### 4.4 Authority boundary

A creator-authored action while Performing B:

```text
CREATOR PERFORMANCE INPUT
        -> candidate Performance material
        -> applicable Take / authority process
        -> only then may later become effective history
```

Perform must never visually imply:

```text
creator chose action
=
authoritative Production history changed
```

Patch 0012 causal-effect semantics remain downstream authority; final creator-facing Take/rehearsal interaction remains open.

### 4.5 Exit boundary

Leaving Perform returns portrayal control according to the future approved Performer-assignment semantics without silently changing perspective, history, or authoritative state.

The exact handback control remains open.

### 4.6 Visual implication

A strong Perform rendering should feel:

```text
I am still bounded to B's reality.
Now I am responsible for B's next performance expression.
The world has not changed merely because I have taken the seat.
```

## 5. What changes visually between Watch and Perform

The design should preserve enough common structure that the user experiences one continuous workspace rather than two products.

### Stays stable

- Production identity;
- Scene identity;
- A / B / C identity;
- Character-B disclosure boundary;
- relational composition;
- observable/available evidence;
- current versus historical state;
- causal-effect status;
- accessibility semantics.

### Changes because posture changes

**Watch:**

- performance input stays absent;
- B remains observed within the field;
- creator action area remains minimal/non-portrayal;
- attention may be B-centered without becoming control.

**Perform:**

- a clearly bounded performance-input region appears;
- B becomes the creator's temporary portrayal locus;
- action availability is constrained by Character-B context;
- explicit posture state communicates that creator agency changed;
- non-effective/provisional status becomes relevant after a performance input is produced.

The transition should not recolor the entire interface or transform the Production into a separate `Character Mode` application.

---

## 6. Evidence hierarchy inside Character-bounded states

Recent renders also exposed the danger of synthesizing private psychology into convenient summaries.

Character-bounded information should distinguish, where useful and supported:

```text
OBSERVED
what B can directly perceive

HEARD / RECEIVED
what B has actually been told or otherwise received

ESTABLISHED / AVAILABLE TO B
authoritative facts legitimately available under B's disclosure contract

UNRESOLVED / UNKNOWN
what is not established or not available to B
```

These are **semantic evidence classes for testing**, not frozen UI labels or storage enums.

Do not convert evidence into private-mind assertions such as:

- `A wants ...` unless A's desire is legitimately established and available to B;
- `C believes ...` from posture or silence;
- psychological confidence meters;
- quantified relationship truth.

Absence is a valid disclosure result.

---

## 7. Relational-field implication

VSG-B render evidence supports the law:

> **Relationship as composition, not relationship as drawn connection.**

The Watch/Perform distinction must therefore survive without requiring:

- graph connectors;
- halos;
- relationship meters;
- perspective-colored global themes;
- first-person-game camera as the only Character treatment.

The durable visual mechanism may use human orientation, scale, proximity, occlusion, negative space, attention, and information hierarchy while keeping accessible textual equivalents.

Character-bounded Perform does not require literal over-the-shoulder cinematography. The requirement is relational re-centering plus bounded agency, not a camera trope.

---

## 8. Accessibility and input contract

Both Watch and Perform must expose programmatically:

- active Presentation Perspective: Character B;
- active creator posture: Watch or Perform;
- current versus historical locus;
- available actions;
- non-effective/effective status where applicable.

A screen reader or keyboard user must not have to infer posture from the presence/absence of a visual composer alone.

Entering Perform should move focus deliberately to the newly available performance region or announce its availability without destroying the user's safe return point.

Exiting Perform must restore a predictable focus location.

No essential distinction may rely on color, motion, depth, human portrait recognition, or spatial arrangement alone.

---

## 9. ARM64 / battery implication

This distinction is state-driven and should require no baseline continuous computation.

Switching Watch <-> Perform should be representable as:

```text
semantic state transition
+
localized view/command reconfiguration
```

No idle inference, continuous graph solving, persistent animation, GPU effect, or NPU activity is required merely to communicate posture.

## 10. Controlled falsification test

The next render should not compare broad visual branches.

Use one relational-field visual law and one neutral Character-B packet to create two isolated states:

### B3-W — Character-Bounded Watch

Must show:

- same P0 / S0 / A / B / C;
- B-bounded disclosure;
- relational composition;
- no B performance actions;
- no `I act through B` semantics;
- creator may exit perspective or inspect permitted context;
- private/creator-only information absent.

Test question:

> **Can Character-Bounded Watch feel rich and relational without granting the creator B's agency?**

### B3-P — Character-Bounded Perform

Must show:

- the exact same P0 / S0 / A / B / C and disclosure boundary;
- explicit posture change to Perform;
- bounded performance-input affordance;
- candidate/non-effective status after creator input where needed;
- no creator-only information leak;
- no implication that performance input automatically changes history.

Test question:

> **Can the creator clearly feel that agency changed while reality and disclosure did not?**

Compare the two only after both original unrefined outputs exist.

## 11. Recursive audit

Audit sequence:

`product truth -> posture definitions -> perspective orthogonality -> disclosure authority -> Watch agency -> Perform agency -> candidate Performance authority -> Take/effect separation -> relational-field continuity -> private-mind inference -> accessibility -> input/focus semantics -> ARM64 quiescence -> renderer test validity -> scope`

Material corrections made during construction:

1. removed the assumption that Character perspective automatically implies Perform;
2. removed the assumption that Perform grants additional disclosure privilege;
3. separated creator-level perspective controls from Character performance actions;
4. prevented Watch from exposing B-action controls;
5. prevented Perform inputs from implying immediate authoritative history;
6. kept final Take/rehearsal terminology open;
7. treated `Observed / Heard / Established / Unknown` as testing evidence classes rather than frozen ontology;
8. rejected inferred private psychology from visual/social evidence;
9. rejected first-person cinematography as a mandatory Character-perspective implementation;
10. required Watch and Perform to preserve the same relational workspace and disclosure boundary;
11. added explicit accessibility semantics for both posture and perspective;
12. kept the transition state-driven and computationally quiescent.

After each material correction, the audit restarted from product truth.

Final complete pass:

> **PASS — no remaining material contradiction, authority leak, posture/perspective conflation, scope violation, or worthwhile improvement within this semantic-contract scope.**
