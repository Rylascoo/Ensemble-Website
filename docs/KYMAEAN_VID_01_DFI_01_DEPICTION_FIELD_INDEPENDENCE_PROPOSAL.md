## 1. Purpose

Resolve one method ambiguity exposed by V0R5 before spending additional render budget:

> **How can a controlled formal grammar be specified so the renderer changes the depiction itself rather than satisfying the instruction through one dominant physical object inside an otherwise familiar naturalistic scene?**

V0R5 demonstrated that `broad shape mass` and `action changes the field` can be literalized as a large sheet of material. That produces visual impact without proving the intended H5 performance field.

DFI-01 therefore separates:

```text
depiction-level field
from
scene-level physical carrier
```

## 2. Governing distinctions

### Depiction-level field

A depiction-level field is created by how the image is organized across people and space:

- contour behavior;
- figure-ground relationships;
- distributed overlaps;
- gaps and negative space;
- shifts of density;
- local plane changes;
- cropping/interruption;
- repeated directional structures;
- changes in visual weight attached to different people's actions.

The field remains dependent on several human actions even if no one large object connects them.

### Scene-level physical carrier

A scene-level physical carrier is an object inside the fictional world whose size, placement or movement organizes the picture:

- one dominant prop;
- one shared sheet/surface;
- one large mechanism;
- one physical connector;
- one scenic element that carries most of the composition.

Such an object may be artistically valid, but it does not by itself prove H5.

## 3. Candidate method law

> **A formal-control prompt should describe where organization must appear in the depiction, not merely what visual effects should exist somewhere in the image.**

Corollary:

> **If one physical object can satisfy most of the requested formal behavior, the control is under-specified for depiction-field testing.**

## 4. Human-dependency requirement

Any future H5 depiction-field test should require distributed dependency across at least several distinct people.

Conceptual requirement only—not an implementation enum or score:

```text
person A acts
-> one local region of the depicted field changes

person B acts
-> a different region changes

person C responds or continues
-> those changes alter the compositional situation C occupies
```

The image does not need to encode a literal causal diagram. It must simply make more than one person's action structurally necessary to the field.

## 5. Removal test

A future H5 render should fail if removing any one of several relevant human actions leaves essentially the same field organization intact.

The test is not `every person must be indispensable to every pixel`.

The intended threshold is:

> **The field should be distributed enough that the ensemble's actions, not one dominant object, explain why the composition has its current organization.**

## 6. Positive formal-control direction

If a later renderer packet is approved, use positive depiction-level language such as:

- organize figure and surrounding field as interdependent planes;
- let contour break, resume or redirect near different human actions;
- let negative space open or compress around separate actions;
- distribute repeated directional structures across several people;
- allow local shifts of density and plane shape to follow different actions;
- keep environment selective enough that these depiction-level changes remain primary.

Do not provide a historical blacklist of prior objects/settings/materials.

Do not name artists, movements, cultures or eras.

Do not require a specific palette or final medium.

## 7. Anti-literalization constraint

A later packet may include one direct constraint:

> **Do not let one shared physical object or scenic element carry most of the requested field organization.**

This is not a surface blacklist. It is a falsification constraint derived directly from V0R5.

Local objects remain allowed when they do not become the sole explanation for the field.

## 8. Independent-agency protection

DFI-01 preserves the V0R5 success:

- different people act differently;
- no synchronized collective pose;
- no generic figures;
- no assumption of shared intent;
- no private-mind claim;
- no requirement that everyone cooperate toward one goal.

A future field may contain interruption, refusal, missed timing, partial alignment or unrelated continuation. H5 does not mean harmony.

## 9. Surface-divergence requirement

A future render must still change underlying pictorial logic, not merely setting, costume or dominant prop.

At minimum, a successful next probe should materially change several of:

- spatial depth;
- figure-ground logic;
- contour behavior;
- abstraction depth;
- material/shape grammar;
- negative-space use;
- environment modeling;
- temporal/action carrier.

Passing DFI-01 does not itself select any of those choices as Kymaean identity.

## 10. Accessibility / cross-product portability boundary

This is brand-art research, not a native-app control specification.

Any transferable law must later survive:

- text/programmatic identity equivalents;
- reduced motion;
- high contrast;
- image-unavailable states;
- narrow layouts;
- static equivalents;
- no dependence on biometric recognition or hidden inference.

No WinUI implementation, animation system, Windows AI/NPU behavior or website production implementation is authorized here.

## 11. Falsification conditions

A later DFI-derived render fails if:

1. one large physical object carries most field organization;
2. bodies remain conventional naturalistic figures placed on top of a decorative abstract background;
3. graphic marks do not depend on human action;
4. several people can be removed without materially changing the field;
5. all people synchronize into one collective gesture;
6. human specificity collapses into anonymous icons;
7. the image becomes a diagram, poster, interface or explanation board;
8. the renderer simply returns another deeply modeled painterly interior with one new prop;
9. the result depends on inferred emotion or private intent to explain action-response;
10. the formal grammar is treated as selected Kymaean identity after one successful experiment.

## 12. Recommended next validation after approval

That packet should preserve H5's independent-action kernel while tightening depiction-level formal distribution and the anti-single-carrier constraint.

Render budget:

After return: archive, evaluate, recursively audit and stop.

No automatic second correction, H5 refinement chain, convergence or implementation is authorized.
