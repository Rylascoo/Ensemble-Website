Create exactly one uninterrupted landscape artwork centered on two distinct adults. Make the two people the primary visual mass of the image: large, specific, non-interchangeable human figures rather than small occupants of an environment.

They share a sparse human-scale field crossed by one broad, continuous, flexible, opaque matte material band. Across the center-right, a wide segment of that same band has already been folded back on itself along an extended crease. Its exposed underside, displaced edge and continuing alignment on either side should make the altered state visibly recoverable as a change in the otherwise continuous band. Keep this fold broad, oblique and human-scale rather than frame-shaped or deep.

The fold must visibly change what is physically possible between the people. At the folded-back segment, one person’s hand or forearm is now on the same physical side of the material as the other person’s hand or forearm, and the two make one clear point of direct human contact there. Unfolded portions of the same band remain visibly between other parts of their bodies, so the changed material state is what makes that contact possible. Treat the contact as a simple physical fact rather than an emotional or symbolic climax; posture or expression must not carry the required meaning. Neither person is manipulating the material; show only the already-existing physical condition and relation, not what either person knows, believes, remembers or intends.

Keep roughly the left 40% calm and low-information while using it as active compositional balance. Concentrate both people, the folded-back segment and the contact relation in the center-right. Keep both people visually substantial — approximately half the image height or more — and compose the cluster so both people plus the folded-condition evidence survive a tall crop of the center-right region.

Use shallow compressed depth, clear human silhouettes, selective contours, restrained modeling, low environmental detail, substantial negative space, low texture density, a restrained low-saturation tonal range, matte non-metallic material behavior and broad soft light. The material field must remain subordinate to the people rather than becoming monumental architecture or the main protagonist. Keep the changed segment continuous with the rest of the band; do not turn it into a separate spectacular object.

Do not organize the image around a recognizable ordinary domestic or occupational task, or a routine accident response. The changed condition is already complete before the depicted moment.

Return exactly one uninterrupted artwork only. No text, captions, labels, arrows, panels, storyboard, interface, branding, logo, palette display, presentation layout or explanatory surround.
