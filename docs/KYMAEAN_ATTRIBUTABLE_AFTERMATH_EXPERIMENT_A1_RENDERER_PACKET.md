# ATTRIBUTABLE AFTERMATH — EXPERIMENT A1 RENDERER PACKET

Use this packet only in one fresh regular non-project renderer conversation.

Create one uninterrupted wide artwork with exactly two specific adult people in one shared physical world.

COMPOSITION
- Wide landscape composition, approximately 16:10.
- Keep the left roughly 40% genuinely quiet and expendable negative space: sparse matte mineral architecture/floor only, with no indispensable person or causal evidence.
- Place both adults plus all indispensable evidence inside one compact cluster in the right half that can also survive a 4:5 portrait crop.
- Do not use a doorway, threshold, bridge, road, arch, portal, trench, corridor, path-as-metaphor, machine, monument, table, workstation, household room, workshop, farm, construction site, or ordinary task setting.
- The space should feel sparse, temporally non-specific, materially real, and slightly impossible without reading as fantasy scenery or a gallery installation.

TIME
The causal human act is already complete before the depicted moment.

No one is currently creating, cleaning, repairing, breaking, pouring, pushing, activating, arranging, constructing, or maintaining the changed condition.

The frame must read as AFTERMATH.

PEOPLE
Show exactly two adults, both visually specific and non-interchangeable.

SOURCE PERSON:
- a compact, broad-shouldered adult woman in a dark, knee-length coat with a visibly asymmetric lower edge;
- the coat's right rear hem is missing a distinctive three-ridge woven band about the width of two fingers;
- one side of her stance is slightly turned outward, giving her body a specific asymmetrical orientation;
- she now stands fully separated from the changed field on stable untouched ground, hands relaxed, not touching the aftermath, looking across the shared space rather than performing an action.

AFFECTED PERSON:
- a taller, lean adult man in a pale structured jacket and straight trousers with clearly different silhouette/material from the source;
- he stands at the near edge of the changed field, one foot withdrawn because the altered ground cannot safely support the position he would otherwise occupy;
- he is physically adapting to the aftermath, not expressing an explanatory emotion.

Do not use facial guilt, surprise, fear, or theatrical expression to tell the story.

ATTRIBUTABLE AFTERMATH
Across the shared ground is a broad region of dense, fine, dark mineral filaments that once stood upright.

A completed earlier passage by the source woman has permanently changed part of this substrate.

Show the aftermath as a distributed physical deformation, not as a magic effect:
- a broad body-scale swath of the filaments has collapsed, fused, and set into a low rigid laminated surface;
- the deformation contains a repeated asymmetric compression geometry matching the source woman's distinctive body orientation: a recurring shallow diagonal fold that consistently turns outward on the same side;
- embedded visibly INSIDE the fused laminated aftermath—not merely lying on top—are multiple short sections of the exact same three-ridge woven band that is visibly missing from the source woman's coat hem;
- the woven sections should be distributed through the depth of the fused material in several places, making it visually clear they became incorporated when the field was formed, not stuck to her afterward;
- the source woman has small clean gaps where that same band is missing from the coat, but she carries no loose residue from the field on her current clothing or shoes.

SECOND, INDEPENDENT AUTHORSHIP CUE
In addition to the embedded woven-band continuity, the fused field must preserve a second nonredundant correspondence to the source:
- repeated full-body compression arcs within the laminated material reproduce the same asymmetrical outward-turn geometry as her stance;
- this geometry appears several times across the altered region, at human scale, and does NOT match the affected man's straight, narrow stance.

These two cues must converge on the source woman:
1. transferred woven material embedded inside the aftermath;
2. repeated human-scale deformation geometry matching her asymmetrical body orientation.

Do not use matching color as evidence.

Do not use a tool, container, footprint trail, written clue, obvious object, or single symbolic prop to explain causality.

LATER-CONTACT CONFOUND MUST BE VISUALLY WEAK
Make the physical direction of transfer legible:
- the coat has missing woven material;
- matching pieces are trapped beneath/within the fused aftermath;
- the source is now cleanly separated from the field and carries no adhered aftermath material;
- untouched upright filaments remain elsewhere, making the fused region read as something formed by the earlier human passage rather than a pre-existing anomaly she merely crossed later.

CHANGED PRESENT POSSIBILITY
The affected man is now materially constrained by the fused aftermath:
- the laminated region has buckled into low rigid ribs and narrow unsupported voids;
- his withdrawn foot and shifted stance show that the place he would naturally occupy is no longer usable;
- the condition changes his available footing/occupancy now.

This must read through physical affordance, not mood or symbolism.

SURFACE ESCAPE
The image must NOT read primarily as:
- a person doing a recognizable ordinary task;
- cleanup;
- repair;
- farming;
- workshop labor;
- construction;
- maintenance;
- domestic accident;
- period genre painting;
- occupational documentary.

If the impossible filament physics were removed, the remaining image should reduce to two adults in sparse, non-domestic, temporally non-specific space—not a coherent ordinary-task tableau.

VISUAL LANGUAGE
- Human relationship before effect.
- Quiet before spectacle.
- Deep slate, limestone, graphite, weathered mineral surfaces; restrained material contrast.
- No color-coded identity.
- No glowing aura, beam, halo, ring, force bubble, magical energy, hologram, cyberpunk neon, HUD, AI symbol, text, logo, or interface.
- No literal theater stage, spotlight, or cone.
- No portal/threshold imagery.
- No giant mechanism.
- Ground every impossible behavior through weight, contact, compression, embedded material, occlusion, and physical deformation.
- Fine-art cinematic or materially rich illustrative realism is acceptable, but avoid nostalgic historical realism, luxury editorial posing, videogame key art, and generic surreal spectacle.

ONE WORLD
Everything occurs in one authoritative shared world.

Do not depict private thoughts, beliefs, memories, guilt, intent, alternate realities, or explanatory symbolism.

FINAL SELF-CHECK BEFORE RENDERING
The finished image must let a viewer infer, from the frame alone:
- the act already happened;
- the dark filament aftermath persists;
- the source woman is specifically attributable through TWO independent present physical cues;
- she did not merely pick up residue by later contact;
- the affected man cannot use part of the shared ground because of that aftermath;
- both people and both attribution cues remain recoverable in one 4:5 crop;
- the left 40% can disappear under website identity/copy;
- removing the impossible physics would NOT leave a conventional household/work/task scene.

Return only the uninterrupted artwork. Do not add text, branding, interface, labels, captions, borders, or presentation layout.
