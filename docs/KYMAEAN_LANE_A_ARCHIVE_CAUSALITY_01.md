# KYMAEAN LANE A — ARCHIVE CAUSALITY 01

Status: LANE A BUILDABLE REFINEMENT / DESIGN EVIDENCE / NO PRODUCTION IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORIZED

Date: 2026-09-05

## Purpose

Connect the completed Take-acceptance surface to the revived O0 Archive lineage without turning history into a transcript dump or historical-state mode:

```text
successful committed Take
-> accepted historical Performance
-> What Changed
-> Still in Motion
-> trace consequence to cause
-> return to the same changed Production
```

## Authority contract

- Accepted Performance history is historical texture and later Character-legible context; it is not automatically durable projected state.
- Durable consequences remain separate authoritative state.
- Claims, suspicions, beliefs and other subjective domains must remain typed; accepted history does not promote them to objective truth.
- History inspection must not rewind or replace current Production authority.
- Successful commit consumed the prior Current Opportunity. This prototype does not invent the next one.
- Presentation Perspective remains semantic state. This prototype uses Creator perspective only and does not freeze switching UX.

## Surface thesis

> **History should answer what changed and why it matters now.**

The O0 Archive phrases `What Changed` and `Still in Motion` are retained because they express causal history better than a generic timeline. Exact labels remain design evidence, not frozen product vocabulary.

### What Changed

Shows only effective durable consequences caused by the selected accepted Performance. The prototype keeps domain meaning visible in human language: Wren's claim remains a claim; her suspicion remains subjective; relationship change remains semantic rather than numeric psychology.

### Still in Motion

Shows unresolved continuity capable of affecting later scenes. It does not imply that every displayed question is a dedicated engine enum or persisted `UnresolvedProposition`; this is a creator-facing projection over supported state/history.

### Cause trace

A selected consequence can be traced back to the exact accepted Performance and the creator review that made the consequence eligible. Product copy expresses atomicity as the Performance and approved changes becoming effective together rather than exposing commit-engine terminology.

## Changed-present return

`Return to current Production` removes the historical inspector and restores the same Production in its changed present. The Stage-like return state shows:

- last Performance = accepted history;
- current consequence state is now effective;
- Current Opportunity = none;
- no implied next Character action.

This validates the information-architecture rule **stable Production, fluid focus** without establishing `History` / `Present` as final top-level navigation labels.

## O0 refinement

Preserved: causal-first Archive, `What Changed`, `Still in Motion`, dark editorial hierarchy, Character identity continuity, direct path back to Stage/current Production.

Removed from the ancestor for this slice: scene-ended requirement, impact/tension meters, claims/truth dashboard density, knowledge matrix, export/session-summary chrome, and anything not needed to prove the causal loop.

## Semantic/accessibility requirements

Current/history and effective/unresolved meanings are textual, not color-only. Character/domain labels remain explicit. Keyboard focus is visible; reduced-motion dependence is absent. No WinUI, screen-reader, forced-colors or device validation is claimed.

## Audit corrections

The first render was corrected because: `what became true` could elevate subjective records to objective truth; `atomic commit` exposed engineering vocabulary unnecessarily; and returning to the present initially left the history rail attached.

Final bounded result: **no material authority contradiction found within prototype scope.**
