# R0 Renderer Cartography — Persistent Affordance Carrier Design 02

Status: **DIRECTOR DESIGN CHECKPOINT — NO RENDER AUTHORIZED**

Updated: 2026-09-03

## Purpose

PAC-01A showed that permitting one ordinary visible carrier restores plural independent affordance, but generic built infrastructure does not make prior human configuration recoverable.

Canonical result:

`docs/R0_RENDERER_CARTOGRAPHY_PAC_01A_RESULT.md`

The next test isolates the missing variable:

> **visible configuration history**

without reintroducing repair, access, monumental architecture, helper hierarchy, or object symbolism.

## New hypothesis

> **A persistent enabling condition may become more causally legible when ordinary human-made elements visibly appear to have been deliberately repositioned or combined, and present uses depend on that configuration.**

The elements remain experimental measurement devices. They have no aesthetic or metaphorical inheritance rights.

## Why PAC-01A was insufficient

PAC-01A succeeded at showing people using a persistent built condition in different ways. It did not strongly distinguish:

- a condition deliberately configured by prior human activity;
- generic infrastructure that simply already exists.

It also showed actions that were supported by the carrier but not always strongly dependent on it.

Therefore PAC-01B adds two measurement requirements:

1. **configuration evidence** — the arrangement should visibly read as intentionally positioned/combined rather than generic fixed architecture;
2. **affordance dependence** — at least two present actions should make particular sense because of the way the elements are configured together.

## PAC-01B

Name:

**Persistent Affordance Carrier 01B — Repositioned Ordinary Elements / Configuration-Dependent Use**

Question:

> Can ordinary human-made elements make prior human configuration and expanded present capability visually recoverable when several people independently use the resulting arrangement?

## Controlled semantic requirements

- several distinct people remain the primary visual structure;
- a small set of ordinary human-made elements has been deliberately positioned together before the depicted moment;
- the original arranging action is not shown;
- the elements remain recognizable as ordinary rather than a special installation, product or symbolic device;
- several people independently use different parts/aspects of the resulting arrangement at the same time;
- at least two of those uses should visibly depend on how the elements are positioned together;
- no person's action depends on another person guiding, helping, directing, rescuing or controlling them;
- the configuration remains modest and visually subordinate to the people;
- the surrounding world stays restrained.

## Draft sterile packet — NO RENDER AUTHORIZED

> Create one uninterrupted artwork centered on several distinct people whose presence and relationships are necessary to the image.
>
> A small set of ordinary human-made elements was deliberately positioned together before this moment and now remains as part of ordinary shared life. Several people are independently using different parts or aspects of the resulting arrangement at the same time. Their different actions should make particular sense because of the way the elements are positioned together.
>
> The elements should still feel like ordinary everyday things rather than a special installation, product, exhibit, or symbol. The original act of arranging them is not shown.
>
> Keep every person specific and individually active. No one should guide, help, direct, rescue, or control anyone else. Keep the arrangement modest and visually subordinate to the people.
>
> Keep the surrounding world restrained. Let posture, orientation, spacing, and independent use reveal how the persistent configuration supports different actions.
>
> Return only the uninterrupted artwork, with no text or surrounding presentation layout.

## Recursive packet audit

### Setting/object seeding

The packet names no specific object, workplace, studio, home, institution, public site, culture, era, architecture, technology or medium.

### Metaphor suppression

The packet does not ask for route, access, progress, repair, achievement, crossing, opening or transformation symbolism.

### Configuration-history risk

`deliberately positioned together` may cause the renderer to invent furniture, modular objects, equipment or an improvised social arrangement. That is acceptable diagnostic evidence if the elements remain ordinary and subordinate.

Reject if the configuration becomes:

- a designed installation;
- a product showcase;
- a sculpture;
- a stage;
- a bridge/path/door/threshold;
- repair scaffolding or damage response;
- a contraption whose mechanism dominates the people.

### Affordance-dependence risk

The renderer may merely place people near objects while their actions remain unrelated to the configuration. If so, record PAC-01B as a failure; do not rerender to cosmetically improve it.

## Evaluation gates

### PACB1 — Configuration evidence

Does the arrangement plausibly read as intentionally positioned/combined by prior human activity rather than generic fixed architecture or accidental clutter?

### PACB2 — Persistence

Is the configuration present and usable after the arranging action is over?

### PACB3 — Configuration-dependent plural affordance

Are at least two meaningfully different independent actions visibly supported by the specific relationship among the elements, rather than merely occurring nearby?

### PACB4 — Independent agency

Do people remain self-directed without helper/recipient, leader/follower or controller/controlled hierarchy?

### PACB5 — Human primacy

Do people carry most of the artwork's action and meaning?

### PACB6 — Ordinary/non-symbolic carrier

Do the elements remain ordinary rather than becoming a metaphor, exhibit, technology demo or special installation?

### PACB7 — Attractor suppression

Does the result avoid route/access/threshold/repair/monumental/progress imagery and environment-first spectacle?
