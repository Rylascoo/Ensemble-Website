## Frozen material roles

1. **VOID / THEATRICAL FIELD** — quiet, presence, acquisition framing.
2. **ARCHIVE / MATERIAL FIELD** — history/consequence as materially different surface; not “light mode.”
3. **WARM CAUSAL / HUMAN SIGNAL** — authorship/consequence/human emphasis; never universal accent.
4. **COOL PRESENCE / COUNTER-SIGNAL** — presence/asymmetry reinforcement; never identity/state alone.

## A — Ash & Linen

Working values: Void `#090b0d`; Archive `#d8d0c4`; Warm `#b56d43`; Cool `#6f95ab`.

Tactile and approachable, but linen/clay risks cultivated literary/editorial familiarity and is the least otherworldly viable family.

Result: **RANK 2 / PRESERVE AS MATERIAL REFERENCE.**

## B — Mineral Theater

Working values:
- Void `#080c0f`
- Secondary dark field `#10171b`
- Archive `#cfcbbc`
- Archive ink `#1a1e1e`
- Archive supporting text `#4e544d`
- Warm causal `#a96743`
- Warm high-contrast signal `#cc936a`
- Cool presence `#5f8790`
- Cool high-contrast signal `#8eabb0`
- Primary light text `#ece9e0`
- Dark-field supporting text `#9ea29d`

Character: deep slate / limestone / oxidized copper / blue-green mineral.

Strengths:
- most coherent with Quiet Stage without permanent black;
- Archive reads as material shift rather than theme inversion;
- copper feels human/causal rather than luxury-gold;
- blue-green feels like presence rather than software-blue;
- one physical family without literal stone styling;
- strongest distance from fantasy, luxury, SaaS, and literary-brand shorthand.

Static audited contrast examples: primary text/Void `16.17:1`; supporting/Void `7.58:1`; Archive ink/Archive `10.35:1`; Archive supporting/Archive `4.79:1`; warm high-contrast/Void `7.43:1`; cool high-contrast/Void `8.04:1`.

Full Site Spine application: **PASS**.

### Audit correction

The first full-site application inherited oxidized copper in CTA/focus chrome. That violated the material role by turning causal/human warmth into generic accent. CTA/focus returned to neutral limestone/slate values.

Law: **Warm causal is not generic brand or interaction chrome.**

Result: **RANK 1 / RECOMMENDED TEMPORARY MATERIAL-FAMILY INCUMBENT.**

## C — Ink & Bone

Working values: Void `#070a10`; Archive `#ded8ca`; Warm `#9d5c4a`; Cool `#637f9d`.

Dramatic and legible, but most likely to drift into prestige editorial or dark-fantasy publishing; the bone surface is more narratively loaded than required.

Result: **RANK 3 / DO NOT ADVANCE AS PRIMARY MATERIAL FAMILY.**

## Cross-family decision

**Kymaean should feel materially varied, not colorfully branded.**

Recommended temporary family: **B — Mineral Theater** with these role laws:
1. Void is deep slate-black, not absolute featureless black.
2. Archive changes to limestone/material only when the subject earns a material shift.
3. Warm copper is reserved for human/causal emphasis; never default CTA/focus/brand accent.
4. Cool mineral signal reinforces presence/counter-pressure; never identity/state alone.
5. Threshold K and O3 remain neutral enough to survive independently.
6. Texture remains low-amplitude and deterministic; identity/meaning never requires texture.
7. Material roles preserve sufficient text contrast and redundant semantic encoding.

## Accessibility/static boundary

All explicitly audited normal-text pairs in all three families meet/exceed conservative `4.5:1` after correcting Mineral Theater Archive supporting text. Static color-pair evidence only; no full WCAG, forced-colors, gamut, Windows High Contrast, screen-reader, or runtime claim.

## Selection boundary

Approval would select **Mineral Theater as temporary V0 website material-family incumbent for continued testing** plus the seven role laws. It would not select final brand palette, final production tokens, texture/noise values, hero artwork, Threshold K, O3 production master, or website implementation.
