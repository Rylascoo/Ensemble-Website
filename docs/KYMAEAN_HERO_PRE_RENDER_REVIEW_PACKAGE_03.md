## Candidate 03 experimental hypothesis

Candidate 03 replaces separator/access causality with **gravity-dependent support-state causality**.

The one-frame proposition is:

> a broad continuous field has a recoverable simpler baseline; one pre-existing continuous deformation physically bears Adult A's body load at a position the baseline could not support; mentally restoring the visible baseline removes the support geometry and therefore removes that present bodily possibility.

This uses gravity as an external physical reference rather than asking the viewer to infer access, contact availability, intention, knowledge or prior authorship.

Adult B remains comparably substantial and materially borne by a different continuation of the same changed field so the image reads as a shared human condition rather than a solitary mechanics demonstration.
