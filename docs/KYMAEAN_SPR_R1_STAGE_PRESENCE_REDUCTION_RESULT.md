## 4. Remaining unresolved identity boundary

Removing the portrait roster reveals a narrower question.

The living field can carry human presence and visual identity, but a final system must also ensure that Characters remain recoverable when:

- the creator is seeing the Stage for the first time;
- representation depth is reduced;
- a Character is cropped or partially visible;
- the layout becomes narrow;
- imagery is unavailable or disabled;
- color/high-detail portrait recognition is unavailable;
- assistive technology traverses the Stage;
- Perspective or Current Opportunity references a Character whose name is not otherwise continuously visible.

This is not a reason to restore a permanent roster.

It is the basis for the next non-render gate:

`CIR-01 — Character Identity Recoverability`.
