# Residual Presence — Replication R1

Use this packet only in one fresh regular non-project renderer conversation.

Create one uninterrupted wide artwork containing exactly two specific adult people in one shared physical world.

## Composition

- exactly 2 adults; no child;
- neither person is entering through a doorway;
- no doorway, threshold, bridge, trench, wall-crossing, path, arch, portal or giant mechanism as causal geometry;
- reserve the left roughly 40% as genuinely quiet, expendable negative space;
- place both adults plus all indispensable causal evidence inside one compact cluster that can survive a 4:5 portrait crop;
- use a lateral or oblique relationship rather than a central actor with secondary figures arranged behind/right.

## Time

The causal act is already complete before the depicted moment. Show aftermath only.

No one is actively performing the act that produced the changed condition.

## Causal physics

One adult previously changed the shared environment through a completed human action. The world has not returned to neutral.

The persistent change must be distributed through the shared material or spatial field itself. It must not be liquid, milk, water, ice, a spill, a stain being cleaned, smoke, glowing energy, aura, beam, force field, magic effect, or one giant symbolic object.

Make the earlier human authorship recoverable through present physical evidence that matches the originating adult — for example coherent trace, wear, pressure, residue, compression, orientation, displacement, imprint, adhered material, repeated bodily scale, or another non-color physical correspondence.

The second adult encounters the already-changed condition after the act has ended. That persistent condition materially changes what the second adult can occupy, place, use, reach, avoid, perceive, or do now.

Neither person should be performing a recognizable ordinary household, maintenance, repair, farming, construction, cleaning or workshop task.

## Humanity

Keep both people specific and non-interchangeable through age, build, posture, gesture, clothing structure and physical relationship to the shared field. Do not depict private thought, belief, memory or knowledge.

## World

One authoritative shared world. Quiet before spectacle. Ground impossible behavior through weight, contact, occlusion, compression, residue or displacement. No UI, text, logos, diagrams, arrows, nodes or presentation framing.

Do not use a conventional historical/domestic genre-painting setup. Keep the setting sparse, non-domestic and temporally non-specific.

Return only the uninterrupted artwork.
