## 2. Overall result

Judgment:

> **CRV-01 PASS — CHARACTER REPRESENTATION VIABILITY DEMONSTRATED ACROSS DEPTHS.**

The core Kymaean relational model survived meaningful reduction in depiction richness.

The experiment supports:

> **Rich depiction improves immediacy but is not required for persistent identity, relational presence, bounded perspective, or independent salience.**

No representation depth is selected.

---

## 3. CRV-01A — Reduced Human Invariant

### Positive evidence

The reduced branch still preserved:

- three distinct people;
- B as the bounded perspective;
- C as independently salient/current opportunity;
- shared Scene membership;
- relational arrangement;
- stable identity cues despite minimal facial realism.

This demonstrates that Kymaean is not structurally dependent on:

- photoreal faces;
- cinematic lighting;
- micro-expression;
- realistic skin/hair rendering;
- highly detailed gesture.

Useful identity carriers included combinations of:

- name;
- silhouette;
- hair geometry;
- body/shape distinction;
- persistent authored color/shape cues;
- relational placement.

### Risk

The compact identity thumbnails can drift toward:

`avatar + name = Character`

That is insufficient as a final product model.

The stronger law remains:

> **Character identity must participate in the living relational Stage rather than exist only as a profile icon beside it.**

---

## 4. CRV-01B — Expressive Figurative

### Positive evidence

This branch supplied the strongest first-pass balance between:

- human specificity;
- representation independence;
- relational readability;
- identity persistence;
- reduced dependence on photographic realism.

It suggests a useful distinction:

> **Kymaean may need authored human identity more than simulated human appearance.**

This is research evidence only; it does not select illustration or this exact figurative style.

---

## 5. CRV-01C — Rich Representational Control

### Positive evidence

Rich depiction increases immediate human recognition and scene legibility.

### Risk

It also increases temptation to infer unauthorized internal state from:

- facial expression;
- gaze;
- posture;
- gesture;
- photographic realism.

Patch 0014 forbids the UI from converting those cues into authoritative knowledge/observation/intent/private psychology unless independently supported by state.

Therefore:

> **A representation may portray human behavior without turning interpretation of that portrayal into Production authority.**

## 6. Cross-depth findings

CRV-01 materially supports the following laws:

### 6.1 Identity and circumstance are separate

```text
Character identity remains stable
+
Scene relationship / evidence / opportunity changes
=
changed present
```

Causal consequence does not normally require visual identity mutation.

### 6.2 Representation depth is not creator-facing ontology

The research labels `Reduced Human Invariant`, `Expressive Figurative`, and `Rich Representational Control` are test dimensions only.

Do not implement an R0/R1/R2/R3 or equivalent creator setting from this evidence.

### 6.3 Relationship survives representation reduction

Relational structure can remain legible without photorealism, provided individual identity invariants remain specific enough.

### 6.4 Perspective and salience remain independent

`B = bounded perspective` and `C = current opportunity` remained simultaneously legible across representation depths.

### 6.5 Accessibility direction strengthened

Because the core model survives reduced depiction, a future system has a plausible path toward high-contrast, lower-detail, image-limited, or alternate representation states without destroying the Product model.

Programmatic/textual equivalents remain required.
