## 0. Purpose

Run one genuinely lateral visual-world experiment after V0R3 validated H2 `Inherited Condition` at carrier level.

The next underrepresented question is H3 `Transformational Continuity`:

> **Can an individual's identity remain legible when the visual manner of representing that person changes materially, without requiring one fixed portrait or turning transformation into spectacle?**

This changes the primary carrier from altered environment/history to persistence of personhood through transformed representation.

It is not a request to restyle V0R3 and not a request for a more surreal version of the same painterly world.

## 1. Director-only rationale

The first three isolated renders have supplied useful evidence for:

- H1 `Living Relational Field`;
- H4-adjacent shared-condition asymmetry;
- H2 `Inherited Condition`.

All three clean renderer chats also independently recurred toward painterly social realism. That recurrence is treated as renderer-prior evidence, not brand convergence.

A fourth environment-centered causal scene would add diminishing evidence and risk converting recurrence into accidental incumbency.

H3 is therefore the smallest justified lateral move because it tests a different location for visual identity:

```text
environment/history carrier
-> personhood-through-representation carrier
```

## 3. VID-01 / V0 sterile render packet 04

Use verbatim:

```text
Create one complete, uninterrupted artwork centered on distinct people sharing a meaningful present situation.

Within the same coherent artwork, allow the visual manner of representing at least one person to change materially while keeping that individual recognizably the same person. Preserve identity through a redundant set of coherent cues rather than through one identical repeated portrait.

Treat the change as a property of depiction rather than requiring a literal supernatural transformation in the fictional world. The shift in representation should matter to the person's relation to the situation or to other people, not exist merely as a decorative effect or technical showcase.

Keep every person specific and non-interchangeable. Do not let transformed representation collapse a person into a generic symbol, silhouette, archetype, or anonymous fragment. The shared human situation should remain intelligible even if the visual representation becomes incomplete, altered, or less literal in some parts of the artwork.

Invent every unstated visual circumstance yourself, including medium, palette, setting, era, scale, material language, atmosphere, composition, degree of abstraction, and the nature of the representational transformation. Do not optimize for software, branding, explanation, or category familiarity. The result should stand first as a compelling artwork.

Return only the uninterrupted artwork. Add no text, labels, interface, logo, promotional framing, presentation board, explanatory border, split panel, portrait lineup, or literal before/after comparison.
```

## 4. What this packet intentionally changes

V0R4 does **not** ask the environment to prove a prior human decision.

Instead it tests whether Character-like identity can survive representational transformation within one artwork.

The renderer remains free to choose:

- what kind of world exists;
- how literal or abstract the work is;
- how many people appear;
- what causes the representational contrast aesthetically;
- what medium/material grammar emerges;
- whether transformation is subtle or severe.

The packet constrains only the identity law strongly enough to make H3 falsifiable.

## 6. Falsification conditions

V0R4 fails the central H3 test if:

- the supposedly same person cannot be reliably recognized across changed representation;
- recognition depends on one trivial immutable cue only;
- the work simply duplicates an identical portrait several times;
- transformation becomes generic surrealism, morph spectacle or effects demonstration;
- the image implies literal fictional body transformation when the only intended change is depiction;
- transformed people become anonymous symbols or silhouettes;
- the result becomes a split-panel, before/after board, lineup or diagram;
- human relationship/situation disappears beneath representational technique;
- the renderer merely returns another conventional painterly social scene with no meaningful representational transformation.
