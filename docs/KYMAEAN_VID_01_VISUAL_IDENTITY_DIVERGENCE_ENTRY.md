## 2. Why V0 precedes logo/wordmark convergence

The first divergence work begins in the `V0 — Visual World & Atmosphere` lane rather than attempting to solve the symbol, wordmark, or final app UI immediately.

Reason:

- a premature logo can force the entire identity to orbit one metaphor before the visual world is understood;
- a premature app screen can pull the renderer back into conventional software/SaaS grammar;
- a premature wordmark can bias typography and materiality before Kymaean has earned an artistic world;
- expressive visual-world research can reveal transferable properties later tested in symbol, wordmark, motion, app, and web lanes.

This ordering does not make V0 authoritative over later lanes. It merely reduces premature convergence pressure.

## 4. VID-01 / V0 sterile render packet 01

Use the following packet verbatim in the isolated Renderer Context:

```text
Create one complete, uninterrupted artwork centered on distinct people sharing a present condition that has been materially changed by an earlier human choice.

Make the earlier cause recoverable through the organization of the present rather than through explanatory text, diagrams, flashbacks, split panels, or a literal before/after comparison. The consequence should materially change at least one person's present possibilities and their relationship to someone else.

Do not give everyone equal access to what happened or what it means. Let asymmetry be visible through the situation itself without claiming private thoughts that the image cannot establish.

Keep each person specific and non-interchangeable. If identity is expressed through transformation, variation, crop, scale, pose, or incomplete representation, preserve enough coherent invariants that the same person can remain recognizable without depending on an identical portrait.

Invent every unstated visual circumstance yourself, including medium, palette, setting, era, scale, material language, atmosphere, composition, and degree of abstraction. Do not optimize for software, branding, explanation, or category familiarity. The result should stand first as a compelling artwork.

Return only the uninterrupted artwork. Add no text, labels, interface, logo, promotional framing, presentation board, or explanatory border.
```

## 6. Evaluation rubric after the first render

The Director Context evaluates the first artwork after generation. The Renderer Context does not self-select or explain a direction.

### A. Artistic integrity

- Does the image stand as artwork without project explanation?
- Does it possess a coherent visual language rather than appearing like a product mockup or concept diagram?
- Does it create specificity, atmosphere, and human presence without relying on caption text?

### B. Human primacy

- Are people causal participants rather than decorative scale figures?
- Are individual identities specific and non-interchangeable?
- Does relationship matter to the composition itself?

### C. Causal recoverability

- Can the present plausibly carry evidence of an earlier human choice?
- Does consequence alter current possibility, relationship, access, constraint, affordance, or composition rather than merely decorate the image?
- Does the image avoid requiring a storyboard/flashback to explain causality?

### D. Epistemic restraint

- Does unequal access remain legible without pretending the image proves private Knowledge, Belief, Memory, Observation, or intent?
- Does visual prominence avoid being mistaken for authoritative fictional truth?

### E. Identity robustness

- If representation varies, can the same person remain recognizable through redundant invariants rather than a fixed identical portrait?
- Would the identity logic plausibly survive crop, scale, partial representation, or later medium changes?

### F. Surface originality

- Is the work genuinely independent of the recurring photoreal office/SaaS laboratory?
- Does it avoid obvious accidental regression into historical visual attractors?
- More importantly, does it possess affirmative properties worth studying rather than merely satisfying an exclusion list?

### G. Cross-product potential — diagnostic only

Do not ask whether the exact artwork should become the app or website.

Ask only whether any underlying properties might later translate into:

- shared brand identity;
- Character representation;
- Stage atmosphere;
- editorial web expression;
- symbol/icon language;
- motion behavior;
- temporal/consequence expression.

Cross-product potential does not grant inheritance rights.
