# KYMAEAN LANE A — STAGE CARRIER SELECTION 01

Status: DIRECTOR-SELECTED STAGE MEDIUM / GEOMETRY OPEN

Date: 2026-09-05

## Decision

The Lane A Stage representation carrier is selected as:

> **Characters as volumes of light on a dark floor.**

This resolves the prior authority discrepancy between the current design instructions and repository lineage. The selection is now explicit repository authority rather than an instruction-only assumption.

## Selected carrier contract

- No human figures, faces, scenery or audience seating are required in the default Stage representation.
- Each present Character may be represented by a light volume with a floor relationship, restrained Character-associated colour and a distinct slow internal texture.
- The creator-facing Character name remains the primary explicit identity channel when shown.
- Stable human-readable/programmatic Character identity is mandatory regardless of colour, image, motion or exact spatial position.
- Colour and internal texture are presentation/data channels only. They do not establish identity, Current Opportunity, knowledge, observation, belief, intent or authority by themselves.
- Representation may depict; authority must establish.
- The Stage remains human/Character-first and quiet. It does not gain permanent roster, marketing copy, generic AI composer, dashboard perimeter or product-thesis narration merely because the carrier is abstract.
- No AI generation is required to keep the carrier alive. Runtime realization should be deterministic gradients/light volumes, texture/noise and text; final WinUI/GPU implementation remains an engineering task.
- Stage Performance/motion timing remains unfrozen pending Read-Through evidence.

## What this selection does not select

This decision does **not** select:

- final Character colours;

## Geometry authority

Cone versus cylinder remains a Director choice.

### Cone

Repository ancestry favors cone: O0-C1 already implemented `.spotlight-cone`. It reads as theatrical illumination and visually connects an overhead source to a floor pool. Its main objection is weaker object-like boundedness/selectability if hit-testing is incorrectly coupled to the visible beam shape.

### Cylinder

Cylinder offers a clearer discrete volume and obvious visual boundary. Its main objection is semantic: the bounded vertical body can read as a portal, containment chamber or selectable UI object rather than theatrical presence.

### Design recommendation

**Recommend cone.** It inherits the executable O0 ancestor, is more natively theatrical, and avoids turning a Character into a hard-edged container. Selectability should be solved in the interaction/accessibility layer through the Character's semantic target and floor/name region, not by forcing the visible carrier to become a cylinder.

This recommendation is not a Director selection. The comparison artifact must present both under identical conditions.

## Validation boundary

This is static/executable design authority only. It does not claim WinUI runtime behavior, GPU performance, high-contrast behavior, screen-reader behavior, ARM64 validation, WACK or Store certification.
