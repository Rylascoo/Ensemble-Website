# KYMAEAN — HERO SURFACE OWNERSHIP OPERATIONAL DEFINITION 01

Status: DIRECTOR APPROVED / CURRENT BOUNDED SURFACE-OWNERSHIP AUTHORITY  
Date: 2026-09-06

## Purpose

Define the phrase **“recognizably Kymaean surface”** before any surface-ownership falsification criteria are applied.

This document is definition-only. It authorizes no renderer prompt, Candidate 03, new image generation, method proposal, final hero selection or Lane A change.

## What “recognizably” means here

For this bounded hero-art problem, **recognizably Kymaean** means **visibly continuous with the project’s currently approved temporary material / graphic / visual-world authority**.

It does **not** mean:
- proven public brand recognition;
- uniqueness against every other visual culture;
- trademark distinctiveness;
- final brand identity;
- a new image-style invention.

The definition must therefore be satisfied by already-approved laws, not by adding a new aesthetic doctrine.

## Approved source authority

### Quiet Stage
Director-approved temporary V0 visual-world authority preserves:
- theatrical absence;
- human / presence priority;
- restrained product truth rather than app-UI imitation;
- responsive asymmetry;
- Threshold K as concentrated prelude where appropriate;

Quiet Stage explicitly does **not** mean making the whole site look like the Stage or relying on generic darkness / cones.

### Mineral Theater
Director-approved temporary V0 material authority establishes:
- deep slate-black void rather than featureless absolute black;
- limestone / material field only when the subject earns a material shift;
- warm copper reserved for human / causal emphasis, never generic accent chrome;
- cool mineral signal as presence / counter-pressure reinforcement, never identity or state alone;
- low-amplitude deterministic texture;
- meaning independent of texture;
- material variation rather than colorful branding.

### Threshold K
Historical working symbol candidate/control establishes:
- the K is **discovered in negative space; it is not drawn**;
- the mark needs breathing room more than geometric correction;
- surrounding frames must not visually complete the mark;
- mark and name retain distinct jobs.

For hero artwork this is a boundary, not permission to echo the K inside the scene.

### O3 Balanced Hierarchy
Director-approved temporary wordmark authority establishes:
- ownership through proportion, spacing and deliberate construction;
- quieter optical hierarchy rather than equal-weight insistence;
- lower emblematic density than Threshold K;
- standalone identity without logo echo;
- no decorative complexity added merely to prove authorship.

For hero artwork this contributes a discipline of hierarchy / restraint, not literal glyph geometry.

## Operational definition

A **recognizably Kymaean hero surface** is:

**a human-first, materially restrained, compositionally asymmetric field in which presence and living consequence are carried by spatial / material relationships and meaningful quiet; visual authorship is expressed through proportion, spacing, optical hierarchy and disciplined material roles rather than through brand-symbol imitation, generic darkness, decorative density or one spectacular effect. The artwork must coexist with Threshold K and O3 as separate identity assets rather than reproducing their geometry inside the image.**

## Constituent properties of the definition

1. **Human-first salience**  
   People and their consequential relationship remain the primary meaningful event; system ornament or environmental spectacle does not become the subject.

2. **Theatrical absence / meaningful quiet**  
   Empty or low-information space participates in hierarchy and tension. “Theatrical” means controlled presence and absence, not literal stage scenery, cones, spotlights or permanent black.

3. **Material-role discipline**  
   Surface variation follows Mineral Theater’s role logic: slate/void quiet, earned limestone/material shifts, bounded warm human/causal emphasis, bounded cool presence/counter-signal, low-amplitude texture, and no color-only identity.

4. **Authored restraint**  
   The image should feel deliberately composed through proportion, spacing, selective emphasis and optical weight. Ownership is not supplied by ornament, busy texture, faux-historical styling or a dominant special effect.

5. **Discovery rather than emblem imitation**  
   Negative space and relationships may reveal structure, but the artwork does not literally draw, complete, quote or hide Threshold K / O3 geometry as a scene mechanism or symbol puzzle.

6. **Responsive asymmetry / identity coexistence**  
   The artwork can sustain a calm identity/copy region and a denser consequential region without forcing a universal lockup ratio, flattening everything into symmetry, or making the artwork compete with Threshold K + O3 for brand ownership.

## What this definition intentionally does not select

This does not select a final illustration genre, realism versus abstraction, final palette/tokens, literal mineral subject matter, final texture system, photographic/painterly/vector technique, final hero, motion, new symbol or broader typography system.

A future surface may therefore be realistic, illustrative, abstracted or hybrid **only if** it remains continuous with the operational properties above.

## Important limitation

Because Threshold K, O3, Mineral Theater and Quiet Stage are temporary—not final—authorities, this definition establishes **current-project conformance**, not proof that an unfamiliar viewer would uniquely identify the image as Kymaean without branding.

That stronger public-recognition claim is not established and is not required by the current hero problem.
