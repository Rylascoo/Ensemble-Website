## 2. Why R0 replaces V0.5B

V0.5B correctly identified contextual visual inertia and established the useful rule:

> **Carry the discovery. Do not carry the picture.**

A subsequent historical-recovery audit exposed a larger continuity problem: important original Ensemble concept artwork and source explorations had not been consistently available to later visual-development chats. Those recovered sources revealed that several later discoveries—persistent character presence, theatrical social geometry, bounded perspective, living consequence, causal Archive structure, typographic identity, and distributed visual identity—were already latent in the original product vision.

The correction is not to restore the old artwork as the answer. It is to **re-found the exploration process**.

R0 therefore applies a stronger principle:

> **Preserve the lineage. Restart the search.**

Every earlier visual branch remains available as inspiration, evidence, critique history, and conceptual ancestry. No earlier branch retains incumbent status in the active search.

V0.5B and all earlier visual phases remain valid historical research. They are no longer the active continuation phase.

## 5. What O0 contributes without prescribing appearance

The following are newly recovered **abstract discoveries**, not motifs:

- **Persistent identity can have visual presence without fixed photorealistic portraiture.** A character can be recognizable without becoming a generic avatar, provider badge, or anonymous token.
- **Character-specific presence can remain systemic without becoming identical.** Distinct people can share one visual universe while retaining different gestures, structures, rhythms, or signatures.
- **Attention can be spatial and relational.** Opportunity, address, listening, withholding, and handoff can influence composition without becoming narrative authority.
- **Bounded perspective can itself be an experience.** What a character knows, believes, remembers, wants, hides, and can perceive can change what the viewer experiences without changing authoritative Production truth.
- **Typography can carry theatrical and literary identity.** Language need not function only as neutral interface copy.
- **Perspective can change disclosure without changing reality.** Creator/Production, Audience, and Character views may deserve distinct presentation behavior over one authoritative world.
- **History can be represented by changed meaning, not only chronology.** What Changed, Still in Motion, Claims & Truth, and Who Knows What are examples of causal/history thinking rather than frozen UI requirements.
- **Identity can be distributed.** Kymaean need not force one emblem to summarize the product; cast, word, stage, perspective, consequence, history, motion, and symbol can share the identity load.
- **A surface family is not intrinsically invalid because later work overused it.** Darkness, gold, serif typography, particles, geometry, or any other prior feature may be independently rediscovered. The actual failure mode is generic spectacle, luxury shorthand, or decoration replacing specific human causality.

## 7. Historical inspiration is not a blacklist

The Historical Inspiration Library serves four legitimate purposes:

1. **Memory** — preserve what the project actually explored and valued.
2. **Inspiration** — provide artistic and conceptual material once an experiment is ready for historical comparison.
3. **Critique** — preserve why attractive branches failed, stalled, or became overextended.
4. **Lineage** — explain where later discoveries came from and prevent future chats from falsely treating rediscoveries as unprecedented.

Historical failures do not permanently ban their palette, medium, motif, or subject. Historical favorites do not grant those surfaces restoration rights.

The clean-slate search is allowed to rediscover, transform, reject, or surpass every historical branch.

## 8. Working thesis and durable creative laws

The internal website/brand exploration thesis remains:

> **Human Causality, Living Consequence.**
>
> Kymaean should feel like a world that notices people, remembers what they do, and is changed by what follows.

This remains an internal design thesis, not a public tagline, app-product constitution, or selected aesthetic.

Durable laws:

- person before effect;
- cause before decoration;
- evidence before generic symbolism;
- consequence may change structure;
- history accumulates rather than resets;
- distinct identities influence one another without merging;
- mystery comes from bounded perspective;
- quiet normally precedes spectacle;
- humanity, agency, imperfection, relationship, and lived evidence outrank machinery;
- shared reality does not imply shared understanding;
- people should live inside consequences rather than observe them from outside;
- human causality includes friction, misunderstanding, refusal, affection, asymmetric benefit, repair, interruption, compromise, and surprise;
- artistic integrity precedes explanation;
- exploration remains broad until explicit mutual convergence.

These laws select no palette, medium, protagonist, culture, architecture, typography, geometry, scenery, visual genre, or website composition.
