# KYMAEAN — HERO RENDERER METHOD 01

Status: PRE-REGISTERED DRAFT / AUTHORIZED METHOD DESIGN / DIRECTOR REVIEW REQUIRED / NO PACKET OR RENDER AUTHORIZED
Date: 2026-09-06

## 0. Purpose

Define the controlled method by which future Kymaean Website V0 hero-surface candidates may be generated and evaluated against the already-frozen measurement contract.

This is **not** a style guide, image prompt, candidate packet, render authorization, final-hero selection, or reopening of the hero requirements.

The method exists to answer one bounded experimental question:

> **Can a newly generated static hero artwork satisfy Hero Artwork Brief 02, Hero Surface Ownership S1-S6, and the frozen Surface Escape gate simultaneously while remaining usable beside the current temporary identity system on desktop and mobile?**

The renderer creates falsifiable candidates. It does not decide whether they pass and it does not define what Kymaean is.

## 2. Correct hero semantic burden

ODR-33 is resolved. The hero is **not** required to prove the exact prior author of the changed condition or the full product causal chain.

The mandatory static hero burden is:

1. at least two specific, non-interchangeable people;
2. one materially changed shared condition;
3. a consequence that materially changes another depicted person's present possibility;
4. one shared observable reality without asserting private Knowledge / Belief / Memory / intent;
5. relationship carried by composition rather than explanatory graphics;
6. quiet before spectacle.

Specific prior-author recoverability remains advisory only.

### 2.1 Correction to earlier method reasoning

A renderer packet must **not** require a hidden `prior cause`, `knowledge state`, `awareness state`, `belief`, `memory`, or `intent` merely because those ideas are meaningful elsewhere in Ensemble/Kymaean.

Observable position, access, orientation, occlusion, possession, action, changed structure and other visible relationships may be used when useful. They may not be scored as proof of private mental state.

This preserves the R0 observability finding and, more importantly, the current Epistemic Restraint requirement.

- old renderer prompts;
- candidate rankings or user preference history.

Do not attempt to create isolation by adding unverifiable memory-negation prose such as `ignore all memories`. Context separation and a minimal packet are the controllable project mechanism.

If the available image-generation surface cannot provide the intended isolation boundary, record that limitation and stop for a Director execution decision before treating an output as a qualifying clean-slate round. Do not silently redefine isolation after seeing a result.

## 4. Measurement contract versus renderer language

The Director sees the complete measurement contract. The renderer does not.

A successful method must distinguish:

- **what must be true of the result**, which is governed by Brief 02 + S1-S6 + Surface Escape; from
- **what must be said to the renderer**, which should be only the minimum picture-making information needed to create an evaluable candidate.

Dumping the measurement rubric into the renderer packet would turn evaluation language into visual seed, invite checklist imagery, and make the experiment partly self-referential.

Therefore:

- do not name S1-S6 in renderer-facing language;
- do not include numeric thresholds;
- do not ask for a `recognizably Kymaean` image;
- do not name Quiet Stage, Mineral Theater, Threshold K or O3;
- do not convert the operational definition into prose the renderer is asked to illustrate;
- do not expose prior failure imagery as a negative example menu.

The frozen Surface Escape gate is the deliberate exception: its own frozen rule requires the next surface prompt to be written to satisfy the gate. A future packet may therefore contain **one compact gate-level negative constraint** against an ordinary domestic/occupational task or ordinary-accident tableau. Do not copy its historical example list into the renderer packet.

## 5. Pre-render semantic construction

Every future qualifying packet must be derived from a Director-side **Hero Candidate Specification** before any renderer-facing prose is written.

The specification is not itself an image prompt.

### 5.1 Necessary people

Record:

- participant count, with at least two;
- why each depicted person is structurally necessary to the shared situation;
- the minimum observable identity anchors that make the people non-interchangeable.

Do not create character biography that the image cannot observe.

A person should be distinguishable through a small combination of perceptual invariants such as silhouette, bodily proportion, hair or garment geometry, posture, characteristic movement/gesture, or another visible feature. Identity may not depend only on color, portrait likeness, labels or exact position.

No cross-round recurring-character system is required. Each candidate is judged independently unless a later separately authorized experiment explicitly tests identity persistence.

### 5.2 Changed shared condition

Record the one present condition that is materially different from an otherwise ordinary shared state.

The condition must be visible enough to function as part of the image's causal/relational structure. It cannot be arbitrary atmosphere.

Do not require the candidate to prove who previously authored the condition.

Do not let one clue prop, gadget, wound, mechanism or symbolic object carry nearly all of the proposition. The condition may involve objects or environment, but meaning must remain distributed through people + condition + relationship.

### 5.3 Altered present possibility

Record the observable coupling between the changed condition and another person's present options.

Before a packet is written, the Director must be able to state, without private-state vocabulary, the relationship:

`visible changed condition -> materially different present possibility for another depicted person`.

If that relationship cannot be stated cleanly, the candidate specification is not ready.

Do not substitute emotion, facial reaction, intention, understanding or narrative implication for changed possibility.

Avoid using a menu of example affordances in renderer-facing language. Historical R0 evidence shows that concrete example menus become visual seeds, while generic `changed access` language can collapse toward accessibility/traversal shorthand. The Director therefore chooses one specific candidate mechanism but expresses only the minimum observable relation necessary for the image.

### 5.4 One-world / epistemic restraint

The candidate must inhabit one shared reality.

Renderer language may establish observable asymmetry through position, visibility, orientation, occlusion, access to evidence, possession, distance or contact when the composition needs it. It must not require the image to prove what anyone knows, believes, remembers, intends or privately interprets.

Expression and posture may enrich characterization but cannot be the only evidence for a mandatory causal or epistemic claim.

### 5.5 Relational composition

The people and the changed condition must generate the composition together.

The specification should identify which spatial relationships matter — separation, proximity, overlap, orientation, interruption, negative space, partial visibility, contact, distributed attention, or another observable relation — without turning those options into a mandatory visual checklist.

Environment is subordinate. If the scene still carries essentially the same proposition after the people are mentally removed, the specification is already drifting toward S1 failure and should be corrected before rendering.

### 5.6 Quiet before spectacle

The specification must leave meaningful low-information or empty space available to carry hierarchy and tension.

Quiet is compositional function, not generic darkness, literal stage scenery, cone/spotlight transcription, or empty padding.

The changed condition should not require a single spectacular effect to be legible.

## 6. Responsive composition pre-commitment

Hero Brief 02 makes website integration part of the artwork burden. Responsive survival cannot be invented after seeing the image.

Before each packet is written, record a **Responsive Composition Plan**:

- one calm identity/copy region occupying roughly 35-45% of the desktop composition;
- one denser consequential region;
- a predeclared meaningful tall/mobile crop zone;
- confirmation that at least two necessary people plus changed-condition evidence are intended to survive that tall crop;
- the fixed crop/alignment rule that the deterministic integration harness will use after generation.

The renderer receives this as picture geometry, not as website/brand vocabulary.

Do not solve mobile after the fact by independently rerendering a second mobile scene. Mobile is tested as a crop/survival property of the same artwork candidate unless a later Director decision changes the product requirement.

Do not manually hunt for a favorable post-hoc crop. The crop rule must be frozen for that packet before generation.

## 7. Formal grammar control

### 7.1 R0 evidence inherited as method evidence

Completed R0 renderer work established two durable empirical facts relevant here:

1. leaving formal grammar open repeatedly invited high-probability historical/architectural/cinematic genre shortcuts;
2. explicit control of picture-plane, massing, negative space, contour, overlap and occlusion materially changed the output while keeping people structurally primary.

R0 calibration is closed. The current hero rounds are **not** new renderer-cartography probes.

Therefore every qualifying hero packet must deliberately control enough formal grammar to prevent `open artistic freedom` from becoming the dominant untracked variable.

### 7.2 Formal Grammar Declaration

Before renderer-facing prose is written, declare which picture-making variables are controlled and which are intentionally left free.

Available control dimensions include, without selecting any as Kymaean identity:

- picture-plane depth;
- human massing / figure completeness;
- contour / edge behavior;
- negative-space proportion and location;
- overlap / occlusion / separation;
- representational distance;
- environment-detail budget;
- tonal range and contrast organization;
- chroma distribution;
- material/surface treatment;
- texture density;
- lighting structure;
- medium/render treatment.

The packet need not specify every dimension. It must specify a coherent tightly coupled subset sufficient to make the dominant formal organization intentional rather than accidental.

If medium/render treatment is intentionally left open, record that decision as an uncontrolled variable before generation. Do not notice it only after the renderer chooses a familiar painterly/cinematic surface.

### 7.3 No aesthetic inheritance

Formal controls are experimental construction, not brand selection.

Do not use named artists, cultures, art movements, historical eras, competitor brands or previous generated images as shorthand for the intended grammar in the default qualifying method.

No palette, medium, garment, setting, object family, brush/line treatment, architecture or composition receives inheritance rights merely because it appears in a candidate that performs well.

## 8. Material / surface translation discipline

Mineral Theater remains Director-side material-role authority, not renderer subject matter.

Do not tell the renderer to depict slate, limestone, oxidized copper, minerals, patina or another literalized brand-material story merely to satisfy S3.

A future packet may use relative picture-making language sufficient to keep material/color behavior restrained and subordinate, for example by controlling overall chroma, contrast, texture density or material coherence. It should not convert Mineral Theater's role names into a palette checklist.

The raw candidate is later tested by the frozen S3 role-remap criterion:

- every material/surface signal actually used must be compatible with the approved role discipline;
- the candidate need not instantiate every material role;
- the evaluation may not recolor, repaint or edit the candidate before S3 is scored.

A conceptual role remap is an evaluation. A corrective recolor is a new artwork state and cannot be used to manufacture a pass.

## 9. Brand-geometry isolation

Threshold K and O3 stay outside the artwork.

The renderer packet must not include:

- Threshold K imagery or geometry;
- O3 imagery or glyph construction;
- instructions to hide, discover or complete a `K`;
- logo puzzles;
- brand marks;
- requests to make the image itself function as a second logo event.

S5 is tested after generation.

Accidental geometric rhyme is evaluated under S5; it is not pre-seeded by warning the renderer what the mark looks like.

## 10. Surface Escape preflight

The frozen Surface Escape gate is applied twice: once as packet-design preflight and again to the returned image.

Before generation ask:

- Does the candidate specification primarily describe a recognizable routine domestic/occupational task or ordinary accident response?
- If impossible/material physics were removed from the intended idea, would the remaining concept still be a conventional period/domestic/work genre tableau?

If yes, do not render that packet. Redesign the candidate specification before an image exists.

The renderer-facing packet may contain one compact gate-level negative constraint. Do not enumerate cleaning, farming, repair, cooking, construction and other historical failure examples; examples are visual seeds.

Secondary Surface Escape observations — fantasy/videogame drift, luxury/editorial drift, AI-tech/sci-fi drift, portal/threshold shorthand, generic cinematic-surrealism and single-effect dependence — remain recorded observations unless they independently violate another frozen hard requirement. They are not silently promoted to new Surface Escape gates by this method.

## 11. Artwork-only execution contract

A qualifying request asks for exactly **one uninterrupted artwork**.

No:

- text;
- captions;
- arrows or connectors;
- labels;
- app UI;
- website UI;
- storyboard/multi-panel explanation;
- presentation board;
- moodboard;
- logo lockup;
- palette swatches;
- promotional framing;
- surrounding experiment collateral.

This contract exists because the hero is artwork, not because these formats are universally forbidden elsewhere in Kymaean design.

## 12. Sterile Renderer Packet schema

After this method is separately approved, a future candidate-specific packet may be authored from the following schema.

This section defines the schema only. It does **not** instantiate Round 01 language.

### Director-only packet record

Record:

- packet ID and qualifying-round number;
- authority versions;
- candidate question;
- participant count and minimum identity anchors;
- changed shared condition;
- altered-present-possibility coupling;
- relational-composition intent;
- Responsive Composition Plan;
- Formal Grammar Declaration;
- controlled versus intentionally free variables;
- Surface Escape preflight result;
- anticipated known renderer attractor risks;
- exact renderer-facing text once separately authorized;
- renderer-context isolation state;
- requested output count = 1.

### Renderer-facing content classes

The future sterile packet may contain only the minimum needed from these classes:

1. human structure and distinctness;
2. visible changed shared condition + altered present possibility;
3. relational / responsive composition geometry;
4. selected formal-grammar controls and restrained surface behavior;
5. one compact Surface Escape constraint;
6. artwork-only return contract.

### Content excluded by default

Do not transfer:

- project or product naming;
- public/marketing thesis;
- scoring thresholds;
- S-gate labels;
- historical candidate names;
- prior outputs;
- long anti-attractor lists;
- narrative backstory not visually required;
- private-state assertions;
- named style/artist/era/culture shorthand;
- generic `AI`, software, app, website or brand framing;
- renderer-calibration language;
- instructions to explain the concept.

Reference images are excluded by default from qualifying rounds. A reference-image experiment requires a separate explicit Director decision naming the single intended transferred variable and still counts against the qualifying-round budget if it produces an evaluable candidate.

## 13. One qualifying round = one visible candidate

The qualifying-round counter begins at **0 / 3**.

Method design, packet design, packet correction before any image exists, deterministic integration-harness setup and evaluation preparation do not consume a round.

A qualifying round begins when an approved packet produces a visible candidate that is sufficiently judgeable against the frozen hard requirements.

### 13.1 One-output law

Request exactly one image.

No same-context alternate, correction chain, aesthetic retry, `one more version`, seed hunt, inpainting repair or second interpretation is permitted inside one qualifying round.

If the renderer returns an evaluable but weak/nonconforming visible image, **it counts**. Do not protect the budget by relabeling an unattractive, ordinary, branded, storyboard-like, semantically weak or surface-failing visible output as `method-invalid` merely because it fails.

### 13.2 Technical-null exception

An event does not consume a qualifying round only when no judgeable visual candidate exists, for example:

- no image is returned;
- the output is corrupt/unreadable;
- the provider/tool fails before producing a usable image;
- a safety/service refusal returns no candidate.

Any retry after a technical null must be logged. If wording must change for execution/safety/format reasons before any image exists, the diff and reason must be recorded and the semantic/formal intent may not be quietly redesigned under the guise of an execution repair.

If a renderer unexpectedly returns multiple images despite an explicit one-image request, archive all outputs and stop for Director accounting before selecting or rerunning. Do not silently cherry-pick one.

## 14. Qualifying rounds are hero experiments, not new calibration probes

Historical Renderer Cartography used one-variable contrastive experiments to learn renderer behavior. That calibration phase is closed.

The current three-round budget is too small and too consequential to spend on sacrificial calibration images.

Therefore:

- every rendered round must be intended as a genuine complete hero candidate against the unchanged frozen contract;
- no hidden image-bearing preflight generations are permitted;
- no uncounted style tests are permitted after this method enters qualifying execution;
- known renderer behavior should be taken from the existing R0 dataset instead of relearned through extra renders.

Textual packet alternatives, compositional diagrams or non-image reasoning may be used before a packet is approved as long as they do not create an image that could function as an uncounted candidate.

## 15. Material difference between rounds

A failed first round does not authorize a correction chain.

If another round is proposed, record **before generation**:

- the smallest failure set from the previous round;
- what the next packet preserves;
- what it changes;
- why the change is expected to test the unresolved problem rather than merely beautify the image;
- whether the planned candidate will be materially different under the frozen definition.

For the two-round suspected-unsatisfiable inference, candidates must be more than correction variants and differ substantially in at least two of:

1. composition;
2. human spatial arrangement;
3. changed-condition mechanism;
4. dominant material/surface organization.

A Director may intentionally authorize a narrower correction variant, but it still consumes a qualifying round if an evaluable image is returned and it cannot be paired with the prior image as `materially different` evidence unless the frozen definition is actually met.

Because of the three-round ceiling, narrow correction variants are disfavored by default.

## 16. Per-round Director stop

There is no automatic image sequence.

After every qualifying candidate:

1. archive the untouched raw output and exact packet;
2. evaluate it completely;
3. update round accounting;
4. apply the frozen stop conditions;
5. stop for Director review before authoring or executing another candidate-specific packet.

If a candidate passes the complete hard set, stop. It becomes eligible for Director hero-candidate review; do not spend another round by default.

If it fails, the next justified action is evidence synthesis and, only if allowed by the frozen stop rules, a new bounded packet proposal.

## 17. Deterministic integration harness

Brand Integration, Mobile Survival and S6 cannot depend on candidate-specific rescue work.

Before Round 01 is generated, freeze one deterministic hero-integration harness using current temporary website authority.

The harness must preserve:

- unchanged Threshold K and O3 assets as separate identity elements;
- the same representative website copy geometry across candidates;
- the same desktop frame/container rules across candidates;
- the packet's predeclared crop/alignment rule;
- the same mobile frame/crop rule across candidates;
- the untouched raw artwork pixels except for deterministic crop/scale/composite operations needed to place the artwork in the harness.

Do not alter the artwork through inpainting, object removal, repainting, recoloring, relighting, extension/generative outpainting or candidate-specific masking before it is scored.

The harness is evidence, not website implementation.

A candidate that only passes after bespoke rescue treatment has not passed as the qualifying raw candidate.

## 18. Evaluation order

The evaluation sequence is pre-registered to reduce aesthetic halo and preserve the independent gates.

### E0 — provenance / execution record

Archive before critique:

- raw output;
- exact approved packet;
- renderer context type;
- product/model surface when known;
- generation time/date;
- output count;
- technical deviations;
- asset location / identifier;
- qualifying-round number.

Unknown implementation details remain `UNKNOWN`; do not invent precision.

### E1 — technical validity

Determine whether a judgeable visual candidate exists.

If no: apply the technical-null rule.

If yes: the round counts and proceeds through evaluation even if the output is obviously weak.

### E2 — frozen Surface Escape hard gate

Apply the frozen operational question **before aesthetic preference**.

Record PASS/FAIL and the literal primary-reading rationale.

Do not let secondary drift observations replace this hard gate.

### E3 — Hero Brief 02 raw-artwork gates

Score independently, 0-5 where specified:

- Human necessity;
- Changed-condition legibility;
- Altered possibility;
- Epistemic restraint;
- Removal test.

Record evidence/rationale for each score. Do not average.

Brand Integration and Mobile Survival are completed after the deterministic harness/crop evidence exists.

Apply the Brief 02 immediate-failure list as written. Do not invent a new immediate-failure category.

### E4 — Surface ownership S1-S5 on raw artwork

Apply the frozen tests verbatim in meaning:

- S1 Human-first salience;
- S2 Theatrical absence / meaningful quiet;
- S3 Material-role discipline;
- S4 Authored restraint;
- S5 Discovery without emblem imitation.

Each is independent PASS/FAIL.

No candidate can compensate for one failed S-gate with strength elsewhere.

### E5 — deterministic desktop/mobile evidence

Apply the frozen integration harness.

Produce the standard desktop coexistence view and standard meaningful tall/mobile crop from the same raw candidate.

No bespoke rescue edit.

### E6 — remaining hard gates

Complete:

- Brand Integration score;
- Mobile Survival score;
- S6 Responsive asymmetry / identity coexistence.

Recheck whether the mobile crop still carries the Brief 02 minimum: at least two necessary people plus changed-condition evidence.

### E7 — complete-set result

A qualifying candidate advances only if:

- Human necessity >=4;
- Changed-condition legibility >=4;
- Altered possibility >=4;
- Epistemic restraint >=4;
- Brand Integration >=3;
- Mobile Survival >=3;
- Removal test >=4;
- Surface Escape = PASS;
- S1-S6 = PASS independently.

There is no compensating average.

### E8 — advisory / diagnostic observations

Only after the hard result is recorded, judge and archive:

- relational composition;
- Quiet Stage belonging;
- Mineral Theater compatibility beyond the hard S3 test;
- category distinctiveness;
- specific prior-author recoverability — advisory only;
- Surface Escape secondary observations;
- renderer-attractor lineage;
- artistic magnetism / Director taste as a separate selection consideration.

Advisory observations cannot rescue a hard failure and cannot be silently promoted into a new mandatory gate after seeing the output.

### E9 — historical comparison

Only after independent scoring, compare the candidate to G1/R1/A1, R0 evidence and other quarantined surfaces for diagnostic lineage.

This order prevents historical resemblance or user preference from biasing the initial frozen-gate reading.

## 19. Failure record and protected-requirement collision

For every failed qualifying round, record the **smallest failed requirement set**.

For each failure record:

- requirement ID/name;
- exact observed evidence;
- whether the failure is independent or appears caused by satisfying another protected requirement;
- any implicated renderer attractor;
- material-difference dimensions of the round;
- whether the evidence is strong enough to support a cross-round collision claim.

A protected-requirement collision requires evidence that satisfying one current requirement is causing failure of another. Mere simultaneous failure is not automatically a collision.

Do not rewrite a criterion to avoid recording a collision.

## 20. Frozen whole-approach stop behavior

This method inherits the frozen stop contract unchanged.

### Early suspected-unsatisfiable stop

As soon as two **materially different qualifying rounds** establish either:

1. the same hard requirement failing in both; or
2. the same protected-requirement collision;

the state immediately becomes:

**CONSTRAINT SET SUSPECTED UNSATISFIABLE / DIRECTOR RECONCILIATION REQUIRED**

Do not automatically run a third round.

### Three-round exhaustion

If no early repeated-failure/collision stop is established but three qualifying rounds all fail the complete set, the state becomes:

**THREE-ROUND EXPERIMENTAL EXHAUSTION / DIRECTOR REVIEW REQUIRED**

No fourth round is authorized.

Only the Director may renegotiate an implicated requirement according to the frozen Falsification Criteria 01 contract.

## 21. Passing does not equal selection

A complete hard-set PASS means the candidate has survived the pre-registered measurement contract.

It does **not** automatically mean:

- final hero;
- final art direction;
- final brand recognition;
- final material system;
- final palette;
- final character system;
- final website implementation.

A passing candidate becomes eligible for a separate Director selection/reverse-audit decision.

The Director may decline a technically passing candidate on broader artistic/strategic grounds without rewriting the measurement contract or pretending it failed a gate it actually passed.

## 23. Historical renderer evidence: what transfers and what does not

### Transferable method evidence

The new method may rely on these completed R0 findings:

- people can remain structurally primary across materially different formal grammars;
- relational geometry can carry meaning;
- identity can remain specific without fixed portrait dependence;
- private mental state is not reliably provable from a still image;
- open formal grammar invites high-probability renderer shortcuts;
- formal geometry is controllable through explicit picture-making constraints;
- concrete examples are visual seeds;
- brand/website framing can produce collateral instead of artwork;
- environment-first monumentality can turn people into scale figures;
- one static image has a semantic observability boundary;
- further R0 calibration renders are not justified.

### Non-transferable surface inheritance

Do not inherit R0 painterly realism, ink/wash, muted beige/brown, worn rooms, furniture constellations, workspaces, bridges/walls/thresholds, ruins, repair, accessibility shorthand, monuments, two-panel layouts, specific people, costumes, settings or compositions.

Do not restart R0 Renderer Cartography merely because its protocol remains useful historical evidence.

## 24. Method preflight checklist

Before a candidate-specific packet can be submitted for Director approval, the Director-side record must answer YES to all of the following:

1. Is the current frozen authority fresh-read?
2. Are at least two people necessary and non-interchangeable without relying only on color/face/position?
3. Is one changed shared condition visibly definable without requiring exact prior authorship?
4. Is another person's altered present possibility observably coupled to that condition?
5. Have private-state assertions been removed from mandatory meaning?
6. Does composition carry relationship rather than an explanatory device?
7. Is meaningful quiet structurally planned?
8. Is the desktop identity/copy region and mobile crop zone predeclared?
9. Is formal grammar intentionally controlled rather than left open by accident?
10. Is material/color/texture restraint defined without literalizing Mineral Theater?
11. Are Threshold K/O3/project names absent from the artwork packet?
12. Does the candidate specification pass Surface Escape preflight?
13. Is the packet free of long historical blacklists and concrete example menus?
14. Is exactly one uninterrupted artwork requested?
15. Is the renderer isolation state known?
16. Are output count and archival/accounting rules fixed before generation?
17. If this is Round 02/03, is the planned difference from prior rounds pre-recorded and is any claimed `materially different` status justified under the frozen definition?
18. Has the Director explicitly approved this specific packet before generation?

Any `NO` means stop before image generation.
