# Kymaean Renderer Isolation — First Cross-Branch Checkpoint

Status: **ACTIVE R0 RESEARCH CHECKPOINT — NO CONVERGENCE**

Purpose: preserve the first controlled renderer-isolation laboratory and its cross-branch discoveries without promoting any generated surface into Kymaean identity.

## 1. Method established

Clean-slate rendering now separates two contexts:

- **Director Context:** the persistent Ensemble project, with product truth, visual lineage, critique history, user signals, and evaluation law.
- **Renderer Context:** a new regular non-project chat, given only one sterile render packet and closed after one generated image.

Core law:

> **The director remembers everything. The canvas remembers nothing.**

The first isolated renders demonstrated that this separation materially changes output. The in-project R0 generation immediately reproduced branded collateral and familiar monumental/cinematic attractors; the first isolated render instead became a literal contemporary repair workshop.

## 3. Laboratory RI — Causal Physics

Purpose: determine which visual mechanisms can make prior human action, changed possibility, unequal knowledge, and persistent history legible in one still image.

### RI-001A — Sterile Literal Causality

Documentary repair-workshop scene. Strong causal readability and complete escape from prior project-surface attractors; almost no artistic-world discovery.

### RI-001B — Artistic Latitude on Identical Scene

Painterly version of the same workshop. Demonstrated partial independence of meaning and surface, but broad artistic latitude mostly changed medium rather than inventing a new world.

### RI-002 — Open-World Causality / Cinematic Fantasy Prior

Greater freedom increased aesthetic magnetism while reducing causal recoverability and activating generic cinematic-fantasy concept-art priors.

### RI-003 — Textile Consequence / Illustration Prior

Extremely clear causality, but over-specified story object caused the medium and aesthetic to rhyme with the narrative, producing folk/storybook illustration.

### RI-004 — Relationship First / Evidence Invented

Renderer-invented causal evidence drifted toward rustic historical craft-world imagery and remained too ambiguous.

### RI-005 — Clean Synthetic Causality

Broke the equation between history and antiquity. Demonstrated that changed state can be recent, maintained, clean, colorful, or modular. Also exposed accessibility-accommodation as a learned semantic shortcut.

### RI-006 — Mixed Motive / Renderer-Invented Mechanism

Escaped most old attractors but failed causal recoverability. Delegating both world and mechanism to the renderer is too unconstrained when history must be legible.

### RI-007 — Deliberate Division / Partial Visibility

Strong middle point. Director specified the mechanism; renderer invented the world. A translucent boundary changed access while preserving moral ambiguity and unequal knowledge.

### RI-008 — Interrupted Continuity

Stronger pictorial language. Demonstrated that history can be visible as interrupted alignment/correspondence rather than age or damage.

### RI-009 — Broken Pair / Symbolic Mechanism Failure

Abstract pair/mechanism language became a monumental gold lock/key-like object. Demonstrated that examples and abstract mechanism language are visual seeds, not neutral explanation.

### RI-010 — Exception Against Pattern

Repeated boat/berth relationships established a normal rule; one reassigned berth made prior organization reconstructable. Also demonstrated that optional concrete examples are likely to be selected literally by the renderer.

### RI-011 — Pattern Without Examples

Repeated horse stalls and one reassigned stall preserved causal readability without concrete examples. This closed the initial RI series successfully.

## 4. RI transferable laws

- **Present state can prove prior state.**
- **Exception against repetition can reveal history.**
- **Interrupted correspondence can reveal former continuity or belonging.**
- **Boundaries matter when they alter actual possibility, not merely symbolize separation.**
- **Partial visibility can express unequal access to one authoritative reality.**
- **Consequence is strongest when it changes affordance or constraint.**
- **Attention, possession, interrupted action, posture, and orientation can expose unequal knowledge.**
- **History does not require antiquity, damage, patina, ruins, flashbacks, or magical traces.**
- **Meaning engineering and art direction are separate jobs.**

## 5. Laboratory AL — Artistic Language

Purpose: discover genuinely different artistic grammars without demanding that every image explain the product thesis.

### AL-A01 — Distributed Presence / Melancholic Atrium Prior

Quiet human states, but scenery remained the protagonist.

### AL-A02 — Figure-Constructed Composition

People became structurally necessary to the composition; relationships remained too psychologically isolated.

### AL-A03 — Relational Current

Attention, avoidance, and response began to organize the field. Surface drifted toward contemporary painterly editorial/social portraiture.

### AL-B01 — Graphic Human Field

Strong orthogonal divergence. Negative space between people functioned as relational geometry; bodies created hierarchy without realistic depth; specificity survived abstraction.

### AL-C01 — Material Human Relief

People and world became one material system. Occlusion, openings, seams, gaps, and structural interference carried social asymmetry. Individuality weakened under the material system.

### AL-D01 — Line, Gesture & Void

Empty distance and sparse marks carried relationship. Gesture connected people without a shared object; mark density changed perceived participation.

### AL-E01 — Overlap & Changed Color

One of the strongest conceptual results. Distinct figures altered color/form where they intersected while remaining traceable as individuals.

> Interaction can alter the shared visual field without erasing participant identity.

### AL-F01 — Indirect Presence Through Light

Branch hypothesis mostly failed; the renderer produced atmospheric sunlight rather than person-caused illumination relationships. Reflection remained a useful partial-access mechanism.

### AL-G01 — Fragmented Presence / Identity Attribution Failure

Incomplete human representation remained powerful, but recurring fragments could not be reliably attributed to recurring people.

> Stylistic continuity alone does not create identity continuity.

### AL-H01 — Identity Through Invariants

Strongest identity-system proof so far. Three people remained recognizable across scale, crop, orientation, pose, completeness, and viewpoint through combined perceptual invariants rather than one permanent face.

Potential invariant families include silhouette, bodily proportion, garment construction, hair geometry, characteristic movement/gesture, and restrained color association.

> Transformation + persistent perceptual invariants can create durable identity without a fixed portrait.

## 6. AL transferable laws

- **Human structural importance:** the strongest images collapse compositionally if the people are removed.
- **Relationship as visual geometry:** distance, orientation, overlap, negative space, interruption, occlusion, and recurrence can carry social meaning.
- **Distinct identity without portrait dependence:** a character can remain specific through a family of perceptual invariants.
- **Identity can survive transformation:** scale, crop, pose, viewpoint, completeness, and context can change while recognition persists.
- **Influence without merger:** interaction can change a shared visual condition while individuals remain legible.
- **Partial access:** openings, occlusion, reflection, interruption, and selective visibility can make one person differently available from different positions.
- **Absence is active:** gaps, missing portions, negative space, and interrupted forms can carry relational information.
- **The shared world need not be scenery:** it can emerge from relationships among bodies, gaps, overlaps, recurrence, and material conditions.

## 7. First cross-lab system hypothesis

RI contributes **causal physics**: how present conditions can prove prior action, how consequence changes possibility, how history persists in current structure, and how unequal understanding can become visually legible.

AL contributes **artistic physics**: how people can become the composition, how relationships structure space, how identities persist across transformed representations, and how interaction changes shared visual conditions without merging people.

The strongest combined direction is therefore **not a selected aesthetic**. It is a system hypothesis:

> Distinct people are structurally important; relationships can alter the shared visual field; identities persist across transformed representations; visibility/access can be asymmetric; and present arrangements can contain evidence of prior human causes.

This selects no palette, medium, architecture, portrait style, material system, symbol, layout, or website surface.

This includes workshop realism, painterly workshop texture, cinematic fantasy, folk/quilt illustration, rustic craft worlds, clean civic/accessibility editorial illustration, movable studio partitions, modern figurative mural geometry, monumental paired mechanisms, boats, stables, melancholic atriums, painterly social montage, modernist geometric mural language, cubist/bas-relief construction, expressive line studies, translucent watercolor/cut-paper treatment, sparse sunlit-room realism, beige fragmented figures, and H01's painterly repeated-character montage.

A later branch may independently rediscover any surface only if newly earned from first principles.

## 9. Renderer prompt laws recovered

- Use a fresh regular non-project renderer chat; one generated image per renderer chat is now the preferred laboratory discipline.
- Do not transfer Kymaean/Ensemble naming, historical imagery, project files, branch names, or visual-history blacklists into clean-slate packets.
- **Examples are visual seeds.** Do not provide examples of what the renderer is free to invent unless deliberately selecting those seeds.
- When causal recoverability matters, the Director specifies the smallest physical/causal truth that must be legible; the renderer invents the surrounding world.
- Do not ask the renderer to invent both a complex world and a complex causal mechanism when the result must be reconstructable from one still image.
- Broad world-building/artistic-latitude language activates strong learned genre priors.
- Over-specified narrative objects can make the aesthetic literally rhyme with the story.
- Do not confuse history with age, humanity with handcrafted patina, consequence with damage, benevolence with accessibility accommodation, or originality with cinematic worldbuilding.

## 10. Next research rule

The next round should begin from the cross-branch laws and test whether several surviving principles can coexist in a genuinely new artistic grammar without collapsing into:

- a recent surface attractor;

- a causal diagram;
- a character sheet;
- a product/brand explanation.

No convergence is authorized by this checkpoint.
