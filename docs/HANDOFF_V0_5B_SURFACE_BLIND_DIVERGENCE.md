## New abstract discoveries earned in V0.5A

Carry forward only the following **visual-physics discoveries**. None selects a style or composition.

### 1. History can become habitat

Past action can be legible through the present condition of a shared reality rather than through flashbacks, timelines, labels, or explanatory diagrams.

The present should be capable of feeling historically specific because earlier people changed the conditions that now exist.

### 2. Consequence can become affordance or constraint

A prior action should be capable of changing what another person can do next.

The most useful visual translation is not merely a visible mark. It is a changed possibility: something becomes newly usable, inaccessible, easier, harder, safer, riskier, more inviting, more limited, or otherwise materially different because someone acted before.

### 3. Bounded perspective can be experiential

Different people can inhabit one authoritative reality while perceiving different portions of it.

Mystery should be capable of arising from position, timing, attention, access, incomplete evidence, or differing experience rather than from arbitrary obscurity.

### 4. Shared reality does not imply shared understanding

Several people may affect the same environment or situation without possessing the same knowledge, interpretation, goal, or intention.

Do not visually collapse ensemble activity into synchronized collaboration or collective authorship.

### 5. Provenance can live in the world

A changed state can carry evidence that it came from distinct prior actions without requiring a literal diagram of causality.

The visual world may imply that different interventions had different origins, ages, intentions, and consequences.

### 6. People should live inside consequences

The strongest direction is not humans observing an abstract effect from outside. People should inherit, use, misunderstand, resist, repair, exploit, adapt to, or further change conditions produced by earlier action.

### 7. Human causality needs friction as well as care

A consequential world should support compatible intentions and incompatible ones: misunderstanding, disagreement, withholding, accidental side effects, asymmetric benefit, interruption, affection, refusal, repair, compromise, or surprise.

Do not make social harmony a default simply because multiple people share a scene.

## Generation-isolation rule

The first V0.5B artwork must be generated from a **positive human situation**, not from a list of forbidden motifs and not from a reconstructed summary of prior imagery.

The image-generation instruction should describe:

1. an ordinary or emotionally legible human circumstance;
2. evidence that the present conditions were altered by prior people;
3. at least one current person acting from incomplete or bounded perspective;
4. a consequential action whose effect changes a later person's possibility, constraint, or circumstance;
5. enough ambiguity that the viewer can infer history without being told the design thesis.

Do not use product, brand, marketing, website, app-icon, moodboard, identity-system, design-system, or presentation-board language in the artwork prompt. Those terms have repeatedly biased generation toward collateral rather than art.

## Artwork-only contract

The first V0.5B output is **one uninterrupted full-canvas artwork**.

- palette swatches;

The artwork should make the causal experience felt before any explanation is supplied.

If the generator nevertheless inserts branding or presentation structure, treat that output as an execution failure rather than a visual candidate. Do not refine the contaminated board. Reissue the artwork-only premise or move to a cleaner generation context.

## First V0.5B positive premise

The next chat should independently interpret this premise without adding inherited surface vocabulary:

> **Someone enters a situation already shaped by people who came before, acts without access to the whole story, and leaves a change that materially alters what becomes possible for someone else.**

This is a causal premise, not a composition prescription.

It does not require visible sequential panels, literal before/after imagery, explicit repair, a communal object, a built environment, fantasy, realism, any particular number of people, or any particular artistic medium.

The assistant should choose an artistically specific interpretation confidently and generate the work rather than reopening process questions.

## Evaluation after generation

Evaluate the artwork internally against stable dimensions, not resemblance to previous work:

- emotional magnetism;
- distinctiveness;
- human importance;
- Kymaean causality;
- historical specificity;
- bounded perspective;
- evidence of provenance;
- consequence changing future possibility;
- relational complexity;
- mystery;
- artistic integrity;
- expandability into a broader visual system;
- attractor risk;
- surprise.

Do not collapse this into a numeric score.

A beautiful image that merely symbolizes causality is weaker than an image in which the current situation could not exist in the same form without prior human action.
