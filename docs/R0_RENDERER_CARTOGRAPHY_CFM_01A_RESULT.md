## Probe

`CFM-01A — Flat Mass / Negative Space`

Fixed semantic kernel:

> Three distinct people share one present moment. One person takes a visible action that changes the relationship among the three. The other two respond differently in ways that make unequal understanding legible. The people must carry the structure and meaning.

Formal package:

- shallow picture plane;
- large contiguous human shapes;
- deliberate negative space;
- minimal depth cues;
- proximity, overlap, separation and void carry relationship;
- surroundings subordinate and visually simple;
- individual recognizability through silhouette, bodily proportion, posture and characteristic gesture;
- even tonal field with moderate contrast.

## Result

The returned image strongly follows the formal package. Three people dominate a nearly empty shallow field. The central figure physically links toward both others; one lower-left figure actively grasps while the standing right figure holds back. The renderer independently chose a restrained painterly figurative treatment, but did not produce a deep scenic environment.

### Formal-grammar gate

**PASS — STRONG.**

- shallow picture plane achieved;
- human masses dominate;
- negative space is structurally active;
- depth cues remain minimal;
- surroundings remain subordinate;
- relationship is carried by bodies, gesture, spacing and overlap.

### Human structural importance

**PASS — STRONG.**

Removing the people would remove essentially all composition, action, tension and meaning.

### Identity / distinctness

**PASS for this single-image probe.**

The people are distinguishable through silhouette, scale/proportion, posture, clothing mass and characteristic gesture. Matrix 01 does not test identity persistence across transformed representations.

### Relational / semantic gate

**PARTIAL-STRONG.**

The present action and unequal responses are legible. Exact before/after relational change remains inferential rather than reconstructable.

### Unequal understanding

**PARTIAL.**

Different response is visually clear, but unequal knowledge/understanding cannot be independently proven from posture alone.

## Attractor result

The old monumental architecture / bridge / threshold / period-world / deep-cinematic attractor family did **not** recur.

The renderer still selected painterly figurative realism as an unconstrained medium, but formal control prevented that medium from becoming an environment-first historical or cinematic world.

This clears the Matrix 01 early-stop condition after A and warrants B.

## Transferable discovery

> **Explicit picture-plane depth, human massing, negative-space and environmental-subordination controls can materially reduce recurrence of environment-first spectacle while preserving a legible human relational field.**

This is comparative project evidence, not strict proof that one isolated factor caused the historical CSR-E04 output. Matrix 01 is not a one-variable reproduction of E04.
