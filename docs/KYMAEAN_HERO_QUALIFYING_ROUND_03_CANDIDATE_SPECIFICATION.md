# KYMAEAN — HERO QUALIFYING ROUND 03 CANDIDATE SPECIFICATION

Status: **SPECIFICATION COMPLETE / DIRECTOR REVIEW REQUIRED / NO RENDER AUTHORIZED**
Date: 2026-09-06
Mechanism direction: **DIRECTOR APPROVED — GRAVITY-DEPENDENT SUPPORT-STATE CAUSALITY**

## 1. Purpose

Define one pre-render Candidate 03 semantic/compositional architecture under the Director-approved mechanism class while preserving every frozen hero requirement.

This document is **not** a renderer packet and does not authorize image generation. It pre-registers what Candidate 03 is intended to test, what the returned pixels must make visible, and what counts as failure before any renderer request exists.

The qualifying counter remains **2 / 3**.

## 2. Candidate question

Can one static hero artwork make altered present possibility and the Removal Test directly observable by using **gravity-dependent human support** rather than another separator/access mechanism — while keeping two specific people primary, the changed state recoverable, the scene outside ordinary-task genre, the material field subordinate, and the fixed website identity surfaces viable?

The causal proposition is:

> **pre-existing changed support state -> body-load support that the recoverable baseline state cannot provide -> a present human position exists that would physically fail under baseline restoration**

The image must make this proposition visible without explanatory text, private-state inference, an earlier frame, a familiar piece of furniture, or a spectacular engineered device.

## 3. Human structure

Candidate 03 contains **exactly two distinct adults**.

Both must be:

- visually substantial and human-primary;
- specific and non-interchangeable;
- readable through redundant identity cues such as body proportion, silhouette, hair, garment construction, posture and contact geometry rather than color alone;
- independent occupants of one shared condition, not helper/recipient, rescuer/endangered person, controller/controlled pair, performer/audience pair or worker/client pair.

Neither adult is shown creating, repairing, holding, tensioning, operating or maintaining the changed material state. The deformation already exists before the depicted moment.

### Asymmetric semantic jobs

The adults should not merely duplicate one another.

**Adult A — necessity anchor**

- occupies the clearest gravity-dependent body position;
- has substantial body load visibly borne by the changed deformation;
- has no visually credible alternative support at the same depicted position if the material is restored to its recoverable baseline;
- makes the altered possibility / counterfactual collapse legible.

**Adult B — shared-condition anchor**

- is comparably specific and visually substantial;
- occupies a different physical relation to another continuous portion of the same changed field;
- helps establish that the material condition is a shared human environment rather than a private prop or solitary sculptural device;
- must itself have a materially consequential relation to the changed field, not stand apart as witness, observer or scale figure.

Adult B need not duplicate Adult A's strongest gravity proof. Its role is to make the changed field visibly shared and lived while remaining physically engaged enough that removing B materially weakens the human/relational reading.

**Pre-registered Human Necessity failure:** if Adult B can be removed with the image retaining essentially the same complete human/causal meaning, or if either adult reads mainly as a scale figure, witness, helper, model posing for a sculpture, or decorative occupant, Human Necessity fails.

## 4. Baseline state and changed condition

Use **one broad continuous opaque matte material field**, not a collection of objects.

The image must show enough uninterrupted portions of that same field in a simpler, near-planar / low-relief state that a viewer can recover its baseline organization directly from the picture.

Within the center-right human cluster, one large human-scale portion of the field has undergone a **broad, pre-existing continuous deformation**. The deformation must:

- remain visibly continuous with the baseline portions on more than one side;
- be legible through contour, fold/buckle direction, surface continuity, edge continuation and/or repeated structural alignment rather than color coding;
- create a genuinely load-bearing three-dimensional relation to at least Adult A;
- remain broad and integrated, not become a detachable object, gadget, seat, platform, bridge, frame, portal or apparatus;
- appear already settled rather than actively moving or being manipulated.

The changed state may use curvature, inversion, buckling, compression or another continuous support-producing deformation within the approved gravity mechanism class. Exact material chemistry and fabrication history remain unspecified.

**Pre-registered Changed-Condition failure:** if the image reads as an arbitrary sculptural form with no recoverable simpler baseline, as a separate object attached to a field, or as ordinary furniture/architecture rather than a changed state of one continuous material field, Changed-condition legibility fails.

## 5. Altered present possibility — binding semantic test

The depicted possibility is **stable human bodily occupancy at a specific position under gravity**.

For Adult A, the changed deformation must visibly carry enough body load that the current body position cannot remain physically stable at the same place if that deformed segment is mentally restored to the visible baseline state.

This is stronger than:

- the changed field making a pose more comfortable;
- giving a convenient place to sit;
- raising or lowering someone while leaving equivalent support underneath;
- bringing two people closer while they could still reach one another;
- acting as scenery beside a human relation.

The image should contain **visible negative space or otherwise unambiguous absence of alternate support** beneath/behind the load-dependent body relation. The viewer must not have to assume hidden furniture, an unseen floor, a harness, a rigid bracket or another support outside the frame.

### Frozen counterfactual / Removal Test

Mentally restore the changed portion to the baseline organization already visible elsewhere in the same field.

PASS is possible only if:

1. the load-bearing contact geometry supporting Adult A disappears or moves away;
2. Adult A's depicted body position at that location therefore cannot remain stable against gravity;
3. the image's core living-consequence relation materially collapses rather than becoming merely less convenient or less aesthetically interesting.

**Pre-registered Altered Possibility / Removal failure:** if the same body position could plausibly remain with the baseline restored, if another visible surface would still support the body, or if the consequence reduces to comfort, proximity, symbolism or elevation preference, both Altered Possibility and Removal Test fail.

## 6. Shared living consequence

The hero must read as **two people inhabiting one changed physical condition**, not as a demonstration of material mechanics.

Required:

- the two adults occupy one coherent human-scale spatial field;
- their positions and the continuous material deformation form one compositionally interdependent cluster;
- Adult B's relation to the changed field differs from Adult A's and contributes to the shared-condition reading;
- the image contains no claim about what either person knows, remembers, believes, intends or feels;
- expression and gaze may exist naturally but cannot carry the required causal meaning.

There is no requirement that the image reveal who caused the deformation.

## 7. Surface Escape / anti-literalization preflight

The depicted moment is a condition already being lived, not a task.

Hard exclusions for Candidate 03:

- ordinary domestic/occupational activity;
- repair, installation, construction, cleanup or accident response;
- rescue, injury treatment, escape, stunt, climbing challenge or athletic feat;
- ritual, ceremony or staged performance;
- recognizable chair, bench, bed, hammock, cot, shelf, ledge, platform, staircase, bridge, scaffold or conventional architectural support as the explanatory carrier;
- straps, ropes, cables, brackets, visible machinery or engineered rigging that turn the support relation into a contraption;
- portal/opening/crossing as the causal mechanism;
- interface, diagram, storyboard, labels, arrows or explanatory graphics.

The impossible/unusual material geometry must be necessary to the image. If it is removed and the remaining picture becomes a coherent conventional scene of people sitting, resting, working, climbing, being rescued or posing around furniture/architecture, Surface Escape fails.

## 8. Anti-spectacle / human-first construction

The gravity proof must not make the material structure the protagonist.

Required:

- people read before mechanism;
- both adult figures are approximately half the image height or more where feasible within the wide composition;
- the material deformation occupies only the amount of visual complexity needed to establish baseline, change and support;
- no giant machine, monumental architecture, impossible void, glowing energy, magical levitation, hazardous precipice or cinematic danger cue;
- no visual climax built around falling, rescue or imminent failure;
- broad soft/non-spectacular light;
- low environmental detail and substantial meaningful quiet;
- selective contour / restrained modeling rather than high-density surface spectacle.

**Pre-registered S1 failure:** if the field is the first or dominant subject and the people read as occupants of an impressive structure, S1 fails even if the mechanics are legible.

This tonal requirement is functional, not a final palette selection. The artwork is not required to be globally dark.

### Mobile

That crop must retain:

No alternate mobile render, focal shift or candidate-specific crop is permitted.

**Pre-registered S6 failure:** if desktop or the fixed mobile crop removes the semantic evidence necessary for altered possibility / Removal Test or reduces Adult B to a fragment/witness, S6 fails even if the uncropped artwork is strong.

## 11. Epistemic restraint

The pixels may establish only externally visible facts:

- two distinct adults are present;
- one continuous field has a recoverable baseline and a changed deformation;
- body load is visibly borne by that changed deformation;
- Adult A's current stable position would lose its visible support under baseline restoration;
- Adult B has a materially consequential relation to the same shared condition.

The image must not depend on claims about authorship, memory, knowledge, belief, intention, emotion, consent or future action.

## 12. Material difference from prior qualifying rounds

2. **human arrangement:** two substantial adults in body-load relations rather than three separated scale-like occupants;
3. **mechanism:** gravity-dependent support rather than rigid spatial separation / viewpoint-dependent availability;

## 14. Candidate 03 pre-registered failure summary

Candidate 03 fails if any frozen hard criterion fails. Especially:

1. **Support ambiguity:** Adult A could remain in the same position after baseline restoration.
2. **Hidden-support ambiguity:** floor/furniture/rigging/another surface could explain the same stability.
3. **Furniture collapse:** the changed field reads primarily as chair/bed/hammock/ledge/platform or equivalent familiar support.
4. **Task/accident collapse:** the image reads primarily as ordinary work, recreation, rescue, stunt, injury or routine response.
5. **Sculpture collapse:** people become models/witnesses around an impressive material installation.
6. **Adult B redundancy:** removing Adult B leaves essentially the same complete human/causal meaning.
7. **Baseline ambiguity:** the viewer cannot recover what state is being mentally restored.
8. **Spectacle substitution:** danger, levitation, glow or impossible architecture carries meaning instead of visible support mechanics.
9. **Desktop integration failure:** calm left field is too light/complex for frozen identity/copy without rescue.
10. **Mobile semantic loss:** fixed crop loses either person, baseline evidence, load-bearing deformation or gravity-dependent relation.

There is no compensating average.
