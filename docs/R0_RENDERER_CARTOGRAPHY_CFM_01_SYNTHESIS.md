## Purpose

Controlled Formal Matrix 01 tested whether one relational semantic kernel could remain legible across materially different Director-controlled formal grammars while reducing recurrence of the prior environment-first architectural/cinematic attractor family.

Fixed semantic kernel:

> Three distinct people share one present moment. One person takes a visible action that changes the relationship among the three. The other two respond differently in ways that make unequal understanding legible. The people must carry the structure and meaning.

## Matrix-level findings

### 1. Formal grammar is controllable

The current renderer materially responds to explicit picture-plane, massing, negative-space, contour, void, overlap and occlusion instructions. A, B and C are meaningfully different formal organizations despite sharing substantially the same semantic route.

### 2. Human structural importance survives formal variation

Across all three probes, the people themselves carry essentially all structure, action and meaning. The previous failure mode in which architecture became protagonist did not recur.

### 3. Individual attribution survives these three grammars

A, B and C preserve distinct people through combinations of silhouette, bodily proportion, posture, hair geometry, orientation and characteristic gesture.

This is single-image attribution evidence only. Matrix 01 does not test identity persistence across transformed representations.

### 4. The old environment-first attractor was strongly suppressed

No Matrix 01 image returned to monumental inherited architecture, literal bridge/threshold logic, ruins, period-world scenery or deep cinematic environmental spectacle.

This is strong comparative evidence that explicit formal control plus people-dominant composition can materially reduce those prior renderer shortcuts.

### 5. Matrix 01 does not isolate one causal variable

The historical comparison to CSR-E04 is not a strict A/B test. Participant count, observable-action wording, people-dominant composition and formal controls were fixed together.

Do not claim that one isolated prompt factor caused the historical architecture/bridge outputs.

### 6. The semantic bottleneck is now clearer than the surface bottleneck

Across A, B and C, visible action and different responses are legible, but the exact before/after relational state remains inferential.

Unequal understanding is also only partial because a still image cannot independently prove what each person knows from expression/posture alone.

Repeating more surface variations with the same semantic packet is unlikely to solve this limitation.

### 7. Medium/render treatment is a separate uncontrolled variable

A and C independently returned to painterly figurative realism while B selected gestural ink/wash.

Formal geometry remained controlled despite this. Therefore geometry, composition and medium should be treated as separable variables in future Renderer Cartography.

Do not infer a selected painterly Kymaean aesthetic from recurrence.

### 8. Memory-negation prompts are not required for useful control

Matrix 01 used the established disposable non-project Renderer Context without memory-negation preambles. Controlled formal instructions produced substantially different surfaces and suppressed the old environment-first family.

This does not prove account context is absent. It establishes that experimentally controllable prompt variables are more actionable than unverifiable memory-negation language.
