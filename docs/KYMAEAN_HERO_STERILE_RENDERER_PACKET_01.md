Create exactly one uninterrupted landscape artwork centered on three distinct adults whose presence is necessary to the image.

They occupy one shared, shallow material field organized by broad interlocking planes rather than by a detailed conventional setting. Across the center-right, a connected band of several interlocking planes has been visibly shifted out of the otherwise coherent alignment. Treat this as one shared changed condition distributed through the field, not as a separate object. The displacement changes physical availability between the people: from one person's position there is now an unobstructed line to a second person's figure, while from the third person's position the same shared field still materially occludes that second person. Show only the observable spatial condition; do not claim what anyone knows, believes, remembers or intends.

Keep roughly the left 40% calm and low-information while using it as part of the image's balance. Concentrate the three people and the changed-field evidence in the center-right, with at least two people and the displaced condition surviving a tall crop of that region.

Use shallow depth, broad figure-ground masses, selective contours, deliberate overlap and occlusion, substantial negative space, restrained modeling, low texture density, a narrow low-saturation tonal range, matte material behavior and broad soft light. Keep the people recognizably human, individually specific and non-interchangeable through more than color or facial likeness. The unusual field should remain one continuous shallow material reality rather than becoming a separate spectacular object.

Do not organize the image around a recognizable ordinary domestic or occupational task, or a routine accident response.

Return exactly one uninterrupted artwork only. No text, captions, labels, arrows, panels, storyboard, interface, branding, logo, palette display, presentation layout or explanatory surround.
