## Phase 1 — Semantic observability progression

### SOV-01A — Pattern + exception

Result: pattern repetition and visible departure were readable; active causal linkage remained weak.

Discovery:

> Repetition can establish a present comparison baseline, but pose difference alone does not prove active causal change.

### SOV-02 — Actor transition

Result: visible transition improved strongly; the consequence remained mostly a change in the actor's own state.

Discovery:

> Motion is not relational consequence.

### SOV-03 — Shared relational consequence

Result: group geometry visibly changed; causal authorship remained ambiguous.

Discovery:

> The renderer can depict relational consequence more readily than directional causal authorship.

### SOV-04 — Visible authorship

Result: the acting person became physically continuous with the changed group configuration. Authorship became much stronger, but the meaning of the action remained ambiguous and drifted toward separation/redirect/control.

Discovery:

> Visible authorship is possible, but direct physical causality tends to introduce hierarchy or force semantics.

### SOV-05 — Internal comparison attempt

Result: action, relational tension and consequence coexisted, but the immediately prior/common relation remained underdetermined.

Discovery:

> Relational tension is not enough to encode a recoverable reference state.

## Phase 2 — Persistent trace / reference state

### RST-01A — Persistent human trace

Result: visible relational continuity survived the transition, but the renderer mapped the trace to conflict / choosing-sides semantics.

Discovery:

> Human relational traces can carry history, but unresolved contact strongly attracts interpersonal conflict.

### RST-02A — Constructive trace

Result: connection persistence passed; expanded possibility remained ambiguous and drifted toward guidance/assistance.

Discovery:

> Connection is not the same as enabled possibility.

### RST-03A — Emergent agency

Result: independent agency was preserved; causal linkage became weaker when physical guidance was removed.

Discovery:

> Agency is easy to preserve once control is removed, but causal dependency without control is difficult to make visible.

### RST-03B — Shared-state transition

Result: all three people retained independent agency and shared relational state, but who changed what became underdetermined.

Discovery:

> The research target lies at the intersection of visible influence and independent agency.

## Phase 3 — Consequence visibility

### RST-04A — Divergent possibility / asymmetric condition

Result: changed condition and asymmetric relational/evidentiary position were strong; causal origin remained partial.

Discovery:

> Asymmetry is a stronger visual carrier of consequence than direct event depiction.

### RST-04B — Origin trace

Result: persistent relational consequence and shared human history were strongly suggested without showing an event. Specific authorship remained unresolved.

Discovery:

> Relationship states can imply history more reliably than they imply an unseen originating actor.

### RST-04C — Authorship without action

Result: strongest partial evidence that a present condition can feel human-authored while all people retain agency. The arrangement could still be read as ordinary personality difference rather than consequence.

Discovery:

> A still image can suggest people living inside conditions shaped by human choices, but an origin signal remains fragile when every visible carrier is prohibited.

### RST-04D — Origin signal

Result: persistent human history remained visible but drifted toward melancholy/isolation/shared aftermath. Constructive agency weakened.

Discovery:

> Shared consequence is not shared creation. Removing too much causal structure produces aftermath rather than generative possibility.

## Phase 4 — Enabled possibility

### RST-05A — Enabled possibility without a carrier

Result: human identity, relational field and independent agency passed. Enabled possibility failed.

Discovery:

> Independent agency does not demonstrate enabled agency. An invisible capability property cannot be reliably recovered from posture, gaze and spacing alone.

### RST-05B — Persistent human-created condition — CORRECT FIVE-PERSON IMAGE

Result:

- human specificity: strong pass;
- distributed importance: strong pass;
- independent agency: strong pass;
- relational field: strong pass;
- visible persistent human-created condition: fail;
- recoverable causal history: weak/fail;
- enabled possibility: fail.

The renderer interpreted the abstract request for a persistent human-created enabling condition primarily as a diverse autonomous group sharing a social field.

Discovery:

> **When a persistent enabling condition is described abstractly while concrete carriers are prohibited, the renderer collapses the condition into the social grouping itself.**

This is the principal result of the completed sequence.

## Durable semantic findings

The following are supported strongly enough to use as R0 empirical design constraints:

1. **People can remain structurally primary across controlled formal grammars.**
2. **Individual attribution can survive substantial formal simplification.**
3. **Relational geometry can carry action, attention, continuity and asymmetry.**
4. **Direct physical causality improves attribution but tends to create control/help/conflict semantics.**
5. **Removing physical control preserves agency but weakens visible causal linkage.**
6. **Persistent human relational traces can imply a prior state.**
7. **Asymmetric evidentiary/relational position is more defensible than claims about private knowledge.**
8. **Human-history language without constructive structure tends to drift toward aftermath, melancholy or isolation.**
9. **Independent agency is not the same as enabled agency.**
10. **A persistent system property needs a visible carrier if the image is expected to make that property recoverable.**

## Methodological correction

The sequence has reached diminishing returns from purely human-only abstract prompting.

The project should no longer demand that an invisible property such as `persistent enabled possibility` be inferred only from posture/gaze/spacing while simultaneously forbidding every visible carrier of persistent condition.

The next research lane is therefore:

> **R0 Renderer Cartography — Persistent Affordance Carrier Design**

The test must deliberately permit a visible carrier while controlling its role tightly enough that it remains ordinary, subordinate and non-symbolic.

## Persistent Affordance Carrier hypothesis

A still image may communicate the desired chain more reliably when it contains:

> **human-made persistent condition -> multiple independent present uses -> people remain primary**

The carrier is not the identity and not a metaphor. It exists only to make `history + persistence + changed capability` visually observable.

### Required carrier properties

The carrier must be:

- visibly human-made or human-shaped;
- persistent rather than momentary;
- ordinary rather than monumental;
- non-symbolic;
- subordinate to the people;
- usable by more than one person in distinct ways;
- compatible with independent agency;
- legible without implying rescue, guidance, triumph or a single beneficiary;
- free of bridge/door/path/threshold/crossing shorthand.

### Current design gate

No render is authorized yet.

Before generation, the Director must design the smallest carrier class that satisfies those requirements while avoiding a named setting, culture, era, branded technology or literal example that would over-seed the renderer.

The carrier itself is now the controlled experimental variable.
