## 2. SIPD-V1 attempt 1 — expanded context arrived too early

Conversation render identifier:

`6795ea29-f1c4-4c47-b419-b29f0b6c83e7`

The renderer produced a human-first Stage with an already-open contextual evidence inspector.

### Positive evidence

The render demonstrated that:

- the human relational field can remain primary while contextual evidence is open;
- evidence can appear as a dismissible adjacent surface rather than a permanent dossier rail;
- Current Opportunity C remains independent from Character-B perspective and from obligation;
- no permanent transcript/history/diagnostics are required;
- a still, quiescent UI can carry the semantic state.

### Material failures / cautions

It did not prove the **default closed Stage** because the inspector was already open.

The inspector also overreached by:

- exposing several evidence classes simultaneously;
- using `A is listening`, which risks promoting visible/social arrangement into epistemic interpretation;
- naming `C's private reasoning` under `Unknown to Me`, which can reveal the existence of an inaccessible private category that B may not legitimately know exists.

The bottom universal `What would you like to explore?` composer also pulled the product toward generic AI-assistant architecture and gave too much persistent prominence to an optional exploratory action while Watch posture was active.

The left editorial slogan was thematic narration rather than current Production information.

Judgment:

> **PARTIAL PASS — CONTEXTUAL-INSPECTOR VIABILITY SHOWN / DEFAULT-STAGE PURITY NOT TESTED.**

## 5. Remaining partials

SIPD-V1B does not select a final Stage surface because several issues remain open:

### 5.1 Duplicate Character representation

The full human field already depicts A/B/C, while a compact portrait row repeats A/B/C below it.

That may later support selection, accessibility or perspective clarity, but the current duplication risks drifting toward a profile/roster system.

No permanent roster row is authorized by this result.

### 5.2 Perspective identity clarity

The top-right `B` cue plus `B / Wren / (You)` makes Character-B bounded perspective recoverable to the research team, but the exact semantic distinction among:

- creator/account identity;
- Presentation Perspective;
- selected Character;
- Performer locus;

is not yet resolved as final UI.

Perspective must remain explicit enough to avoid disclosure ambiguity without becoming a large mode banner.

### 5.3 Environmental text leakage

The fictional whiteboard contained `Next Steps` and changed-state wording.

This effectively smuggled explanatory current-state material into the environment.

Required quarantine:

> **Environmental text must not become the renderer's substitute for legitimate information architecture.**

### 5.4 Rich-representation inference risk

Photoreal gaze/posture/expression invites interpretation of trust, confidence, skepticism, attention or intent.

Such portrayal never grants authority to infer those private states.

### 5.5 Generic polished-productivity shell

The clean top shell remains useful structural scaffolding but may read like conventional premium SaaS/productivity UI.

That is a surface-identity question, not a SIPD information-presence failure, and is intentionally deferred.
