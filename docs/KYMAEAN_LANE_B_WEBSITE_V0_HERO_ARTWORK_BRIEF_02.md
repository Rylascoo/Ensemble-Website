## Hero job

The hero should create a felt understanding before product vocabulary:

**Specific people inhabit one shared situation whose changed condition materially alters present possibility. The image should feel consequential and alive without functioning as forensic proof, diagram, storyboard or product UI.**

Artwork lens:

**HUMAN CAUSALITY, LIVING CONSEQUENCE.**

At hero level, the observable burden is necessary people, relational structure, changed shared condition and altered present possibility. Exact causal attribution is deferred to later deterministic sections.

## Required truths

A candidate must visibly establish:
1. at least two specific, non-interchangeable people;
2. one materially changed shared condition rather than arbitrary atmosphere;
3. consequence that changes what another person can presently reach/use/enter/avoid/inspect/resist/inherit/further alter;
4. one shared reality without inventing Knowledge/Belief/Memory/intent;
5. relationship carried by composition rather than connector/explanatory graphics;
6. quiet before spectacle.

Evidence of prior human history may strengthen a candidate, but identifying one specific earlier author is **not** required.

Identity may not depend only on color, portrait likeness, labels or exact position.

## Immediate failure

Reject if:
- people are decorative/interchangeable;
- changed condition is merely scenic or cosmetic;
- present possibility is not materially changed;
- private epistemic/mental state is asserted as fact;
- one dominant clue prop/mechanism carries nearly all meaning;
- portal/bridge/ruin/fantasy/AI shorthand dominates;
- it reads as app UI, explanatory storyboard or literal Stage-cone transcription;
- color alone carries meaning;
- mobile semantic read collapses;
- removing the consequential change leaves the composition's core proposition intact;
- **ORDINARY TASK TABLEAU MUST BE ABSENT** fails under the frozen Surface Escape gate.
